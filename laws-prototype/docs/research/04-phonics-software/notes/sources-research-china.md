# Research evidence + Chinese market — read 2026-11-01 (index)

## Evidence
- NRP 2000 (via ASHA summary https://apps.asha.org/EvidenceMaps/Articles/ArticleSummary/286a03c7-4785-4862-90de-62437ed45f82): systematic phonics mean ES 0.44; no significant difference between phonics TYPES (synthetic 0.45-ish, larger-unit, misc 0.27-0.43 in ASHA summary; Ehri 2001 breakdown: regular words d=0.67, pseudowords 0.60, irregular 0.40, comprehension 0.51); delivery mode ns (tutoring .57, small group .43, whole class .39); strongest K-1, at-risk.
- Rose 2006: SSP = 'prime approach'; teach GPCs in clearly defined incremental sequence; blending all through word; segmenting for spelling; blending/segmenting reversible; discrete teaching within broad language-rich curriculum; replace searchlights with Simple View.
- EEF Toolkit phonics: +5 months, moderate impact, very low cost, extensive evidence; improves accuracy more than comprehension; esp disadvantaged. https://educationendowmentfoundation.org.uk/education-evidence/teaching-learning-toolkit/phonics
- Castles Rastle Nation 2018 'Ending the Reading Wars': phonics central because alphabetic system; necessary not sufficient; morphology etc beyond. Critique side: Bowers (Bristol) argues meta-analyses mischaracterised, effects small (NRP overall d=0.41 immediate, 0.27 delayed; text reading 0.25, comprehension 0.27), PSC gains don't transfer to PIRLS; advocates SWI. Buckingham rebuttal: evidence strongly favours SSP. Present both.
- Ehri: phases pre-alphabetic (visual cues, logo reading) → partial (first/final letter connections, guessing) → full (complete GPC connections, true decoding) → consolidated (multi-letter units, morphemes/syllables as chunks). Orthographic mapping (2014): bonding spelling-pronunciation-meaning via phoneme segmentation + GPC knowledge; a 'sight word' = ANY word recognised automatically, result of mapping NOT whole-shape memorisation. https://eric.ed.gov/?id=EJ1027413
- Heart words: UFLI/RGR/Farrell model: irregular word = only 1-2 graphemes unexpected; map all phonemes→graphemes, heart ONLY the irregular part; temporary vs permanent irregularity (the: th+e hearts in Lesson 1, only e once th taught). RGR Heart Word Magic: underline irregular spelling + heart above; RGR distinguishes Tricky Heart Words (irregular HFW) vs Simple/Flash (regular HFW). Dolch 220: only 37% heart words, 63% decodable flash words (ThriveEd citing Farrell). UFLI heart word cards: hearts+boxes, introduce with symbols covered, find irregular part together. RWI Red Words + Jolly Tricky Words are older whole-word-ish versions of same slot (RWI parent docs literally say red words 'just have to learn off by heart' — criticisable under Ehri).
- Decodable vs leveled: Shanahan review (Reading Rockets https://www.readingrockets.org/blogs/shanahan-on-literacy/should-we-teach-decodable-text): only handful of studies since 1980s, messy definitions; Mesmer 2005 decodable→more decoding attempts, Mesmer 2010 leveled→more fluency; Cheatham & Allor 2012 review: decodables help apply phonics, mainly brief window in G1; Shanahan position: use decodables as phonics practice, severely limited dose, mix of text types. Recent meta-analysis cited: programmes combining decodable + other texts > only-decodable / no-decodable.
- App RCTs: Lexia NI RCT (above, small positive). GraphoGame Rime UK RCT (Frontiers https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/education/articles/10.3389/feduc.2020.00132/full): onset-rime analogy version; original UK RCT headline was weak/null overall; re-analysis of 'top half' players (played above mean, n=95) found gains vs business-as-usual — dose/compliance-dependent, subgroup caveat essential. Finnish GraphoGame (transparent orthography) stronger (Saine 2011: letter naming, fluency, spelling). Teach Your Monster: NO published RCT of the game itself found; makers themselves say building stronger evidence base into next version (MediaTaylor 2026 https://mediataylor.com/firm-but-unfair-the-spectators-war-on-edtech/); vendor-adjacent claims of '1.5 grade levels / 1000 students' trace to low-quality content sites, not peer review — flag as unverified. Systematic review of reading games/apps (Frontiers Psychol 2021): 55 RCT/quasi studies, 33 tools, heterogeneous, most medium risk of bias, need n>65/group, >15h interventions.
- Multisensory scrutiny: see OG note. Also Little Wandle drops songs/actions deliberately (cognitive overload). Jolly actions/songs = engagement/mnemonic value plausible, no isolated RCT evidence that action-per-sound adds to GPC learning beyond systematic teaching.
- Sound walls vs word walls: sound wall organised BY PHONEME (44), consonants by place/manner of articulation + vowel valley by mouth position, mouth photos per phoneme, graphemes listed under phoneme in frequency order; word wall organised by initial letter (adult logic, print-to-speech, promotes shape memorisation). Digital implication: sound wall = phoneme-first browsing; code chart = grapheme browsing. Sources: https://www.ldatschool.ca/shifting-from-word-walls-to-sound-walls/ ; https://pld-literacy.org/sound-walls-structured-synthetic-phonics-programs/
- Morphology/SWI: Bowers & Kirby 2010 grade 4/5: SWI group better at defining UNTAUGHT words sharing taught base (generative vocab); word matrices + word sums (base+affixes built visually, e.g. sign/signal/signature explains silent g); English is morphophonemic — spelling preserves meaning (ph in phone = Greek). SWI's 4 questions: meaning? structure? relatives? graphemes? UFLI handoff: lessons 119-128 affixes (-sion/-tion, -ture, -er/-or/-ist, bi-/tri-/uni-) + UFLI 'Word Origins' as morphology sequel. Lexia structural analysis strand similar. https://structuredwordinquiry.com/about/

## Chinese market （自然拼读）
- Generic Chinese sequence (British Council China handout https://www.britishcouncil.cn/sites/default/files/handout_apr_2026.pdf): Part1 Alphabet, Part2 Short Vowels, Part3 Long Vowels + Consonant Digraphs, Part4 Consonant Blends, Part5 Complex Vowels — i.e. organised by vowel complexity, NOT by early-word-building utility (contrast s-a-t-p). BC principles stated: 先听后读 (listen first 3-5 min pure listening), 短元音未吃透不教长元音， 用熟词学规则、用规则拼生词、拼后必理解， decodable passage per unit.
- 斑马 (Zebra): AI课听说为主， phonics inside system course; 斑马童书《斑马英语自然拼读训练》5册 (2024, GeekPark https://www.geekpark.net/news/343247): claims age-graded phoneme acquisition order for Chinese children, '感知-训练-拼读-阅读' progression, 67 teaching videos + 107 口型视频 (mouth-shape videos!) + 97 explanation videos, 5000 practice reps, 点读笔. Research book《自然拼读全攻略》(Tsinghua UP, Zebra教研中心 https://www.tup.tsinghua.edu.cn/upload/books/yz/103759-01.pdf): uses 美音 (American) pronunciation videos + mouth diagrams — most Chinese products default American.
- 叽里呱啦 (Jiliguala): 美语课 Lv0-Lv6, phonics as 主攻拼写的 strand inside main course; sequence per vendor/PR (Pedaily https://news.pedaily.cn/20250113/100909.shtml ; DTNews https://www.dtnews.net/archives/148176): 26 letter sounds → short vowel rules → long vowel rules → consonant combinations → autonomous reading; lesson loop = animation story (meaning in context) → foreign-teacher modelled reading （带读） → listening/speaking/tracing games → chant/song串联复习； matched decodable readers per rule + dedicated 拼读练习 function; explicit pitch: solves '会拼读，不懂词义'. American (CCSS-aligned), high song reliance.
- 洪恩 (iHuman): 洪恩英语 App Store: '字母拼读→单词积累→整句表达→流利对话' four-stage climb; BBC animations + Oxford leveled reading imported; phonics is entry ramp inside a听说 product, no published GPC sequence found. Chengdu media investigation criticised '7天掌握100个单词' style marketing / 超前 learning claims.
- ABCmouse.cn / 腾讯开心鼠英语： US ABCmouse localised by Tencent (PRNewswire 2018 https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/tencent-partners-with-age-of-learning-to-launch-abcmouse-immersive-english-learning-program-for-children-in-china-300625932.html): ESL sequence explicitly listening/understanding → speaking → phonics and reading → writing (i.e. phonics deliberately AFTER oral base — the EFL-correct order); 5000+ activities, full phonics course in 图书馆 zone + 600 graded readers; free-browse 'learning path + playground' hybrid; American pronunciation.
- 伴鱼 (Banyu): 伴鱼自然拼读 standalone (https://www.banyu.com/web/phonics): 26 letters, 130 core words, 156 picture-book sentences, 26 picture-book stories — i.e. letter-by-letter, each letter anchored to a decodable-ish story; 伴鱼绘本 A-Z graded library + 录绘本 recording/ranking. Lock-step criticism in reviews: must finish previous to unlock next, slow, cannot skip.
- Criticism of Chinese phonics teaching: (1) Prerequisite inversion — phonics in L1 assumes 3000-5000 hearing vocab; Chinese children often start with near-zero, so rules map print→sound→nothing (Douban essay https://www.douban.com/note/787591857/ : native = concept→sound→print, Chinese child = print→sound→concept, reversed). 盖兆泉： 1000-3000 listening words before phonics, age ~6; (2) 只背规则不读书 — rules memorised （先记规则后套词） without reading volume don't internalise; VIPKid misconceptions piece https://www.vipkid.com.cn/posts/30049.html lists 万能钥匙/太早/只背规则不读书/学完就扔； (3) exceptions overload (-ough six ways) used to argue phonics oversold '见词能读、听音能写'; (4) marketing 超前/速成 claims. Counter-design in better products: meaning-first animation, matched readers, listening-first staging (ABCmouse.cn, BC principles).
