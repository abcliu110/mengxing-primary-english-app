# Synthesis notes — read 2026-10-31/11-01 via web index (browser_search)

Key sources & facts:
- Refutation text meta: PubMed 35095236 — 44 comparisons n=3869 g=0.41 p<.001. Danielson et al 2025 Educ Psychologist cited therein.
- Muller thesis: collectedny.org summary — clear expository video: confidence up, scores unchanged; misconception-dialogue video: scores nearly doubled, rated confusing. CelebritySpeakers: thesis Designing Effective Multimedia for Physics Education, finding addressing misconceptions first essential.
- Contrasting cases: Schwartz & Bransford 1998 A Time for Telling (aaalab PDF) — contrasting cases + lecture > controls; cases alone insufficient. Holmqvist replication with class 4 (9-11y) English to have/to be contrast positive for generative learning.
- Concreteness fading: Fyfe McNeil Borjas 2015 grades 2-3 math equivalence, fading > concrete-only & abstract-only for transfer; Carbonneau 2013 meta 55 studies small-moderate, least effective 3-6y. Also Kokkonen physics secondary: order no advantage — conflict.
- Productive failure: Sinha & Kapur 2021 meta 53 studies g=0.36 PS-I > I-PS, but grades 2-5 trend favors I-PS; Mazziotti 5th grade no diff.
- Worked examples: Barbieri 2023 math meta g=0.48; expertise reversal meta: low prior d=0.505 high-assist, high prior d=-0.428, less clear for young + language/humanities.
- Seductive details: Sundararajan & Adesope via summary; 2026 meta cited g=-0.16, 50 studies 177 effects — small consistent tax. Decorative animations impair recall Pink & Newton 2020.
- Animation vs static: Tversky et al 2002 — no unqualified superiority; Kim et al 2007 grades 4&6 animation no comprehension advantage, transience.
- Prediction in children: Carvalho & Godwin 2025 age ~7 n=90 prediction attention d=1.92 but retrieval practice better overall learning; equal if attention maintained. J Cognition prediction-error boost: little evidence 5-10y, failed adult replication.
- Tyler 2012 book: CL experiments on modals, prepositions, clause constructions; motivated meaning-based explanations improve comprehension/retention — participants older learners, not 6-12.
- Prepositions CL: Hung et al 2018 quasi-exp 10 preps 4x90min CL > traditional spatial+metaphorical. Tyler & Evans principled polysemy: proto-scene + systematic extensions; in=containment, on=contact/support, over proto higher-than.
- Tense CL: Bielak & Pawlak 2011 n=50 high school CG only comparable to traditional, CG too complex/short treatment; 2013 claim outperformance cited in Hung paper — inconsistent summaries, treat as mixed. Simple past/past perfect CG study n=36 CG outperformed immediate posttest. 12-tense vs compositional tense+aspect reduces load for beginners (ERIC EJ1511004).
- Articles: Ionin Ko Wexler fluctuation — learners fluctuate definiteness vs specificity, overuse the with specific indefinites, a with nonspecific definites. Kim VIAL 2021 Korean n=84 same pattern + construe from syntax/modifiers + numerical approach to a. Cambridge multifactorial: the=identifiable discourse referent, a=introducing specific referent per learners (mis-mapping).
- Count/mass: Langacker bounded construal, homogeneous/heterogeneous internal composition, bounding NOT inherent (Romero Munoz et al citing Drozdz); Nicolas critique boundedness not necessary/sufficient; TESL-EJ ICNALE Mandarin writers: prototypical count/mass NOT main difficulty, flexible nouns are locus of errors.
- Gentner: structure-mapping 1983 systematicity, 1-to-1, relations > attributes; relational shift ~6-7y Gentner 1988 Child Dev 59:47-59 — attributional before relational; genuine relational metaphor beyond 10 in Winner studies cited.
- Glynn TWA: 6 steps incl. step 5 indicate where analogy breaks down; consensus guideline, direct RCT of step-5 alone thin.
- Phonics: Ehri et al 2001 NRP meta 66 comparisons d=0.41, early d=0.55.
- Etymology: Boers et al etymological elaboration for idioms — retention benefit, dual coding + levels of processing; mostly higher-ed adults; Boers 2011 review warns comparison groups weak → effect may be overestimated (Haugh scoping review summary).
- Working memory: Cowan et al visual WM ~3.5 items 7-8y, 4.4 at 9-10, 4.8 at 11-12; Riggs 1.52 at 5, 2.89 at 7, 3.83 at 10.
- ToM/common ground: Wilson & Katsos — 5-7y derive implicatures in common ground but fail to suppress when privileged ground; adolescents 11-13 audience design not adult-like (Gregoire). False belief ~4-5 is only first step.
- Chinese transfer: no tense inflection, no articles, no plural/agreement marking → top errors: articles, verb tense/form, plural, 3rd person -s, prepositions (ERIC EJ1075686 Chinese EFL free writing: articles most difficult). le/guo do not parallel present perfect; learners treat perfect as completed event, adverbial-reliance. Classifier system: Chinese requires classifier for counting — actually resource for count individuation, contrast with English bare mass.
- Bruner spiral: 1960 p33 bold hypothesis quote, no direct RCT base; treat as design heuristic + spacing/retrieval support indirect.
- 3B1B: Wikipedia/Stanford — visual perspective, inventing math/discovery, emotion wonder imagination over usefulness. Kurzgesagt own How to Make video (LingQ transcript of their script): research papers+experts fact-check, script rewritten many times, visual metaphors/transitions, ~1200h/video, nothing is boring if tell good story (PluggedIn).
