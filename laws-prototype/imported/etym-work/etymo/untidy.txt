Origin and history of untidy untidy (adj.) early 13c., untidi , "untimely, unseasonable, unsuitable" (senses now obsolete), from un- (1) "not" + tidy (adj.). The meaning "wretched, mean; poorly cared for; not neat, orderly, or clean" is recorded from mid-14c. Similar formation in West Frisian ontidich , Middle Dutch ontidich , Dutch ontijdig , Old High German unzitich , German unzeitig , Norwegian utidig "untimely, unseasonable, unfavorable." also from early 13c. untidy (v.) "make untidy," 1891, from untidy (adj.). Related: Untidied ; untidying . also from 1891
