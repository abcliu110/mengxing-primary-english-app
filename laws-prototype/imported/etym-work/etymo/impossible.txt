Origin and history of impossible impossible (adj.) late 14c., from Old French impossible (14c.), from Latin impossibilis "not possible," from assimilated form of in- "not, opposite of" (see in- (1)) + possibilis (see possible ). Weakened sense of "unable to be accomplished or tolerated" is from mid-19c. Related: Impossibly . Less common unpossible also is from late 14c. also from late 14c.
