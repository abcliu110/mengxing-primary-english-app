Origin and history of your your denoting something in the charge, control, or possession of the person addressed, Old English eower , possessive pronominal adjective, genitive of ge "ye" (see ye ). It is from the Proto-Germanic base of you . Germanic cognates include Old Saxon iuwar , Old Frisian iuwer , Old Norse yðvarr , Old High German iuwer , German euer , Gothic izwar "your." Its use in titles of honor (e.g. your majesty ) is attested by mid-14c.
