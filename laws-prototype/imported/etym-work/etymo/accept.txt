Origin and history of accept accept (v.) late 14c., accepten , "take what is offered; admit and agree to (a proposal, etc.)," from Old French accepter (14c.) or directly from Latin acceptare "take or receive willingly," frequentative of accipere "receive, get without effort," from ad "to" (see ad- ) + capere "to take" (from PIE root *kap- "to grasp"). Related: Accepted ; accepting . also from late 14c.
