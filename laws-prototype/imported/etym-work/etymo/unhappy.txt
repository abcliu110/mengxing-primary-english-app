Origin and history of unhappy unhappy (adj.) c. 1300, unhappi , "causing misfortune or trouble" to oneself or others, from un- (1) "not" + happy . The meaning "unfortunate, unlucky" is recorded from late 14c.; the sense of "miserable, wretched (originally via misfortune or mishap), not cheerful or gay" is by late 14c. also from c. 1300
