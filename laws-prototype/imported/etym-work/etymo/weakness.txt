Origin and history of weakness weakness (n.) c. 1300, "quality, state, or character of being weak," from weak (adj.) + -ness . The meaning "a disadvantage, vulnerability, a weak point" is from 1590s. That of "self-indulgent fondness" is from 1712; the meaning "thing for which one has an indulgent fondness" is from 1822. also from c. 1300
