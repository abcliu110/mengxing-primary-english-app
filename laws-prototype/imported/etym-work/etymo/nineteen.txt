Origin and history of nineteen nineteen (adj., n.) "1 more than eighteen, nine more than ten; the cardinal number composed of 10 and 9; a symbol representing this number;" Middle English nīntene , from late Old English nigontene (Anglian), nigontyne (West Saxon); see nine + -teen . Cognate with Old Saxon nigentein , Old Frisian niogentena , Dutch negentien , Old High German niunzehan , German neunzehn , Old Norse nitjan , Danish nitten .
