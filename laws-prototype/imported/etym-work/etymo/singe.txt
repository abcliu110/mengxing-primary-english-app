Origin and history of singe singe (v.) Middle English sengen , from Old English sengan , sæncgan "to burn (something) lightly or superficially, burn the edges" (of hair, wings, etc.), from Proto-Germanic *sangjanan (source also of Old Frisian of-sendza , Middle Dutch singhen , Dutch zengen , Old High German sengan , German sengen "to singe"). The root is said to be related to that of sing (v.), on the idea of some sort of sound produced by singeing (Century Dictionary), but Klein's sources reject this. Related: Singed ; singeing .
