Origin and history of because because (conj.) late 14c., from phrase bi cause , introducing a subordinate clause or phrase, "by cause, for the reason that," from by (prep.) + cause (n.). Modeled on French par cause . Originally often followed by that or why . As an adverb, "by reason, on account" (with of ), from late 14c. Clipped form cause (sometimes 'cause ) is attested in writing by mid-15c. also from late 14c.
