Origin and history of tourist tourist (n.) 1772, "one who makes a journey for pleasure, stopping here and there to see the sights" (originally especially "a travel-writer"), from tour (n.) + -ist . Tourer in this sense is by 1931. Tourist center is by 1922. Tourist trap is 1891, originally typically a hotel for tourists (perhaps on the "mouse trap" image), later a district that caters to them, especially one in Europe (Montmartre, e.g.) as considered by American travelers. It is a well-established fact that Italy is one vast tourist trap and that every Italian is laying for American gold. ["Santa Monica's Fame as Winter Resort Growing," Los Angeles Times, Aug. 6, 1911] Tourist-trap also is by 1912 as "thing made to lure tourists or be sold to them." Related: Touristic . also from 1772
