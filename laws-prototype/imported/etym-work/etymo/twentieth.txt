Origin and history of twentieth twentieth (adj., n.) "next in order after the nineteenth; an ordinal numeral; being one of twenty equal parts into which a whole is regarded as divided;" 16c., from twenty + -th (1). It replaced Middle English twentithe , from Old English twentigoða . Middle English also had as a numeral twentiand , with substitution of Old Norse suffix. The Twentieth Century Limited was an express train from New York to Chicago 1902-1967. also from 16c.
