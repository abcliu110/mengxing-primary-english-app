Origin and history of stellar stellar (adj.) 1650s, "of or pertaining to stars;" 1660s as "star-shaped, star-like;" from Late Latin stellaris "pertaining to a star, starry," from stella "star," from PIE *sterla- , suffixed form of root *ster- (2) "star." The meaning "outstanding, leading" (1883) is from the theatrical sense of star . also from 1650s
