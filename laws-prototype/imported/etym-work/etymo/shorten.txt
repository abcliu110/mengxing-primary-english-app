Origin and history of shorten shorten (v.) mid-14c., shortenen , "make shorter;" late 14c., "grow shorter," from short (adj.) + -en (1); the earlier form of the verb was simply short , from Old English sceortian "to grow short, become short; run short, fail," gescyrtan "to make short." For cookery sense (1733) see shortening . Related: Shortened . also from mid-14c.
