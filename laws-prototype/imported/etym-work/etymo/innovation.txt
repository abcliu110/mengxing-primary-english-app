Origin and history of innovation innovation (n.) mid-15c., innovacion , "restoration, renewal," from Late Latin innovationem (nominative innovatio ), noun of action from past-participle stem of innovare "to change; to renew," from in- "into" (see in- (2)) + novus "new" (see new ). Meaning "a novel change, experimental variation, new thing introduced in an established arrangement" is from 1540s. also from mid-15c.
