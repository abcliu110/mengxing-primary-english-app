Origin and history of refill refill (v.) also re-fill , "to fill again," 1680s, from re- "back, again" + fill (v.). Related: Refilled ; refilling . also from 1680s refill (n.) "an act of filling again; that which serves to refill anything," 1884, from refill (v.). Meaning "a second drink" is from 1909; by 1923 as "the renewed contents of a glass." also from 1884
