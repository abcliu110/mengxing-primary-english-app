Origin and history of disagree disagree (v.) late 15c., "refuse assent to," from Old French desagreer (12c.), from des- "not, opposite of" (see dis- ) + agreer "to please, satisfy; to receive with favor, take pleasure in" (see agree ). Sense of "differ in opinion, express contrary views" is from 1550s. Related: Disagreed ; disagreeing . also from late 15c.
