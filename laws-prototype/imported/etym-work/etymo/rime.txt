Origin and history of rime rime (n.) "hoarfrost, white frost," Old English hrim , from Proto-Germanic *khrima- (source also of Old Norse hrim , Dutch rijm , German Reif ). Old French rime is of Germanic origin. Rare in Middle English and usually there as rime-frost ; after c. 1500 it seems to have survived mainly in Scottish and northern English until it was revived in literary use in late 18c.
