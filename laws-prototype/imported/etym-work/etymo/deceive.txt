Origin and history of deceive deceive (v.) "mislead by false appearance or statement," c. 1300, from Old French decevoir "to deceive" (12c., Modern French décevoir ), from Latin decipere "to ensnare, take in, beguile, cheat," from de "from" or pejorative (see de- ) + combining form of capere "to take," from PIE root *kap- "to grasp." Related: Deceived ; deceiver ; deceiving . also from c. 1300
