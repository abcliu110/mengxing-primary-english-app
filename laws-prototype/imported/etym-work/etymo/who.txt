Origin and history of who who (pron.) Old English hwa "what person," sometimes also "what; anyone, someone; each; whosoever," from Proto-Germanic *hwas (source also of Old Saxon hwe , Danish hvo , Swedish vem , Old Frisian hwa , Dutch wie , Old High German hwer , German wer , Gothic hvo (fem.) "who"), from PIE root *kwo- , stem of relative and interrogative pronouns.
