Origin and history of announce announce (v.) c. 1500, "proclaim, make known formally," from Old French anoncier "announce, proclaim" (12c., Modern French annoncer ), from Latin annuntiare , adnuntiare "to announce, make known," literally "bring news to," from ad "to" (see ad- ) + nuntiare "relate, report," from nuntius "messenger" (from PIE root *neu- "to shout"). Related: Announced ; announcing . also from c. 1500
