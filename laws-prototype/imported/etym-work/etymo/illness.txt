Origin and history of illness illness (n.) "disease, attack of sickness, ailment, malady," 1680s, from ill (adj.) + -ness . Earlier it meant "bad moral quality, evilness" (c. 1500). Sick and sickness have been considered until within the present century essentially synonymous with ill and illness . Of late, English usage has tended to restrict sick and sickness to nausea, and American usage has followed it so far as to regard illness as a rather more elegant and less definite term.
