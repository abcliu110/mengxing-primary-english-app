Origin and history of painter painter (n.1) early 14c., peintour , "artist who paints pictures," from Old French peintor , from Latin pictor "a painter," from pingere (see paint (v.)). Sense of "workman who colors surfaces with paint" is from c. 1400. As a surname, Painter is attested from mid-13c. but it is difficult to say which sense is meant. Related: Painterly . also from early 14c. painter (n.2) mid-14c., "rope or chain that holds an anchor to a ship's side," probably from Old French peintor , ultimately from Latin pendere "to hang, cause to hang" (from PIE root *(s)pen- "to draw, stretch, spin"). Extended generally to "rope attached to the bow of a boat." also from mid-14c.
