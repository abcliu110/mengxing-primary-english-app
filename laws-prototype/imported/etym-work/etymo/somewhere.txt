Origin and history of somewhere somewhere (adv.) late 12c., "in an unspecified or undetermined place," from some + where . The meaning "elsewhere, in some other place" is from c. 1300; somewhere else is attested from late 14c. A dateline of somewhere in ____ making reference to some locality without identifying it for security or censorship purposes dates from World War I ( somewhere in France ). also from late 12c.
