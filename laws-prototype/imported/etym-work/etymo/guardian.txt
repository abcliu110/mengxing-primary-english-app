Origin and history of guardian guardian (n.) "one who guards," early 14c., garden ; early 15c., gardein , from Anglo-French gardein (late 13c.), Old French gardien "keeper, custodian," earlier guarden , from Frankish *warding- , from the Germanic source of guard (v.). Specific legal sense is from 1510s. Guardian angel is from 1630s. also from early 14c.
