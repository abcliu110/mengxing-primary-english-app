Origin and history of kowtow kowtow (n.) also kow-tow , 1804, from Chinese k'o-t'ou, custom of touching the ground with the forehead while kneeling as a gesture of respect or submission, literally "knock the head," from k'o "knock, bump" + t'ou "head." also from 1804 kowtow (v.) also kow-tow , 1826 in the figurative sense of "act in an obsequious manner," from kowtow (n.).
