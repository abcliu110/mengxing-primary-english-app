centigrade (adj.) "consisting of 100 degrees, divided into 100 equal parts," 1799, from French, from centi- "hundred" (see centi- ) + second element from Latin gradi "to walk, go, step" (from PIE root *ghredh- "to walk, go"). The centigrade thermometer (see Celsius ) divides the interval between the freezing and boiling points of water into 100 degrees.
