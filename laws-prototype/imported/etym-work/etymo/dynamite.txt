Origin and history of dynamite dynamite (n.) powerful explosive consisting of a mixture of nitroglycerine with an absorbent, 1867, from Swedish dynamit , coined 1867 by its inventor, Swedish chemist Alfred Nobel (1833-1896), from Greek dynamis "power" (see dynamic (adj.)) + -ite (2). Figurative sense of "something potentially dangerous" is from 1922. Positive sense of "dynamic and excellent" by mid-1960s, perhaps originally African-American vernacular. also from 1867 dynamite (v.) "to blow up or destroy by dynamite," by 1878, from dynamite (n.). Related: Dynamited ; dynamiting . also from 1878
