Origin and history of myself myself (pron.) emphatic or reflexive form of I or me , c. 1500, mi-self , alteration of meself (c. 1200), from Old English phrase (ic) me self , where me is "a kind of ethical dative" [OED]. See my + self . The alteration from meself is by analogy of herself , where her- was felt as genitive (though analogous hisself remains bad form). also from c. 1500
