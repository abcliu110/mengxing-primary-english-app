Origin and history of evoke evoke (v.) "to call or summon forth or out," 1620s, from French évoquer or directly from Latin evocare "call out, rouse, summon," from assimilated form of ex "out" (see ex- ) + vocare "to call," which is related to vox (genitive vocis ) "voice" (from PIE root *wekw- "to speak"). Often more or less with a sense of "calling spirits," or being called by them. Of feelings, memories, etc., by 1856. Related: Evoked ; evokes ; evoking . also from 1620s
