Origin and history of speech speech (n.) Middle English speche , from Old English spæc "act of speaking; power of uttering articulate sounds; manner of speaking; statement, discourse, narrative, formal utterance; language." It is a variant of Old English spræc , which is from Proto-Germanic *sprek- , *spek- (source also of Danish sprog , Old Saxon spraca , Old Frisian spreke , Dutch spraak , Old High German sprahha , German Sprache "speech"). See speak (v.). The spr- forms were extinct in English by 1200. In reference to written words by c. 1200. The meaning "address delivered to an audience" is recorded by 1580s.
