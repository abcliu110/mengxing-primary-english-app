Origin and history of goldfish goldfish (n.) type of ornamental fish, 1690s, from gold (adj.) + fish (n.). The fish were introduced into England from China, where they are native. A type of carp, they are naturally a dull olive color; the rich colors (also red, black, silver) are obtained by selective breeding. Goldfish bowl , figurative of a situation of no privacy, was in use by 1931. also from 1690s
