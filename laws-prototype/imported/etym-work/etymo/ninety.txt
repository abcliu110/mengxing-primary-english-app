Origin and history of ninety ninety (adj., n.) "9 times ten; the number which is one more than eighty-nine or 10 less than one hundred; a symbol representing this number;" Middle English nīntī (late 13c.), from Old English nigontig , from nine + -tig "group of ten" (see -ty (1)). Cognate with Old Frisian niontich , Middle Dutch negentich , Dutch negentig , German neunzig , Old Norse nintigir . also from late 13c.
