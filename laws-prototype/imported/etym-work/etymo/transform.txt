Origin and history of transform transform (v.) late 14c., transformen , "alter or change the form of supernaturally" (transitive), also generally "change the condition of; cause to take the form of" (with into ); from Old French transformer (14c.), from Latin transformare "change in shape, metamorphose," from trans "across, beyond" (see trans- ) + formare "to form" (see form (v.)). By 1550s as "change the nature, character, or disposition of." The intransitive sense "undergo a change of form" is from 1590s in English. Related: Transformed ; transforming . also from late 14c.
