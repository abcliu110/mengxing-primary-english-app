Origin and history of introduce introduce (v.) early 15c., introducen , "convey or bring (something) in or into," a back-formation from introduction or else from Latin introducere "to lead in, bring in," from intro- "inward, to the inside" (see intro- ) + ducere "to lead" (from PIE root *deuk- "to lead"). The meaning "bring forward, open to notice" (of a subject, etc.) is from 1550s. The sense of "bring into personal acquaintance, make known" (as of one person to another) is from 1650s. Related: Introduced ; introducing . also from early 15c.
