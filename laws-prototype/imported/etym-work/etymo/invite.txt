Origin and history of invite invite (v.) "solicit to come," 1530s, a back-formation from invitation , or else from French inviter (15c.), from Latin invitare "to invite," also "to summon, challenge; to feast, to entertain," a word of uncertain origin. Related: Invited ; inviting . also from 1530s invite (n.) "an invitation," 1650s, from invite (v.). also from 1650s
