Origin and history of winner winner (n.) "one who or that which earns, keeps, or produces wealth," late 14c. (c. 1200 as a surname), agent noun from win (v.). Also "unscrupulous profiteer" (mid-15c.), and "one who triumphs in a contest or game" (mid-15c.). Adjectival winner-take-all is attested from 1901. also from late 14c.
