Origin and history of homework homework (n.) also home-work , 1680s, "work done at home," as opposed to work done in the shop or factory, from home (n.) + work (n.). In the specific sense of "lessons and exercises to be done by a school-child at home" it is attested by 1889. Holiday task "school work to be done during holiday" is attested by 1827. To do (one's) homework in the figurative sense of "be prepared" is attested by 1934. also from 1680s
