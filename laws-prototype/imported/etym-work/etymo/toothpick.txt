toothpick (n.) also tooth-pick , "implement (of wood, ivory, etc.) for cleaning teeth of substances stuck between them," late 15c., from tooth (n.) + pick (n.). Old English had toðsticca , and pick-tooth , tooth-picker in the same sense are attested from 1540s. As U.S. slang for "Bowie knife" by 1867. also from late 15c.
