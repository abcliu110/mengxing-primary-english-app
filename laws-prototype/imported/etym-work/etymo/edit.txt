Origin and history of edit edit (v.) 1791, "to publish," perhaps a back-formation from editor , or from French éditer (itself a back-formation from édition ) or from Latin editus , past participle of edere "give out, put out, publish" (see edition ). Meaning "to supervise for publication" is from 1793. Meaning "make revisions to a manuscript, etc.," is from 1885. Related: Edited ; editing . As a noun, by 1960, "an act of editing." also from 1791
