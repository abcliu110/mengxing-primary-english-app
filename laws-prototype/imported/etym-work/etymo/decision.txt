Origin and history of decision decision (n.) mid-15c., decisioun , "act of deciding," from Old French décision (14c.), from Latin decisionem (nominative decisio ) "a decision, settlement, agreement," noun of action from past-participle stem of decidere "to decide, determine," literally "to cut off," from de "off" (see de- ) + caedere "to cut" (from PIE root *kae-id- "to strike"). The meaning "final judgment or opinion in a case" is from 1550s. The meaning "quality of being decided in character, ability to make prompt determinations" is attested by 1781; the sense of "a resolution, a fixing of purpose" is by 1886. Decision-making (adj.) is recorded by 1946. also from mid-15c.
