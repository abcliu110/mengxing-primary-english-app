Origin and history of drought drought (n.) Old English drugaþ , drugoþ "continuous dry weather injurious to vegetation, dryness," from Proto-Germanic *drugothaz , from Germanic root *dreug- "dry" with *-itho , Germanic suffix for forming abstract nouns. See dry (adj.) + -th (2), and compare high/height , etc. Drouth was a Middle English variant continued in Scottish and northern English dialect and in poetry.
