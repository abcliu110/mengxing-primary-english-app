Origin and history of quiche quiche (n.) "onion pie," a specialty of the Alsace-Lorraine region, ca. 1884 in a French context, from French quiche (1810), from Alsatian German Küche , diminutive of German Kuchen "cake" (see cake (n.)). The food became fashionable 1970s; became contemptible as indicative of wimpiness 1980s. also from ca. 1884
