Origin and history of unfriend unfriend (v.) in the Facebook sense, attested from November 2007, from un- (2) "opposite of" + friend (v.). Unfriended (adj.) is by 1510s in the sense "friendless." A noun unfriend "enemy, adversary" is recorded from late 13c., survived chiefly in Scottish, and was still in use in 19c. Unfriendship "enmity" is from mid-14c. also from 2007
