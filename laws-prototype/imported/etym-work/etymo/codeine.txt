Origin and history of codeine codeine (n.) "white crystalline alkaloid present in opium," 1838, codeina , from French codéine , coined, with chemical suffix -ine (2), from Greek kodeia "poppy head," related to koos "prison," literally "hollow place;" kodon "bell, mouth of a trumpet;" koilos "hollow, hollowed out, spacious, deep," all from PIE root *keue- "to swell," also "vault, hole." Modern form is from 1881. also from 1838
