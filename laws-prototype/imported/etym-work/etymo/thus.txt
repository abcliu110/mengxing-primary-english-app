Origin and history of thus thus (adv.) "in the manner or state being indicated," from Old English þus "in this way, as follows," related to þæt "that" and this ; from Proto-Germanic *thus- (source also of Old Saxon and Old Frisian thus , Middle Dutch and Dutch dus ). Thus far "to this point" is attested by 1590s. Middle English also used thuswise , thusgates "in this manner;" also thiskin (c. 1300), and compare thusly .
