Origin and history of animate animate (v.) 1530s, "to fill with boldness or courage," from Latin animatus past participle of animare "give breath to," also "to endow with a particular spirit, to give courage to, enliven," from anima "life, breath" (from PIE root *ane- "to breathe"). The sense of "give natural life to" in English is attested from 1742. The meaning "render in moving pictures" is by 1888 ( animated pictures ); in reference to cinematic cartoons by 1911.
