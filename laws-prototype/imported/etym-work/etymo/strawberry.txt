Origin and history of strawberry strawberry (n.) Middle English strauberi , used of the plant or its "fruit," from Old English streawberige , streaberie ; see straw + berry . The reason for the name is uncertain; perhaps it is in reference to the tiny chaff-like external seeds which cover the fruit, or the long stems on which the fruit grows. The natural fruits could be colored red, white or green. A cognate Old English name was eorðberge "earth-berry" (compare Modern German Erdbeere ). As a color adjective from 1670s. Strawberry blonde (adj.), indicating a hair shade with some red in it, is attested from 1879, perhaps from the coloring of white strawberries. Strawberry mark (1818) so called for its resemblance.
