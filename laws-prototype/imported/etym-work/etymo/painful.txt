Origin and history of painful painful (adj.) mid-14c., peineful , "characterized by or attended by pain" (originally of the Crucifixion), from pain (n.) + -ful . Meaning "causing physical pain" is from c. 1400; that of "inflicting pain" (of punishments, etc.) is by mid-15c. Related: Painfully ; painfulness . also from mid-14c.
