Origin and history of math math (n.1) American English shortening of mathematics , 1890; the British preference, maths , is attested from 1911. " Math. is used as an abbreviation in written English in the U.K. but not in speech, the normal form being Maths " [OED]. also from 1890 math (n.2) "a mowing, what is gathered from mowing," Old English mæð "mowing, cutting of grass," from Proto-Germanic *mediz (source also of Old Frisian meth , Old High German mad , German Mahd "mowing, hay crop"), from PIE root *me- (4) "to cut down grass or grain." Obsolete except in figurative aftermath .
