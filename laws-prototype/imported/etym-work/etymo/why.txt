Origin and history of why why (adv., conj.) Middle English whi , for what cause, reason, or purpose; on account of which," from Old English hwi , instrumental case (indicating for what purpose or by what means) of hwæt (see what ), from Proto-Germanic adverb *hwi (source also of Old Saxon hwi , Old Norse hvi ), from PIE *kwi- (source of Greek pei "where"), locative of root *kwo- , stem of relative and interrogative pronouns. As an interjection of surprise or emphasis, recorded by mid-14c. As a noun, "cause, reason" from c. 1300.
