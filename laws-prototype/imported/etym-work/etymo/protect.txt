Origin and history of protect protect (v.) "cover or shield from danger, harm, damage, exposure, trespass, temptation, insult, etc.," early 15c., protecten , from Latin protectus , past participle of protegere "to protect, defend, cover over, cover in front" (source also of French protéger , Old French protecter , Spanish proteger ). This is from pro "before" (from PIE root *per- (1) "forward," hence "in front of, before") + tegere "to cover" (from PIE root *(s)teg- "to cover"). Applied with a wide range, both literal and figurative. The sense in political economy, "guard or strengthen against foreign competition by means of tariffs, etc.," is by 1789. Related: Protected ; protecting . also from early 15c.
