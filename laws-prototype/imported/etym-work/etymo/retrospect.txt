Origin and history of retrospect retrospect (n.) c. 1600, "a regard or reference" (to something), from Latin retrospectum , past participle of retrospicere "look back," from retro "back" (see retro- ) + specere "look at" (from PIE root *spek- "to observe"). Meaning "survey of past events" is from 1660s. also from c. 1600
