Origin and history of regal regal (adj.) "kingly, pertaining to a king," late 14c., from Old French regal "royal" (12c., Modern French réal ) and directly from Latin regalis "royal, kingly; of or belonging to a king, worthy of a king," from rex (genitive regis ) "king," from PIE root *reg- "move in a straight line," with derivatives meaning "to direct in a straight line," thus "to lead, rule." Related: Regally . also from late 14c.
