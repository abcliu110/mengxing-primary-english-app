Origin and history of thermal thermal (adj.) 1756, "having to do with hot springs," from French thermal (Buffon), from Greek therm ē "heat, feverish heat" (from PIE root *gwher- "to heat, warm"). The sense of "having to do with heat" is recorded by 1837. The noun meaning "rising current of relatively warm air" is recorded from 1933. also from 1756
