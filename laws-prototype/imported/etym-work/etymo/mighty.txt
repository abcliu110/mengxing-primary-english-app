Origin and history of mighty mighty (adj.) "possessed of or endowed with might; having much ability, strength, or power," Old English mihtig , earlier mæhtig , from Proto-Germanic *mahtiga- (source also of Old Frisian mechtig , Old Saxon mahtig , Dutch machtig , German mächtig ), from the source of might (n.). As an adverb, "very, exceedingly, greatly," it is attested from c. 1300, though such use now is considered colloquial.
