Origin and history of bamboo bamboo (n.) type of giant grass common in the tropics, 1590s, from Dutch bamboe and/or Portuguese bambu , earlier mambu (16c.), probably from Malay (Austronesian) samambu , though some suspect this is itself an imported word, perhaps from Kanarese (Dravidian). Bamboo curtain in reference to communist China (based on iron curtain ) is from 1949. also from 1590s
