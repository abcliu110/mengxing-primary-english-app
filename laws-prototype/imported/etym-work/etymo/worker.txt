Origin and history of worker worker (n.) mid-14c., werker , "laborer, toiler, performer, doer," agent noun from work (v.). By late 14c. as "maker, crafter." As a type of bee that performs labor and services for the hive community, 1747 ( worker-bee is by 1816). As "one employed for a wage," 1848. Old English had wyrcend "worker, laborer." also from mid-14c.
