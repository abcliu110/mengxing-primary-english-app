Origin and history of eighteen eighteen (adj., n.) "1 more than seventeen, twice nine; the number which is one more than seventeen; a symbol representing this number;" late 14c., eightene , earlier ahtene (c. 1200), from Old English eahtatiene , eahtatyne ; see eight + -teen . Cognate with Old Frisian schtatine , Old Saxon ahtotian , Dutch achttien , Old High German ahtozehan , German achtzehn , Old Norse attjan , Swedish adertån . also from late 14c.
