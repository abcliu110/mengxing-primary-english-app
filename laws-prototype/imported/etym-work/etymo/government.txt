Origin and history of government government (n.) late 14c. (Chaucer) governement , "act of governing or ruling;" also "system by which a thing is governed" (celestial bodies, states, etc.), from Old French governement "control, direction, administration" (Modern French gouvernement ), from governer "to steer, be at the helm of; govern, rule, command, direct," from Latin gubernare "to direct, rule, guide, govern," originally "to steer, to pilot"(see govern ). The meaning "the governing power" in a given place is from 1702. Compare governance . also from late 14c.
