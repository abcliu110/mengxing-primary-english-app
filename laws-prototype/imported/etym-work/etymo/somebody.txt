Origin and history of somebody somebody (n.) c. 1300 as two words, from 16c. as one word, "indeterminate person, person unnamed or unknown," from some + body . Used in place of the name of a person whose name is to be unspoken, by c. 1600. The meaning "important person, person of consequence" is from 1560s. Related: Somebodies . Somebody else is from 1640s as "some other person;" in the sense of "romantic rival" it is from 1911. also from c. 1300
