Origin and history of good-bye good-bye salutation in parting, also goodbye , good bye , good-by , 1590s, from godbwye (1570s), a contraction of God be with ye (late 14c.), influenced by good-day , good evening , etc. As a noun from 1570s. Intermediate forms in 16c. include God be wy you , God b'uy , God buoye , God buy , etc. Compare adieu .
