Origin and history of wooden wooden (adj.) 1530s, "made of wood," from wood (n.) + -en (2). Figurative sense of "stiff, ungainly, clumsy" is by 1560s. Wooden nickel as a type of something worthless ( wouldn't give a wooden nickel for .... ) is by 1892, American English. Related: Woodenly ; woodenness . also from 1530s
