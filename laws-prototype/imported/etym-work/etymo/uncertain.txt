Origin and history of uncertain uncertain (adj.) early 14c., "of indeterminate time or occurrence," also "unknown and unknowable," also "not firm or fixed, unreliable, random;" from un- (1) "not" + certain (adj.). In reference to persons, "not fully confident, lacking in sure knowledge," it is recorded from late 14c. (implied in uncertainty ). Related: Uncertainly . also from early 14c.
