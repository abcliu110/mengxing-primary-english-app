Origin and history of earthquake earthquake (n.) "movement or vibration of a part of the earth's crust," late 13c., eorthequakynge , from earth + quake (n.). In this sense Old English had eorðdyn , eorðhrernes , eorðbeofung , eorðstyrung . Middle English also had terre-mote "earthquake" (late 14c.), from Old French terremote, from Latin terrae motus , from terra "earth" (see terra ) + motus "a moving, motion," past participle of movere "to move" (see move (v.)). also from late 13c.
