Origin and history of hearth hearth (n.) Old English heorð "hearth, fireplace, part of a floor on which a fire is made," also in transferred use "house, home, fireside," from Proto-Germanic *hertha- "burning place" (source also of Old Saxon and Old Frisian herth , Middle Dutch hert , Dutch haard , German Herd "floor, ground, fireplace"), from PIE *kerta- , from root *ker- (3) "heat, fire." Hearth-rug is from 1824. Hearth-stone is from early 14c.
