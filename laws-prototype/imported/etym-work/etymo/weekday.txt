Origin and history of weekday weekday (n.) Middle English weke-dai , "one of the seven days of the week," but especially any day other than Sunday; from Old English wicudæge , wucudæge "day of the week" (similar formation in Old High German wehhatag , Old Norse vikudagr ); see week + day . Week-night , weeknight is attested by 1859. Also compare weekend . Archaic week's day was "day of last or next week corresponding to the present day."
