Origin and history of feeling feeling (n.) late 12c., "act of touching, sense of touch," verbal noun from feel (v.). The meaning "a conscious emotion" is mid-14c. The sense of "what one feels (about something), opinion" is from mid-15c. The meaning "capacity to feel" is by 1580s. also from late 12c. feeling (adj.) c. 1400, "pertaining to the physical senses, sensory," present-participle adjective from feel (v.). Related: Feelingly . also from c. 1400
