Origin and history of espionage espionage (n.) 1793, from French espionnage "spying," from espionner "to spy," from espion "a spy" (16c.), probably via Old Italian spione from a Germanic source akin to Old High German spehon "to spy" (see spy (v.)). For initial e- see e- . Middle English had espiouress "female spy" (early 15c.). also from 1793
