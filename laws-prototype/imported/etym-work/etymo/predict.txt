Origin and history of predict predict (v.) 1620s (implied in predicted ), "foretell, prophesy, declare before the event happens," a back formation from prediction or else from Latin praedicatus , past participle of praedicere "foretell, advise, give notice," from prae "before" (see pre- ) + dicere "to say" (from PIE root *deik- "to show," also "pronounce solemnly").
