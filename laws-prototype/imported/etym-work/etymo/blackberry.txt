Origin and history of blackberry blackberry (n.) "fruit of the bramble," early 12c., from Old English blaceberian , from black (adj.) + berry . So called for the color. Also in Old English as bremelberie , bremelæppel (from bramble ). The wireless handheld device of the same name was introduced 1999 and decommissioned in 2022. Related: Blackberrying . also from early 12c.
