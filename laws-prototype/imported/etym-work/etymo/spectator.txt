Origin and history of spectator spectator (n.) "one who looks on, a beholder," 1580s, from Latin spectator "viewer, watcher," agent noun from past-participle stem of spectare "to view, watch" (see spectacle ). Related: Spectatorial; spectatorship . Fem. form spectatress (1630s) is less classically correct than spectatrix (1610s). Spectator sport , one which affords entertainment for spectators, is attested from 1943. also from 1580s
