Origin and history of activity activity (n.) c. 1400, "active or secular life," from Old French activité , from Medieval Latin activitatem (nominative activitas ), a word in Scholastic philosophy, from Latin activus "active" (see active ). The meaning "state of being active, briskness, liveliness" is recorded from 1520s; that of "capacity for acting on matter" is from 1540s. As "an educational exercise," by 1923. also from c. 1400
