Origin and history of sunflower sunflower (n.) 1560s, "heliotrope, any sun-following flower," from sun (n.) + flower (n.). In reference to the Helianthus, the flowering plant of central North America (introduced to Europe 1510 by the Spaniards), it is attested from 1590s, so called from the appearance of the heads. Kansas has been the Sunflower State since 1881. also from 1560s
