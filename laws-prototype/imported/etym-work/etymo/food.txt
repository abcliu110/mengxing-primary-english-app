Origin and history of food food (n.) Middle English foode , fode , from Old English foda "food, nourishment; fuel," also figurative, from Proto-Germanic *fodon (source also of Swedish föda , Danish föde , Gothic fodeins ), from Germanic *fod- "food," from PIE *pat- , extended form of root *pa- "to feed." Food chain is by 1915. Food poisoning attested by 1864; food processor in the kitchen appliance sense from 1973; food stamp (n.) is from 1962.
