Origin and history of darkness darkness (n.) Old English deorcnysse "absence of light," from dark (adj.) + -ness . The 10c. Anglo-Saxon treatise on astronomy uses þeostrum for "darkness." Figurative use for "sinfulness, wickedness" is from early 14c. From late 14c. as "obscurity," also "secrecy, concealment," also "blindness," physical, mental, or spiritual.
