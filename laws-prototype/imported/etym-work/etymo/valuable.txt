Origin and history of valuable valuable (adj.) 1580s, "of great value or price;" 1640s, "of great utility or importance;" from value (v.) + -able . From 1620s as "capable of having the value measured or estimated," used so by Locke but now perhaps obsolete. As a noun, "a valuable thing," by 1775, often in plural valuables . Related: Valuably . also from 1580s
