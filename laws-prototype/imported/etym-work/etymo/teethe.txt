Origin and history of teethe teethe (v.) "cut teeth, grow teeth," early 15c., tethen , probably from an unrecorded Old English verb *teþan , from toþ "tooth" (from PIE root *dent- "tooth"). Related: Teethed ; teething . Middle English earlier had tooth (v.) in this sense ( tothen "develop or grow teeth," c. 1300), also tothinge (n.) "teething, the cutting of teeth" (mid-15c.). also from early 15c.
