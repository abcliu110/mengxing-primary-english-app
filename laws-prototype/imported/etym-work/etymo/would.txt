Origin and history of would would Middle English wolde , from Old English wolde , past tense and past subjunctive of willan "to will" (see will (v.)). As a noun, late 14c., "that which (one) would have;" from c. 1600 as "one who affects to be what he is not." Would-be (adj.) "wishing to be, vainly pretending" is recorded from c. 1300. A woulder (c. 1500) was "one given to saying 'I would.' "
