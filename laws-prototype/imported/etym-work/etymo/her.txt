Origin and history of her her (objective case) Old English hire "her," third person singular feminine dative pronoun, which replaced accusative hie beginning in 10c. See he . Cognate with Old Frisian hiri , Middle Dutch hore , Dutch haar , Old High German iru , German ihr . also from 10c. her (possessive case) Old English hire , third person singular feminine genitive form of heo "she" (see she ). With absolute form hers .
