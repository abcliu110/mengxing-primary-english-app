Origin and history of shun shun (v.) Middle English shunnen , "keep out of the way of, avoid (a person or place); refrain from, neglect (a practice or behavior)," from Old English scunian "run away from, avoid; abhor, loathe; seek safety by concealment," a word of uncertain origin; according to OED not found in other Germanic languages. Perhaps it is ultimately from PIE root *skeu- "to cover, to hide." Related: Shunned ; shunning . A shun-pike (American English, by 1805 as a name of a road in New York) was a road constructed to avoid tolls.
