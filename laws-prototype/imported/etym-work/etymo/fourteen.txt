Origin and history of fourteen fourteen (adj., n.) "1 more than thirteen; the number which is one more than thirteen; a symbol representing this number;" c. 1300, from Old English feowertyne ; see four + -teen . Compare Old Saxon fiertein , Old Frisian fiuwertine , Dutch veertien , Old High German fiorzehan , German vierzehn , Danish fjorten , Gothic fidwortaihun . also from c. 1300
