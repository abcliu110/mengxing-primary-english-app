Origin and history of mango mango (n.) 1580s, "fruit of the mango-tree," which is extensively cultivated in India and other tropical countries, from Portuguese manga , from Malay (Austronesian) mangga and Tamil (Dravidian) mankay , from man "mango tree" + kay "fruit." Mango trees were brought from Timor to British gardens in Jamaica and St. Vincent 1793 by Capt. Bligh on his second voyage.
