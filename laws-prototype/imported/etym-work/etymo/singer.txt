Origin and history of singer singer (n.) c. 1300, "one who makes music with the voice, a singer," male or female (mid-13c. as a surname), agent noun from sing (v.). It replaced Old English songer "psalm-writer," sangere "singer, poet" (also see songster ). Fem. form singestre is attested from early 13c. (translating Latin cantrix ); singeresse is from late 14c. also from c. 1300
