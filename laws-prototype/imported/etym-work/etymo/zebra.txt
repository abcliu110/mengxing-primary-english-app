Origin and history of zebra zebra (n.) African mammal related to the horse and ass, having the body more or less completely striped, c. 1600, from Italian zebra , perhaps via Portuguese, earlier applied to a now-extinct wild ass, a word of uncertain origin, said to be Congolese [OED, 1989], or Amharic [Klein], but perhaps ultimately from Latin equiferus "wild horse," from equus "horse" (see equine ) + ferus (see fierce ). Related: Zebrine ; zebroid . also from c. 1600
