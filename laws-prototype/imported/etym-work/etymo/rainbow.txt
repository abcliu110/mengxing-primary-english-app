Origin and history of rainbow rainbow (n.) "arc of prismatic colors formed by the refraction of light rays by drops of rain or vapor," Middle English rein-bowe , from Old English renboga ; see rain (n.) + bow (n.). Common Germanic compound (Old Frisian reinboga , Old Norse regnbogi , Swedish regenbåge , Dutch regenboog , German Regenbogen ). The American rainbow trout (1876) is so called for its resplendent colors. Old English also had scurboga "shower-bow."
