Origin and history of provide provide (v.) early 15c., providen , "make provision for the future; arrange, plan; take care, relieve of needs, supply the needs of," from Latin providere "look ahead, prepare, supply, act with foresight," from pro "ahead" (from PIE root *per- (1) "forward") + videre "to see" (from PIE root *weid- "to see"). Related: Provided ; providing; providance ("act or condition of providing," by 1740). Earlier in same sense was its doublet purvey , which is from the same Latin verb, deformed in Old French ( pourvoir ).
