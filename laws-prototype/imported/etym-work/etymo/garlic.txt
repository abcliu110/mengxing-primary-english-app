Origin and history of garlic garlic (n.) "onion like bulbous plant allied to the leek, known to the ancients and much used in cookery," Middle English garlek , from Old English garlec (West Saxon), garleac (Mercian), "garlic," from gar "spear" (in reference to the clove), see gar , + leac "leek" (see leek ). Garlic-bread is attested by 1947.
