Origin and history of eighth eighth (adj., n.) "next in order after the seventh; an ordinal numeral; being one of eight equal parts into which a whole is regarded as divided;" late 14c., eighthe , contracted from Old English eahtoða, from Proto-Germanic *ahtudon (source also of Old High German ahtoda , Old Frisian achta , German achte , Gothic ahtuda); see eight + -th (1). also from late 14c.
