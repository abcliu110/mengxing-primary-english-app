Origin and history of unit unit (n.) 1560s, "single number regarded as an undivided whole," an alteration of unity on the basis of digit . Popularized in John Dee's English translation of Euclid, to express Greek monas (Dee writes that unity formerly was used in this sense). The meaning "single thing or person regarded as a member of a group" is attested from 1640s. The extended sense of "a quantity adopted as a standard of measure" is from 1738. The sense of "group of wards in a hospital" is attested from 1893. also from 1560s
