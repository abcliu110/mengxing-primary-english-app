watermelon (n.) vine-plant highly cultivated as a foodstuff, also its fruit, 1610s, from water (n.1) + melon . So called for being full of thin juice. Compare French melon d'eau . The modern sweet variety dates from Roman times. It was a garden-plant in Europe by 17c. and was introduced in the New World by 1570s by Spanish colonists. also from 1610s
