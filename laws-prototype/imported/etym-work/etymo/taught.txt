Origin and history of taught taught past tense of teach (v.), from Old English tahte , past tense of tæcan . As an adjective, of a person, "instructed, trained," by late 14c. The 1382 Wycliffe Bible has taughtly (adv.) "with craft, skillfully." For the unrelated adjective meaning "stretched or pulled tight" (which formerly was also spelled taught ), see taut .
