Origin and history of apathy apathy (n.) c. 1600, "freedom from suffering, passionless existence," from French apathie (16c.), from Latin apathia , from Greek apatheia "freedom from suffering, impassibility, want of sensation," from apathēs "without feeling, without suffering or having suffered," from a- "without" (see a- (3)) + pathos "emotion, feeling, suffering" (from PIE root *kwent(h)- "to suffer"). Originally a positive quality; the sense of "indolence of mind, indifference to what should excite" is by 1733. also from c. 1600
