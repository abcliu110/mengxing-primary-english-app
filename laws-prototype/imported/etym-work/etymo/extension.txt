extension (n.) c. 1400, "a swelling, a bulging," from Latin extensionem / extentionem (nominative extensio / extentio ) "a stretching out, extension," noun of action from past-participle stem of extendere (see extend ). In a concrete sense, "extended portion of something" (a railroad, etc.), from 1852. The telephone sense is from 1906. also from c. 1400
