Origin and history of courageous courageous (adj.) c. 1300, of persons, "valiant, brave, full of courage," also "desirous," from Anglo-French corageous , Old French corageus, corajos "eager, spirited, brave," also "capricious, inconstant" (12c., Modern French courageux ), both on the notion of "following one's inner impulses," from corage "heart, innermost feelings; temper" (see courage ). Of actions, speech, etc., "manifesting courage," by 1795. Related: Courageously ; courageousness . also from c. 1300
