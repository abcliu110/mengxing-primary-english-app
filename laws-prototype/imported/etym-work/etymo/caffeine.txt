Origin and history of caffeine caffeine (n.) 1817 (as caffein. ) Discovered by Irish chemist Richard Chenevix, the name is first attested in the writings of Thomas Thomson. From "coffee" (see coffee ) + chemical suffix -ine (2). So called because the alkaloid was found in coffee beans; its presence accounts for the stimulating effect of coffee and tea.
