Origin and history of textbook textbook (n.) also text-book , "book used by students as a standard work for a branch of study," 1779, from text (n.) "authoritative writing" + book (n.). Earlier (1730) it meant "book printed with wide spaces between the lines" for notes or translation (such as would have been wanted by students), It is attested from 1861 in reference to a book containing a selection of passages of Scripture arranged for reference. As an adjective from 1916, "typical of a textbook." also from 1779
