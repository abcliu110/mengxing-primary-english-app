Origin and history of forgetful forgetful (adj.) "disposed to forget, having a poor memory," late 14c., foryetful , from forget + -ful . A curious formation. It was used in the sense of "causing forgetting" from 1550s, but this is almost exclusively in poetry (Milton, Tennyson, etc.). An older word in this sense was Middle English forgetel , from Old English forgitel "forgetful," from a formation similar to that in Dutch vergetel . German has vergesslich "forget-ly." Related: Forgetfully ; forgetfulness . also from late 14c.
