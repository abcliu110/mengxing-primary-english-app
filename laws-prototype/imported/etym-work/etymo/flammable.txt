Origin and history of flammable flammable (adj.) "capable of being kindled into flame," 1813, from stem of Latin flammare "to set on fire" (from flamma "flame, blazing fire;" see flame (n.)) + -able . In modern (20c.) use, a means of avoiding the ambiguity of inflammable . Related: Flammability; flammation "act of setting on fire." also from 1813
