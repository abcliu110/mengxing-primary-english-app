Origin and history of veal veal (n.) early 14c., vel , "calf meat as food," from Anglo-French vel , veal , Old French veel "a calf" (12c., Modern French veau ), earlier vedel , from Latin vitellus "little calf," diminutive of vitulus "calf," which is perhaps originally "yearling," if it is related, as some think, to Sanskrit vatsah "calf," literally "yearling;" Gothic wiþrus , Old English weðer (see wether ; also compare veteran ). also from early 14c.
