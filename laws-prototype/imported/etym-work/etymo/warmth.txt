Origin and history of warmth warmth (n.) late 12c., warmeth , wearmth , "state of being warm; gentle or moderate heat of the atmosphere," Proto-Germanic *warmitho- (source also of Middle Low German wermede , Dutch warmte ), from *warmo- (see warm (adj.); also see -th (2)). By 1590s as "excited state of feeling; lively geniality." By 1710 in reference to a heated temper approaching anger. By 1717 in reference to color in paintings. also from late 12c.
