Origin and history of piano piano (n.) "percussion musical instrument in which tones are produced by blows of hammers upon stretched strings, the hammers being operated from a keyboard," 1803, from French piano (18c.), Italian piano , shortened forms of pianoforte (q.v.). Essentially, the pianoforte is a large dulcimer with a keyboard ; but historically it replaced the clavichord and harpsichord, which were keyboard-instruments more akin to the harp than to the dulcimer. [Century Dictionary] Piano wire "kind of strong steel wire used for strings of pianos," is attested from 1831.
