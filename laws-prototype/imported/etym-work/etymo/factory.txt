Origin and history of factory factory (n.) 1550s, "estate manager's office," from French factorie (15c.), from Late Latin factorium "office for agents ('factors')," also "oil press, mill," from Latin factor "doer, maker," agent noun from past-participle stem of facere "to do" (from PIE root *dhe- "to set, put"). It is attested from 1580s as "establishment of merchants and factors in a foreign place." The sense of "building for making goods" is attested by 1610s. Factory farm is attested from 1890. also from 1550s
