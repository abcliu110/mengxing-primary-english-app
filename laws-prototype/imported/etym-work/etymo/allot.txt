Origin and history of allot allot (v.) "parcel out, divide or distribute as by lots," late 15c., also alot , from Old French aloter (Modern French allotir ) "to divide by lots, to divide into lots," from à "to" (see ad- ) + loter "lot," a word of Germanic origin (cognates: Gothic hlauts , Old High German hloz , Old English hlot ; see lot ). Related: Allotted ; allotting ; allotter ; allottee . also from late 15c.
