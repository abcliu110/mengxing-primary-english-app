Origin and history of TV TV (n.) 1948, shortened form of television (q.v.); it was used in station call letters from 1947 (as KSD-TV , etc.). It was spelled out as tee-vee by 1949. TV dinner (1954), meant to be eaten from a tray while watching a television set, is a proprietary name registered by Swanson & Sons, Omaha, Nebraska, and was marketed to housewives as a convenience. Watch your favorite TV Shows and serve a turkey dinner, too! New Swanson TV Turkey Dinners mean you can skip cooking entirely ... no pots and pans to clean, either!
