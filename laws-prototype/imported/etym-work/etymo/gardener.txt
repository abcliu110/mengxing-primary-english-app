Origin and history of gardener gardener (n.) late 13c. (early 12c. as a surname), from Old North French *gardinier (Old French jardineor "gardener," 12c., Modern French jardinier ), from gardin "(kitchen) garden" (see garden (n.)). Compare German Gärtner . An Old English word for it was wyrtweard , literally "plant-guard." also from late 13c.
