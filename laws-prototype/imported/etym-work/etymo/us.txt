Origin and history of us us (pron.) Old English us (cognate with Old Saxon, Old Frisian us , Old Norse, Swedish oss , Dutch ons , German uns ), accusative and dative of plural personal pronoun we . Used as a singular by sovereigns and other potentates. It is reconstructed (Watkins) to be from PIE *nes- (2), forming oblique cases of the first person plural personal pronoun. The prehistoric nasal is preserved in Germanic in Dutch ons , German uns . The PIE root is considered to be the source also of Sanskrit nas , Avestan na , Hittite nash "us;" Greek no "we two;" Latin nos "we, us;" Old Church Slavonic ny "us," nasu "our;" Old Irish ni , Welsh ni "we, us."
