seventy (adj., n.) "seven times ten; the number which is one more than sixty-nine; a symbol representing this number;" Old English (hund)seofontig , from seofon (see seven ) + -tig (see -ty (1)). Similar formation in Old Saxon sibuntig , Old Frisian soventich , Middle Dutch seventich , Old High German sibunzug , Old Norse sjautugr . Seventy-eight ( 78 ) "gramophone record played at a speed of seventy-eight revolutions per minute," which was the standard until the introduction of long-play (see LP ) in 1948; the use of seventy-eight to distinguish the old discs is by 1951.
