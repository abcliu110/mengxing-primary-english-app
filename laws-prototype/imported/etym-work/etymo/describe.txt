Origin and history of describe describe (v.) mid-13c., descriven , "interpret, explain," a sense now obsolete; c. 1300, "represent orally or by writing," from Old French descrivre , descrire (13c.) and directly from Latin describere "to write down, copy; sketch, represent," from de "down" (see de- ) + scribere "to write" (from PIE root *skribh- "to cut"). From late 14c. as "form or trace by motion;" c. 1400 as "delineate or mark the form or figure of, outline." Reconstructed with Latin spelling from c. 1450. Related: Described , describes , describing . also from mid-13c.
