Origin and history of ascend ascend (v.) late 14c., ascenden , "move upward," from Latin ascendere "climb up, mount," of planets, constellations, "come over the horizon," figuratively "to rise, reach;" from ad "to" (see ad- ) + scandere "to climb" (see scale (v.1)). The meaning "slope upward" is from 1832. Related: Ascended ; ascending . An Old English word for it was stigan (see sty (v.)). also from late 14c.
