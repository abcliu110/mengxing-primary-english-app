Origin and history of version version (n.) 1580s, "a translation, that which is rendered from another language," from French version , from Medieval Latin versionem (nominative versio ) "a turning; a translation," from past-participle stem of Latin vertere "to turn, turn back, be turned; convert, transform, translate; be changed" (from PIE root *wer- (2) "to turn, bend"). Also in Middle English ( versioun , early 15c.) with a sense of "destruction." The meaning "particular form of a description; an account of incidents from a particular point of view" is attested by 1788. also from 1580s
