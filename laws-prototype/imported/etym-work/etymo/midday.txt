Origin and history of midday midday (n.) also mid-day , "the middle of the day," from Old English middæg "midday, noon," contracted from midne dæg ; see mid (adj.) + day . Similar formation in Old Frisian middei , Dutch middag , Old High German mittitag , German Mittag , Old Norse miðdagr . As an adjective, "of or pertaining to midday," from early 14c.
