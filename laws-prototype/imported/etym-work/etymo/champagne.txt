Origin and history of champagne champagne (n.) effervescent wine, 1660s, from French, short for vin de Champagne "wine made in Champagne ," the former province in northeast France, the name of which is etymologically "open country" (see campaign (n.)). Originally any wine from this region (especially from the vineyards south of Reims), the sense then narrowed to the "sparkling" wines made there (the effervescence is artificially produced), and by late 18c. expanded to effervescent wines made anywhere. also from 1660s
