Origin and history of rewrite rewrite (v.) 1560s, "reply in writing," from re- "back, again" + write (v.). The sense of "write again, write a second time" especially in a different form is by 1730. Related: Rewrote ; rewritten ; rewriting . The newspaper rewrite man , who works up copy for publication from information or stories phoned or sent in by reporters, is recorded from 1901. As a noun, "act of revising copy or a text," from 1926. also from 1560s
