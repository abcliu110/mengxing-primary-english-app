Origin and history of submarine submarine (adj.) also sub-marine , "situated, acting, or living under the sea," 1640s, from sub- "under, beneath" + marine (adj.). also from 1640s submarine (n.) "vessel that can remain underwater and be propelled when entirely submerged," 1899, short for earlier submarine boat (1640s, as a possibility), submarine vessel (1732), from submarine (adj.). Earlier as a noun it meant "a creature living under the sea" (1703, of coral). The short form sub is attested from 1917. S ubmarine sandwich is attested by 1931 as an Italian-American food, presumably so called from the shape of the roll. Related: Submariner . also from 1899
