spent (adj.) "consumed, nearly exhausted or worn out," mid-15c., of material things, past-participle adjective from spend . Of time, "passed, over," from 1520s. By 1560s of persons or animals, "worn out, exhausted from overwork." The expression be spent , "be on the point of death," also of wilting flowers, is attested from early 14c. also from mid-15c.
