Origin and history of survive survive (v.) mid-15c. (implied in surviving ), transitive, "outlive, live longer than, continue in existence after some point in time or the death of another," originally in the legal (inheritance) sense, from Anglo-French survivre , Old French souvivre (12c., Modern French survivre ), from Late Latin supervivere "live beyond, live longer than," from super "over, beyond" (see super- ) + Latin vivere "to live" (from PIE root *gwei- "to live"). The intransitive sense "live on, remain in existence" is from late 15c. Related: Survived . also from mid-15c.
