Origin and history of went went (v.) past tense of go ; originally a past tense and past participle of wend (v.), as sent from send . The past tense forms of wend were wende , wended, but variants wente , went developed from c. 1200 as part of a Middle English pattern in which the -d of the past tense and past participle becomes -t after -t- , -p- , -s- , -f- , in some cases -l- and -n-. C ompare keep/kept , leave/left , gird/girt , build/built , feel/felt , dwell/dwelt , Middle English kissen/kiste , etc. Went began to replace older past tenses of go in Middle English. By c. 1500 they were fully employed in that function, and wend retained the past tense form wended .
