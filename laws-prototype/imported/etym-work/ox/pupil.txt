senses 1 to 2 late Middle English (in the sense ‘orphan, ward’): from Old French pupille , from Latin pupillus (diminutive of pupus ‘boy’) and pupilla (diminutive of pupa ‘girl’). sense 3 late Middle English: from Old French pupille or Latin pupilla , diminutive of pupa ‘doll’ (so named from the tiny reflected images visible in the eye).
