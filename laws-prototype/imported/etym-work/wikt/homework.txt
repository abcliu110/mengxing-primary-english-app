====Usage notes====
* The term {{m|en||homework}} generally implies that the work is mandatory and worth marks; exercises that are optional are usually referred to as, for example, {{m|en||practice problems}}, {{m|en||review problems}}, {{m|en||extra practice}}, {{m|en||exercises}}. Work of a larger scale than homework (which involves a series of relatively simple exercises) is usually referred to as an {{m|en||assignment}} or {{m|en||project}}
