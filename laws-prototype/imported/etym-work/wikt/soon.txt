===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|sone}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sōna|t=immediately, at once}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*sān(ō)}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*sēna}}, {{m|gem-pro|*sēnô|t=immediately, soon, then}}, from {{m|gem-pro|*sa|pos=demonstrative pronoun}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*só|pos=demonstrative pronoun}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|sone}}, {{m|sco|sune}}, {{m|sco|schone|t=soon, quickly, at once}}, {{cog|frr|san|t=immediately, at once}}, dialectal {{cog|nl|zaan||soon, before long}}, {{cog|gml|sân|t=right afterwards, soon}}, {{cog|gmh|sān}}, {{m|gmh|son|t=soon, then}}, {{cog|goh|sār|t=immediately, soon}}. Compare also {{cog|got|𐍃𐌿𐌽𐍃|t=immediately, soon}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*suniz|t=soon}}.
