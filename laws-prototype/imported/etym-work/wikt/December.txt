{{unc|la}}, perhaps by {{lg|haplology}}{{haplological form|la|notext=1}} of earlier {{m|la||*deke(m)-mēmbris|t=of or pertaining to the tenth month}}, from {{m|la||*deke(m)-mēnzris}}, from {{m|la|decem|t=ten}} + {{m|la||*mēnzris}}, from {{m|la||*mēnzlis}}, from {{affix|itc-pro|*mēns|t1=month|*-elis<alt:*-lis>|nocat=1}}; see {{m|la|decem}}, and {{m|la|mēnsis}}. In the [[w:Roman calendar|Roman calendar]], the year began with {{m|la|Mārtius||March}}, and ''December'' was the tenth month of the year.
