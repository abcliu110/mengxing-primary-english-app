The noun is from {{inh|en|enm|kyte}}, {{m|enm|kite|kīte}}, {{m|enm|kete|t=a kite endemic to Europe, especially the red kite (''Milvus milvus'')}}, from {{inh|en|ang|cȳta|t=kite; bittern}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=kīte|pos=n|id=MED24360|accessdate=18 April 2019}}</ref><ref name="OED">{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=103752|date=1901|nodot=yes}}; {{R:Lexico}}</ref> from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*kūtijō}}, {{glossary|diminutive}} of {{der|en|gmw-pro|*kūt|t=bird of prey}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gewH-|*gewH-d-|t=to cry, screech}}. The English word is cognate with {{cog|sco|kyt}}, {{m|sco|kyte||kite; bird of prey}}, {{cog|gmh|kiuzelīn}}, {{m|gmh|kützlīn||owling}} (modern {{cog|de|Kauz||owl}}). Possibly a {{doublet|en|coot|nocap=1}}.

Sense 3 (“lightweight toy”) is from the fact that it [[hover]]s in the air like the bird.<ref name="OED"/>

The verb is derived from the noun.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=v|id=103753|date=1901}}</ref>
