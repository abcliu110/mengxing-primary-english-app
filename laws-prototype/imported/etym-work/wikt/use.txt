=====Usage notes=====
* When meaning "accustom, habituate" or "habitually do (or employ)", the verb ''use'' is pronounced {{IPAchar|/juːs/}} (like the noun ''use''); these senses and hence this pronunciation are now found chiefly in the past tense or as a past participle ({{IPAchar|/juːst/}}), or in the (past) negative form ''did not '''use''''' (as in ''I did not '''use''' to like her'' or ''the dragoons did not '''use''' [habituate, become habituated] to the Russian cold''). In all other senses, it is pronounced {{IPAchar|/juːz/}} (past tense/participle {{IPAchar|/juːzd/}}).
* See also the usage notes at {{m|en|used to}} (and {{m|en|use to}}) for more, especially on the use of this sense in interrogatives, negatives, and the past tense.
