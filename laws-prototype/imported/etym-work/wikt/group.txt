===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=set|:bor|fr:groupe<id:set>|:bor|it:gruppo<id:group>|tree=1}}
From {{bor|en|fr|groupe|t=cluster, group}}, from {{der|en|it|gruppo}}, {{m|it|groppo|t=a knot, heap, group, bag (of money)}}. Displaced native {{noncog|ang|hād||tribe, group}}.

In the "{{w|group theory}}" sense, {{l|en|calque#Verb|calqued}} from {{cal|en|fr|groupe|notext=1}}, a term coined by the young French mathematician {{w|Évariste Galois}} in 1830.

Cognate with {{cog|de|Kropf||crop, craw, bunch}}; {{cog|ang|cropp}}, {{m|ang|croppa||cluster, bunch, sprout, flower, berry, ear of corn, crop}} (whence {{cog|en|crop}}); {{cog|nl|krop||craw}}, {{cog|is|kroppr||hump, bunch}}. {{doublet|en|crop|croup}}. Compare {{noncog|fa|گروه}}.
