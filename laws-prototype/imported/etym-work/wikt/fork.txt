===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:forke}}
From {{inh|en|enm|forke|t=digging fork}}, from {{inh|en|ang|force}}, {{m|ang|forca|t=forked instrument used to torture}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*furkō|t=fork}}, from {{der|en|la|furca|t=pitchfork, forked stake; gallows, beam, stake, support post, yoke}}, of uncertain origin. The {{cog|enm|-}} word was later reinforced by {{der|en|xno|-}}, {{der|en|fro-nor|forque}} (= {{cog|fro|forche}} whence {{cog|fr|fourche}}), also from the Latin. {{doublet|en|fourche|furcate}}. Cognate also with {{cog|frr|forck|t=fork}}, {{cog|nl|vork|t=fork}}, {{cog|da|fork|t=fork}}, {{cog|de|Forke|t=pitchfork}}. Displaced native {{m|ang|gafol}}, {{m|ang|ġeafel}}, {{m|ang|ġeafle|t=fork}}, from {{cog|ang|-}}.

In its primary sense of {{m-g|fork}}, {{cog|la|furca}} appears to be derived from {{der|en|ine-pro|*ǵʰerk(ʷ)-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*ǵʰerg(ʷ)-|t=fork}}, although the development of the -c- is difficult to explain. In other senses this derivation is unlikely. For these, perhaps it is connected to {{der|en|gem-pro|*furkaz}}, {{m|gem-pro|*firkalaz|t=stake, stick, pole, post}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*perg-|t=pole, post}}. If so, this would relate the word to {{cog|ang|forclas|t=bolt|g=p}}, {{cog|osx|ferkal|t=lock, bolt, bar}}, {{cog|non|forkr|t=pole, staff, stick}}, {{cog|no|fork|t=stick, bat}}, {{cog|sv|fork|t=pole}}.

===Etymology 1===
{{inh+|nb|non|forkr|t=boathook; pole, staff}}, itself borrowed from {{der|nb|la|furca|t=two-pronged fork, pitchfork; fork-shaped prop or pole}}.

The further origin of the Latin word is uncertain. It has been connected with {{der|nb|ine-pro|*ǵʰerk(ʷ)-|t=fork}} and {{m|ine-pro|*ǵʰerg(ʷ)-|t=fork}}, although the consonant development is problematic. Alternatively, it may be related to {{der|nb|gem-pro|*furkaz|t=stake, stick, pole, post}} and {{m|gem-pro|*firkalaz|t=stake, stick, pole, post}}, ultimately associated with {{der|nb|ine-pro|*perg-|t=pole, post}}.

Compare {{cog|nn|fork|t=stick; forked stick; large person}}, {{cog|da|fork|t=pitchfork}}, {{cog|sv|fork|t=pole}}, {{cog|nl|vork|t=fork}} and {{cog|en|fork}}.
