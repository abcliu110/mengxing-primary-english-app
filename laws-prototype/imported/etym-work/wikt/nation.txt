=====Usage notes=====
* {{qualifier|British}} Following the establishment of the Scottish and Welsh [[parliament|parliaments]], [[England]], [[Scotland]] and [[Wales]] are normally considered distinct nations. Application of the term ''nation'' to the United Kingdom as a whole is [[deprecate]]d in most style guides, including the BBC, most newspapers and in UK Government publications. [[Northern Ireland]], being of less clear legal status, generally remains a [[province]].
