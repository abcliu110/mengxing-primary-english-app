===Etymology 1===
The current spelling replaced the spelling ''key'' in the 1690s  to emulate the spelling but (at least originally) not the pronunciation of the equivalent modern {{cog|fr|quai}}. From {{inh|en|enm|kay}}, {{m|enm|key}}, {{m|enm|kaye}}, {{m|enm|keye}}, from {{der|en|fro|kay}}, {{m|fro|cail}}, from {{der|en|cel-gau|*kagyum}}, {{m|cel-gau|cagiíun||enclosure}}, from {{der|en|cel-pro|*kagyom||pen, enclosure}} (compare {{cog|cy|cae||field}}), from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kagʰyóm||enclosure}}. {{doublet|en|hedge|hey|id2=dance|t2=choreographic figure}}.
