==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|outrage}}, from {{der|en|fro|outrage}}, {{m|fro|oultrage||excess}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*ultrāticum}} ("a going beyond"), derived from {{der|en|la|ultrā||beyond}}. Later reanalysed as {{af|en|out-|rage}}, whence the contemporary pronunciation, though neither of these are etymologically related.

The verb is from {{inh|en|enm|outragen}}, from {{der|en|fro|oultragier}}.
