===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*ḱers-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|carien}}, from {{der|en|xno|carier}} (modern {{cog|fr|charrier}}); from a derivative of {{der|en|la|carrus|t=four-wheeled baggage wagon}}, ultimately of {{der|en|cel-gau|-}} origin. Compare also {{ncog|enm|cairen|t=to go, bring carry}}, whence Modern {{ncog|en|cair}}, with which it was confused.
