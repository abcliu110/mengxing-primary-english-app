===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|id=ground|:from|en:laune<ety:from<en:laund>>|tree=1}}
Early Modern English {{m|en|laune||turf, grassy area}}, alteration of {{m|en|laund||glade}}, from {{inh|en|enm|launde}}, from {{der|en|fro|lande||heath, moor}}, of {{der|en|gem|-}} or {{der|en|cel-gau|-}} origin, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*landą||land}} or {{der|en|cel-pro|*landā}}, both from {{der|en|ine-pro|*lendʰ-||land, heath}}.

Akin to {{cog|br|lann||heath}}, {{cog|non|-}} & {{cog|ang|land}}. {{doublet|en|land|lande}}.
