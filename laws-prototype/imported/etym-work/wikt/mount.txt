=====Usage notes=====
As with the names of {{m|en|river|rivers}} and {{m|en|lake|lakes}}, the names of mountains are typically formed by adding the word before or after the unique term. {{m|en||Mount}} is used in situations where the word precedes the unique term: {{m|en|Mount Everest}}, {{m|en|Mount Rushmore}}, {{m|en|Mount Tai}}. Except in the misunderstood translation of foreign names (as with China's {{m|en|Mount Hua}}), the terms used with {{m|en||mount}} will therefore usually be nouns: {{m|en|Mount Olympus}} but {{m|en|Rugged Mountain}} and {{m|en|Crowfoot Mountain}}. It thus corresponds to the earlier {{m|en||the mount}} or {{m|en||mountain of ~}}.

{{m|en||Mount}} is no longer used as a generic synonym for {{m|en|mountain}} except in poetry and other literary contexts. An example is the [[fossil word|fossilized]] form within the phrase ''{{w|Sermon on the Mount}}''.
