===Etymology===
From {{confix|en|tele-|-scope}}.
From {{der|en|la|tēlescopium}}, from {{der|en|grc|τηλεσκόπος||far-seeing}}, from {{m|grc|τῆλε||afar}} + {{m|grc|σκοπέω||to look at}}.

Coined in 1611 by the Greek mathematician [[w:Giovanni Demisiani|Giovanni Demisiani]] for one of [[w:Galileo Galilei|Galileo Galilei]]'s instruments presented at a banquet at the [[w:Accademia dei Lincei|Accademia dei Lincei]]. {{doublet|en|Telescopium}}.
