===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*perkʷ-}}
{{inh+|en|enm|ferme}}, {{m|enm|farme|t=rent, revenue, produce, factor, stewardship, meal, feast}}, influenced by {{der|en|xno|ferme|t=rent, lease, farm}}, from {{der|en|la-med|ferma}}, {{m|la|firma}}.

There is debate as to whether Medieval Latin acquired this term from {{der|en|ang|feorm|t=rent, provision, supplies, feast}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*fermō}}, {{m|gem-pro|*firhuma-|t=means of living, subsistence}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*ferhwō|t=life force, body, being}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*perkʷ-|t=life, force, strength, tree}}; or from {{der|en|la|firmus|t=solid, secure}}, from {{der|en|itc-pro|*fermos}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro||*dʰer-mo-s|t=holding}}, from the root {{m|ine-pro|*dʰer-|t=to hold}}. If the former etymology is correct, the term is related to {{cog|ang|feorh|t=life, spirit}}, {{cog|is|fjör|t=life, vitality, vigour, animation}}, {{cog|got|𐍆𐌰𐌹𐍂𐍈𐌿𐍃|t=the world}}. Compare also {{cog|ang|feormehām|t=farm}}, {{m|ang|feormere|t=purveyor, supplier, grocer}}. Cognate with {{cog|sco|ferm|t=rent, farm}}.
