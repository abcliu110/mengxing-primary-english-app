====Usage notes====
The use of {{m|bi||red}} to cover red, purple and pink reflects traditional Melanesian colour categorisation systems, in which many indigenous languages group these colours together as variations of “red” or warm/reddish hues. Although {{m|bi|perpol}} (from {{m+|en|purple}}) can sometimes serve as a more precise term for “purple”, {{m|bi||red}} is still the most widely used term for this colour spectrum, with the specific hue usually inferred from context.
