===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)kleh₂w-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|includen}}, borrowed from {{der|en|la|inclūdō|inclūdere|t=to shut in, enclose, insert}}, from {{m|la|in-||in}} + {{m|la|claudo|claudere|to shut}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)kleh₂w-|*kleh₂w-|key, hook, nail}}. {{doublet|en|enclose}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|belūcan}} (“to include,” also “to shut in”).

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# To bring into a group, class, set, or total as a (new) part or member.
#: {{ux|en|I will purchase the vacation package if you will '''include''' car rental.}}
# To consider as part of something; to [[comprehend]].
#: {{ux|en|The vacation package '''includes''' car rental.}}
#: {{ux|en|Does this volume of Shakespeare '''include''' his sonnets?}}
#: {{ux|en|I was '''included''' in the invitation to the family gathering.}}
#: {{ux|en|up to and '''including''' page twenty-five}}
#* {{RQ:Milton Paradise Lost|book=8|passage=The whole '''included''' race, his purposed prey.|year=1873}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Richard 3|act=I|scene=iii|passage=The loss of such a lord '''includes''' all harm.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} To [[enclose]], [[confine]]. {{defdate|from early 15th c.}}
#* {{RQ:Burton Melancholy|edition=2001|page=107|passage=I could have here willingly ranged, but these straits wherein I am '''included''' will not permit.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} To [[conclude]]; to [[terminate]].
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Two Gentlemen of Verona|V|iv|passage=Come, let us go; we will '''include''' all jars / With triumphs, mirth, and rare solemnity.}}
# {{lb|en|programming}} To use a [[directive]] that allows the use of [[source code]] from another file.
#: {{ux|en|You have to '''include''' the strings library to use this function.}}
