====Usage notes====
* When used in reference to a cook with no [[sous-chef]]s or other workers beneath him, the term [[connote]]s a certain degree of [[prestige]]&mdash;whether [[culinary]] education or ability&mdash;distinguishing the chef from a “cook”. As a borrowing, ''chef'' was originally italicized, but such treatment is now obsolete.
Within a catering establishment, the head cook (and no-one else) will normally be addressed simply as "chef" as a term of respect.
