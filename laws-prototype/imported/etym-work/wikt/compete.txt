====Usage notes====
A person will ''compete [[for]]'' a prize received for winning a competition. Two or more persons ''compete [[against]]'' one another if they are [[rival]]s. Two or more persons can ''compete [[with]]'' each other as teammates, however ''compete with'' is also used to indicate two persons ''competing [[against]]'' each other.
