# {{lb|en|geography|often|_|“the [[Equator]]”}} An imaginary [[great circle]] around Earth, [[equidistant]] from the two poles, and dividing earth's surface into the northern and southern [[hemisphere]]s.
#* {{quote-web|en|year=2018|url=https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/china-s-melting-glacier-brings-visitors-concern-over-climate-change/4627742.html|title=VOA Learning English > China's Melting Glacier Brings Visitors, Adds to Climate Concerns
|site=learningenglish.voanews.com
|passage=Baishui is about as close to the '''equator''' as Tampa, Florida. [[File:Baishui is about as close to the equator as Tampa, Florida.ogg]]}}
# {{lb|en|astronomy}} A similar great circle on any [[sphere]], especially on a [[celestial body]], or on another reasonably symmetrical three-dimensional body.
# The midline of any generally spherical object, such as a fruit or vegetable, that has identifiable poles.
#: {{ux|en|Slice the onion through the '''equator'''.}}
# The [[celestial equator]].
