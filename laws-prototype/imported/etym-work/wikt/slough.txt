===Etymology 1===
{{etymid|en|from skin-referent origins}}
From {{inh|en|enm|slogh}}, {{m|enm|slugh}}, {{m|enm|slouh}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*sluk-}}, perhaps related to {{m|gem-pro|*sleupaną|t=to slip, sneak}} (compare {{cog|got|𐍃𐌻𐌹𐌿𐍀𐌰𐌽}}).

Akin to {{cog|gml|slô|t=sheath, skin on a hoof}}. Perhaps also related with {{cog|osx|slūk|t=snakeskin}}, {{cog|gmh|slūch}}, whence {{cog|de|Schlauch|t=waterskin, hose}}.
