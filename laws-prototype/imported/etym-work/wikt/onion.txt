===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|onyoun}}, {{m|enm|oynoun}}, from {{der|en|fro|oignon}}, from {{der|en|la|ūniōnem}}, accusative of {{m|la|unio#Etymology 3|ūniō|t=onion}}, which had also been borrowed into Old English as {{m|ang|yne}}, {{m|ang|ynnelēac|t=onion}} (> {{cog|enm|hynne-leac}}, {{m|enm|henne-leac}}). Displaced the inherited term {{m|en|ramsons}}.
* {{sense|soy}} Stems from a {{w|4chan}} [[wordfilter|word filter]] which changes the word {{m|en|soy}} to ''onions''. The word filter was implemented in relation to the "alpha onion eater" [[meme]], which is depicted as the direct opposite of the [[soy boy]].
