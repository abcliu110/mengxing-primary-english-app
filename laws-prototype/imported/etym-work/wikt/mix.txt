===Etymology 2===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*meyḱ-}}
A merger of a nominal use of the verb and a borrowing from {{der|en|xno|mixte}}, from {{der|en|la|mixtus}}, past participle of {{m|la|misceō||mix}}. Nowadays regarded automatically as the nominal form of the verb.
