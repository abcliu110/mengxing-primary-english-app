===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=creator|:inh|enm:auctour<id:author>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|auctour}}, from {{der|en|xno|autour}}, from {{der|en|fro|autor}}, from {{der|en|la|auctor}}, from {{m|la|augeō||to increase, originate}}. The {{m|en||h}}, also found in {{cog|frm|autheur}}, is [[unetymological]] as there is no {{m|la||h}} in the original Latin spelling. The OED attributes the ''h'' to [[Appendix:Glossary#contamination|contamination]] by {{m|en|authentic}}. {{doublet|en|auteur}}.
