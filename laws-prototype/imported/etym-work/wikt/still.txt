===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)telH-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|stille||motionless, stationary}}, from {{inh|en|ang|stille||still, quiet}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*stillī}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)telH-||to be silent; to be still}}. Cognate with {{cog|sco|stil||still}}, {{cog|stq|stil||motionless, calm, quiet}}, {{cog|fy|stil||quiet, still}}, {{cog|nl|stil||quiet, silent, still}}, {{cog|nds|still||quiet, silent, still}}, {{cog|de|still||still, quiet, tranquil, silent}}, {{cog|sv|stilla||quiet, silent, peaceful}}, {{cog|is|stilltur||set, quiet, calm, still}}. Related to {{l|en|stall}}.

{{sense|noun: Falkland Islander}} Military slang, short for ''still a [[Benny]]'', since the military had been instructed not to refer to the islanders by the derogatory term {{m|en|Benny}} (which see).
