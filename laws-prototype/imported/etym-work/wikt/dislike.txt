===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# An [[attitude]] or a [[feeling]] of [[distaste]] or [[aversion]].
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=1957 February|author=H. A. Vallance|title=The Deeside Railway—2|journal=Railway Magazine|page=96|text=In deference to Queen Victoria's '''dislike''' of high speed, the specials were allowed about 75 min. for the journey on the Deeside line, but after her death, they were slightly accelerated.}}
# {{lb|en|usually|in the plural}} Something that a person dislikes (has or feels aversion to).
#: {{ux|en|Tell me your likes and '''dislikes.'''}}
# {{senseid|en|internet}} {{lb|en|Internet}} An individual vote showing disapproval of, or lack of support for, something posted on the Internet.

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|obsolete|transitive}} To [[displease]]; to [[offend]]. {{ng|In third-person only.}} {{defdate|16th|19th c.}}
#* {{RQ:Montaigne Florio Essayes|chapter=12|book=II|passage=customes and conceipts differing from mine, doe not so much '''dislike''' {{transterm|frm|desplaisent}} me, as instruct me{{nb...}}.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To have a [[feeling]] of [[aversion]] or [[antipathy]] towards; not to [[like]]. {{defdate|from 16th c.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=1951|month=December|author=Michael Robbins|title=John Francis's "History of the English Railway"|journal=Railway Magazine|page=800|text=In the thick of the railway controversies of his day, Francis naturally had his prejudices. It seems that he cordially '''disliked''' the aristocracy in general.}}
# {{lb|en|Internet}} To leave a vote to show disapproval of, or lack of support for, something posted on the Internet.
#: {{ux|en|Rebecca Black's "Friday" video has gained notoriety for being one of the most '''disliked''' videos in YouTube history.}}
