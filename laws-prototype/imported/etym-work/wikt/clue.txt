===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*glew-}}
Variant of {{m|en|clew|t=a ball of thread or yarn}}, from {{inh|en|enm|clew}}, {{m|enm|clewe}}, {{m|enm|clowe}}, {{m|enm|clue}}, {{m|enm|clwe}}, {{m|enm|clyw}}, {{m|enm|clywe}}, from {{inh|en|ang|clēowen}}, {{m|ang|clīewen}}, {{m|ang|clīowen}}, {{m|ang|clīwen}}, {{m|ang|clȳwe}}, {{m|ang|clȳwen|t=ball, sphere; skein}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*kliuwīn|t=ball, clump}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*kliuwīną}}, {{m|gem-pro|*klewô|t=bale, ball, clump, mass}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*glew-|t=to ball up, clump together; lump, swelling}}. Sense evolution with reference to the one which the mythical [[w:Theseus|Theseus]] used to guide him out of the [[Minotaur]]'s labyrinth. More at {{l|en|clew}}.
{{col-top|2|cog}}
Cognate with {{cog|cim|khnaul|t=ball of yarn}}, {{cog|nl|kluwe}}, {{m|nl|kluwen|t=ball thread or yarn, clew}}, {{cog|de|Knäuel|t=ballyarn; tangle}}, {{cog|nn|kljå|t=a loom weight}}; also {{cog|sa|ग्लौ|t=the moon; camphor; the earth}}.
{{col-bottom}}

===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{lb|en|now|_|rare}} A strand of [[yarn]] etc. as used to guide one through a labyrinth; something which points the way, a [[guide]].
#* {{RQ:James Maisie|passage=she had even had in the past a small smug conviction that in the domestic labyrinth she always kept the '''clue'''.}}
# {{U|information}} which may lead one to a certain point or [[conclusion]].
#: {{ux|en|Give me a '''clue''' because the question is too vague.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=1965|journal=Popular Mechanics|volume=123|number=3|page=140
|passage=Black tracks on the magneto, a hairline crack in the flywheel casing, pitted points or a spitless exhaust — all are '''clues''' to potential conkouts}}
# An object or a kind of [[indication]] which may be used as [[evidence]].
#: {{ux|en|The detectives were looking for some '''clues''' at the scene of the crime.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1999|author=John Vernon|chapter=Heat|title=A Book of Reasons|location=Boston, Mass.; New York, N.Y.|publisher=[[w:Houghton Mifflin Harcourt|Houghton Mifflin Company]]|page=17|pageurl=https://archive.org/details/bookofreasons00vern/page/17/mode/1up|isbn=0-395-94477-5|passage=And to unravel the question of why I was doing this, I develop the negative, inspect the shadows, search for hidden '''clues'''.}}
# Insight or understanding ("to have a clue [about]" or "to have clue". See [[have a clue]], [[clue stick]])
#: {{ux|en|I had little '''clue''' that I was being carefully monitored by the CCTV.}}
# {{lb|en|crosswording}} The text that [[indicate]]s an answer in a [[crossword puzzle]].
