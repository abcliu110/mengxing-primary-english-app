===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:horn|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|[[horn]], [[horne]]}}, from {{inh|en|ang|horn}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*horn}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*hurną}}. Compare {{cog|fy|hoarn}}, {{cog|nl|hoorn}}, {{cog|nds|Hoorn}}, {{m|nds|horn}}, {{cog|de|Horn}}, {{cog|da|-}} and {{cog|sv|horn}}, {{cog|got|𐌷𐌰𐌿𐍂𐌽}}.

Ultimately from {{inh|en|ine-pro||*ḱr̥h₂-nó-m}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*ḱerh₂-||head, horn|id=head}}. Compare {{cog|br|kern||horn}}, {{cog|la|cornū}}, {{cog|grc|κέρας}}, {{cog|sla-pro|*sьrna}}, {{cog|cu|сьрна||roedeer}}, {{cog|hit||tr=surna||horn}}, {{cog|fa|سر|tr=sar}}, {{cog|sa|शृङ्ग||horn}}. {{doublet|en|corn<id:callus><t:callus>|corno|cornu}}.

{{sense|telephone}} From the horn-shaped earpieces of old communication systems that used air tubes.

====Usage notes====
* When used alone to refer to an instrument, {{m|en|horn}} can mean either {{m|en|hunting horn}} or {{m|en|French horn}}, depending on context. Other instruments are identified by specific adjectives such as {{m|en|English horn}} or {{m|en|basset horn}}.
