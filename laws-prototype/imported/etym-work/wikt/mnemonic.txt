===Etymology 2===
{{glossary|noun|Noun}} sense 1 (“something used to help in remembering a thing”) is borrowed from {{bor|en|la|mnēmonicum|t=something used to help in remembering a thing}}, from {{der|en|grc|μνημονικόν|t=something used to help in remembering a thing}}, a noun use of the {{glossary|neuter}} form of {{m|grc|μνημονῐκός|pos=adjective}}: see [[#Etymology 1|etymology 1]]. Noun sense 3 (“''synonym of'' mnemonics”) is borrowed from {{bor|en|la-lat|mnemonica}}, a noun use of the {{glossary|feminine}} form of {{m|la-lat|mnemonicus}}: see etymology 1.<ref name="OED"/>
