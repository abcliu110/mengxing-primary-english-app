===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|id=path|enm:line<id:rope>|:influence|frm:ligne|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|line}}, {{m|enm|lyne}}, from {{inh|en|ang|līne||line, cable, rope, hawser, series, row, rule, direction}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*līnā}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*līnǭ||line, rope, flaxen cord, thread}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*līną||flax, linen}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*līno-||flax}}.
Influenced in {{m+|enm|-|w=1}} by {{m|frm|ligne||line}}, from {{m|la|linea}}. More at {{m|en|linen}}.

The oldest sense of the word is “rope, cord, thread”; from this the senses “path”, “continuous mark” were derived.
