===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*gʷeyh₃-}}
The {{lg|verb}} is derived from Late {{inh|en|enm|reviven}}, {{m|enm|revyven|t=to recover from illness; to regain consciousness; to return to life after death; to happen again, recur; to be rejuvenated, renewed; (''figurative'') to bring back; (''alchemy'') of a metal: to be restored to its original form}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=revīven|pos=v|id=MED37386}}</ref> from {{der|en|xno|reviver}}, {{m|xno|revivre|t=to return to life after death; to rejuvenate, renew; to make (a law or legal document) valid again}}, {{der|en|frm|revivre}}, and {{der|en|fro|revivre|t=to return to life after death; to rejuvenate, renew}} (modern {{cog|fr|revivre}}), and directly from their {{lg|etymon}} {{der|en|la|revīvere}}, the {{lg|present}} {{lg|active}} {{lg|infinitive}} of {{m|la|revīvō|t=to live again}}, from {{m|la|re-|pos={{lg|prefix}} meaning ‘again’}} + {{m|la|vīvō|t=to be alive, survive; to live}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gʷeyh₃-|t=to live}}).<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=v|id=5072193422|date=September 2025}}</ref>

The {{lg|noun}} is derived from the verb.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=8813648752|date=December 2024}}</ref>
