===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|marien}}, from {{der|en|xno|marïer}}, from {{der|en|la|marito|marītāre|t=to wed}}, from {{m|la|marītus|t=husband, suitor}}, from {{m|la|mās|t=man, male}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin. Possibly from {{der|en|ine-pro|*méryos||young man}}, same source as {{cog|sa|मर्य|tr=márya|t=suitor, young man}}. Compare its feminine derivatives: {{cog|cy|morwyn||girl}}, {{m|cy|merch||daughter}}, {{cog|gme-cgo|marzus||wedding}}, {{cog|grc|μεῖραξ||boy; girl}}, {{cog|lt|martì||bride}}, {{cog|ae|𐬨𐬀𐬌𐬭𐬌𐬌𐬀|t=yeoman}}.<ref>J.P. Mallory and D.Q. Adams, ''Encyclopedia of Indo-European Culture'', s.v. "woman" (London: Dearborn Fitzroy, 1997), 656.</ref>) Displaced native {{ncog|ang|hīwian}}.
