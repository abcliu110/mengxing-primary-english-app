===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|herth}}, {{m|enm|herthe}}, from {{inh|en|ang|heorþ}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*herþ}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*herþaz}}, possibly from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kerh₃-||heat; fire}}. Cognate with {{cog|fy|hurd}}, {{cog|nl|haard}}, {{cog|de|Herd}}, {{cog|sv|härd}}. {{etydate|{{w|Old English}} period}}.

The modern spelling is from an Middle English/Early Modern English {{IPAchar|/hɛːrθ/}}, from earlier {{IPAchar|/heːrθ/}}, levelled from inflected forms with {{IPAchar|/rð/}} where the vowel would have been lengthened.<ref>{{R:enm:DES|2|1|333}}</ref> The reflex of this pronunciation was preserved in obsolete dialectal {{IPAchar|/ˈhɜɹθ/}}.
