===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*ten-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|tente||}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|tente|}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*tenta||tent}}, from the feminine of {{der|en|la|tentus||}}, ptp. of {{m|la|tendere||to stretch, extend}}, or contracted from {{m|la||*tendita}} as an alternate past participle. Displaced native {{cog|enm|tilt|tild, tilt|tent, tilt}}, from {{cog|ang|teld||tent}}. Compare {{cog|es|tienda||store, shop; tent}}.

# {{senseid|en|Q170544}}A [[pavilion]] or portable [[lodge]] consisting of skins, [[canvas]], or some strong cloth, stretched and sustained by poles, used for sheltering people from the weather.
