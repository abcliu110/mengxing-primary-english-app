====Usage notes====
* The pejorative sense of "an overly sensitive person" arose from the idea that no two snowflakes are alike. "Snowflake" as a derogatory term was popularized by its use in the 1996 novel [[w:Fight Club (novel)|''Fight Club'']], by [[w:Chuck Palahniuk|Chuck Palahniuk]] (see quotation above), but the insult had existed for a significant amount of time prior to this, although not in popular use. In recent years, the meaning has expanded from "a person who believes they are unique" to also denote someone who is too sensitive and is easily offended, based on conceptions of snowflakes' {{l|en|fragility}} and {{l|en|weakness}}.
