===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|illuminaten}}, borrowed from {{bor|en|la|illūminātus}}, {{glossary|perfect}} {{glossary|passive}} {{glossary|participle}} of {{m|la|illūminō||lighten, light up, show off}} (see {{af|en|-ate|id1=verb|pos1=verb-forming suffix}} for more), from {{af|la|in|lūminō|t2=light up|nocap=1|nocat=1}}, from {{m|la|lumen|lūmen|light}}. Cognate with {{cog|ang|lȳman||to glow, shine}}. More at {{l|en|leam}}.
