===Etymology===
From {{der|en|fr|apathie}}, from {{der|en|la|apathīa}}, from {{der|en|grc|ἀπάθεια|t=impassibility”, “insensibility”, “freedom from emotion}}, from {{m|grc|ἀπαθής|t=not suffering or having suffered”, “without experience of}}, from {{m|grc|ἀ-|t=not}} + {{m|grc|πάθος|t=anything that befalls one”, “incident”, “emotion”, “passion}}. {{doublet|en|apatheia}}, which was borrowed directly from [[Ancient Greek]].
