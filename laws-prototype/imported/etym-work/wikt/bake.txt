====Usage notes====
In the dialects of northern England, the simple past ''[[book]]'' and past participle ''[[baken]]'' are sometimes encountered.

The ''incorporate into something greater'' definition is often by an object that is a metaphorical cake, as in the example given: ''the causes of governmental dysfunction are simply '''baked''' into the cake of American politics''. It doesn't have to have such an object though, and the meaning doesn't change: ''the causes of governmental dysfunction are simply '''baked''' into American politics''.
