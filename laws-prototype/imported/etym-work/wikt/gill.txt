===Etymology 1===
{{etymid|en|piscine organ}}
From {{inh|en|enm|gille}}, {{m|enm|gylle|t=gill}}, of {{der|en|gmq|-}} origin, akin to {{cog|da|gælle}}, {{cog|sv|gäl}}, {{cog|no|gjelle}}, and further to {{cog|non|gjǫlnar|t=lips}}, which also may have had the meaning of "gills" (based on Old Danish {{m|non|fiskegæln|t=gills}}). The Old Norse word has been suggested as deriving from {{der|en|gem-pro|*gelunō|t=jaw}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gʰel-}}, which would make it root-cognate to {{cog|grc|χελύνη|t=lip, jaw}}, {{m|grc|χεῖλος|t=lip}}.

Displaced native {{ncog|ang|ċīe}}.
