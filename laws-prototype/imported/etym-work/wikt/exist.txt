==English==

===Etymology===
From {{der|en|fr|exister}}, from {{der|en|la|exsisto|existō, exsistō|I am, I exist, appear, arise}}, from {{m|la|ex||out}} + {{m|la|sisto|sistere|to set, place}} (related to {{m|la|stō|stare|to stand, to be stood}}), ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*stísteh₂ti}}, from the root {{m|ine-pro|*steh₂-||stand}}; see {{m|en|stand}}. Compare {{m|en|assist}}, {{m|en|consist}}, {{m|en|desist}}, {{m|en|insist}}, {{m|en|persist}}, {{m|en|resist}}. Cognate with {{cog|es|existir}}, {{cog|fr|exister}}, {{cog|it|esistere}}, {{cog|de|existieren}}.
