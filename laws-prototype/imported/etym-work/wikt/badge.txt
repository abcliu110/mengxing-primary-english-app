===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*bʰewgʰ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|badge}}, {{m|enm|bagge}}, {{m|enm|bage}}, {{m|enm|bagy}}, from {{der|en|xno|bage}} or {{der|en|la-med|bagea}}, {{m|la|bagia|t=sign, emblem}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin. Possibly derived from {{der|en|la-med|baga|t=ring}}, from {{der|en|osx|bāg}}, {{m|osx|bōg|t=ring, ornament}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*baugaz|t=ring, bracelet, armband}}; or possibly the Anglo-Norman word is derived from an earlier, unattested English word (compare {{cog|ang|bēag|t=ring, bracelet, collar, crown}}. Cognate with {{cog|sco|bagie}}, {{m|sco|badgie}}, {{m|sco|bawgy|t=badge}}.
