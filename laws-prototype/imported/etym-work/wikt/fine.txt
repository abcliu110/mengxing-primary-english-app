===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|fin}}, {{m|enm|fyn}}, from {{der|en|fro|fin|t=fine, minute, exact}}, of obscure origin, but probably derived from {{der|en|la|fīniō|fīnīre|t=to finish}} or {{m|la|fīnis|t=boundary, limit, end}}, with an abstract sense of ''fine'' or ''thin'' also arising in many Romance languages (compare {{cog|it, pt, es|fino}}). {{doublet|en|fino}}.
