=====Usage notes=====
The usual plural of ''pair'' is ''pairs''. This is a recent innovation; the plural ''pair'' was formerly predominant and may be found in older texts like "[https://books.google.ru/books?id=Y_hZAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA207&dq=20+pair+of+shoes&hl=en&sa=X&ved=0ahUKEwiA0Knu__TVAhUqIpoKHXznDKsQ6AEILTAB#v=onepage&q=20%20pair%20of%20shoes&f=false A Key to Joyce's Arithmetic]" (compare {{cog|enm|paire}}, plural ''paire''). That is, a native English speaker, back in the early 19th century, would say ''20 pair of shoes'', as opposed to today's ''20 pairs of shoes''. In colloquial or dialectal speech, forms such as ''20 pair'' may still be found; because of their relegation to informal speech, they are now sometimes [[proscribed]].
