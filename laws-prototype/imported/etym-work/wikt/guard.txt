===Etymology 2===
{{etymon|en|id=verb|:der|frm:garder|fro:guarder|tree=1}}
From early {{der|en|frm|garder}} or late {{der|en|fro|-}} (circa 14th cent) {{m|fro|guarder|t=to keep, ward, guard, save, preserve, etc.}}, from {{der|en|la-eme|wardo}}, from {{der|en|frk|*wardēn|}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*wardāną|t=to guard, protect}}. Cognate with {{cog|fr|garder}}, {{cog|ang|weardian}} (whence also the English inherited {{glossary|doublet}} {{doublet|en|ward|notext=1}}). See also {{cog|en|regard}}.
