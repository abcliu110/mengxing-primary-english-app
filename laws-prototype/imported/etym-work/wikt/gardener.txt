==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|gardener}}, either calqued or loaned from {{der|en|xno|-}} and {{der|en|fro-nor|gardinier}}.

See [[garden]], and compare {{m+|de|Gärtner||gardener}}, which is equivalent to a derivative of the German cognate to {{m+|en|garden}}, {{m|de|Garten||garden}}, + {{m|de|-er}}.

Displaced native {{ncog|ang|wyrtweard}} (effectively "[[wort]][[ward]]").
