===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=winner|:inh|enm:champioun<id:combatant>|tree=1}}
{{root|en|ine-pro|*kh₂em-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|champioun}}, from {{der|en|fro|champion}}, from {{der|en|la-med|campio|t=combatant in a duel, champion}}, from {{der|en|frk|*kampijō|t=fighter}}, from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*kampijō|t=combat soldier}}, a derivative of {{m|gmw-pro|*kampijan|t=to battle, to campaign}}, itself a derivative of {{m|gmw-pro|*kamp|t=battlefield, battle}}, ultimately a borrowing in West-Germanic from {{der|en|la|campus|t=a field, a plain, a place of action}}.

Cognate with {{cog|ang|cempa|cæmpa}}, {{m|ang|cempa|t=soldier, warrior, champion}}, {{cog|goh|kempfo|kempfeo}}, {{m|goh|kempfo|t=fighter, warrior, champion}}, whence archaic {{cog|de|Kempfe|t=fighter}}.
