===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*weyḱ-|*nem-|id2=distribute}}
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:economike<alt:economike, iconomique><ety:bor<enm:iconomique<ety:der<fro:iconomike<ety:der<la:oeconomicus>>>>>>|tree=1}}

The {{lg|noun}} is derived from {{inh|en|enm|economike}}, {{m|enm|iconomique|t=household management}},{{ref|{{R:MED|īconomique|n|MED21657}}}} and then:

* from {{der|en|frm|iconomique}}, {{m|frm|oeconomique}}, and {{der|en|fro|iconomike|t=(''noun'') household management; person in charge of household management; (''adjective'') relating to household management; relating to domestic or family matters; relating to management of a state; reducing costs or expenses, economical}} (modern {{cog|fr|économique}}); and
* from their {{lg|etymon}} {{der|en|la|oeconomicus|t=(''noun'') household manager, housekeeper, steward; (''adjective'') relating to orderly arrangement of written material}} (whence {{cog|la-lat|economicus|t=relating to (management of) a household}}), and {{m|la|economica|t=household management}}, both from {{der|en|grc|οἰκονομῐκός|t=skilled in household management; frugal, thrifty, economical}} (whence {{cog|grc-koi|οἰκονομῐκός|t=relating to orderly arrangement of written material}});

from {{m|grc|οἰκονόμος|t=master of a house; household manager, steward; administrator, manager}} + {{m|grc|-ῐκός|pos={{lg|suffix}} meaning ‘of or relating to’ forming {{lg|adjective}}s}}.{{ref|name=OED|{{R:OED|economic|n.'' & ''adj|7782161419|March 2026|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=adj}}}} {{m|grc||Οἰκονόμος}} is derived from {{m|grc|οἶκος|t=dwelling place, house; estate}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*weyḱ-|t=(''verb'') to enter in; to settle; (''noun'') settlement}}) + {{m|grc|νόμος|t=law, ordinance}} (from {{m|grc|νέμω|t=to distribute; to possess}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*nem-|t=to distribute; to give; to take}}) + {{m|grc|-ος|pos=suffix forming [[o-grade]] action nouns from verbs}}).

{{senseno|en|Christianity|uc=1}} (“one who manages the income of a vacant benefice”) is from {{der|en|it|economo}}, from {{der|en|la-lat|oeconomus|t=housekeeper, steward; administrator, especially of a religious foundation}}, from {{der|en|grc|οἰκονόμος|t=master of a house; household manager, steward; administrator, manager}}: see above.{{ref|name=OED}}

The {{lg|adjective}} is derived from the noun; compare the adjective senses in Middle and Old French, Latin, and Ancient Greek.{{ref|name=OED}}

====Usage notes====
Modern usage prefers ''economic'' when describing the economy of a region or country (and when referring to personal or family budgeting). {{m|en|economical|Economical}} is preferred when referring to [[thrift]] or [[value]] for money.
