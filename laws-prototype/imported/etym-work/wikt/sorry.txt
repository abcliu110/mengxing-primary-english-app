==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|sory}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sāriġ|t=feeling or expressing grief, sorry, grieved, sorrowful, sad, mournful, bitter}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*sairag}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*sairagaz|t=sad}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*seh₂yro|t=hard, rough, painful}}. Cognate with {{cog|sco|sairie||sad, grieved}}, {{cog|stq|seerich|t=sore, inflamed}}, {{cog|fy|searich||sad, sorry}}, {{cog|nds|serig||sick, scabby}}, {{cog|de|-}} dialectal {{m|de|sehrig||sore, sad, painful}}, Swedish {{m|sv|sårig}}. {{surf|+suf|en|sore|y|alt1=sor(e)}}. Unrelated to {{m|en|sorrow}} despite the similarity in form and meaning.

====Usage notes====
* The interjection is generally understood as a clipping of {{m|en|I'm sorry}}.
* A typically sarcastic use slows down the rhythm and stresses the final syllable, as {{IPAchar|/ˌsɒˈɹiːː/}}. This is often spelled as {{m|en|sorree}} or emphatically as something like ''sor-REE!!'' in print.
