=====Usage notes=====
* In later Middle English, distinctive regional forms (e.g. Southern {{m|enm|everech}}, Northern {{m|enm|everilk}}, West Midland {{m|enm|everuch}}) tend to be replaced with ''every'' (or less commonly, {{m|enm|everich}}).
* The form {{m|enm|everich}} is especially found prevocalically, while ''every'' is often employed preconsonantally; this is almost exceptionless in the {{w|Hengwrt manuscript}} of {{w|Chaucer}}'s ''{{w|Canterbury Tales}}''.
