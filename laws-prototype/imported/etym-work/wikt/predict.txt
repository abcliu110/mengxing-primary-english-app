Early 17th century, from {{bor|en|la|praedicō||to mention beforehand}} (perfect passive participle {{m|la|praedictus}}), from {{m|la|prae-||before}} + {{m|la|dīcō||to say}}. Equivalent to Germanic {{m|en|forespeak}}, {{m|en|foretell}}, and {{m|en|foresay}}.
