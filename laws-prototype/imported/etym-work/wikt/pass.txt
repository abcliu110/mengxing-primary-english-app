===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=verb|:inh|enm:passen|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|passen}}, from {{der|en|fro|passer||to step, walk, pass}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*passo|*passāre|t=step, walk, pass}}, derived from {{der|en|la|passus||a step}}, from {{der|en|itc-pro|*pattus|*pat-s-tus}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*peth₂-||to spread, stretch out}}. Cognate with {{cog|ang|fæþm||armful, fathom}}. More at {{l|en|fathom}}.
Displaced native {{ncog|ang|genġan}}.
