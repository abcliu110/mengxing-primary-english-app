{{also|over night}}
==English==

===Alternative forms===
* {{alt|en|o'ernight|qq=poetic|over night|overnite|qq3=informal}}

===Etymology===
Inherited from {{inh|en|enm|overnyght}}, from {{inh|en|ang|ofer niht|t=through the night, overnight}}, equivalent to {{af|en|over|night}}. Verbal use (late 19th c.) may have been influenced by {{cal|en|de|übernachten|notext=1}} (16th c.), though it could also have developed independently. Compare also {{cog|nl|overnachten|t=to overnight}}, {{cog|gml|ȫvernachten|t=to overnight}}, {{cog|fy|oernachtsje|t=to overnight}}, {{cog|stq|uurnoachtje|t=to overnight}}.
