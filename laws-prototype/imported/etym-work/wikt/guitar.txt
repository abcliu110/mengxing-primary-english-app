===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|Q6607}}{{lb|en|musical instrument}} A [[string]]ed [[musical instrument]], of European origin, usually with a [[fretted]] fingerboard and six strings, played with the fingers or a [[plectrum]] (guitar pick).
#: {{ux|en|Learning to [[strum]] the '''guitar''' rhythmically is essential.}}
#: {{ux|en|She was carrying her '''guitar''' in a fancily-decorated case.}}
#: {{ux|en|The band is looking for a new '''guitar''' player.}}
# {{lb|en|musical instrument}} Any type of [[musical instrument]] of the [[lute]] family, characterized by a flat back, along with a [[neck]] whose upper surface is in the same plane as the [[soundboard]], with strings along the neck and parallel to the soundboard.
