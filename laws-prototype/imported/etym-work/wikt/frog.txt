===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*prew-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|frogge}}, from {{inh|en|ang|frocga}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*froggō|t=frog}}. Cognate with {{cog|non|frauki}}, and {{cog|ang|frox}}, {{m|ang|frosc}}, whence Modern {{cog|en|frosh}} and {{m|en|frosk|t=frog}}.

Possibly related to {{cog|stq|Poage|t=frog}}, {{cog|nds-de|Pogg}}, {{m|nds-de|Pogge|t=frog}}. <!--Cognate with {{cog|sa|प्लवक||frog}}.-->
