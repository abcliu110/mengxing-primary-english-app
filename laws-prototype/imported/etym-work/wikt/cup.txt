===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:cuppe<alt:cuppe, coppe><ety:inh<ang:cuppe><ang:copp<id:summit>>>|tree=+}}
{{inh+|en|enm|cuppe}}, {{m|enm|coppe}}, from the merger of {{inh|en|ang|cuppe|t=cup}} and {{inh|en|ang|copp|t=cup, vessel}}.

{{cog|ang|cuppe}} is a borrowing from {{der|en|la-lat|cuppa}}, itself of {{unk|en|title=obscure}} origin, but probably from earlier {{der|en|la|cūpa|t=tub, cask}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kewp-|t=a hollow}}. {{cog|ang|copp}}, however, is from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*kopp|t=round object, bowl, vessel, knoll, summit, crown of the head}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*kuppaz}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gew-|t=to bend, curve, arch}} (whence also obsolete {{cog|en|cop|t=top, summit, crown of the head}}, {{cog|de|Kopf|t=top, head}}).

The Middle English word was further reinforced by {{cog|xno|cupe}} and {{cog|fro|cope}}, {{m|fro|coupe}}, from {{cog|la|cuppa}}. Compare also {{cog|stq|Kop|t=cup}}, {{cog|fy|kop|t=cup}}, {{cog|nl|kop|t=cup}}, {{cog|nds-de|Koppke}}, {{m|nds-de|Köppke|t=cup}}, {{cog|da|kop|t=cup}}, {{cog|sv|kopp|t=cup}}. {{doublet|en|coupe|hive|keeve}}.
