===Etymology===
A folk word, {{etydate|c|1600s|nocap=1}}, apparently from a [[Norfolk]] ([[w:East Anglian English|East Anglian]]) dialect, of uncertain origin:

* perhaps from some {{bor|en|nds|-}} word (compare {{m|de|dümpeln|t=bobbing up and down}}),
* or from the rare dialectal adjective {{m|en|dump|t=lump, of the consistency of dough}} ({{etydate|late 1800s|nocap=1}}), {{suffix|en||-ling|pos2=diminutive suffix}}, although [[dumpling]] is recorded much earlier. However, compare {{m|en|dumpy|t=short and stout}}.

===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{lb|en|culinary}} A ball of [[dough]] that is cooked and may have a filling and/or additional ingredients in the dough. {{defdate|from 17th c.}}
## {{lb|en|chiefly|UK}} Specifically, a ball of dough used in stews and other sauced dishes; a thick [[noodle]].
## {{lb|en|chiefly|US}} Specifically, a food composed of a dough wrapper around a filling.
# {{lb|en|endearing}} {{n-g|A [[term of endearment]].}}
