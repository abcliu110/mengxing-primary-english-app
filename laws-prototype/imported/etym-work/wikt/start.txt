{{ety|en|id=to begin|:inh|enm:sterten}}
From {{inh|en|enm|sterten|t=to leap up suddenly, rush out}}, from {{inh|en|ang|styrtan|t=to leap up, start}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*sturtijan|t=to startle, move, set in motion}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)ter-|t=to be stiff}}. Cognate with {{cog|ofs|stirta|t=to fall down, tumble}}, {{cog|dum|sterten|t=to rush, fall, collapse}} ({{cog|nl|storten}}), {{cog|goh|sturzen|t=to hurl, plunge, turn upside down}} ({{cog|de|stürzen}}), {{cog|goh|sterzan|t=to be stiff, protrude}}. More at {{l|en|stare}}.
