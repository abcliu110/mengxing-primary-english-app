===Verb===
{{en-verb}} {{tlb|en|American spelling|Oxford British spelling}}

# {{lb|en|transitive}} To cause two or more events or actions to happen at exactly the same [[time]] or same [[rate]], or in a time-coordinated way.
#: {{syn|en|sync#Verb|synch#Verb|sync up#Verb}}
## {{lb|en|intransitive}} To occur at the same time or with coordinated timing.
##* {{quote-book|en|year=1839|author=w:Thomas De Quincey|title=Philosophy of Roman History|text=The path of this great empire, through its arch of progress, '''synchronized''' with that of Christianity.}}
## {{lb|en|transitive}} To cause ([[video]] and [[audio]]) to play in a coordinated way.
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To set (a [[clock]] or [[watch]]) to display the same time as another.
#: {{ux|en|We '''synchronized''' our watches and agreed to meet at four o'clock precisely.}}
# {{lb|en|computing|ambitransitive}} To cause (a set of [[file]]s, [[data]], or [[settings]]) on one computer or device to be (and try to remain) the same as on another.
# {{lb|en|intransitive|of inanimate entities}} To [[agree]], be [[coordinated]] with, or [[complement]] well.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1986|title=In The Life: A Black Gay Anthology|editor=Joseph Beam|chapter=By the Year 2000|author=Max C. Smith|page=224|text=Gay men often have interests and concerns that don't '''synchronize''' with the issues considered top priorities by lesbians.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[coordinate]] or [[combine]].
