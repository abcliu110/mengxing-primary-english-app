===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[loosen]], as something interlaced or [[knotted]]; to disengage the parts of.
#: {{ux|en|to '''untie''' a knot}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Antony and Cleopatra|act=V|scene=ii|page=367|passage=Haſt thou the pretty vvorme of Nylus [an asp] there, / That killes and paines not? / {{...}} / Come thou mortal vvretch, / VVith thy ſharpe teeth this knot intrinſicate, / Of life at once '''vntye''': Poore venomous Foole, / Be angry, and diſpatch.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1645|author=w:Edmund Waller|title=To Amoret
|passage=Sacharissa's captive fain / Would '''untie''' his iron chain.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[free]] from fastening or from restraint; to let loose; to [[unbind]].
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Macbeth|4|1|text=Though you '''untie''' the winds, and let them fight / Against the churches.}}
#* {{RQ:Taylor Holy Living|text=All the evils of an '''untied''' tongue we put upon the accounts of drunkenness.}}
# To [[resolve]]; to unfold; to clear.
#* '''1668''', {{w|John Denham (poet)|John Denham}}, ''Of Prudence'' (poem)
#*: They quicken sloth, perplexities '''untie'''.
# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To become untied or loosed.
# {{lb|en|programming|transitive}} In the [[Perl]] [[programming language]], to undo the process of [[tie|tying]], so that a variable uses default instead of custom functionality.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2002|author=Dave Roth|title=Win32 Perl Programming: The Standard Extensions|page=151
|passage=After you finish with the INI file, all you need to do is '''untie''' the hash. Then you really are finished!}}
