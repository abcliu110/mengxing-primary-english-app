=====Usage notes=====
* Sense group 1 is now most often found with words bearing a sense of ability, such as "can tell" or "one way to tell".
* For many of the senses of ''tell'', the word ''show'' would have a contrasting meaning, and in other senses, it would have a nearly identical meaning.
* In dialects, other past tense forms (besides ''told'') may be found, including {{m|en|tald}}/{{m|en|tauld}}<!--taul', tau'd--> {{q|Scotland}}, {{m|en|tawld}} {{q|Devonshire}}, {{m|en|teld}} {{q|Yorkshire|Devonshire}}, {{m|en|telled}} {{q|Northern England, Scotland, and in nonstandard speech generally}}, {{m|en|telt}} {{q|Scotland|Geordie}}, {{m|en|tole}} {{q|AAVE|Southern US|and some dialects of England}}, {{m|en|toll}} {{q|AAVE}}, {{m|en|tolt}} {{q|AAVE}}.
