{{en-verb|+|+|+|+,~n<l:archaic>}}

# {{lb|en|archaic}} To [[cut]].
#* {{RQ:Tennyson Poems 1842|volume=II|poem=Sir Galahad|stanza=I|page=174|passage=My good blade '''carves''' the casques of men, / My tough lance thrusteth sure, / My strength is as the strength of ten, / Because my heart is pure.}}
# To cut meat in order to serve it.
#: {{ux|en|You '''carve''' the roast and I’ll serve the vegetables.}}
# To shape to sculptural effect; to produce (a work) by cutting, or to cut (a material) into a finished work, especially with cuts that are [[curved]] rather than only straight [[slice]]s.
#: {{co|en|to '''carve''' a name into a tree}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1920|year_published='''2008'''|author=Edgar Rice Burroughs|title=Thuvia, Maiden of Mars|url=http://www.gutenberg.org/files/72/72-h/72-h.htm|publisher=The Gutenberg Project|passage=The facades of the buildings fronting upon the avenue within the wall were richly '''carven'''{{...}}.}}
#* {{RQ:Allingham China Governess|chapter=I|passage=The half-dozen pieces {{...}} were painted white and '''carved''' with festoons of flowers, birds and cupids. To display them the walls had been tinted a vivid blue which had now faded, but the carpet, which had evidently been stored and recently relaid, retained its original turquoise.}}
# {{lb|en|snowboarding}} To perform a series of turns without pivoting, so that the tip and tail of the snowboard take the same path.
# {{lb|en|figuratively}} To take or make, as by cutting; to provide.
#* {{RQ:South Twelve Sermons|passage={{...}} who could easily have '''carved''' themselves their own food.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=December 29, 2010|author=Sam Sheringham|title=Liverpool 0 - 1 Wolverhampton|work=BBC|url=http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport1/hi/football/eng_prem/9308904.stm|passage=The Reds '''carved''' the first opening of the second period as Glen Johnson's pull-back found David Ngog but the Frenchman hooked wide from six yards.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20230110155247/http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport1/hi/football/eng_prem/9308904.stm|archivedate=10 January 2023}}

#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Much Ado About Nothing|act=II|scene=iii|passage=Lie ten nights awake '''carving''' the fashion of a new doublet.}}
