===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|menen||to intend; remember; lament; comfort}}, from {{inh|en|ang|mǣnan||to mean, complain}}, {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*mainijan}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*mainijaną||to mean, think; complain}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*meyn-||to think}}, or perhaps from {{der|en|ine-pro|*meyno-}}, extended form of {{der|en|ine-pro|*mey-}}.

Germanic cognates include {{cog|fy|miene||to deem, think}} ({{cog|ofs|mēna||to signify}}), {{cog|nl|menen||to believe, think, mean}} ({{cog|dum|menen||to think, intend}}), {{cog|de|meinen||to think, mean, believe}}, {{cog|osx|mēnian}}. Indo-European cognates include {{cog|sga|mían||wish, desire}} and {{cog|pl|mienić||to signify, believe}}. Non-Indo-European cognates include {{cog|fi|mainita||to mention}}, {{cog|fi|meinata||to mean, to plan, to intend}} {{cog|et|mainima||to mention}}, {{cog|se|máinnastit||to tell}}. Related to {{m|en|moan}}.
