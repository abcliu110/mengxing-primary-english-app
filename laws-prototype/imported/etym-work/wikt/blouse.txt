===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|fr|id=uniform}}
1788, of obscure origin. Three hypotheses are:
* {{cog|fr|blousse||scraps of wool}}, from {{bor|fr|oc|lano blouso||pure or short wool}}, from {{m|oc|blous|blous, blos|pure, empty, bare}}, from {{der|fr|goh|blōz|t=naked, bare}} ({{cog|de|bloß|t=bare}})
* A conflation of the aforementioned and {{cog|fr|bliaud}}, {{m|fr|bliaut}} (a kind of smock or robe, whence {{cog|en|bliaus}}, {{m|en|bliaut}}), from {{der|fr|fro|bliau}}, also from {{der|fr|frk|*blīfald||topcoat of scarlet colour}}, from {{m|gmw-pro|*blīu|t=coloured, bright}} + {{m|gmw-pro|*falþan|*fald|t=crease, fold}}. More at {{cog|en|blee}}, {{m|en|fold}}, and {{m|fr|bliaut}}.
* From {{uder|fr|la-med|pelusia}}, from {{m|la|Pelusium}}, a city of Upper Egypt, a clothing manufacturer during the Middle Ages.
