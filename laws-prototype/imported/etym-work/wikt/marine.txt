==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:marin}}
Recorded since c.1420 as {{inh|en|enm|marin}}, borrowed from {{der|en|frm|marin}}, from {{der|en|fro|-}}, from {{der|en|la|marinus||of the sea}}, itself from {{m|la|mare||sea}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*móri||body of water, lake}} (cognate with {{cog|ang|mere||sea, lake, pool, pond}}, {{cog|nl|meer}}, {{cog|de|Meer}}, all from {{cog|gem-pro|*mari}}). The modern pronunciation is presumably due to the influence of modern {{cog|fr|marine}}, feminine singular of {{m|fr|marin}}.
