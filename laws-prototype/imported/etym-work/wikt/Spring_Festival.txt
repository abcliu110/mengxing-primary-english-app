===Etymology===
The Chinese New Year sense is a {{calque|en|zh|春節|tr=Chūnjié|nocap=1}}, the usual modern name for the holiday. By {{glossary|surface analysis}}, and when not referring to the Chinese holiday, a [[proprialization]] of {{compound|en|spring<id:Q1312>|festival}}.
