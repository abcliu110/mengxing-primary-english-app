=====Usage notes=====
Rime ''({{senseno|en|meteorology}})'' technically differs from [[hoar frost]], as the latter is formed by [[water vapour]] which has undergone [[deposition]] or {{l|en|desublimation|t=transformation directly into ice crystals without first turning into liquid water}}.
