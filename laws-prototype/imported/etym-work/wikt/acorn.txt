===Etymology===
{{etymon|enm|:inh|ang:æcern|:influence|corn}}
{{inh+|enm|ang|æcern}}, from {{inh|enm|gmw-pro|*akran}}, from {{inh|enm|gem-pro|*akraną}}.

The last element of this word is often remodelled on {{m|enm|corn}}, while the variant {{m|enm|ocorn}} in turn shows remodelling of the first element on {{m|enm|ok|id=oak|t=oak}}; this perhaps represents Northern influence, as forms such as ''acorn'' could there be interpreted as {{af|enm|lang1=enm-nor|ak|t1=oak|corn}}. Meanwhile, forms without {{IPAchar|/n/}} might represent either simplification of {{IPAchar|/rn/}} or reinterpretation of {{IPAchar|/n/}} as the plural suffix {{m|enm|-en|id=noun plural}}.
