===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|piken}}, {{m|enm|picken}}, {{m|enm|pikken}}, from {{inh|en|ang|*piccian}}, {{m|ang|*pīcian}} (attested in {{m|ang|pīcung|t=a pricking}}), and {{m|ang|pīcan}}, {{m|ang|pȳcan|t=to pick, prick, pluck}}, both from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*pikkōn}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*pikkōną|t=to pick, peck, prick, knock}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bew-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*bu-|t=to make a dull, hollow sound}}. {{doublet|en|pitch|peck}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|pikken|t=to pick}}, {{cog|de|picken|t=to pick, peck}}, {{cog|non|pikka}}, {{m|non|pjakka}} (whence {{cog|is|pikka|t=to pick, prick}}, {{cog|sv|picka|t=to pick, peck}}). Compare also {{cog|nds-de|puken|t=to pick out, rip out, pull away, extract}}.
