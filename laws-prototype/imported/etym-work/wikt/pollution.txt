===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|pollucioun}}, {{m|enm|pollucion|t=desecration, impurity}}, from {{der|en|xno|pollutiun}}, {{der|en|frm|pollution}}, {{m|frm|pollucion}}, and their source, post-classical {{der|en|la|pollūtiō||defilement, desecration; nocturnal emission}} (4th century), from the participial stem of {{m|la|polluō||to soil, defile, contaminate}}, from {{m|la|por-||before}} + {{m|la|-luō|t=to smear}}, related to {{m|la|lutum||mud}} and {{m|la|luēs||filth}}. Compare {{cog|grc|λῦμα|t=filth, dirt, disgrace}} and {{m|grc|λῦμαξ|t=rubbish, refuse}}, {{cog|sga|loth|t=mud, dirt}}, {{cog|lt|lutynas|t=pool, puddle}}. {{surf|en|pollute|-ion}}.
