===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:gate<id:entryway>|id=doorway}}
From {{inh|en|enm|gate}} (the forms {{m|enm|ȝate}} and {{m|enm|ȝeat}} yielded the dialectal doublet {{m|en|yate}}), from the plural of {{inh|en|ang|ġeat}} (specifically {{m|ang|gatu}}), from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*gat}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*gatą||hole, opening}}.

See also {{cog|non|gat}}, {{cog|sv|-}} and {{cog|nl|gat}}, {{cog|nds|Gaat}}, {{m|nds|Gööt}}.
