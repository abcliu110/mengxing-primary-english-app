===Etymology===
{{back-formation|en|collaborator}}, from {{der|en|fr|collaborateur}}, or else modeled on {{uder|en|fr|collaborer}} or directly from {{uder|en|la-lat|collabōrātus}}, past participle of {{m|la|collabōrō|collabōrāre}}.<ref>{{R:Online Etymology Dictionary|pos=v}}</ref> Displaced native {{ncog|ang|efnwyrċan}}.
