===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|windy}}, from {{inh|en|ang|windiġ||windy}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*windigaz||windy}}, equivalent to {{af|en|wind<id:air>|-y<id:adjectival>}}. Cognate with {{cog|stq|wiendich||windy}}, {{cog|fy|winich||windy}}, {{cog|nl|winderig||windy}}, {{cog|nds-de|windig||windy}}, {{cog|de|windig||windy}}, {{cog|sv|vindig||windy}}, {{cog|is|vindugur||windy}}.

The “frightened” sense probably derives from the phrase [[have the wind up]].
