===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*gʷreh₂-}}
{{bor+|en|hi|गुरु}} / {{bor|en|pa|ਗੁਰੂ}}, from {{der|en|sa|गुरु||venerable, respectable}}, originally "heavy" and in this sense cognate to English {{m|en|grieve}} and, more distantly, {{m|en|brute}}. {{doublet|en|grave}}. A traditional, though flawed etymology based on the [[w:Advayataraka Upanishad|Advayataraka Upanishad]] (line 16)<ref>{{cite-web|url=http://www.yousigma.com/religionandphilosophy/advayataraka.html|title=Advaya Taraka Upanishad(English Translation)|accessdate=December 15, 2011|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120206140012/http://www.yousigma.com/religionandphilosophy/advayataraka.html|archivedate=6 February 2012}}</ref> describes the syllables ''gu'' as “darkness” and ''ru'' as “destroyer”, thus ascribing the meaning of “one who destroys/dispels darkness” to the word.
