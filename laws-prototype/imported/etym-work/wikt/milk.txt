=====Usage notes=====
* ''Milk'' is often taken to mean [[cow's milk]] when the context makes that meaning certain or likely.
* ''Milk'' is often held to mean solely mammary milk from {{l|en|animal|animals|id=Q729}} (including humans), rather than any other liquid that mimics it.
** Following a [[European Union]] (EU) court ruling in 2017, the term ''milk'' cannot legally be used to market purely plant-based products ({{senseno|en|white liquid from a vegetable source}}) in the EU.<ref>{{cite-web|url=https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:62016CJ0422&from=EN|date=14 June 2017|work=EUR-Lex|title=Judgment of the Court (Seventh Chamber) of 14 June 2017|passage=The term ‘milk’ shall mean exclusively the normal mammary secretion obtained from one or more milkings without either addition thereto or extraction therefrom.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20251021114743/https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX%3A62016CJ0422&from=EN|archivedate=21 October 2025}}</ref>
