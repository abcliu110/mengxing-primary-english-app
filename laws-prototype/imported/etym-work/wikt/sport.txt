===Etymology===
{{ety|en|id=physical|:inh|enm:sporten<ety:from<enm:disporten<id:relax>>>|tree=1}}

From {{inh|en|enm|sporten|t=to divert, disport|pos=verb}} and {{m|enm|sport}}, {{m|enm|spoort}}, {{m|enm|sporte|pos=noun}}, apheretic shortenings of {{m|enm|disporten|pos=verb}} and {{m|enm|disport}}, {{m|enm|disporte|pos=noun}}, from {{der|en|fro|desporter|t=to divert, amuse, please, play; to seek amusement}}, etymologically meaning "to carry away (the mind from serious matters)," from {{pre|fro|des-|porter|nocat=1}}, from {{der|en|la|dis-}} + {{der|en|la|portāre}}, ultimately from {{der|en|la|deportāre}}, from {{pre|la|de-|portāre|nocat=1}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*per-|id=fare|t=to lead, pass over}}). Replaced native {{ncog|en|laik}}, {{m|en|lake|t=sport, fun, amusement}}, and {{ncog|enm|spile}}, {{m|enm|spyl|t=fun, sport, play}}. More at {{m|en|disport}}. {{doublet|en|disport|deport}}.
