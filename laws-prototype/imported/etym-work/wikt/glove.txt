==Norwegian Bokmål==

===Etymology===
[[File:Den italienske porten KOLABEKKSTRANDA Tjøme Norway Kystlandskap Kløft steinlagt tursti Svaberg granitt eik Recreational area Coastal nature Eroded smooth slopes of rock Paved hiking foot path boulders cobbles etc 2021-07-16.jpg|thumb|A rock [[cleft]] with a [[footpath]] on Tjøme, Norway.]]
{{inh+|nb|gmq-mno|glófi||cleft, ravine}}, ultimately from {{inh|nb|gem-pro|*glubōn-}}, the noun stem reconstructed by Kroonen.<ref name="Kroonen">{{cite-book|author=Guus Kroonen|year=2013|title=Etymological Dictionary of Proto-Germanic|location=Leiden, Boston|publisher=Brill|page=180}}</ref> Related to {{m|non|gljúfr||gorge, ravine}} (whence {{m|nb|juv}}).

Kroonen places the word in the same Germanic family as {{m|gem-pro|*gleppan-||to slip}} and {{m|gem-pro|*gleupan-||to gape}}, comparing this family with {{cog|lt|glebti|glèbti|to become smooth}}.<ref>{{cite-book|author=Guus Kroonen|year=2013|title=Etymological Dictionary of Proto-Germanic|location=Leiden, Boston|publisher=Brill|pages=180–183}}</ref>

Cognate with {{cog|nn|glove}}; compare {{cog|is|glufa||crack, gap}}, {{cog|ang|glof||cliff}} and {{cog|nl|gleuf||groove, slot}}.<ref name="Kroonen"/>
