==English==

===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:allowen<ety:bor<xno:alouer<ety:from<fro:aloer<id:allaudare>><fro:aloer<id:allocare>>>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|allowen}}, {{m|enm|alowen}}, a borrowing from {{der|en|xno|allouer}}, {{m|fro|alouer}}, from {{der|en|la-med|allaudō|allaudāre}}, merged with {{m|fro|alouer}}, from {{der|en|la-med|allocō||to assign}}. {{doublet|en|allaud}} (via {{m|la|allaudāre}}) or {{m|en|allocate}} (via {{m|la|allocāre}}).

The similarity with {{noncog|enm|alyfen}} (from {{ncog|ang|ālīefan}}) and {{cog|de|erlauben}}, both from {{cog|gem-pro|*uzlaubijaną|t=to allow}}, is coincidental.
