==English==

===Etymology===
{{PIE word|en|sḗh₂l}}
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:salty|tree=1|text=+}} Equivalent to {{suffix|en|salt|y}}.

Compare {{cog|stq|soaltig|t=salty}}, {{cog|fy|sâltich|t=salty}}, {{cog|nl|zoutig|t=salty}}, {{cog|nds-de|soltig|t=salty}}, {{cog|de|salzig|t=salty}}.

{{sense|coarse; irritated, annoyed}} Referencing the sharp, 'spicy' flavor of salt.
{{sense|indignant}} Perhaps implying the person is a crybaby, shedding salty tears, or derived from the preceding.
