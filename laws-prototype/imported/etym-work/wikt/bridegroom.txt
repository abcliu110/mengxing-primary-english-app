===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|brydgrome}}, {{m|enm|bridegome}}, from {{inh|en|ang|brȳdguma}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*brūdigumô}}; equivalent to Old English {{m|ang|brȳd||bride}} + {{m|ang|guma||man}}. Altered by folk etymology to end with {{m|en|groom}}, with it re-analyzed as or influenced by {{m|enm|grom}}, {{m|enm|grome||attendant}}, as ''guma'' was obsolete. Cognate with {{cog|stq|Brüüdicham}}, {{cog|nl|bruidegom}}, {{cog|af|bruidegom}}, {{cog|nds-de|Brödigam}}, {{m|nds-de|Brüdigam}}, {{m|nds-de|Brögam}}, {{m|nds-de|Brügam}}, {{cog|pdt|Briegaum}}, {{cog|de|Bräutigam}}, {{cog|nb|brudgom}}, {{cog|nn|brudgom}}, {{cog|da|brudgom}}, {{cog|sv|brudgum}}, {{cog|is|brúðgumi}}, {{cog|fo|brúðgómur}}.
