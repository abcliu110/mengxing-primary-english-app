==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|hucche||storage chest}}, variation of {{m|enm|whucce}}, from {{inh|en|ang|hwiċe}}, {{m|ang|hwiċċe||box, chest}}. Spelling influenced by {{cog|fro|huche||chest}}, from {{cog|la-med|hūtica}}, from a different {{cog|gem}} root, from {{cog|frk|*hutta}}, from {{cog|gem-pro|*hudjō}}, {{m|gem-pro|*hudjǭ||box, hut, hutch}}. Akin to {{cog|ang|hȳdan||to conceal; hide}}. More at {{m|en|hide}}, {{m|en|hut}}.

{{sense|cricket pavilion or dressing room}} An extension of the [[rabbit]] metaphor.
