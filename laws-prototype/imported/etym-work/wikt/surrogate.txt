==English==

===Etymology===
From {{der|en|la|surrogātus}}, perfect passive participle of {{m|la|surrogō||to choose someone as a substitute}}; a variant of {{m|la|subrogō}}, from {{m|la|sub||under}} + {{m|la|rogō||ask}}.
