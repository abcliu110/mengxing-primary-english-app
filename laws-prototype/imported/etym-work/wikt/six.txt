===Etymology===
{{ety|fr|:inh|frm:six|tree=1}}
{{inh+|fr|frm|six}}, from {{inh|fr|fro|sis}}, {{m|fro|six}}, from {{inh|fr|la|sex}}, from {{inh|fr|ine-pro|*swéḱs}}.

The numbers {{m|fr||six}} and {{m|fr|dix|t=ten}}, as well as the pronoun {{m|fr|tous|t=all}}, are remnants of the Old and Middle French pronunciation system where final ''-s'' or ''-x'' was silent before consonants, pronounced {{IPAchar-lite|/z/}} before vowels, and {{IPAchar-lite|/s/}} in [[pausa]]. The only change is that the pausal pronunciation is now invariably used when these words do not precede their referent.
