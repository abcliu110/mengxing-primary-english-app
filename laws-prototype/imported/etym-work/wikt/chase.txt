===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*kap-|id=seize}}
From {{inh|en|enm|chacen}}, from {{der|en|xno|chacer}}, {{der|en|fro|chacier}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*captiāre}}, from {{der|en|la|captō|captāre}}, frequentative of {{m|la|capiō|capere}}. Compare {{cog|fr|chasser||to hunt”, “to chase}}, {{cog|es|cazar||to hunt}}, {{cog|pt|caçar||to hunt}}
, see {{cog|no|skysse||to hunt}}.
{{doublet|en|catch}} and related to {{m|en|capture}}. Displaced native {{cog|ang|ōht}}, {{m|ang|ēhtnes}}, and {{m|ang|wāþ}}. Broadly overtook {{cog|ang|huntaþ}}.

{{s|to nag; to pressure}} Likely influenced by {{m|yue|催||to urge; to pressure}} and {{m|yue|追||to chase}}, which are near-homophones.
