From Late {{inh|en|enm|fidelite|t=allegiance, fealty; faithfulness, trust; agreement made in good faith}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=fīdẹ̄litẹ̄|pos=n|id=MED15884}}</ref> from {{der|en|fro|fidélité|t=faithfulness, fidelity}} (modern {{cog|fr|fidélité}}), from {{der|en|la|fidēlitās|t=faithfulness, fidelity}}, from {{m|la|fidēlis|t=dependable, trustworthy; faithful, loyal}} + {{m|la|-tās|pos={{lg|suffix}} forming {{lg|feminine}} {{lg|abstract noun}}s denoting a state of being}}. {{m|la||Fidēlis}} is derived from {{m|la|fidēs|t=belief, faith; confidence, trust; loyalty, fidelity; etc.}}<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=3055122470|date=June 2026|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref> (from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰidʰ-}}, the {{lg|zero-grade}} of {{m|ine-pro|*bʰeydʰ-|t=to convince; to trust}} (whence {{cog|en|bide}})) + {{m|la|-ēlis}} (a variant of {{m|la|-ālis|pos=suffix forming {{lg|adjective}}s of relationship}}). {{doublet|en|fealty}}.
