====Usage notes====
* In the mediaeval collection [[w:Diplomatarium Norvegicum|Diplomatarium Norvegicum]], at the end of the 16th century, the masculine '''their''' appears to have entirely superseded the feminine '''thær''' and neuter '''thau'''. In some dialects, the accusative '''tha''' seems to have become dominant whilst disappearing in others.
