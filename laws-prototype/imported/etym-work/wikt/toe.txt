==Afrikaans==

===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|af|nl|toe||then}}, a chiefly dialect variant of {{m|nl|toen}}, from {{inh|af|dum|doe}}. The ''-n'' in Dutch ''toen'' was added by analogy with {{m|af|dan||then}}.

====Adverb====
{{head|af|adverb}}

# {{lb|af|referring to the past}} [[then]]; at that time; at that moment
#: {{ux|af|Ek het '''toe''' nog nie lank in Pretoria gewoon nie.|I hadn’t lived in Pretoria for long '''then'''.}}

=====See also=====
* {{l|af|dan}} {{i|“then” referring to the present and future}}

====Conjunction====
{{head|af|conjunction}}

# {{lb|af|referring to the past}} [[when]]; [[as]]
#: {{ux|af|'''Toe''' ek by my skoonouers kuier, het ons elke dag gebraaide vleis geëet.|'''When''' I stayed at my in-laws’, we had fried meat every day.}}

=====Usage notes=====
* Since “toe” by itself refers always to the past, it is often followed by the simple form of the verb (“present tense”) as in the example above, rather than the perfect. However, verbs that have a preterite use this form.

=====See also=====
* {{l|af|wanneer}} {{i|“when” referring to the present and future}}

===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|af|nl|toe}}, from {{inh|af|dum|toe}}.

====Postposition====
{{head|af|postposition}}

# {{lb|af|local}} [[to]]
#: {{ux|af|Ons gaan skool '''toe'''.|We’re going '''to''' school.}}

=====Usage notes=====
* If an article, determiner, or adjective is to precede the noun, the preposition {{m|af|na}} must be used additionally:
:: ''Ons gaan '''na''' die nuwe skool '''toe.'''''
::: We’re going to the new school.
