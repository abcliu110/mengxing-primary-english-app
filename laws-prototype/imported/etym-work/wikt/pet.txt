===Etymology 1===
Originally northern dialectal, from {{bor|en|sco|pet|t=an animal that has been tamed and is kept as a pet; a darling or favourite; a petted or spoiled child}}, probably from {{der|en|gd|peata|t=pet, tamed animal, spoiled child}}, from {{der|en|mga|petta}}, {{m|mga|peta|t=pet, lap-dog}}, of {{unc|en|title=uncertain}} origin, possibly from a pre-Indo-European {{der|en|qfa-sub}}.<ref>{{R:sga:Schrijver:2000}}</ref> Compare also {{m|en|peat|t=pet, darling, woman}}, which is likely not related.

The verb is derived from the noun.
