==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*weǵ-}}
From late 14th century {{der|en|enm|waiter}}, {{m|enm|wayter|t=attendant, watchman}}. {{surf|en|wait|-er}}. Sense of "servant who waits at tables" is from late 15th century, originally in reference to household servants; in reference to inns, eating houses, etc., it is attested from 1660s. Feminine form {{m|en|waitress}} first recorded 1834.<ref>http://dictionary.reference.com/browse/waiter?s=t</ref>

The London Stock Exchange sense harks back to the early days of trading in coffee-shops.
