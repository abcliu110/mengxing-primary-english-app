===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*gʰebʰ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|yifte}}, {{m|enm|ȝift}}, partly from {{inh|en|ang|ġift}}, {{m|ang|ġyft|t=giving, consideration, dowry, wedding}} and {{der|en|non|gipt|t=gift, present, wedding}}; both from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*giftiz|t=gift}}. Equivalent to {{affix|en|give|-t|id2=th}} (etymologically {{affix|en|yive|-t|id2=th}}).
