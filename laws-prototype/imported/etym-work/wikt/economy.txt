===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*weyḱ-|*nem-|id2=distribute}}
From {{inh|en|enm|yconomye}}, {{m|enm|yconomy}}, borrowed via {{der|en|fro|yconomie}} or [[w:Medieval Latin|Medieval Latin]]<ref>{{R:MED Online|īconomī(e|n|MED21656}}</ref> from {{der|en|la|oeconomia}}, from {{der|en|grc|οἰκονομία||management of a household, administration}}, from {{m|grc|οἶκος||house}} + {{m|grc|νέμω||distribute, allocate}}. {{surf|en|eco-|-nomy}}. The first recorded sense of the word '''economy''', found in a work possibly composed in 1440, is “the management of economic affairs”, in this case, of a monastery.
