====Usage notes====
Although the word ''comfortable'' looks ([[etymonically]]) like one of its senses could be synonymous with {{m|en|consolable}}, it does not have that sense; the absence of that sense is simply a [[w:accidental gap|lexical gap]]. In parallel, the same is true of {{m|en|comfortability}} and {{m|en|consolability}}, as well as {{m|en|uncomfortable}} and {{m|en|inconsolable}}.
