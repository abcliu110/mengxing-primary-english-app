===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|transitive}} To cover with newspaper.
#: {{ux|en|She '''newspapered''' one end of the room before painting the bookcase.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|transitive}} To engage in the business of journalism
#: {{ux|en|His '''newspapered''' his way through the South on the sports beat, avoiding dry towns.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|obsolete}} To [[harass]] somebody through [[newspaper]] articles.
#: {{ux|en|He got '''newspapered''' out of public life.}}

====Usage notes====
* The ''engage in journalism'' sense usually appears in its [[gerund]] form, newspapering.
* The ''harass'' sense usually appeared in passive constructions. It is no longer current.
