==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*per-|id1=before|*(s)teg-|id2=cover}}
Attested in English since 1530, from {{der|en|la|prōtēctus||covered, protected}}, past participle of {{m|la|prōtegō|prōtegō, prōtegere|to cover the front, protect}} from {{m|la|prō}}, {{affix|la|prō-|t1=before, in front of|tegō|alt2=tegō, tegere|t2=to cover|nocat=1}}, see {{m|en|tegument}}. Largely displaced native Middle English {{m|enm|shelden}}, from Old English {{m|ang|sċildan}} (“to protect,” literally “to shield”).
