===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:pocket<ety:bor<xno:pochete<alt:poket>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|pocket||bag, sack}}, from {{der|en|xno|fro|poket}}, {{der|en|fro-nor|poquet}}, {{m|fro|poquete}}, diminutive of {{m|fro|poque}}, {{m|fro|poke||bag, sack}} (compare modern Norman {{m|no|pouquette}} and modern French {{m|fr|pochette}} from Old French {{m|fro|pochete}}, from {{m|fro|puche}}), from {{der|en|frk|*pokō|t=pouch}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*puhô||bag; pouch}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bew-||to blow, swell}}. Equivalent to {{suf|en|poke|-et|id2=diminutive}}. {{doublet|en|pochette}}. Cognate with {{cog|dum|poke}}, {{cog|gsw|Pfoch||purse, bag}}, {{cog|ang|pocca}}, {{m|ang|pohha||poke, pouch, pocket, bag}}, {{cog|non|poki||bag, pocket}}. Compare the related {{m|en|poke|t=sack or bag}}. See also Modern {{cog|fr|pochette}} and {{cog|la|bucca}}.
