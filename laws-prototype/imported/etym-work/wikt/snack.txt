===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:bor|dum:snacken<ety:uder<gmw-pro:*snakkōn<ety:inh<gem-pro:*snakkōną>>>>}}
From {{bor|en|dum|snacken|t=to snack}}, possibly from {{der|en|gem-pro|*snakkōną||to smack one's lips, open one's mouth to eat; to snatch (with one's mouth?), snack on}}, whence also {{dbt|en|''[[snack#Etymology_2|etymology 2]]''|notext=1}}; ultimately {{onomatopoeic|en|nocap=1|nocat=1}}. Cognate with {{cog|de|schnäken|t=to snack}}. More at {{l+|gem-pro|*snak-}}.
