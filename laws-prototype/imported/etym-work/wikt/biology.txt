===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=life science|:lbor|la-new:biologia<id:biology>|tree=1}}
{{bor+|en|la-new|biologia}} (1766), itself from {{der|en|grc|-}} {{suffix|grc|βίος|gloss1=bio-, life|λογία|gloss2=-logy, branch of study, to speak|nocat=1}}. {{surf|+af|en|bio-|-logy}}.

In English, first attested in the modern meaning in the work of English physician {{w|Thomas Beddoes}} in 1799. The term is also recorded in the sense of ''a biographical history'' in the work of {{w|Dudley Loftus}} in 1686, but this is considered by the ''{{w|Oxford English Dictionary}}'' to be an isolated use.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=2466813778}}</ref> The modern {{cog|el|βιολογία}} is borrowed from the English term and {{cog|fr|biologie}} via {{w|international scientific vocabulary}}. {{piecewise doublet|en|zoology}}.
