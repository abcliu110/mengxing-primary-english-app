===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|en|enm|signen}}, {{m|enm|seinen}}, {{m|enm|seinien}}, partly from {{inh|en|ang|seġnian|t=to mark; sign}} and partly from {{der|en|xno|en|seigner}}, {{m|en|seiner}} et al., {{uder|en|fro|signer}} et al., and their source {{der|en|la|signare|signāre|to mark, seal, indicate, signify}}; all from {{der|en|la|signum||a mark, sign}}; see Etymology 1, above. Compare {{m|en|sain}}.
