===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|id=month|:der|enm:August<ety:der<ang:Augustus>>|tree=1}}
Early {{der|en|enm|August|August(us)}}, re-Latinized from {{der|en|ang|Agustus}}, from {{cog|la-lat|Agustus}}, from {{der|en|la|augustus||month of August}}, from the agnomen {{m|la|Augustus||venerable}} of {{w|Augustus|Gaius Julius Caesar Augustus}}, possibly from either Old Latin {{m|itc-ola|*augos|t=increase}}, from {{der|en|itc-pro|*augos}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|-}} base {{m|ine-pro|*h₂ewg-||to increase}}; or {{cog|la|avis||bird}}, referring to divination by observing bird flights, singing, feeding or entrails + {{m|la|garrio|garrīre|t=to chatter}}. {{dbt|en|august|Augustus}}.
