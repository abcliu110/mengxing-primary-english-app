===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=tree|:inh|enm:peche|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|peche}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|pesche}} ({{cog|fr|pêche}}), {{der|en|la-vul|*pessica}} (cf. Medieval Latin {{m|la|pesca}}) from {{der|en|la-lat|persica}}, from Classical {{der|en|la|mālum}} {{m|la|persicum}}, from {{der|en|grc|[[μῆλον|μᾶλον]] [[Περσικός|περσικόν]]|t=Persian apple}}. Displaced {{cog|enm|persogȝe}}, from {{cog|ang|persoc}}, ultimately from the same Latin root above.
