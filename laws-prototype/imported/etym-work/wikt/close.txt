===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)kleh₂w-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|closen||to close, enclose}}, partly continuing (in altered form) earlier {{inh|en|enm|clusen|t=to close}} from {{inh|en|ang|clȳsan||to close, shut}}; compare {{m|en|beclose}}, {{m|en|foreclose}}, etc.), and partly derived from {{inh|en|enm|clos|pos=a|t=close, shut up, confined, secret}}, from {{der|en|fro|clos||close, confined|pos=adjective}}, from {{der|en|la|clausus||shut up|pos=past participle}}, from {{m|la|claudō||to bar, block, close, enclose, bring an end to, confine}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kleh₂w-||key, hook, nail}}, related to {{cog|la|clāvis||key, deadbolt, bar}}, {{m|la|clāvus||nail, peg}}, {{m|la|claustrum||bar, bolt, barrier}}, {{m|la|claustra||dam, wall, barricade, stronghold}}. Cognate with {{cog|grc|κλείω}}, {{m|grc|κλείς||bar, bolt, key}}, {{cog|ru|заключи́ть}} (via {{m|ru|ключ}}), {{cog|de|schließen||to close, conclude, lock}}, {{cog|nl|sluiten||to close, conclude, lock}}. Partially replaced {{cog|ang|lūcan||to close, lock, enclose}}, (whence English [[lock]]). Doublet of {{m|en|clause}}.

=====Usage notes=====
Due to the near-opposite meanings relating to fluid flow and electrical components, these usages are deprecated in safety-critical instructions, with the words ''to "on"'' or ''to "off"'' preferred, so instead of ''Close valve A; close switch B'' use ''Turn valve A to OFF; turn switch B to ON''.
