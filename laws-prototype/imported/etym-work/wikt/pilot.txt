===Etymology===
From {{der|en|frm|pilot}}, {{m|frm|pillot}}, from {{der|en|it|pilota}}, {{m|it|piloto}}, older also {{m|it|pedotta}}, {{m|it|pedot(t)o}} (the form in ''pil-'' is probably influenced by {{m|it|pileggiare||to sail, navigate}}); ultimately from unattested {{der|en|gkm|*πηδώτης||helmsman}}, from {{der|en|grc|πηδόν||blade of an oar, oar}},<ref>[http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.04.0057%3Aentry%3Dphdo%2Fn Liddell-Scott entry for πηδόν]</ref> hence also [[Ancient Greek|Ancient]] and [[Modern Greek]] {{m|grc|πηδάλιον||rudder}}.<ref>[http://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.04.0057%3Aentry%3D%2382894 Liddell-Scott entry for πηδάλιον]</ref>
