===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*peh₂-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|feden}}, from {{inh|en|ang|fēdan||to feed}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*fōdijan}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*fōdijaną||to feed}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*peh₂-||to guard, graze, feed}}. Cognate with {{cog|fy|fiede||to nourish, feed}}, {{cog|nl|voeden||to feed}}, {{cog|da|føde||to bring forth, feed}}, {{cog|sv|föda||to bring forth, feed}}, {{cog|is|fæða||to feed}}, and more distantly with {{cog|la|pāscō||feed, nourish|pos=verb}} through Indo-European.
