===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:filme|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|filme}}, from {{inh|en|ang|filmen|t=film, membrane, thin skin, foreskin}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*filmīn-}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*filmīn-|t=thin skin, membrane}} (compare {{cog|gem-pro|*felma-|t=skin, hide}}), from {{der|en|ine-pro||*pél-mo-|t=membrane}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*pel-|t=to cover, skin}}. Cognate with {{cog|ofs|filmene|t=thin skin, human skin}}, {{cog|dum|velm}}, {{m|dum|vilm|t=fleece, film, membrane}}, {{cog|goh|felm|t=peel, skin, wrap}}, {{cog|ang|*felma}} (in {{m|ang|ǣġerfelma|t=egg membrane}}). Related also to {{cog|nl|vel|t=sheet, skin}}, {{cog|de|Fell|t=skin, hide, fur}}, {{cog|sv|fjäll|t=fur blanket, cloth, scale}}, {{cog|no|fille|t=rag, cloth}}, {{cog|lt|plėvē|t=membrane, scab}}, {{cog|ru|плева́|t=membrane}}, {{cog|grc|πέλμα|t=sole of the foot}}. More at [[fell#Etymology_2|fell]]. Sense of ''a thin coat of something'' is 1577, extended by 1845 to the coating of chemical gel on photographic plates. By 1895 this also meant the coating plus the paper or celluloid.
