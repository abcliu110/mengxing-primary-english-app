===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:chere}}
From {{inh|en|enm|chere}}, from {{der|en|xno|chere}}, from {{der|en|fro|chiere||head, face; appearance; reception, hospitality; meal, dinner, food}} (obsolete Modern {{cog|fr|chère|id=visage}}—not related to {{m|fr|cher||dear}}), from {{der|en|la-lat|cara||head|id=head}}. The verb is derived from the noun.
