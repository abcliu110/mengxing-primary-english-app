===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=biscuit|:bor|nl:koekje<alt:koekie>|tree=1}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|nl|koekie}}, dialectal diminutive of {{m|nl|koek|t=cake}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*kōkô}} (compare {{cog|nds-de|Kookje|t=biscuit, cookie, cracker}}, {{cog|nds|Kook|t=cake}}, {{cog|de|Kuchen|t=cake}}). More at {{m|en|cake}}. Not related to {{ncog|en|cook}}.

The computing senses derive from {{m|en|magic cookie}}.
