===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*Hrewp-}}
{{partial calque|en|it|bancarotta|lit=a broken bench}}, from {{m|it|banca||[[bank]]|lit=[[bench]]}} + {{m|it|rotto|rotta|broken, rupted}}, which refers to an out-of-business bank, having its bench physically broken, signifying that the working moneylender was insolvent.
