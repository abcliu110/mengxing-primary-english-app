=====Usage notes=====
* The spelling ''metre'' is used by both the [[w:International Bureau of Weights and Measures|International Bureau of Weights and Measures]] and the [[w:International Organization for Standardization|International Organization for Standardization]] in their English-language texts, and is used in all English-speaking countries other than the US. The spelling used in the US is ''meter''—for instance, the US [[w:National Institute of Standards and Technology|National Institute of Standards and Technology]] and the United States Government Printing Office Style Manual use ''meter''.
