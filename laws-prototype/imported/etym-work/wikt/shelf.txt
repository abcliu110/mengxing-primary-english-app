===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:schelfe|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|schelfe}}, probably from {{inh|en|ang|sċielfe|sċylfe}}, {{m|ang|sċielfe|sċilfe|t=shelf, ledge, deck of a ship}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*skilfijā}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*skelfō|t=shelf, ledge, cliff}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)kelH-|t=to cut}}, distantly related to {{m|en|sculpt}}, {{m|en|carve}} and {{m|en|shell}}. Cognate with {{cog|nl|schelf|t=hay loft, haystack}}, {{cog|nds-de|Schelf|t=haystack}}, {{cog|non|skjalf|t=bench}}.
