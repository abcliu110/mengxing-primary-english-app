===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|top}}, {{m|enm|toppe}}, from {{inh|en|ang|topp|t=top, highest part; summit; crest; tassel, tuft; (spinning) top, ball; a tuft or ball at the highest point of anything}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*topp}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*tuppaz|t=braid, pigtail, end}}, of unknown ultimate origin. Compare typologically {{m+|la|apex}} (<< {{m+|ine-pro|*h₂ep-||to join, attach, fasten, fit|id=join}}).

Cognate with {{cog|stq|Top|t=top}}, {{cog|cim|sòpf|t=braid}}, {{cog|nl|top|t=top, summit, peak}}, {{cog|de|Topp|t=top of a mast}}, {{m|de|Zopf|t=braid, pigtail, plait, top}}, {{cog|lb|Zapp|t=plait, tress}}, {{cog|wym|cöp|t=braid, plait}}, {{cog|yi|צאָפּ|t=braid}}, {{cog|da|top|t=top}}, {{cog|is|toppur|t=top}}, {{cog|nb|-}}, {{cog|nn|-}} and {{cog|sv|topp|t=top, peak, summit, tip}}, {{cog|it|zuffa|t=brawl}}.

The sense of a spinning toy is separated from this, obscurely related to {{cog|nl|top}} and {{m|nl|dop}} in this sense, against Standard {{m+|nl|tol}}, and {{cog|fr|toupie}} having this sense.
