===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=layer|:inh|enm:skyn<id:skin>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|skyn}}, {{m|enm|skinn}}, from {{inh|en|ang|scinn}}, from {{der|en|non|skinn|t=animal hide}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*skinþą}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*sken-|t=to split off}}, nasal variant of {{m|ine-pro|*skeh₁i-d-||to cut}}. Partially displaced native Old English {{m|ang|hȳd|t=skin, hide}}, from which derives {{l|en|hide}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|schinde||bark}}, dialectal German {{m|de|Schinde||fruit peel}}; also {{cog|br|skant||scales}}, {{cog|sga|cenn|id=skin|t=covering, shell}}, {{cog|ga|scáin|t=to tear, burst}}, {{cog|la|scindō|t=to split, divide}}, {{cog|sa|छिनत्ति|tr=chinátti|t=to split}}.
