===Etymology 1===
First attested in the early 18th c. as a verb meaning “to press, be pressed, be wedged in”. Compare dialectal {{m|en|jammock|t=to press, squeeze, crush into a soft mass, chew food"; also "a soft, pulpy substance}}. Perhaps from {{inh|en|enm|chammen}}, {{m|enm|champen}} {{gl|"to bite upon something, gnash the teeth"; whence modern {{m|en|champ}}, {{m|en|chomp}}}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin; probably originally {{onomatopoeic|en|title=onomatopoeic}}.

The "performance" sense is first attested with regards to jazz in 1929, and its origin, though {{unc|en|nocap=1}}, is likely metaphorical, "something sweet made by the combination of many things", with influence from {{m|en|jamboree}}.
