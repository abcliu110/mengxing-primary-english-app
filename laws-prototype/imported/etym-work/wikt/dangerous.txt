===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|daungerous||difficult, severe, domineering, arrogant, fraught with danger}}, {{m|enm|dangerous}}, from {{der|en|xno|}}, from {{der|en|fro|dangereus||threatening, difficult}}, from {{m|fro|dangier}}. Equivalent to {{af|en|danger|-ous}}.

Displaced native {{noncog|ang|frēcne}}.

====Usage notes====
The standard comparative and superlative are ''more dangerous'' and ''most dangerous''; the forms {{l|en|dangerouser}} and {{l|en|dangerousest}} or {{l|en|dangerest}} exist but are nonstandard.
