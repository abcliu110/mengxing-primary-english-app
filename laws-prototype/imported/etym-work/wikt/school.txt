===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*seǵʰ-}}
[[Image:Moye Elementary School.JPG|thumb|Elementary school]]
From {{inh|en|enm|scole}}, from {{inh|en|ang|scōl|t=place of education}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*skōlu}}, from {{der|en|la-lat|schola}}, {{m|la|scola|t=learned discussion or dissertation, lecture, school}}, from {{der|en|grc|σχολή|t=spare time, leisure}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*seǵʰ-|t=to hold, have, possess}}. {{doublet|en|schola|shul}}.

Compare {{cog|ofs|skūle}}, {{m|ofs|schūle|t=school}} ({{cog|fy|skoalle}}, {{cog|stq|Skoule}}), {{cog|nl|school|t=school}}, {{cog|nds|School|t=school}}, {{cog|goh|scuola|t=school}}, {{cog|de|Schule|t=school}}, {{cog|non|skóli|t=school}}.

Influenced in some senses by {{m+|enm|schole||group of persons, host, company}}, from {{cog|dum|scole||multitude, troop, band}}. See {{m|en|school||group|id=group}}. Related also to {{cog|goh|sigi|t=victory|tr={{cog|de|Sieg}}}}, {{cog|ang|siġe}}, {{m|ang|sigor|t=victory}}.
