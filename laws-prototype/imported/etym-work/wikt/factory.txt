==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*dʰeh₁-}}

Probably from {{af|en|factor|-y}}, although in {{senseno|en|Q83405|t=a place where manufacturing takes place}} apparently influenced strongly by association with {{der|en|la-cla||fact-}}, the {{glossary|past participle|past participial}} {{glossary|stem}} of {{m|la|faciō|t=to make}}; compare {{m|en|manufactory}} and {{cog|la|factōrium|t=an oil press}}. In {{senseno|en|trading establishment|t=a trading establishment}}, originally after {{der|en|pt|feitoria}}. In {{senseno|en|invoice|t=an invoice or inventory}}, probably after obsolete {{der|en|nl|facture|t=bill}}; see {{m|en|facture}}.<ref>{{R:OED Online|factory|noun|2560710538|2014}}</ref>
Compare {{cog|nl|factorij}}, {{cog|frm|factorie}}, {{cog|es|factoría}}.
