===Noun===
{{en-noun|~}}

# The place where one lives ({{l|en|reside#Verb|resides}}); one's [[home]].
#* {{RQ:Macaulay Johnson|passage=Johnson took up his '''residence''' in London.}}
# A [[building]] or portion thereof used as a [[home]], such as a {{l|en|house#Noun}} or an {{l|en|apartment#Noun}} therein.
# The place where a [[corporation]] is established.
# The state of living in a particular place or [[environment]].
#* '''1713''', ''The History of the Common Law of England,'' Sir {{w|Matthew Hale (jurist)}}, [https://books.google.hu/books?id=xQNXAAAAcAAJ&pg=PA87&q=confessor%20residences Google Books, page 87]
#*: The confessor had often made considerable '''residences''' in Normandy.
# [[accommodation|Accommodation]] for students at a [[university]] or [[college]].
# The place where anything rests permanently.
#* {{RQ:Milton Eikonoklastes|passage=But when a king sets himself to bandy against the highest court and '''residence''' of all his regal power, he then {{...}} fights against his own majesty and kingship.}}
# [[subsidence|Subsidence]], as of a [[sediment]]
#* {{RQ:Bacon Sylva Sylvarum|passage=Separation{{...}}is wrought by Weight; as in the ordinary '''Residence''' or Settlement of Liquors.}}
# That which falls to the bottom of [[liquor]]s; [[sediment]]; also, [[refuse]]; [[residuum]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1638|author=w:Jeremy Taylor|title=Sermon on Gunpowder Treason
|passage=waters of a muddy '''residence'''}}
