=====Usage notes=====
* The singular form {{m|en||ship}} is sometimes used without any [[Appendix:Glossary#article|article]], producing such sentences as "In all, we spent three weeks aboard ship." and "Abandon ship!". (Similar patterns may be seen with many place nouns, such as {{m|en|camp}}, {{m|en|home}}, {{m|en|work}}, and {{m|en|school}}, but the details vary between them.)
* Ships were traditionally regarded as ladies, especially by the sailor, and the pronouns {{m|en|her}} and {{m|en|she}} are still sometimes used instead of {{m|en|it}}, though this usage is in decline.
