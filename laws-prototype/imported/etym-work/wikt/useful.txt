====Usage notes====
* Prepositions: ''useful'' is used in ''useful for <nowiki><purpose></nowiki>'', ''useful for <nowiki><person></nowiki>'' and ''useful to <nowiki><person></nowiki>''. The words ''useful to'' are also found in constructions such as ''It is useful to do'', in which ''to'' marks an infinitive rather than being a preposition.
