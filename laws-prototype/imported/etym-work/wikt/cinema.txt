===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:bor|fr:cinéma|tree=1}}
{{bor+|en|fr|cinéma}}, clipping of {{m|fr|cinématographe}} (term coined by the {{w|Lumière brothers}} in the 1890s), from {{der|en|grc|κίνημα|t=movement}} + {{m|grc|γράφω|t=write, record}}. Compare {{cog|de|Kino|t=cinema}}, ultimately from the same Greek source.
