===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*pleth₂-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|place}}, conflation of {{inh|en|ang|plæċe|t=place, an open space, street}} and {{der|en|fro|place|t=place, an open space}}, both from {{der|en|la|platēa|t=plaza, wide street}}, from {{der|en|grc|πλατεῖα}}, shortening of {{m|grc|[[πλατεῖα]] [[ὁδός]]|t=broad way}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*pleth₂-|t=to spread}}, extended form of {{m|ine-pro|*pleh₂-|t=flat}}. Displaced native {{noncog|ang|stōw}}, {{m|ang|stede}} (partially), and {{m|ang|-ern}}. Compare also {{noncog|en|pleck|t=plot of ground}}, {{noncog|fy|plak|t=place, spot, location}}, {{noncog|nl|plek|t=place, spot, patch}}. {{doublet|en|piatza|piazza|platz|plaza}}.

In the etymological chain from {{m+|la|platēa}}, note {{m+|fro|place}}, which has multiple descendants — including {{cog|de|Platz}}, itself with many descendants (e.g., {{cog|ru|плац}}). Also note a more distant chain node {{cog|grc|πλατύς}}, whence {{cog|en|Plato}} and {{cog|en|plate}} (via Latin).
