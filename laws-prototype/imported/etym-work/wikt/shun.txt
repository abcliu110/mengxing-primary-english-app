==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)kewH-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|schonen|t=to decline to do, avoid, fear}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sċunian|t=to shun, fear, avoid}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin, though probably ultimately connected to the {{der|en|ine-pro|-}} root {{m|ine-pro|*(s)kewH-|t=to cover, conceal}} via {{inh|en|gem-pro|*skunōną}}; see that entry for more discussion. 

Compare {{m|en|hide}}, {{m|en|shy}}, and {{cog|sa|स्कुनाति|t=to cover}}.
