===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|disagre|t=to refuse to assent to}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=disagrẹ̄ |pos=v|id=MED11756}}</ref> from {{der|en|xno|disagreer}}, {{m|fro|disagrer}}, {{m|fro|desagreer|t=to refuse assent}}, from {{der|en|fro|desagreer}}, {{m|fro|desagrëer|t=to be disagreeable; to be unpleasant}} (modern {{cog|fr|désagréer|t=to displease}});<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=v|id=53455|date=September 2017|nodot=yes}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=v}}</ref> the English word is analysable as {{af|en|dis-|agree}}.
