===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*wenh₁-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|wonder}}, {{m|enm|wunder}}, from {{inh|en|ang|wundor||wonder, miracle, marvel}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*wundr}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*wundrą}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|wunner||wonder}}, {{cog|fy|wonder}}, {{m|fy|wûnder||wonder, miracle}}, {{cog|nl|wonder||miracle, wonder}}, {{cog|nds|wunner}}, {{m|nds|wunder||wonder}}, {{cog|de|Wunder||miracle, wonder}}, {{cog|da,nb,nn,sv|under||wonder, miracle}}, {{cog|fo,is|undur||wonder}}.

The verb is from {{inh|en|enm|wondren}}, from {{inh|en|ang|wundrian}}, which is from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*wundrōną}}. Cognate with {{cog|stq|wunnerje}}, {{cog|fy|wûnderje}}, {{cog|nl|wonderen}}, {{cog|nds-de|wunnern}}, {{cog|de|wundern}}, {{cog|sv|undra}}, {{cog|is|undra}}.

Via PIE cognate with {{cog|en|wish}} (see more).
