===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|pece}}, {{m|enm|peece}}, {{m|enm|peice}}, from {{der|en|fro|piece}}, from {{der|en|la-lat|pettia|pecia}}, {{m|la|pettia|petia|t=broken piece, fragment; parcel of land}}, of {{unc|en|title=uncertain}} origin. Possibly ultimately from {{der|en|cel-gau|*pettyā}}, from {{der|en|cel-pro|*kʷezdis|t=piece, portion, quota}}. Compare {{cog|cy|peth|t=thing, stuff, junk}}, {{cog|br|pez|t=thing}}, {{cog|ga|cuid|t=part, portion}}. Compare {{cog|fr|pièce}}, {{cog|pt|peça}}, {{cog|es|pieza}}, {{cog|it|pezza}}, {{cog|it|pezzo}}. Displaced native {{ncog|enm|sticche}}, {{m|enm|stucche|t=piece, fragment}} from {{ncog|ang|styċċe|t=piece, portion}}, whence also {{ncog|en|styca}}.
