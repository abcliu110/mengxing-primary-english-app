===Etymology===
{{etymon|la|septem<id:seven>}}
{{unc|la}}, perhaps from the {{lg|haplology}}{{haplological form|la|notext=1}} of earlier {{m|la||*septe(m)-mēmbris|t=of or pertaining to the seventh month}}, from {{m|la||*septe(m)-mēnzris}}, from {{m|la|septem|t=seven}} + {{m|la||*mēnzris}}, from {{m|la|*mēnzlis}}, from {{affix|itc-pro|*mēns|t1=month|*-elis<alt:*-lis>|nocat=1}}; see {{m|la|septem}}, and {{m|la|mēnsis}}. Compare {{m|la|celeber}}, {{m|la|faenebris}}, {{m|la|fūnebris}}, {{m|la|lugubris}}, {{m|la|Mulciber}} and {{m|la|salūber}}. In the {{w|Roman calendar}}, the year began with {{m|la|Mārtius|t=March}}, and {{m-self|la|September}} was the seventh month of the year.
