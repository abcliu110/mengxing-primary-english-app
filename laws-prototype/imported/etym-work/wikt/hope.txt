===Etymology 2===
{{etymon|en|id=confidence|:inh|enm:hope|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|hope}}, from {{inh|en|ang|hopa||hope, expectation}}, from the same source as the verb {{m|en|hope}}.

Eclipsed non-native early modern {{ncog|en|esperance}}, borrowed from {{bor|en|frm|esperance}}.

====Noun====
{{en-noun|~}}

# {{lb|en|countable|_|or|_|uncountable}} The feeling of [[trust]], [[confidence]], [[belief]] or [[expectation]] that something wished for can or will happen.
#: {{ux|en|All '''hopes''' for a truce are gone after the latest attack.}}
#: {{ux|en|After losing my job, there's frail '''hope''' of affording my world cruise.}}
#: {{ux|en|There is still '''hope''' that we can find our missing cat.}}
#* {{RQ:Lincoln Pratt's Patients|chapter=3|passage=My '''hopes''' wa'n't disappointed. I never saw clams thicker than they was along them inshore flats. I filled my dreener in no time, and then it come to me that 'twouldn't be a bad idee to get a lot more, take 'em with me to Wellmouth, and peddle 'em out.}}
# {{lb|en|countable}} The actual thing wished for.
# {{lb|en|countable}} A person or thing that is a source of hope.
#: {{ux|en|We still have one '''hope''' left: my roommate might see the note I left on the table.}}
# {{lb|en|Christianity|uncountable}} The virtuous [[desire]] for future good.
#* {{RQ:KJV|1 Corinthians|13|13|passage=But now abideth faith, '''hope''', love, these three; and the greatest of these is love.}}
