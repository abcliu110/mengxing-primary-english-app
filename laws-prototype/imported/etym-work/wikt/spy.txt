==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|spien}}, aphetic variant of earlier {{m|enm|espien||to espy}}, from {{der|en|fro|espier||to spy}}, from {{der|en|frk|*spehōn||to spy}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*spehōną||to see, look}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-||to look}}. Akin to {{cog|de|spähen||to spy}}, {{cog|nl|spieden||to spy}}.

The noun displaced native {{ncog|ang|sċēawere|lit=watcher}}, which was also the word for "mirror." In this sense, the verb displaced {{ncog|ang|sċēawian}}, which was also the word for "to watch" and became the Modern English word {{m|en|show}}.

Distant cognate vie PIE with {{cog|la|speculātor}}, {{cog|grc|κατάσκοπος}}.

Compare typologically {{m+|ru|согляда́тай}} (akin to {{m|ru|гляде́ть}}).
