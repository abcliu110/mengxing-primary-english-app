===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:golden|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|golden}}, a restored form (due to the noun {{m|en|gold}}) of earlier {{der|en|enm|gulden}}, {{m|enm|gylden}}, {{m|enm|gilden}} {{gloss|"golden"; > {{cog|en|gilden}}}}, from {{der|en|ang|gylden||golden}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*gulþīn}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*gulþīnaz||golden, made of gold}}, equivalent to {{suffix|en|gold|en|id2=made of}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|gouden}}, {{m|nl|gulden|t=golden}}, {{cog|de|gülden}}, {{m|de|golden||golden}}, {{cog|da|gylden||golden}}. {{doublet|en|gilden}}. More at {{m|en|gold}}.
