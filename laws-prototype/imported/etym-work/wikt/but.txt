====Usage notes====
It is generally considered colloquial to use ''but'' at the beginning of a sentence:<br>
“'''But''' the tool has its uses!”<br>
Other conjunctions, such as ''however'' or ''nevertheless'', are considered more proper for that end, and are thus preferred in formal writing:<br>
“However, the tool still has its uses.”<br>
“Nevertheless, the instrument could still be used for its ''[[raison d'être]]''.”
