=====Usage notes=====
* A plainer term than more elevated {{m|en|beverage}}. '''Beverage''' is of French origin, while ''drink'' is of Old English origin, and this stylistic difference by origin is common; see {{w|list of English words with dual French and Anglo-Saxon variations}}.
* In the sense of ''any body of water'' the term is often associated with (a threat of) [[drowning]].
