===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|brekefast}}, {{m|enm|brekefaste}}, equivalent to {{compound|en|break|fast}} {{gloss|''literally'', "to end the nightly fast"}}, likely a variant of {{inh|en|ang|fæstenbryċe}}, {{gloss|''literally'', "fast-breach"}}. Cognate with {{cog|nl|breekvasten|t=breakfast}}.

====Usage notes====
* In the sense "meal eaten after a period of (now often religious) fasting", the word is more often spelled {{m|en|break-fast}} or {{m|en|break fast}}; in this sense it is also often pronounced as a literal compound of {{m|en|break}} and {{m|en|fast}}.<!--see BGC data cited in [[Wiktionary:Tea_room/2015/July#breakfast]]-->
