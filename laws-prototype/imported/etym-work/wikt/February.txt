===Etymology===
[[File:Abel Grimmer The Month of February.jpg|thumb|A 17th-century depiction of February in [[the Netherlands]]]]
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:Februarie<ety:der<la:Februārius>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|Februarie}}, {{m|enm|februari}}, {{m|enm|februare}}, from {{der|en|la|Februārius||the [[month]] of the [[Februa]]}}, from {{m|la|Februa||the [[purging|Purging]]s, the [[purification|Purification]]s}}, a Roman holiday two days after its [[ides]] (i.e., Feb. 15), + {{m|la|-arius||[[-ary]]: ''[[form]]ing [[adjective]]s''}}. {{m|la|Februa}} from {{m|la|februum||[[purging]]}}, from an earlier {{der|en|sbv}} word, possibly from {{der|en|ine-pro|*dʰewh₂-||[[smoke]], [[haze]]}} and thus cognate with {{m|en|thio-||[[sulfurous]]}} and {{der|en|grc|θεῖον||[[sulfur]]}} or from {{der|en|ine-pro|*dʰegʷʰris}}, an extension of the root {{m|ine-pro|*dʰegʷʰ-|gloss=to [[burn]]}} and thus cognate with {{m|en|fever}} and {{m|la|febris}}. A relatinization abandoning {{der|en|enm|feoverel}}, from {{der|en|fro|feverier}}, which itself displaced {{noncog|ang|solmōnaþ||mud month}}.
{{root|en|ine-pro|*dʰegʷʰ-}}
