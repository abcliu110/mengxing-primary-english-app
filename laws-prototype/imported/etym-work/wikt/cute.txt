===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₂eḱ-}}
[[Appendix:Glossary#aphetic|Aphetic]] form of {{m|en|acute}}, originally meaning “keenly perceptive or discerning, shrewd” (1731). Meaning transferred to “pretty, fetching” by US students (slang) {{circa2|1834|short=yes}}. Meaning drifted further to describe the pleasing attraction to features usually possessed by the young.
