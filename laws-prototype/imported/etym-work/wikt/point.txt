{{ety|en|id=noun|:inh|enm:poynt}}
From {{inh|en|enm|poynt}}, from {{der|en|fro|point|g=m||dot; minute amount}}, from {{der|en|la|pūnctum|t=a hole punched in; a point, puncture}}, substantive use of {{m|la|pūnctus|g=m}}, perfect passive participle of {{m|la|pungō|t=to prick, punch}}; alternatively, from {{der|en|fro|pointe|g=f||sharp tip}}, from {{der|en|la|pūnctus|pūncta|g=f|pos=past participle}}, all from {{der|en|itc-pro|*pungō|t=to sting, prick}}.

Mostly displaced native {{cog|enm|ord||point}}, from {{cog|ang|ord||point}}. {{doublet|en|pointe|ponto|puncto|punctum|punt<id:point>|punto}}.
