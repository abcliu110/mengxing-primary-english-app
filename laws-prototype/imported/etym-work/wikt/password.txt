===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|watchword}} {{lb|en|military|security}} A [[word]] relayed to a [[person]] to [[gain]] [[admittance]] to a [[place]] or to gain [[access]] to [[information]].
#: {{ux|en|Only if a would-be visitor knew the '''password''' du jour could he pass; the guards allowed no exceptions.}}
#* {{RQ:Bramah Max Carrados|passage=Now, Max, by what combination of circumstances is a rogue to know my '''password''', to be able to forge my signature, to possess himself of my key, and to resemble me personally? And, finally, how is he possibly to determine beforehand whether there is anything in my safe to repay so elaborate a plant?}}
# {{senseid|en|cryptography}} {{lb|en|computing|cryptography}} A [[string]] of [[character]]s used to [[log in]] to a [[computer]] or [[network]], to access a level in a [[video game]], and so on; {{l|en|archetype#Noun|archetypally}} a {{l|en|word#Noun}} but nowadays often an {{l|en|alphanumeric#Adjective}} string or a {{l|en|phrase#Noun}}.
#: {{ux|en|A strong '''password''' has a mixture of lowercase and uppercase letters, numbers, and punctuation marks.}}
