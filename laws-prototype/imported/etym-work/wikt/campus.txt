==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:bor|la:campus|tree=1}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|la|campus|t=field}}. {{dbt|en|camp|champ}}.

====Usage notes====
* The Latinate plural form {{m|en|campi}} is sometimes used, particularly with respect to colleges or universities; however, it is sometimes frowned upon. By contrast, the common plural form ''campuses'' is universally accepted.

===Etymology===
{{etymon|la|id=field|:inh<unc:1>|itc-pro:*kampos<id:campus><ety:inh<ine-pro:*kh₂émpos<ety:der<ine-pro:*kh₂em-<unc:1><t:to bend; to curve; to smooth>>>>>|:der<unc:1>|qfa-sub:-}}
Traditionally, from {{inh|la|itc-pro||*kampos}}, perhaps from {{der|la|ine-pro||*kh₂ém-po-s}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*kh₂em-|*kh₂emp-|t=to bend, curve; smooth}}, making it an exact cognate of {{cog|lt|kam̃pas||corner}} and {{cog|grc|καμπή|καμπ-|t=bend}}. Compare {{m|la|camur||curved, bent}} for the root without a ''-p-'' suffix.
{{rfe|la|Sense development: how do we get from "bend, curve" to "flat level ground"?  Was this originally in reference to flatter terrain where rivers form bends and curves?}}
Alternatively, perhaps an agricultural term borrowed from a {{der|la|qfa-sub}} language; this would explain the irregular correspondences between Latin and Greek.<ref>{{R:itc:EDL|campus|86}}</ref>
