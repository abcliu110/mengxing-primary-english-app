===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:moven<ety:bor<fro-nor:movoir<alt:mover>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|moven}}, {{m|enm|moeven}}, {{m|enm|meven}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro-nor|mover}}, {{m|fro|moveir}} and {{der|en|fro|mouver}}, {{m|fro|moveir|t=to move}} (compare modern French {{m|fr|mouvoir}} from Old French {{m|fro|movoir}}), from {{der|en|la|moveō|movēre|t=move; change, exchange, go in or out, quit}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*m(y)ewh₁-|t=to move, drive}}. Cognate with {{cog|lt|mauti|t=to push on, rush}}, {{cog|sa|मीवति|tr=mī́vati|t=pushes, presses, moves}}, {{cog|dum|mouwe|t=sleeve}}. Largely displaced native {{noncog|en|stir}}, from {{noncog|enm|stiren}}, {{m|enm|sturien}}, from {{noncog|ang|styrian}}. See also {{noncog|ang|hrēran}}.
