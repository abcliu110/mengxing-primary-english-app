Cognate with {{cog|da|lytte||to listen}}. Related to {{cog|ang|hlēoþor||noise, sound, voice, song, hearing}}, {{cog|ang|hlūd||loud, noisy, sounding, sonorous}}. More at {{l|en|loud}}.
