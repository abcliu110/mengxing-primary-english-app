===Etymology 1===
{{etymid|en|substance}}
{{ety|en|id=material|:inh|enm:wode|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|wode}}, from {{inh|en|ang|wudu}}, {{m|ang|widu||wood, forest, grove; tree; timber}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*widu}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*widuz||wood}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₁weydʰh₁-||to separate}}. The spelling developed as it did in {{m|en|wool}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|wede|t=wood, twig}}, {{cog|gmh|wite|t=wood}}, {{cog|da,nb,nn,sv|ved|t=wood}}, {{cog|ovd|wið|t=wood, timber}}, {{cog|fo,is|viður|t=wood}}, {{cog|nn|ved|t=wood, firewood}}, {{m|nn|vid|t=wide, broad}}. Further cognates include {{cog|ga|fiodh||t=a wood, tree}}, {{cog|sga|fid||t=tree}} and {{cog|cy|gwŷdd||t=trees}}, from {{cog|cel-pro|*widus||t=wood}}. Unrelated to {{noncog|nl|woud|t=forest}}, {{noncog|de|Wald|t=forest}} (see {{noncog|en|wold}}).

=====Usage notes=====
* In the sense of ''a forested area'', the singular generally refers to a discrete area of forest, while the plural is often used when a more vaguely defined area is meant. For the sense of ''woodwind instrument'', {{m|en|wind}} is the more common shortening.
