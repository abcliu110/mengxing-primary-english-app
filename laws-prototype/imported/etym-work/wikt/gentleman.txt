====Usage notes====
* Although ''gentleman'' is used in reference to a man and {{m|en|gentlemen}} is used as a polite form of address to a group of men, it is more common to directly address a single gentleman as {{m|en|sir}}.
* The singular possessive of the sense "any well-bred, well-mannered, or charming man" can appear in ad hoc compounds to describe a polite way of doing something; e.g. a "gentleman's sweep" when a dominant basketball team allowed the opponent one win in a series[https://web.archive.org/web/20201121111926/https://fivethirtyeight.com/features/the-bucks-played-it-safe-and-made-the-wrong-kind-of-history/].
