===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|ones|}}, from {{inh|en|ang|ānes}}, a remodelling (after {{m|ang|ān|t=one}}) of {{m|ang|ǣnes}}, itself an extension of {{m|enm|ǣne|t=once}} with the genitive suffix {{m|ang|-es}}. Equivalent to {{suf|en|one|-s}}. Cognate with {{cog|stq|enst}}, {{m|stq|insen|t=once}}, {{cog|fy|iens|t=once}}, {{cog|nl|eens|t=once}}, {{cog|nds-de|eens}}, {{m|nds-de|ins|t=once}}, {{cog|de|einst|t=once}}. More at {{l|en|one}} (including regarding the development of the pronunciation) and [[-s#Etymology_3|-s]].
