===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*sed-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|sete}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sǣte}}, possibly from (or simply cognate with) {{der|en|non|sæti|t=seat}}, both from {{der|en|gem-pro|*sētiją|t=seat}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*sed-|t=to sit}}; compare {{cog|ang|set|t=seat}}.

{{senseno|en|site|uc=1}} (“location or site”) is probably derived from {{inh|en|ang|sǣte|t=house}}, which is related to {{cog|goh|sāza|t=sedan, seat, domicile}}.
