{{etymon|en|id=animal|:bor|fr:panda}}
{{bor+|en|fr|panda}}, of {{unknown|en|unclear ultimate origin}} but probably from the second element of ''nigálya-pónya'', a local name for the [[red panda]] recorded in Nepal and Sikkim by {{w|Brian Houghton Hodgson}} (1800 or 1801&nbsp;– 1894), an ethnologist, naturalist and the British Resident of Nepal,<ref>{{cite-journal|author=B[rian] H[oughton] Hodgson|authorlink=Brian Houghton Hodgson|title=On the Cat-toed Subplantigrades of the Sub-Himalayas|journal=[[w:The Asiatic Society|Journal of the Asiatic Society of Bengal]]|location=Calcutta|publisher=Printed by J. Thomas, Baptist Mission Press|month=November|year=1847|year_published=1848|volume=XVI|issue=XI (New Series)|page=1118|pageurl=https://books.google.com/books?id=1U202J609YMC&pg=PA1118|oclc=1047491870|passage=To the Tibetans, Nepaulese, and Sikimites the Ailuri are known by the names Wáh, [...]. Also Yé and Nigálya pónya.}}</ref> possibly from {{der|en|ne|निँगाले|t=relating to a certain species of bamboo}} (the {{glossary|adjective|adjectival}} form of {{m|ne|निँगालो}}, a variant of {{m|ne|निङालो|t={{taxlink|Drepanostachyum intermedium|species}}, a species of bamboo}})<ref>Compare Hodgson, [https://books.google.com/books?id=1U202J609YMC&pg=PA1116 page 1116].</ref> + a regional {{der|en|bo|-}} name for the animal (compare regional {{cog|bo|ཕོ་ཉ|t=messenger}}).<ref name="OED">{{R:OED Online|pos=''n.{{sup|1}}''|noformat=yes|id=136731|date=March 2005|nodot=yes}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=panda{{sup|1}}|pos=n}}</ref>

{{glossary|Attributive}} uses of sense 2 (“a giant panda”) generally refer to that animal’s distinctive black and white coat colour.<ref name="OED"/>
