==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-}}
Recorded since 1793, from {{bor|en|fr|espionnage}}, from {{m|fr|espionner|t=to spy}}, from {{der|en|frm|espionner|t=to spy}}, from {{m|frm|espion|t=spy}}, from {{der|en|fro|espion|t=spy}}, from {{der|en|frk|*spehō|t=spy}}, from {{der|en|frk|*spehōn|t=to spy}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*spehōną|t=to spy, peek, peer}}. In modern times, the French pronunciation of the ''s'', which had fallen silent since the 13th century, was restored due to the influence of {{cog|it|spione|t=spy}}, and was therefore also adopted by the English. More at {{m|en|spy}}.
