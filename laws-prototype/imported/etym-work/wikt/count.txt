===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=compute|:inh|enm:counten|tree=1}}

From {{inh|en|enm|counten}}, borrowed from {{der|en|xno|conter}}, from {{der|en|fro|conter||add up; tell a story}}, from {{der|en|la|computō||to compute}}. In this sense, displaced native {{ncog|ang|tellan}}, whence {{w|Modern English}} {{m|en|tell}}. {{doublet|en|compute}}.

Compare typologically {{m|en|reckon}}, {{m+|ru|счита́ть}}, {{m|ru|счита́ться}}; the semantic evolution to {{m+|mn|санах}}.
