===Etymology 1===
From the phrase {{m|en||jobbe of work|t=piece of work}}, of uncertain origin. Perhaps from a variant of {{inh|en|enm|gobbe|t=mass, lump}}; or perhaps related to {{der|en|enm|jobben|t=to jab, thrust, peck}}, or {{der|en|enm|choppe|t=piece, bargain}}. More at {{m|en|gob}}, {{m|en|jab}}, {{m|en|chop}}.
