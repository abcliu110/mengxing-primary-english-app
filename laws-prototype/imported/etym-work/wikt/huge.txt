===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*kewk-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|huge}}, from {{der|en|fro|ahuge|t=high, lofty, great, large, huge}}, of {{unknown|en|nocap=1}} origin.

One theory derives it from an underlying {{m+|fro||a hoge|t=at height}}, from {{m|fro|a|t=at, to}} + {{m|fro|hoge|t=a hill, height}}, the latter from {{der|en|frk|*haug}} or cognate {{der|en|non|haugr|t=hill}}; both from {{der|en|gem-pro|*haugaz|t=hill, mound}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kowkós|t=hill, mound}}, from the root {{der|en|ine-pro|*kewk-}}, whence also {{m+|en|high}}.
