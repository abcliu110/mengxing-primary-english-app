===Etymology 3===
Generally thought to be a variant, attested since the 1950s, of {{m|en|scoff||eat (quickly)}} (of which {{m|en|scorf}} is another attested variant), itself a variant of {{m|en|scaff}}.<ref>{{R:MWO}}</ref><ref>{{R:Dictionary.com}}</ref> Sometimes alternatively suggested to be a dialectal survival of {{inh|en|ang|scearfian}}<!-- (which survived into Middle English as {{m|enm|scearfen||shred, cut up (a plant)}})-->, {{m|ang|sceorfan||gnaw, bite}} (compare {{m|en|scurf}}).<ref>{{R:Etymonline}}</ref>
