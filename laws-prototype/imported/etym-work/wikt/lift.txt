===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|liften}}, {{m|enm|lyften}}, from {{der|en|non|lypta||to lift, air|lit=to raise in the air}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*luftijaną||to raise in the air}}, related to {{m|gem-pro|*luftuz||roof, air}}, perhaps from {{der|en|ine-pro|*lewp-||to peel, break off, damage}} or from a root meaning roof (see {{m|gem-pro|*luftuz}}). Cognate with {{cog|da|-}} and {{cog|nb|løfte||to lift}}, {{cog|nn|-}} and {{cog|sv|lyfta||to lift}}, {{cog|de|lüften||to air, lift}}, {{cog|ang|lyft||air}}. See above. 1851 for the noun sense "a mechanical device for vertical transport".

{{sense|To steal}} For this sense Cleasby suggests perhaps a relation to the root of Gothic {{m|got|𐌷𐌻𐌹𐍆𐍄𐌿𐍃}} "thief", cognate with {{cog|la|cleptus}} and Greek {{m|grc|κλέπτω}}).<ref>[http://lexicon.ff.cuni.cz/html/oi_cleasbyvigfusson/b0270.html Hlenni] in Cleasby/Vigfusson ''An Icelandic-English Dictionary'' (1874) p. 270</ref> But perhaps simply from the idea of removing an item from a surface.

=====Usage notes=====
