===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:bor|is:w:Geysir<ety:from<is:geysa>>|tree=1}}
From {{bor|en|is|Geysir||Gusher}}, the {{der|en|is|-}} name of a particular [[hot spring]] in [[Iceland]] (see [[w:Geysir|Wikipedia]]), from the verb {{m|is|geysa||to [[gush]]}}, from {{der|en|non|geysa||to [[gush]]}}, which is mentioned in English as early as the 1760s in ''The Annual Register'', as “Geyser, a wonderful spring in the valley of Haukadal”.<ref>{{cite-web |titleurl=http://www.evs-translations.com/blog-com/geyser/ |title=source EVS Translations |accessdate=6 February 2014 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20140125013945/http://www.evs-translations.com/blog-com/geyser/ |archivedate=25 January 2014 }}</ref> {{doublet|en|gusher}}
