====Usage notes====
* In principle, {{m|en||folk}} behaves grammatically like the synonym {{m|en|people}}. As a countable noun (meaning “nation”) it may take the plural {{m|en||folks}}. As a collective plural (meaning “persons”) it remains unchanged. Informally, however, the form {{m|en||folks}} is also used in the collective sense (which is not possible for {{m|en||peoples}}). This latter usage is particularly common in the sense “relatives”.
