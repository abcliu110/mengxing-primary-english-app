===Etymology===
From {{af|en|hand|bag}}. The music genre is named from women dancing around a pile of their handbags in nightclubs. The verb is a reference to {{w|Margaret Thatcher}}'s handbag.<ref>{{cite-book|editor=David Levi-Faur|title=The Oxford Handbook of Governance|year=2012|publisher=OUP Oxford|page=316|pageurl=https://books.google.com/books?id=11qVVxK2y24C&lpg=PA316&dq=%22handbagging%20thatcher&hl=es&pg=PA316#v=onepage&q&f=false|isbn=978-0-19-162842-9|passage=Governance appeared to give way to hierarchical and even impositional Government, hence the invention of the new verb “to handbag” (coined because Mrs Thatcher always carried a large handbag and it was said that she could not look at any existing British institution without hitting it with her handbag).}}</ref>
