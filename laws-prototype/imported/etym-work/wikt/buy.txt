==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:byen|tree=+|text=}}
From {{inh|en|enm|byen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|bycġan||to buy, pay for, acquire, redeem, ransom, procure, get done, sell}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*buggjan}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*bugjaną||to buy}}, of uncertain origin. Perhaps from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰūgʰ-||to bend}}, or from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰewgʰ-||to take away, deliver}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|buy||to buy, purchase}}, obsolete {{cog|nl|beugen||to buy}}, {{cog|osx|buggian}}, {{m|osx|buggean||to buy}}, {{cog|non|byggja||to build, settle}}, {{cog|got|𐌱𐌿𐌲𐌾𐌰𐌽||to buy}}. The spelling with “u” is from the Southwest, while the pronunciation with {{IPAchar|/aɪ/}} is from the East Midlands.
