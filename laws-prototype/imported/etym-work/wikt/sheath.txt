{{root|en|ine-pro|*skey-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|sheth}}, {{m|enm|shethe|t=holder for a sword, knife, etc., scabbard, sheath}}{{nb...|chethe, scǽð, scaþ, schede, schete, scheth, schethe, seaþ, seeth, seth, sethe, shæþ, shede, sheethe, sheith, (Southwest) ssethe, sseþe, (early forms) scæðe, scaþe, seaþe|otherforms=1}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sċēaþ|t=sheath}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=shēth(e|pos=''n.''(1)|noformat=yes|id=MED39908|nodot=1}}; {{R:OED Online|entry=sheath|pos=''n.{{sup|1}}''|noformat=yes|id=177715|date=1914|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|entry=sheath|pos=n}}</ref> from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*skaiþiju}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*skaiþiz|t=sheath; covering}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*skey-|t=to dissect, split}} (possibly from the notion of a split stick with a sword inserted).

The English word is cognate with {{cog|da|skede}}, {{cog|nl|schede}}, {{cog|is|skeið}}, {{cog|de|Scheide}}, {{cog|nds|scheed}}, {{cog|no|skjede}}.

# {{senseid|en|scabbard}} A [[holster]] for a [[sword]]; a [[scabbard]].

# {{lb|en|botany}} The [[base#Noun|base]] of a [[leaf#Noun|leaf]] when [[sheathe|sheathing]] or [[invest]]ing a [[branch#Noun|branch]] or [[stem#Noun|stem]], as in [[grass#Noun|grasses]].
# {{senseid|en|electrical cable}} {{lb|en|electrical engineering}} The [[insulating#Adjective|insulating]] [[outer]] [[cover#Noun|cover]] of an [[electrical]] [[cable#Noun|cable]].

# {{senseid|en|dress}} {{lb|en|fashion}} A [[tight-fitting]] [[dress#Noun|dress]].
# {{lb|en|zoology}} The [[foreskin]] of certain [[animal]]s (for example, [[dog#Noun|dogs]] and [[horse#Noun|horses]]).
# {{lb|en|British|informal}} A [[condom]].
