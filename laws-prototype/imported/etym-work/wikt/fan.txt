#* {{quote-book|en|passage=How apt is the flutter of the '''fan''' to express the different affections of the belle: there is the angry flutter, the modest flutter, the timorous flutter, the confused flutter etc.|page=55|author=August Paul|url=https://www.google.com/books/edition/Addison_s_Influence_on_the_Social_Reform/ofFrDS_naWsC?hl=en&gbpv=1&dq=%22flutter+of%22+that&pg=PA55&printsec=frontcover|year=1876|title=Addison's Influence on the Social Reform of His Age|publisher=T.G. Meissner}}
# {{senseid|en|electrical device}} An electrical or mechanical device for moving air, used for cooling people, machinery, etc.
#: {{ux|en| He didn't switch on the '''fan''' because the temperature was 15 degrees Celsius.}}
# The action of fanning; [[agitation]] of the air.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1998|author=Brock Thoene; Bodie Thoene|title=A New Frontier: Saga of the Sierras|page=181
|passage="If I cannot be of service, then I certainly don't wish to impose," said McGinty, with a quick '''fan''' of breeze that indicated a sweeping bow.}}
# Anything resembling a hand-held fan in shape, e.g., a [[peacock]]’s [[tail]] or a leaf of [[fan coral]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1888|year_published=1928|author=w:Oscar Wilde|title={{w|The Happy Prince and Other Tales}}|volume=|location=London|publisher=Duckworth|chapter=The Nightingale and the Rose|page=43|passage=“My roses are red,” it answered, “as red as the feet of the dove, and redder than the great '''fans''' of coral that wave and wave in the ocean-cavern.”}}

{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[blow]] air on (something) by means of a fan (hand-held, mechanical or electrical) or otherwise.
#: {{ux|en|We enjoyed standing at the edge of the cliff, being '''fanned''' by the wind.}}
#* {{RQ:Carroll Alice|page=19|passage=Alice took up the fan and gloves, and, as the hall was very hot, she kept '''fanning''' herself all the time she went on talking: {{...}}}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[slap]] (a [[behind]], especially).
