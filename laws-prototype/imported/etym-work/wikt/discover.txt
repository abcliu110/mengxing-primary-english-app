|passage=All this has led to an explosion of protest across China, including among a middle class that has '''discovered''' nimbyism.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|obsolete}} To [[remove]] the [[cover]] from; to [[uncover]] (a head, building etc.).
#* {{RQ:Beckett Watt|text=He set down his bags beside him, on the beautiful red floor, and he took off his hat, for he had reached his destination, '''discovering''' his scant red hair, and laid it on the table beside him.}}

#: {{ux|en|The gust of wind '''discovered''' a bone in the sand.}}

#* {{quote-web|en|title=''Gordon v. Canada,'' 2017 FC 454|author=Ward K. Branch|url=https://www.canlii.org/en/bc/bcsc/doc/2019/2019bcsc1067/2019bcsc1067.html|work=CanLII|date=2 July 2019|accessdate=13 April 2021|text=Indeed, the plaintiffs suggest that they may not need to call Ms. Samji at all if they are allowed to '''discover''' the defendant on the new documents before any new trial takes place.}}

#: {{ux|en|I '''discovered''' my plans to the rest of the team.}}

#* {{RQ:Bacon Essayes|chapter=Of Adversity|passage=Prosperity doth best '''discover''' vice; but adversity doth best '''discover''' virtue.}}
#* {{RQ:Swift Gulliver|part=II|page=269|passage=It was in vain to '''diſcover''' my Reſentments, which were always turned into Ridicule; and I was forced to reſt with Patience while my noble and moſt beloved Country was ſo injuriouſly treated.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1828|author=w:Thomas Keightley|title=The Fairy Mythology|volume=I|location=London|publisher=William Harrison Ainsworth|page=312|passage=John '''discovered''' himself to his parents, and Elizabeth to hers, and the wedding-day was soon fixed, and such a wedding was never seen; before or since in the island of Rügen[.]}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|obsolete}} To [[reconnoitre]], [[explore]] (an area).
#* {{RQ:Malory Le Morte Darthur|book=V|chapter=ix|text=they seyde the same, and were aggreed that Sir Clegis, Sir Claryon, and Sir Clement the noble, that they sholde '''dyscover''' the woodys, bothe the dalys and the downys.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|obsolete}} To [[manifest]] without [[design]]; to [[show]]; to [[exhibit]].
