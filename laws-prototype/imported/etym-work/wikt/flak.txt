===Noun===

#: {{syn|sv|isflak|q=ice floe}}
# {{senseid|sv|bed}} a [[bed]]; a [[flatbed]]; a [[box]] ((open) bed of a vehicle, like a (pickup, dump, etc.) truck, trailer, moped with a cargo bed, etc.)

====Usage notes====
Usually of a truck bed in ({{senseno|sv|bed}}). Often specifically of the bed floor when of a trailer, for example when giving its dimensions (''flak{{l|sv|mått}}''). For a trailer, it would normally be more natural to say "Lägg den på {{l|sv|släpet}}" (Put it on the trailer) or the like as well.
