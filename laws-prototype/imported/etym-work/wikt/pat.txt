===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|pat|t=a blow, stroke}}, alteration (with loss of medial ''l'') of {{m|enm|*plat}} (> {{cog|sco|plat|t=a blow, buffet}}), from {{inh|en|ang|plætt|t=a sounding blow, a smack}}, from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*platt|t=a smack, slap, blow}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*plat-|t=to strike, beat}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*blod-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*bled-|t=to strike, beat}}. Cognate with {{cog|dum|plat|t=a smack, blow, slap}}, {{cog|gml|plat|t=a smack, blow, beating}}, {{cog|gmh|plaz}}, {{m|gmh|blaz|t=a resounding blow, bang, crash}}. For loss of {{m|en||l}}, compare {{m|en|patch}} for {{m|en|platch}}; {{m|en|pate}} for {{m|en|plate}}, etc. See {{m|en|plat}}.

===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|en|enm|*patten}}, alteration (with loss of medial ''l'') of {{m|enm|platten}}, {{m|enm|pletten|t=to pat}}, from {{der|en|ang|plættan|t=to buffet, strike, slap, smack, give a sounding blow}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*plattjan}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*plat-|t=to strike, beat}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*blod-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*bled-|t=to strike, beat}}. Cognate with {{cog|dum|platten}}, {{m|dum|pletten|t=to strike, bruise, crush, rub}}, {{cog|de|platzen|t=to split, burst, break up}}, {{cog|bar|patzen|t=to pat}}, {{cog|sv|plätta}}, {{m|sv|pjätta|t=to pat, tap}}. For loss of {{m|en||l}}, compare {{m|en|patch}} for {{m|en|platch}}; {{m|en|pate}} for {{m|en|plate}}, etc. See {{m|en|plat}}.
