===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*peh₂-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|apperen}}, {{m|enm|aperen}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|aparoir}} ({{cog|fr|apparoir}}), from {{der|en|la|appāreō||to appear}}, from {{m|la|ad||to}} + {{m|la|pāreō||to come forth, to become visible}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*peh₂s-|*peh₂-s-|watch, see}}, s-present of {{m|ine-pro|*peh₂-||protect}}.

====Usage notes====
* In the senses ''be obvious'' and ''seem'', '''appear''' is a [[catenative]] verb that takes the ''to infinitive''. See [[Appendix:English catenative verbs]]
* Particularly in the senses ''be obvious'', ''seem'', and ''bring into view'', '''appear''' is a [[stative verb]] that rarely takes the continuous inflection. See [[:Category:English stative verbs]]
