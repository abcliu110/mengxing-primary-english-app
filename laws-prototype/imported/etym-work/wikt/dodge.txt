==English==

===Etymology===
Likely from dialectal {{m|en|dodge}}, {{m|en|dod}}, {{m|en|dodd|t=to jog, trudge along, totter", also "to jerk, jig}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin. Perhaps from unrecorded {{inh|en|enm|*dodden}}, ultimately from {{der|en|gem-pro|*dud-|t=to move}}, related to {{cog|ang|dydrian}}, {{m|ang|dyderian|t=to delude, deceive}}, {{cog|enm|dideren|t=to tremble, quake, shiver}}, {{cog|en|dodder}}, {{cog|no|dudra|t=to tremble}}.
