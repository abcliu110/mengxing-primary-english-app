====Usage notes====
The word 'salad' is often preceded by the principal ingredient (e.g. 'potato salad', 'fruit salad') or a named type of salad (e.g. 'Waldorf salad'). When used alone, 'salad' usually implies the principal ingredient is lettuce or other greens.
