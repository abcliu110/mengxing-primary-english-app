===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:glew|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|glew}}, {{m|enm|glue}}, from {{der|en|fro|glu||glue, [[birdlime]]}}, from {{der|en|la-lat|glūs}} (stem {{m|la||glūt-}}), from {{der|en|la|glūten}}. Related to {{m|en|clay}}.

Partially displaced native {{ncog|ang|līm||glue}} and {{m|ang|ġelīman||to glue}} (whence modern {{m|en|lime}}).
