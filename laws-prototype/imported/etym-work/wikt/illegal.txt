====Usage notes====
According to {{w|Black's Law Dictionary|''Black's Law Dictionary''}} (2nd edition), "illegal" may mean only that something lacks authority of the law or support from law (that is, that it's ''not legal''), not that it's against the law, but that in ordinary usage it means a violation of the law. The "law" however may be a common-law principle rather than a statute.

The use of "illegal" to describe a person rather than an action is often regarded as offensive; see below.<ref name="illegal person">{{cite-web |url=https://www.commondreams.org/headline/2013/04/03-5 |title=Archived copy |accessdate=22 July 2013 |archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20130605004030/http://www.commondreams.org/headline/2013/04/03-5 |archivedate=5 June 2013 }}</ref>
