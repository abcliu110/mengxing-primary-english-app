===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|horien||to rush, impel}}, probably a variation of {{m|enm|hurren||to vibrate rapidly, buzz}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*hurzaną||to rush}} (compare {{cog|gmh|hurren||to hasten}}, {{cog|no|hurre||to whirl around}}), from {{der|en|ine-pro|*ḱers-||to run}} (compare {{cog|la|currō||to run}}, {{cog|xto|kursär}}/{{cog|txb|kwarsär||league; course}}). Related to {{m|en|hurr}}, {{m|en|horse}}, {{m|en|rush}}.

Alternative etymology derives ''hurry'' as a variant of {{m|en|harry|}}.

{{root|en|ine-pro|*ḱers-}}
