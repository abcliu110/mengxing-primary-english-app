The expected Modern English form would be {{IPAchar|/staɪf/}}; {{IPAchar|/stɪf/}} is probably originally from compounds such as {{m|en|stiffly}}, where the vowel was shortened before a consonant cluster.<ref>{{R:Jespersen MEGoHP|I|4.35|124}}</ref>
