===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=tension|:uder|grc:τονῐκός|tree=1}}
From {{uder|en|grc|τονικός}}, from {{m|grc|τόνος}}. 17th century writers believed health to be derived from firmly stretched muscles, thus ''tonic''; the extension of ''tonic medicine'' appeared in the late 18th century. {{surf|en|tone|-ic|alt1=ton(e)}}.
