===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=omnibus|:clip|omnibus|tree=1}}
{{clipping|en|omnibus}}. Superseded earlier {{m|en|'bus}}, where the apostrophe indicated a clipping. The shift in spelling likely reflects the fact that modern speakers no longer perceive this term as a clipping. The electrical sense is derived from figurative application of the automotive sense.
