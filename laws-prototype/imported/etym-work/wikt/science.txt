=====Usage notes=====
Since the middle of the 20th century, the term ''science'' is normally used to indicate the [[natural science]]s (e.g., chemistry), the [[social science]]s (e.g., sociology), and the [[formal science]]s (e.g., mathematics).  In the 18th and 19th centuries, the term was broader and encompassed scholarly study of [[theology]], the [[humanities]] (e.g., grammar) and the [[arts]] (e.g., music).
