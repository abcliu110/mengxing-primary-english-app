===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-}}
{{bor+|en|la|speculātus}}, {{glossary|perfect}} {{glossary|active}} {{glossary|participle}} of {{m|la|speculor||to watch, observe, examine, spy}} (see {{af|en|-ate|id1=verb|pos1=verb-forming suffix}}), from {{m|la|specula||a watchtower}}, ultimately from {{m|la|speciō||to look at}}.

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|obsolete|intransitive}} To [[think]], [[meditate]] or [[reflect]] on a subject; to consider, to [[deliberate]] or [[cogitate]].
#* {{RQ:Hawthorne Scarlet Letter|13|passage=It is remarkable that persons who '''speculate''' the most boldly often conform with the most perfect quietude to the external regulations of society.}}
# {{senseid|en|inconclusive conjecture}}{{lb|en|intransitive}} To make an [[inference]] based on [[inconclusive]] [[evidence]]; to [[surmise]] or [[conjecture]].
#* {{quote-book|en|title=The Book of Sharks| author={{w|Richard Ellis (biologist)|Richard Ellis}}| year=1983| publisher=Knopf| ISBN=0679722106| passage=We can '''speculate''' that in many instances the sharks are not feeding on their victims, but only in a few cases can we guess what they ''are'' doing.| page=171}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=2013-06-07|author=David Simpson| volume=188| issue=26| page=36| magazine=w:The Guardian Weekly| title=[http://www.guardian.co.uk/books/2013/may/18/falling-upwards-richard-holmes-review Fantasy of navigation]| passage=It is tempting to '''speculate''' about the incentives or compulsions that might explain why anyone would take to the skies in [the] basket [of a balloon]: perhaps out of a desire to escape the gravity of this world or to get a preview of the next;&nbsp;[&hellip;].}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|business|finance}} To make a [[risky]] [[trade]] in the hope of making a [[profit]]; to [[venture]] or [[gamble]].
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=1951|month=May|author=R. K. Kirkland|title=The Cavan & Leitrim Railway|journal=Railway Magazine|page=345|text=Some preliminary legal arrangements were made, but the scheme proceeded no further, as the various local authorities concerned were unwilling to '''speculate''' public funds on what was, even at that date, a will o' the wisp.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|programming}} To [[anticipate]] which [[branch]] of [[code]] will be chosen and [[execute]] it [[in advance]].
