==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*pleḱ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|replyen}}, {{m|enm|replien}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|replier||to reply}}, from the {{der|en|la|replico|replicō, replicāre|t=to fold back}} (in Late or Medieval Latin "to reply, repeat"), from {{m|la|re}} + {{m|la|plicō||to fold}}; the noun derives from the verb by [[nominalisation]]. Doublet of {{m|en|replicate}} and {{m|en|replica}}.
