===Noun===
{{en-noun|~}}

# {{lb|en|uncountable}} The typical state of something, or something that is typical.
# {{lb|en|countable|colloquial}} A specific good or service (e.g. a drink) that someone typically orders.
#: {{ux|en|I'll just have '''the usual'''.}}
#: {{ux|en|Two '''usuals''', please.}}

====Usage notes====
Sometimes colloquially shortened to the first syllable ({{IPA|en|/juːʒ/|nocount=1}}), an overwhelmingly spoken-only slang word with no single widely accepted spelling (see {{m|en|uzhe}}).
