===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|bile}}, {{m|enm|bule|büle|t=boil, tumor}}, from {{inh|en|ang|bȳl}}, {{m|ang|bȳle|t=boil, swelling}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*būlijō}}, {{m|gem-pro|*būlō|t=boil}}. 

Akin to {{cog|nl|buil|t=boil, swelling}}, {{cog|de|Beule|t=boil, hump}}, {{cog|is|beyla|t=swelling, hump}}. The expected form is {{m|en||bile}}; the rounding of the diphthong could be caused by the initial ''b-'' and/or by association with etymology 2.
