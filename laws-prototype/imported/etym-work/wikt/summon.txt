===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*men- (think)}}
From {{uder|en|xno|somoundre}}, from {{uder|en|fro|sumundre}}, from {{uder|en|la|summoneo|summonēre}}, itself from {{m|la|sub}} + {{m|la|moneo|monēre}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*monéyeti}}, causative from {{m|ine-pro|*men-||to think}}.

Compare typologically from the same PIE root {{m|en|mention}}, {{cog|ru|помина́ть}}, {{m|ru|упомина́ть}} (developed less further, keeping strong relation with {{m|ru|по́мнить}}), also compare {{m|ru|помина́й как зва́ли}} (<+ {{m|ru|звать}}).

===Etymology 2===
Borrowed from {{bor|en|ms|saman|t=fine, financial penalty}}, which is itself borrowed from {{der|en|en|summons|t=notice summoning someone to appear in court}}. This sense re-emerged in English as ''summon'' likely due to the erroneous belief that the original {{glossary|singular}} English word ''summons'' is {{glossary|plural}}. {{doublet|en|summons|saman}}.
