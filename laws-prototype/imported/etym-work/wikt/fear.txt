===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:fere<id:fear>|id=fright|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|feer}}, {{m|enm|fere|id=fear}}, {{m|enm|fer||fear}}, from {{inh|en|ang|fǣr|id=fear}}, {{m|ang|ġefǣr||calamity, sudden danger, peril, sudden attack, terrible sight|id=danger}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*fērō}}, {{m|gem-pro|*fērą||danger}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*per-||to go through, carry forth, try}}. Cognate with {{cog|nl|gevaar||danger, risk, peril}}, {{cog|de|Gefahr||danger, hazard, risk}}, {{cog|da|fare||danger, hazard, risk}}, {{cog|fo,is|fár||accident, anger, calamity}}, {{cog|nb,nn|fare||danger}}, {{cog|sv|fara||danger, risk, peril}}, {{cog|la|perīculum||danger, risk, trial}}, {{cog|grc|πεῖρα||trial, experiment}}, {{cog|hy|փորձ||attempt}}. {{doublet|en|peril}}.

The verb is from {{inh|en|enm|feren}}, from {{inh|en|ang|fǣran||to frighten, raven}}, from the noun. Cognate with the archaic {{cog|nl|-}} verb {{m|nl|varen||to fear, to cause fear}}.
