===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*ḱerd-}}
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:corage|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|corage}}, from {{der|en|fro|corage}} ({{cog|fr|courage}}), from {{der|en|la-vul|*corāticum}}, from {{der|en|la|cor|t=heart}}. Distantly related to {{m|en|cardiac|t=of the heart}}, which is from Greek, but from the same Proto-Indo-European root. Displaced {{inh|en|enm|elne}}, {{m|enm|ellen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|ellen||courage, valor}}.

===Noun===
{{en-noun|-|s}}

# The quality of being [[confident]], not [[afraid]] or easily [[intimidate]]d, but without being [[incautious]] or [[inconsiderate]].
#* {{RQ:Emerson Conduct of Life|essay=IV|page=120|passage=A great part of '''courage''' is the courage of having done the thing before.}}
#* {{RQ:Austen Pride and Prejudice|passage=There is a stubbornness about me that never can bear to be frightened at the will of others. My '''courage''' always rises at every attempt to intimidate me.}}
#: {{ux|en|It takes a lot of '''courage''' to be successful in business.}}
# The ability to [[overcome]] one's fear, [[do]] or [[live]] things which one finds [[frightening]].
#: {{ux|en|He plucked up the '''courage''' to tell her how he felt.}}
#* {{RQ:Twain Pudd'nhead Wilson|chapter=XII|page=155|passage='''Courage''' is resistance to fear, mastery of fear—not absence of fear.}}
# The ability to [[maintain]] one's [[will]] or [[intent]] despite either the experience of [[fear]], [[frailty]], or [[frustration]]; or the occurrence of [[adversity]], [[difficulty]], [[defeat]] or [[reversal]]; moral fortitude.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1942|author=w:C.S. Lewis|title=w:The Screwtape Letters
|passage=“'''Courage''' is not simply one of the virtues but the form of every virtue ''at the testing point'', which means at the point of highest reality.”}}
#* {{RQ:Harper Lee Mockingbird|passage=I wanted you to see what real '''courage''' is, instead of getting the idea that courage is a man with a gun in his hand. It's when you know you're licked before you begin, but you begin anyway and see it through no matter what.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1993|author=Stanley P. Cornils|title=The Mourning After: How to Manage Grief Wisely|passage='''Courage''' isn't having the strength to go on - it is going on when you don't have strength.}}
#* '''2008''', {{w|Maya Angelou}}, address for the 2008 Cornell University commencement
#*: '''Courage''' is the most important of all the virtues because without courage, you can't practice any other virtue ''consistently''.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2008|author=Eric Roth|title=The Curious Case of Benjamin Button Screenplay|passage=I hope you live a life you’re proud of. If you find that you’re not, I hope you have the '''courage''' to start all over again.}}
