===Etymology 2===
From {{der|en|enm|pressen||to crowd, thring, press}}, from {{der|en|fro|presser||to press}} (Modern {{cog|fr|presser}}) from {{der|en|la|pressāre}}, from ''[[pressus]]'', past participle of ''[[premere]]'' "to press". Displaced native {{cog|enm|thringen||to press, crowd, throng}} (from {{cog|ang|þringan||to press, crowd}}), {{cog|enm|thrasten||to press, force, urge}} (from {{cog|ang|þrǣstan||to press, force}}), {{cog|ang|þryscan||to press}}, {{cog|ang|þēowan||to press, impress}}.
