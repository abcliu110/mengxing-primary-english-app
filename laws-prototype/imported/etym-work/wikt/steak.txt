===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:steike<ety:bor<non:steik>>}}
From {{inh|en|enm|steike}}, from {{der|en|non|steik||roast; meat roasted on a stick}}. The verb is either from the noun or from {{m|non|steikja||to roast}}.

The modern pronunciation shows an irregular change of Early Modern English {{IPAchar|/ɛː/}} to {{IPAchar|/eɪ/}} in the standard language (present also in {{m|en|break}}); contrast this with the development of other words such as {{m|en|speak}} and {{m|en|wreak}}.
