===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*men- (stand out)}}
{{ety|en|id=opening|:inh|enm:mouth|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|mouth}}, from {{inh|en|ang|mūþ}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*munþ}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*munþaz|t=mouth}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*ment-|t=to chew; jaw, mouth}}.
