===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:bor|fr:photographie|tree=1}}
From {{bor|en|fr|photographie}}. {{surf|en|photo-|-graphy}}, together meaning "drawing with light" or "representation by means of lines", "drawing". From {{m|grc|φῶς||of light}}, and {{m|grc|γράφω||write, draw}}.
