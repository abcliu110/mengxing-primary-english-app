===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₂er-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|resoun}}, {{m|enm|reson}}, from {{der|en|xno|raisun}} ({{der|en|fro|raison}}), from {{der|en|la|ratiō}}, from {{m|la|ratus}}, past participle of {{m|la|reor||reckon}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₂reh₁-||to think}}, reanalysed root of {{m|ine-pro|*h₂er-||to put together}}. Displaced {{l+|ang|racu||logic, reason}} and native {{cog|enm|-rede|reden|pos=found in compounds}}, from {{cog|ang|rǣden|t=condition, stipulation, calculation, direction}}, from the same Proto-Indo-European source (compare {{cog|fy|reden|t=reason}}, {{cog|nl|reden|t=reason}}). {{doublet|en|ration|ratio}}.

# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To [[deduce]] or come to a conclusion by being [[rational]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1892|author=Arthur Conan Doyle|title=The Adventure of the Speckled Band
|passage="I had," said he, "come to an entirely erroneous conclusion which shows, my dear Watson, how dangerous it always is to '''reason''' from insufficient data. {{...}}"}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To perform a process of deduction or of induction, in order to [[convince]] or to [[confute]]; to [[argue]].
#: {{syn|en|[[ratiocinate]] (formal)}}
#* {{RQ:Northup Twelve Years|chapter=III|page=47|passage=Still my spirit was not broken. I indulged the anticipation of escape, and that speedily. It was impossible, I '''reasoned''', that men could be so unjust as to detain me as a slave, when the truth of my case was known.}}
#* {{RQ:Walliams Bad Dad|text=“All right, Dad – I did follow you. But only because I was worried about you.” <br>“Worried about me? I was worried about you! Hanging on to the roof of a speeding car! Are you nuts?” <br>“It wasn’t moving when I climbed on to it,” '''reasoned''' the boy.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|obsolete}} To [[converse]]; to compare [[opinion]]s. <!--Chambers 1908 says this is used in the Bible-->
# {{lb|en|ambitransitive}} To arrange and present the reasons for or against; to [[examine]] or [[discuss]] by arguments; to [[debate]] or discuss.
#: {{ux|en|I '''reasoned''' the matter with my friend.}}
#* {{RQ:Connor Glengarry|chapter=9
|passage=The talk was mainly between Aleck and Murdie, the others crowding eagerly about and putting in a word as they could. Murdie was '''reasoning''' good-humoredly, Aleck replying fiercely.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|rare}} To support with reasons, as a [[request]].
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[persuade]] by reasoning or argument.
#: {{ux|en|to '''reason''' one into a belief; to '''reason''' one out of his plan}}
#* {{RQ:Austen Emma|2|10|text=That she was not immediately ready, Emma did suspect to arise from the state of her nerves; she had not yet possessed the instrument long enough to touch it without emotion; she must '''reason''' herself into the power of performance; and Emma could not but pity such feelings, whatever their origin, and could not but resolve never to expose them to her neighbour again.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|with ''[[down]]''}} To [[overcome]] or [[conquer]] by [[adduce|adducing]] reasons.
#: {{ux|en|to '''reason''' down a passion}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|usually with ''[[out]]''}} To find by [[logical]] [[process]]; to [[explain]] or [[justify]] by reason or argument.
#: {{ux|en|to '''reason''' out the causes of the librations of the moon}}
