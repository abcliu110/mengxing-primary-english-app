===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:bor|gmw-msc:golf}}
The word is first known in English from the 17th century as a {{glossary|borrowing}} from {{bor|en|gmw-msc|golf}}, {{m|gmw-msc|gouff}}. Although the etymology is uncertain, the most likely origin is that it comes from the {{der|en|dum|colve}}, {{m|dum|colf||club}}, ultimately from {{der|en|gem-pro|*kulbaz||club}}, related to {{cog|de|Kolben|t=piston, rod}}, {{cog|sv|kolv|t=piston, rod}}, {{cog|ang|clopp|t=rock; cliff}}, {{cog|en|kelp}}.
