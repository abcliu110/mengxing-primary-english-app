From {{inh|en|enm|clovere}}, {{m|enm|claver}}, from {{inh|en|ang|clāfre}}, earlier {{m|ang|clǣfre}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*klaibrā}}. More distantly also related with {{cog|de|Klee}}.
