===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*keh₂p-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|receyven}}, from {{der|en|fro|recevoir|receivre}}, from {{der|en|la|recipiō|recipere|t=take back, accept, etc.}}, from {{m|la|re-||back}} + {{m|la|capiō||to take}}; see [[capacious]]. Compare [[conceive]], [[deceive]], [[perceive]]. Displaced native {{cog|enm|-}} terms in {{m|enm|fon|-fon}}/{{m|enm|fangen|-fangen}} (e.g. {{m|enm|afon}}, {{m|enm|anfon}}, {{m|enm|afangen}}, {{m|enm|underfangen}}, etc. "to receive" from {{cog|ang|fon|-fōn}}), native {{cog|enm|thiggen||to receive}} (from {{cog|ang|þiċġan}}), and non-native {{cog|enm|aquilen}}, {{m|enm|enquilen||to receive}} (from {{cog|fro|aquillir}}, {{m|fro|encueillir}}).
