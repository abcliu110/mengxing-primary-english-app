===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*deh₂p-}}
The {{glossary|noun}} is derived from {{inh|en|enm|tappe|t=hollow device for controlling the flow of liquid from a hole, cock, faucet, spigot; hole through which the liquid flows; the liquid which thus flows}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=tap(pe|pos=''n.''(1)|noformat=1|id=MED44515}}</ref> from {{inh|en|ang|tæppa}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*tappō}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*tappô|t=a plug, tap; peg; tapering stick}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*deh₂p-|t=to lose; to sacrifice}}.<ref>Compare {{R:OED Online|pos=''n.''{{sup|1}}|noformat=1|id=7871448854|date=September 2023|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=tap{{sup|1}}|pos=n}}</ref> {{doublet|en|tapa<id:appetizer>}}.

The {{glossary|verb}} is derived from {{inh|en|enm|tappen|t=to obtain (liquid, chiefly liquor) from a tap; to obtain and sell (liquor)}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=tappen|pos=''v.''(1)|noformat=1|id=MED44518}}</ref> from {{inh|en|ang|tæppian|t=to provide (a container) with a stopper; to obtain (liquid) from a tap}}, and then either:

* from {{inh|en|ang|tæppa}}<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=''v.''{{sup|1}}|noformat=1|id=2045249580|date=September 2023|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=tap{{sup|1}}|pos=v}}</ref> (see above) + {{m|ang|-ian|pos={{glossary|suffix}} forming verbs}}; or
* from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*tappōną}}, from {{m|gem-pro|*tappô|pos=noun}} (see above).

Verb {{senseno|en|games}} (“to turn over (a playing card or playing piece) to remind players that it has already been used in that round”) alludes to the abilities or resources of the card or piece having been drawn on to the point of temporary exhaustion: see verb {{senseno|en|deplete}}.

===Etymology 2===
{{etymid|en|lightly strike}}
The {{glossary|verb}} is derived from {{inh|en|enm|tappen}}, {{m|enm|teppen|t=to give (something) a knock or tap; to hit (something) lightly, pat, tap}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=tappen|pos=v|id=MED44519}}</ref> either:<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=''v.''{{sup|2}}|noformat=1|id=7014007382|date=December 2023|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=tap{{sup|2}}|pos=v}}</ref>

* {{glossary|imitative}} of the making of a tapping sound; or
* from {{der|en|fro|tapper}}, {{m|fro|taper|t=to tap}} (modern {{cog|fr|taper}}), from {{der|en|frk|*tappōn}}, {{m|gmw-pro|*dabbōn||to strike}}, or from {{der|en|gml|tappen}}, {{m|gml|tapen|t=to rap, strike, tap}}, or from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*tabōną||to grope, to grapple, to claw}}, maybe via a {{cog|non|*tapa||to strike}}, as seen in the derivative {{m|non|tapsa||to tap}} {{doublet|en|dab|tave}}.

Verb {{senseno|en|arrest}} (“to arrest (someone)”) and {{senseno|en|designate}} (“to choose or designate (someone) for a duty, etc.”) allude to a police officer or other person tapping someone on their shoulder to catch their attention or to select them.

The {{glossary|noun}} is derived from {{inh|en|enm|tap}}, {{m|enm|tappe|t=light blow or hit}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=tap(pe|pos=''n.''(2)|noformat=1|id=MED44516}}</ref> and then either:<ref>Compare {{R:OED Online|pos=''n.''{{sup|2}}|noformat=1|id=9444893278|date=September 2023|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=tap{{sup|2}}|pos=n}}</ref>

* from {{inh|en|enm|tappen|pos=verb}} (see above); or
* from {{der|en|fro|tape|t=light slap or touch, pat}} (modern {{cog|fr|tape}}), from {{m|fro|tapper}}, {{m|fro|taper|pos=verb}} (see above).
