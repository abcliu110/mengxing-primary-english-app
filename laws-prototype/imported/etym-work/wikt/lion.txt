## {{lb|en|in particular}} A [[male]] lion, as opposed to a [[lioness]], which is characterized by having a flowing shaggy [[mane]] and taking little part in [[hunt]]ing, which can be done [[cooperatively]].
# {{lb|en|by extension}} Any of various [[extant]] and [[extinct]] [[big cat]]s, especially the [[mountain lion]].
