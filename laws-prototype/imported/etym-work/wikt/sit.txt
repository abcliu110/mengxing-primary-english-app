==Afrikaans==

===Etymology===
Formally from {{inh|af|nl|zitten||to sit}}, from {{inh|af|frk|*sittjan}}, from {{inh|af|gem-pro|*sitjaną}}. Semantically from a merger of the former and related {{inh|af|nl|zetten||to set, put}}, from {{der|af|gem-pro|*satjaną}}, whence also {{cog|af|set}} (chiefly in compounds). Both Germanic verbs are eventually from {{der|af|ine-pro|*sed-}}.

===Pronunciation===
* {{IPA|af|/sət/}}
* {{audio|af|LL-Q14196 (afr)-Oesjaar-sit.wav}}

===Verb===
{{af-verb|sittende}}

# {{lb|af|intransitive}} to {{l|en|sit}}; to be in a sitting position {{gloss|usually used with ''[[op]]'', ''[[binne]]'' or ''[[in]]''}}
#: {{ux|af|Sy '''sit''' en sein vir haar dogtertjie.|She '''is sitting''' and gesturing to her young daughter.}}
# {{lb|af|intransitive}} to {{l|en|sit}}; to {{l|af|sit down}} to move into a sitting position
#: {{ux|af|'''Sit''' asseblief.|t=Please '''sit down'''.}}
# {{lb|af|transitive}} to {{l|en|place}}, to {{l|en|put}}
#: {{ux|af|Ek '''sit''' jou sleutels op die tafel.|t=I '''am putting''' your keys on the table.}}
# {{lb|af|transitive}} to {{l|en|deposit}}
#: {{ux|af|Ek gaan al my geld in die bank '''sit'''.|t=I am going to '''deposit''' all my money in the bank.}}

====Usage notes====
* ''Sit'' and its derivatives are usually more commonly used than ''[[plaas]]'' for their overlapping senses, but are sometimes considered less formal than ''plaas'', especially in formal writing.
