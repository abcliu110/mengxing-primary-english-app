==English==

===Etymology===
From {{suf|en|fog|y|id2=adjectival}}, originally in the sense "covered with tall grass; marshy; thick".<ref>{{R:MWO}}</ref> It is not clear whether {{m|en|fog||mist}} is a back-formation from {{m|en|foggy||covered with tall, obscuring grass}}<ref name="DC">{{R:Dictionary.com|fog}}</ref> or has a separate Germanic origin,<ref>{{R:Etymonline}}</ref> and hence whether {{m|en|foggy||covered with tall grass}} and {{m|en|foggy||obscured by mist}} represent one word or two. See {{m|en|fog}} ("mist"; "tall grass") for more.
