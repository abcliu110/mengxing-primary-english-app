====Etymology====
From {{inh|en|enm|separaten||to separate}}, from {{af|enm|separat|-en|t1=separated|id2=infinitival|nocat=1}}, from {{der|en|la|sēparātus}}, {{glossary|perfect}} {{glossary|passive}} {{glossary|participle}} of {{m|la|sēparō|t=to separate}} (see {{af|en|-ate|id1=verb|pos1=verb-forming suffix}}), from {{af|la|''sē-''|t1=apart|parō|t2=prepare|nocat=1}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*per-|t=produce, procure, bring forward, bring forth}}. Displaced {{noncog|enm|scheden}}, from {{noncog|ang|scēadan}} (whence {{noncog|en|shed}}).

Doublet of {{m|en|sever}}, also derived from the same Latin verb.
