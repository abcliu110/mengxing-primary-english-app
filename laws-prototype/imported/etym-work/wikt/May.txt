===Etymology 2===
The surname is converged from several origins:
* As an English surname, from {{der|en|enm|May}}, a pet form of {{m|en|Matthew}} (see {{m|en|Mayhew}}).
* As an English, {{der|en|nl|-}}, {{der|en|de|-}}, {{der|en|pl|-}}, and Jewish surname, from the name of the month.
* Also as an English surname, occasionally a pet form of {{m|en|Mary}} or {{m|en|Margaret}}.
* Also as an English surname, from the obsolete noun {{m|en|may|t=kinsman}}, from {{der|en|ang|maga|id=son|t=son, relative}}.
* Also as an English surname, from obsolete {{der|en|enm|mei|t=physician}}, a borrowing from {{der|en|ang|mege}}, from {{der|en|la|medicus}}. See {{m|en|Mee}}.
* As an {{der|en|ga|-}} surname, Anglicized from {{m|ga|Ó Miadhaigh|t=descendant of [[Miadhach]]}}, a name derived from {{m|ga|miadh|t=honor}}.
* As a {{der|en|fr|-}} surname, shortened from {{m|en|Lemay}}, {{m|en|Dumay}}.
* Also as a French surname, from a derivative of {{cog|la|Marius}}, similar to {{m|en|Mario}}.
* As a Jewish surname, from the town {{m|en|Mayen}} in Germany.
* As a {{der|en|zh|-}} surname, from {{m|zh|麥}} (see {{m|en|Mai}}) and {{m|zh|梅}}) (see {{m|en|Mei}}).
* As an Amerindian (Mexico) surname of {{der|en|myn|-}} origin, from {{m|und|maay|t=cloven hoof}}, originally "young deer."
