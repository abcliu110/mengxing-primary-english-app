Written declension is more complicated due to the difficulty of combining "college" with ''risti''-type endings. Therefore, it might be advisable to avoid inflecting this word in writing by using synonyms, when available. If one has to, one option is to write as if the pronunciation were [[Fennicize|Fennicize / Finnicize]]d to /ˈko̞lːe̞ge̞/, in which case the word would fall into ''nalle''-category with the exception that ''collegeiden'' seems to be more commonly used as genitive plural than ''collegejen'' and ''collegein'' is not used as genitive plural:
{{fi-decl-nalle|colleg|||a}}
