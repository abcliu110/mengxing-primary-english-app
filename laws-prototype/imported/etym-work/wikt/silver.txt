===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:silver|tree=1}}
{{inh+|en|enm|silver,selver,sulver}}, from {{inh|en|ang|seolfor}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*silubr}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*silubrą|t=silver}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin.

{{col-top|2|cognates and etymology discussion}}
Cognate with {{cog|sco|siller||silver}}, {{cog|stq|Säälwer||silver}}, {{cog|fy|sulver||silver}}, {{cog|nl|zilver||silver}}, {{cog|nds-de|Silver}}, {{m|nds-de|Sülver||silver}}, {{cog|de|Silber||silver}}, {{cog|sv|silver||silver}}, {{cog|is|silfur||silver}}. The Germanic word has parallels in Baltic and Slavic ({{cog|cu|сьрєбро}}, {{cog|lt|sidabras}}), Celtic (Celtiberian silaPur-), and outside Indo-European, in {{cog|eu|zilar}} and {{cog|ber-pro|*a-ẓrəf}}, but the ultimate origin of the word is unknown.
