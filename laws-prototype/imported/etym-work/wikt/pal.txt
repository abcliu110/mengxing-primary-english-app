===Pronunciation===
* {{IPA|ppl|/pal/|a=standard}}

===Noun===
{{head|head=-pal|ppl|relational noun}}

# of (''genitive relation, also forms genitive pronouns'')
#: {{ux|ppl|Ne pelu '''ipal''' ne takat|The dog '''of''' the man → The man''''s''' dog.}}
#: {{ux|ppl|Ashan ini kal '''mupal'''|Now this house is '''yours'''}}
# for (''benefactive relation'')
#: {{ux|ppl|Tikpiat se mu'''pal''' wan se nu'''pal'''|We have one '''for''' you and one '''for''' me}}

====Usage notes====
* The relational noun -''pal'' is part of a restricted group of relationals that can be used without a possessive marker when it accompanies an explicit complement, thus acting like a preposition:
*: {{ux|ppl|Ne pelu '''pal''' ne takat|The dog '''of''' the man → The man''''s''' dog.}}
