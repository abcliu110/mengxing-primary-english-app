{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)ker- (turn)}}
Inherited from {{inh|en|enm|arengen}}, {{m|enm|arrangen|t=to draw up a battle line}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|arengier}}, {{m|fro|arangier|t=to put in a line, put in a row}}, derived from {{m|fro|reng}}, {{m|fro|rang}}, {{m|fro|ranc|t=line, row, rank}}, from {{der|en|frk|*hring|t=ring}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*hringaz}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)krengʰ-}}, a form of {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)ker-||to turn, bend}}.
