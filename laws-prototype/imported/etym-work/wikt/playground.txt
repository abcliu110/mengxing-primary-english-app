# {{lb|en|outdoors}} A large open space for children to play in, usually having dedicated play equipment (such as [[swing]]s and [[slide]]s). {{defdate|1780<ref>{{R:Etymonline}}</ref>}}
#: {{ux|en|The kids have to go to the '''playground''' during recess, no matter the weather.}}
#: {{ux|en|The local council have set up a new '''playground''', equipped with [[slide]]s, [[swing]]s, and a [[climbing wall]].}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=2025 October 15|author=Vitali Vitaliev|title=The recipe for Swiss bliss|journal=RAIL|issue=1046|page=68|text=Where else in the world can you pre-order sushi and fondue on some of the trains, travel in family carriages equipped with mini-'''playgrounds''', or use a request stop button at some small stations? Where else can you hear the announcement: "The train at Platform 4 is ten minutes late due to an incident in another country," as I did once at the Swiss station of Spiez?}} 
# {{lb|en|figuratively}} Any physical or metaphysical space in which a person or organization has free rein to do as they please.
#: {{ux|en|The exclusive tropical island was a millionaire's '''playground'''.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=1955|month=July|author=M. D. Greville|title=To the Valdres by Rail|journal=Railway Magazine|pages=457-458|text=[[Kjelsås]] (six miles) marks the end of the suburbs, and the line immediately plunges into [[Nordmarka]]. This vast untamed area of forest and mountain (much of it over 2,000 ft. above sea level), interspersed with innumerable lakes, large and small, is known as [[Oslo]]'s '''playground'''.}}
# {{lb|en|programming}} A [[sandbox]] for [[test]]ing [[call]]s to an [[application programming interface]].
