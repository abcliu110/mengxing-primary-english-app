====Usage notes====
* {{sense|deceased}} {{m|en||Late}} in this sense qualifies named individuals (in phrases like {{m|en||the late Mary Smith}}). In this sense, it generally is confined to usage with the person's full name, or a title, relationship, etc., that would be adequate by itself to identify the person: {{m|en||the late Mary Smith}}; {{m|en||the late queen}}; {{m|en||his late wife}}; {{m|en||the late Mary, Queen of Scots}}; but in most cases '''not''' {{m|en||the late Mary}}.
