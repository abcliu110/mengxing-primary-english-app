==English==

===Etymology===
Probably borrowed from {{bor|en|nl|homp|t=hump, lump}} or {{bor|en|gml|hump|t=heap, hill, stump}}, from {{der|en|osx|*hump||hill, heap, thick piece}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*humpaz||hip, height}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kumb-||curved}}. Compare {{cog|gem-pro|*huppōną||to hop}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kewb-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*ḱewb-}} (unnasalised root), and {{cog|en|hub}} (a softened variant without nasal?).

Cognate with {{cog|fy|hompe||lump, chunk}}, {{cog|is|huppur||flank}}, {{cog|cy|cwm||a hollow}}, {{cog|la|incumbō||to lie down}}, {{cog|sq|sumbull||round button, bud}}, {{cog|grc|κύμβη||bowl}}, {{cog|ae|𐬑𐬎𐬨𐬠𐬀|t=pot}}, {{cog|sa|कुम्ब||thick end of bone|tr=kúmba}}. Replaced, and perhaps influenced by, {{cog|ang|crump||crooked, bent}}. More at {{l|en|cramp}}.
