==English==

===Etymology===
Modification of earlier {{m|en|restiff}}, from {{inh|en|enm|restyf}}, from {{der|en|fro|restif}}, from {{m|fro|rester||to stay, remain}}, from {{der|en|la|restō}}.
* Shares an etymology with [[rest]] ("to remain," ''obsolete'')
*''{{w|Merriam-Webster}}'' states that this word was originally used to describe horses that disobeyed commands. Presumably, then, the word came to mean ''fidgety'' or ''anxious'' more broadly.<ref>{{R:Merriam-Webster Online|restive}}</ref>
