====Usage notes====
The distinction between ''violins'' and {{m|en|fiddles}} is typically contextual and cultural. The same instrument is considered a ''violin'' when playing [[classical music]] in formal settings, a {{m|en|fiddle}} when playing [[folk music|folk]] or [[country music]], and variously described in other settings (such as [[jazz]] and [[rock music|rock]]) depending whichever term seems more appropriate to the speaker.
