===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=new things|:inh|enm:newes<ety:af<newe<id:new>><-s<id:plural>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|newes}}, {{m|enm|newys|t=new things}}, equivalent to {{suf|en|new#Noun<pos:n>|s<pos:plural suffix>}}. Compare {{cog|stq|Näis|t=news}}, [[w:East Frisian Low Saxon|East Frisian]] {{m|nds|näjs|t=news}}, {{cog|fy|nijs|t=news}}, {{cog|nl|nieuws|t=news}}, {{cog|nds-de|Neeis|t=new things; news}}, though, unlike the English word, those {{lg|cognate}}s originated as {{lg|genitive}}s, not as {{lg|plural}}s. Sometimes erroneously claimed to be an [[acronym]] of "North, East, West, South" or "Noteworthy Events, Weather, Sports". {{rfv-etym|en}}
