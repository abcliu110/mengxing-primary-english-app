# The [[present]] [[era]]; [[nowadays]].
#: {{ux|en|This is the sporty jacket for '''today'''{{'}}s modern man on the go.}}
#: {{ux|en|The youth of '''today''' have never known what life is like without a cell phone.}}

====Usage notes====
''Todays'' is a mostly literary plural. It refers to days that we experience, have experienced or will experience as "today". More colloquial are {{m|en|these days}} and {{m|en|nowadays}}.
