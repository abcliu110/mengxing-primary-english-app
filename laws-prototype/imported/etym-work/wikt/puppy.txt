===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*peh₂w-}}
From earlier {{m|en|puppie|t=a woman's pet dog}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin, but probably from {{inh|en|enm|*puppee}}, {{m|enm|*poupee}}, from {{uder|en|fro|poupée}}, {{m|fro|popée|t=a doll; puppet}}. More at {{l|en|puppet}}.

Mostly displaced native {{ncog|enm|whelp|t=puppy}}, from {{m+|ang|hwelp}}, whence Modern {{m+|en|whelp}} and {{m|en|whelpie}}.
