===Etymology 1===
[[File:Scannedsocks.jpg|thumb|A pair of '''socks''' ({{senseno|en|Q43663}})]]

From {{inh|en|enm|socke}}, {{m|enm|sokke}}, {{m|enm|sok}}, from {{inh|en|ang|socc||sock, light shoe, slipper}}, a {{der|en|gmw|-}} borrowing from {{der|en|la|soccus||a light shoe or slipper, buskin}}, from {{der|en|grc|σύκχος||a kind of shoe}}, probably from {{der|en|xpg|-}} or from an {{der|en|ine-ana|-}} language. Beekes compares {{cog|ae|𐬵𐬀𐬑𐬀|t=sole of the foot}}. Cognate with {{cog|sco|sok||sock, stocking}}, {{cog|fy|foetsok|sok|sock}}, {{cog|nl|sok||sock}}, {{cog|de|Socke||sock}}, {{cog|da|sok}}, {{m|da|sokke||sock}}, {{cog|sv|sock}}, {{m|sv|socka||sock}}, {{cog|is|sokkur||sock}}. {{doublet|en|zocco}}; also related to {{l|en|zoccolo}}, {{m|en|socle}}, and {{m|en|zocalo}}.
