===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*gʰed-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|gessen|pos=verb}} and {{inh|en|enm|gesse|pos=noun}}, probably of {{der|en|gmq|-}} origin, from {{der|en|gmq-oda|getse}}, {{m|gmq-oda|gitse}}, {{m|gmq-oda|getsa|t=to guess}}, from {{der|en|non|*getsa}}, {{m|non|*gitsa}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*gitisōną|t=to guess}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*getaną|t=to get}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gʰed-|t=to take, seize}}. 

Cognate with {{cog|da|gisne|t=to guess}}, {{cog|no|gissa}}, {{m|no|gjette|t=to guess}}, {{cog|sv|gissa|t=to guess}}, {{cog|stq|gisje|t=to guess}}, {{cog|nl|gissen|t=to guess}}, {{cog|nds|gissen|t=to guess}}, {{cog|nl|gis|t=a guess}}. Related also to {{cog|is|giska}} {{gloss|"to guess"; from {{cog|gem-pro|*gitiskōną}}}}. Compare also {{cog|ru|гада́ть|t=to conjecture, guess, divine}}, {{cog|sq|gjëzë|t=riddle}} from {{m|sq|gjej|t=find, recover, obtain}}. More at {{l|en|get}}.

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# To reach a partly (or totally) unconfirmed [[conclusion]]; to engage in [[conjecture]]; to [[speculate]].
#: {{ux|en|We can only '''guess''' at what was going through her mind.}}
#: {{ux|en|She '''guessed''' that the delivery driver must have got stuck in traffic.}}
# To solve by a correct [[conjecture]]; to conjecture rightly.
#: {{ux|en|He who '''guesses''' the riddle shall have the ring.}}
#: {{ux|en|You '''guessed''' the right answer!}}
#: {{ux|en|You will never '''guess''' what happened next.}}
# To [[suppose]], to [[imagine]] (introducing a proposition of uncertain plausibility).
#: {{ux|en|That album is quite hard to find, but I '''guess''' you could try ordering it online.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Henry 6-3|IV|i|passage=Not all together; better far, I '''guess''', / That we do make our entrance several ways.}}
#* {{RQ:Pope Horace|passage=But in known images of life I '''guess''' / The labour greater.}}
#* {{RQ:Doyle Valley of Fear|passage="Are you a member of the union?"<br>"Sure."<br>"Then you'll get your job, I '''guess'''. Have you any friends?"}}
# {{lb|en|colloquial}} To think, conclude, or decide (without a connotation of uncertainty). Usually in first person: "I guess".
#: {{ux|en|"I '''guess''' you were right." "What did he say?" "He '''guesses''' you were right."}}
#: {{ux|en|"I '''guess''' I'll go to bed."}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} To hit upon or reproduce by [[memory]].
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Macbeth|II|i|passage=Tell me their words, as near as thou canst '''guess''' them.}}
