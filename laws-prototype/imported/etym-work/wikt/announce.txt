===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:der|fro:anoncier|tree=1}}
From {{der|en|fro|anoncier}}, from {{der|en|la|annuntio|annūntiāre}}, from {{m|la|ad}} + {{m|la|nūntiō|t=report, relate}}, from {{m|la|nūntius|t=messenger, bearer of news}}. See {{m|en|nuncio}}. {{doublet|en|annunciate|}}. See also {{af|en|an-#Etymology_4}}.
