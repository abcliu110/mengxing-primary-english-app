===Verb===
{{en-verb|~s}}

# {{lb|en|transitive|negative polarity}} To [[tolerate]] (something), emotionally, physically, or mentally; to [[stand]] or [[handle]] something.
#: {{ux|en|I really can’t '''stomach''' jobs involving that much paperwork, but some people seem to tolerate them.}}
#: {{ux|en|I can't '''stomach''' her cooking.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete|intransitive}} To be angry.
#* {{RQ:Hooker Laws|passage=Let a man, though never so justly, oppose himself unto them that are disordered in their ways; and what one amongst them commonly doth not '''stomach''' at such contradiction, storm at reproof, and hate such as would reform them?}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete|transitive}} To [[resent]]; to remember with anger; to [[dislike]].
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Antony and Cleopatra|III|iv|line=12|text=O, my good lord, / Believe not all; or, if you must believe, / '''Stomach''' not all.}}
#* {{RQ:L'Estrange Fables of Aesop|passage=The Lion began at first to shew his Teeth, and to '''Stomach''' the Affront.}}
#* {{RQ:Milton Eikonoklastes|passage=The Parliament sit in that body {{...}} to be his counsellors and dictators, though he '''stomach''' it.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete|transitive}} To [[turn]] the stomach of; to [[sicken]] or [[repel]].
