====Usage notes====
* Before the end of the nineteenth century, the plural {{m|en|furnitures}} existed in Standard English in both the U.S. and the U.K.; during the twentieth century, however, it ceased to be used by native speakers.
* A single item of furniture, such as a chair or a table, is often called a {{m|en|piece of furniture}}.
* In many languages "[[piece of furniture]]" is one word, and often its plural form is the equivalent of the English "furniture", for example French [[meuble]] / [[meubles]].
