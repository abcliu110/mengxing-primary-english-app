====Noun====
{{en-noun}}

# {{lb|en|chiefly|in the plural}} A narrow passage, especially a contracted part of a [[stream]], [[lake]], or [[sea]]; a [[strait]] connecting two bodies of water.
#: {{ux|en|the '''narrows''' of New York harbor}}
#* {{RQ:Gladstone Homer|passage=Near the island lay on one side the jaws of a dangerous '''narrow'''.}}

===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|en|enm|narwen|t=to narrow}}; see there for more details, but ultimately derived from the noun.

====Verb====
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[reduce]] in [[width]] or [[extent]]; to [[contract]].
#: {{ux|en|We need to '''narrow''' the search.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To get narrower.
#: {{ux|en|The road '''narrows'''.}}
# {{lb|en|of a person or eyes}} To partially lower one's eyelids in a way usually taken to suggest a defensive, aggressive or penetrating look.
#: {{ux|en|He stepped in front of me, '''narrowing '''his eyes to slits.}}
#: {{ux|en|She wagged her finger in his face, and her eyes '''narrowed'''.}}
# {{lb|en|knitting}} To contract the size of, as a stocking, by taking two stitches into one.
# {{lb|en|transitive|programming}} To convert to a [[data type]] that cannot hold as many distinct values.
#: {{antonyms|en|widen}}
#: {{ux|en|to '''narrow''' an <code>int</code> variable to a <code>short</code> variable}}
