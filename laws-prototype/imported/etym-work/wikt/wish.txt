# {{lb|en|transitive}} To desire; to want.
#: {{ux|en|I'll come tomorrow, if you '''wish''' (it).}}
#: {{ux|en|I '''wish''' it to be known that I '''wish''' them all to leave the company.}}
#: {{ux|en|Anyone who '''wish'''es an aisle seat should see an attendant.}}
#: {{ux|en|Oh, (I '''wish''') [[that]] I'd never [[been born]].}}
#* {{quote-web|en|date=May 13 2018|title=How to Fix the Storylines of Film and Television|work=Return of Kings|passage=Showing the population what we '''wish''' them to be is the best way for them to change.|author=Justin King}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Tempest|act=III|scene=i|passage=I would not '''wish''' / Any companion in the world but you.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1716|author=w:Jonathan Swift|title=[[s:The Works of the Rev. Jonathan Swift/Volume 7/Phyllis, or the Progress of Love|Phyllis, or the Progress of Love]]
|passage=Now John the butler must be sent<br>To learn the road that Phyllis went:<br>The groom was '''wished''' to saddle Crop;<br>For John must neither light nor stop,<br>But find her, wheresoe'er she fled,<br>And bring her back alive or dead.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1899|author=w:Hughes Mearns|title={{w|Antigonish (poem)|Antigonish}}| passage=Yesterday, upon the stair&nbsp;/ I met a man who wasn’t there&nbsp;/ He wasn’t there again today&nbsp;/ I '''wish''', I '''wish''' he’d go away&nbsp;&hellip;}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|now|rare}} To [[hope]] (+ object clause with {{m|en|may}} or in present subjunctive).
#* {{RQ:Richardson Clarissa|letter=84|text=I '''wish''' he mean me well, that he takes so much pains!}}
#* '''1808''', {{w|Jane Austen}}, letter, 1 October:
#*: She hears that Miss Bigg is to be married in a fortnight. I '''wish''' it may be so.
# {{lb|en|intransitive|followed by ''for''}} To [[hope]] (for a particular outcome), even if that outcome is unlikely to occur or cannot occur.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1727|author=w:John Arbuthnot|title=Tables of Ancient Coins, Weights and Measures
|passage=This is as good an argument as an antiquary could '''wish''' for.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1901|author=w:W. W. Jacobs|title=w:The Monkey's Paw
|passage=Mr. White took the paw from his pocket and eyed it dubiously. "I don't know what to '''wish''' for, and that's a fact," he said slowly. "It seems to me I've got all I want."}}
#: {{ux|en|I '''wish''' I could go back in time and teach myself what I know now.}}
#: {{ux|en|I '''wished''' (that) it would rain.}}
# {{lb|en|ditransitive}} To bestow (a thought or gesture) towards (someone or something).
#: {{ux|en|We '''wish''' you a [[merry Christmas]].}}
#: {{ux|en|I wouldn't '''wish''' that on my [[worst enemy]].}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Macbeth|act=V|scene=viii|passage=I would not '''wish''' them to a fairer death.}}
#* {{RQ:KJV|Psalms|40|14|text=Let them be driven backward, and put to shame, that '''wish''' me evil.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|followed by ''to'' and an infinitive}} To [[request]] or [[desire]] to do an activity.
#: {{ux|en|Anyone who '''wish'''es to may leave now.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=2013|month=July-August|author=w:Henry Petroski| title=[http://www.americanscientist.org/issues/pub/2013/4/geothermal-energy Geothermal Energy]| volume=101| issue=4| magazine=w:American Scientist| passage=Energy has seldom been found where we need it when we want it. Ancient nomads, '''wishing''' to ward off the evening chill and enjoy a meal around a campfire, had to collect wood and then spend time and effort coaxing the heat of friction out from between sticks to kindle a flame. With more settled people, animals were harnessed to capstans or caged in treadmills to turn grist into meal.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To recommend; to seek confidence or favour on behalf of.
#* {{RQ:Jonson Alchemist
|passage=I was '''wished''' to your worship by a gentleman.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Taming of the Shrew|1|1|passage=Farewell: yet for the loue I beare my sweet Bianca, if I can by any meanes light on a fit man to teach her that wherein she delights, I will '''wish''' him to her father.}}
