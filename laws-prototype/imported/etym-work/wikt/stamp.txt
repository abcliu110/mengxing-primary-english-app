==English==

===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:stampen|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|stampen||to pound, crush}}, from assumed {{inh|en|ang|*stampian}}, variant of {{cog|ang|stempan||to crush, pound, pound in mortar, stamp}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*stampōn}}, {{m|gmw-pro|*stampijan}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*stampōną}}, {{m|gem-pro|*stampijaną||to trample, beat}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*stemb-||to trample down}}.  

Cognate with {{cog|nl|stampen||to stamp, pitch}}, {{cog|de|stampfen||to stamp}}, {{cog|da|stampe||to stamp}}, {{cog|sv|stampa||to stomp}}, {{cog|oc|estampar}}, {{cog|pl|stąpać||to step, treat}}. See also {{m|en|stomp}}, {{m|en|step}}. Marks indicating that postage had been paid were originally made by stamping the item to be mailed; when affixed pieces of paper were introduced for this purpose, the term “stamp” was transferred to cover this new form.
