===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|bisy}}, {{m|enm|busie}}, from {{inh|en|ang|bisiġ|t=busy, occupied, diligent}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*bisīg|t=diligent; zealous; busy}}. Cognate with {{cog|stq|biesich|t=active, diligent, hard-working, industrious}}, {{cog|nl|bezig|t=busy}}, {{cog|nds|besig|t=busy}}. The spelling with ⟨u⟩ represents the pronunciation of the West Midland and Southern dialects while the Modern English pronunciation with {{IPAchar|/ɪ/}} is from the dialects of the East Midlands.<ref>Upward, Christopher & George Davidson. 2011. [https://books.google.com/books?id=fNfebt79zdwC&pg=PT85 ''The History of English Spelling'']. Wiley-Blackwell.</ref>
