===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*ḱel- (cover)}}
From {{inh|en|enm|helmet}}, {{m|enm|helmett}}, a borrowing from {{der|en|fro|helmet}}, {{m|fro|heaumet}}, a diminutive of {{m|fro|helme}} (Modern French {{m|fr|heaume}}), equivalent to {{suffix|en|helm|et}}. The Old French is itself of {{der|en|gem|-}} origin (whence {{cog|ang|helm}}). Used in English since the 15th century, it has largely displaced {{m|en|helm}} as the general word.
