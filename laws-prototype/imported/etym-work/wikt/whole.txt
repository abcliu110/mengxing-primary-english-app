===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:hol<id:whole>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|whol}}, {{m|enm|hol|id=whole}}, {{m|enm|hole||healthy, unhurt, whole}}, from {{inh|en|ang|hāl||healthy, safe}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*hail}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*hailaz||whole, safe, sound}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*kéh₂ilos||healthy, whole}}. 

The spelling with ''wh-'', attested since ca. 1400, represents an excrescent {{IPAchar|/w/}}, which developed in words with initial {{IPAchar|/(h)ɔː/|/(h)oː/}} in southwestern dialects of {{m+|enm|-}}.<ref name="Dobson">{{R:en:Dobson:1957|II|430|997}}</ref> While this pronunciation did not establish itself in the standard language (except in {{m|en|one}}), the spelling survived in {{m|en||whole}} and {{m|en|whore}}, in the former case likely reinforced by a desire to disambiguate from {{m|en|hole}}.

{{col-top|2|cog}}
Compare {{cog|fy|hiel}}, {{cog|nds|heel}}/{{m|nds|heil}}, {{cog|nl|heel}}, {{cog|de|heil}}, {{cog|da|-}} and {{cog|nb|hel}}, {{cog|nn|heil}}; also {{cog|cy|coel||omen}}, {{cog|br|kel||omen, mention}}, {{cog|prg|kails||healthy}}, {{cog|cu|цѣлъ||healthy, unhurt}}. Related to [[hale]], [[health]], [[hail]], [[hallow]], [[heal]], and [[holy]]. {{fcog|grc|ὅλος}}.
{{col-bottom}}
