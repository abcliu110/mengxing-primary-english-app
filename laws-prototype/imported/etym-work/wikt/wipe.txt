From {{inh|en|enm|wipen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|wīpian||to wipe, rub, cleanse}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*wīpōn||to wipe}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*weyp-||to twist, wind around}}. Cognate with {{cog|de|wippen||to bob}}, {{cog|sv|veva||to turn, wind, crank}}, {{cog|got|𐍅𐌴𐌹𐍀𐌰𐌽||to wreathe, crown}}, {{cog|ang|swīfan||to revolve, sweep, wend, intervene}}, {{cog|sa|वेपते|tr=vépate||to tremble}}. More at {{l|en|swivel}}, {{l|en|swift}}.
