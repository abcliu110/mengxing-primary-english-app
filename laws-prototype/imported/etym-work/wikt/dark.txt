# A complete or (more often) partial absence of [[light]].
#: {{ux|en|'''Dark''' surrounds us completely.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare King Lear|II|i|passage=Here stood he in the '''dark''', his sharp sword out.}}
#* {{RQ:Allingham China Governess|chapter=17|passage=The face which emerged was not reassuring. It was blunt and grey, the nose springing thick and flat from high on the frontal bone of the forehead, whilst his eyes were narrow slits of '''dark''' in a tight bandage of tissue.{{nb...}}.}}

Nor is solar power yet as cheap as the grid. For all that, the rapid arrival of electric light to Indian villages is long overdue. When the national grid suffers its next huge outage, as it did in July 2012 when hundreds of millions were left in the '''dark''', look for specks of light in the villages.}}
