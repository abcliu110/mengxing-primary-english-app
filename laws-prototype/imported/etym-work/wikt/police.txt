====Usage notes====
* In North America and the UK, local ''police'' are generally distinguished from regional, national, and specialized law enforcement officers such as {{m|en|sheriff|sheriffs}}, {{m|en|marshal|marshals}}, {{m|en|bailiff|bailiffs}}, {{m|en|FBI}} {{m|en|special agent|special agents}}, and {{m|en|NCA}} {{m|en|investigator|investigators}}. In Australia and New Zealand and in translation of the law enforcement agencies of other countries, ''police'' may refer indiscriminately to law enforcement agencies and officers at any level.
