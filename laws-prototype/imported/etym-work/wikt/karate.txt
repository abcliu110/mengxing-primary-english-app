==Norwegian Bokmål==

[[File:Release0204-2004-05-karate.jpg|thumb|Two karate practitioners competing in kumite.]]

===Etymology===
{{bor+|nb|en|karate}}, from {{der|nb|ja|空手|tr=karate|t=empty hand}}. The modern Japanese spelling is analyzable as {{m|ja|空|tr=kara|t=empty}} + {{m|ja|手|tr=te|t=hand}}. It replaced the earlier {{m|ja|唐手|tr=karate|t=Chinese hand}}, an orthographic borrowing from {{der|nb|ryu|唐手|tr=tōdī|t=Chinese hand}}. During the early twentieth century, the first character was changed from {{m|ja|唐|tr=kara|t=Tang dynasty; China}} to the homophonous {{m|ja|空|tr=kara|t=empty}}, while the pronunciation remained unchanged.

Compare {{cog|nn|karate}}, {{cog|da|karate}}, {{cog|sv|karate}}, {{cog|is|karate}}, {{cog|de|Karate}}, {{cog|fr|karaté}} and {{cog|fi|karate|t=karate}}.
