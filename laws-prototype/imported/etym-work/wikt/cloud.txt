===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|cloud}}, from {{inh|en|ang|clūd|t=mass of stone, rock, boulder, hill}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*klūt}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*klūtaz}}, {{m|gem-pro|*klutaz|t=lump, mass, conglomeration}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gel-|t=to ball up, clench}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|clood}}, {{m|sco|clud||cloud}}, {{cog|nl|kluit||lump, mass, clod}}, {{cog|nds-de|Kluute|Kluut, Kluute|lump, mass, ball}}, {{cog|de|Kloß||lump, ball, dumpling}}, {{cog|da|klode||sphere, orb, planet}}, {{cog|sv|klot||sphere, orb, ball, globe}}, {{cog|is|klót||knob on a sword's hilt}}. Related to {{cog|en|clod}}, {{m|en|clot}}, {{m|en|clump}}, {{m|en|club}}. Largely replaced {{ncog|enm|welkne}}, from {{ncog|ang|wolcn}} (whence {{ncog|en|welkin}}), from {{ncog|gem-pro|*wulkną}}, the original Germanic word for cloud (compare {{cog|nl|wolk}}, {{cog|de|Wolke}}, {{cog|frr,fy|wolk}}).
