# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To move cautiously or furtively, in the manner of a mouse (the rodent) (''frequently used in the [[phrasal verb]]'' '''to [[mouse around]]''').
# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To hunt or catch mice (the rodents), usually of cats. {{century|12}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|nautical}} To close the mouth of a [[hook]] by a careful binding of [[marline]] or wire.
#: {{ux|en|Captain Higgins '''moused''' the hook with a bit of marline to prevent the block beckets from falling out under slack.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|computing}} To [[navigate]] by means of a computer mouse.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1988|title=MacUser|volume=4|passage=I had just '''moused''' to the File menu and the pull-down menu repeated the menu bar's hue a dozen shades lighter.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2009|author=Daniel Tunkelang|title=Faceted Search|page=35|passage=Unlike the Flamenco work, the Relation Browser allows users to quickly explore a document space using dynamic queries issued by '''mousing''' over facet elements in the interface.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete|nonce|transitive}} To [[tear]], as a cat devours a mouse.
