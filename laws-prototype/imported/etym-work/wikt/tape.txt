==English==

===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:tape|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|tape,tappe}}, from {{inh|en|ang|tæppa,tæppe||ribbon, tape}}; further origin unclear.

Probably akin to {{cog|ofs|tapia||to pull, rip, tear}}, {{cog|gml|tappen}}, {{m|gml|tāpen||to grab, pull, rip, tear, snatch}}, {{cog|gmh|zāfen}}, {{m|gmh|zāven||to pull, tear}}.
