=====Usage notes=====
* ''Better'' in this sense has often been considered an adverb. But in "''We better be going''" it is virtually synonymous with ''should'', and in "''We better go''" it is synonymous with ''ought to''. ''Should'' and ''ought'' are auxiliary verbs.
