===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|prise}}, from {{der|en|fro|prise||a taking, capture, a seizure, a thing seized, a prize, booty, also hold, purchase}},  past participle of {{m|fro|prendre||to take, to capture}}, from {{der|en|la|prēndō|alt=prēndere||to take, seize}}; see {{m|en|prehend}}. Compare {{m|en|prison}}, {{m|en|apprise}}, {{m|en|comprise}}, {{m|en|enterprise}}, {{m|en|purprise}}, {{m|en|reprisal}}, {{m|en|surprise}}, etc. {{doublet|en|prise}}.

====Noun====
{{en-noun}}

# That which is taken from another; something [[captured]]; a thing seized by force, stratagem, or superior power.
#* {{RQ:Spenser Faerie Queene|4|4|page=54|text={{...}} wherefore he now begunne<br>To challenge her anew, as his owne '''prize''',<br>Whom formerly he had in battell wonne,}}
# {{lb|en|military|nautical}} Anything captured by a [[belligerent]] using the rights of war; especially, property captured at sea in virtue of the rights of war, as a [[vessel]].
#* {{RQ:Johnson History of the Pyrates|chapter=Of Captain [[w:Henry Every|Avery]], and His Crew|page=51|passage=Having taken all the Treasure on Board their own Ships, and plundered their '''Prize''' of every Thing elſe they either wanted or liked, they let her go; ſhe not being able to continue her Voyage, returned back: {{...}}}}
# An [[honour]] or [[reward]] striven for in a competitive contest; anything offered to be competed for, or as an [[inducement]] to, or reward of, effort.
#: {{ux|en|A literary '''prize''' has been instituted for this [[SY]].}}
#* {{RQ:Dryden Aureng-zebe|5|text=I fought and conquer’d, yet have lost the '''prize'''.}}
# That which may be won by chance, as in a [[lottery]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1928|author=w:Weston Jarvis|title=Jottings from an Active Life|location=London|publisher=Heath Cranton|page=256|url=https://books.google.ca/books/about/Jottings_from_an_Active_Life.html?id=7J7CtAEACAAJ&redir_esc=y
|passage={{w|Cecil Rhodes}} {{...}} was never tired of impressing upon one that the fact of being an Englishman was “the greatest '''prize''' in the lottery of life,” and that it was that thought which always sustained him when he was troubled.}}
# Anything worth striving for; a valuable possession held or in [[prospect]].
#* {{RQ:KJV|Philippians|3|14|text=I press toward the mark for the '''prize''' of the high calling of God in Christ Jesus.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} A [[contest]] for a reward; [[competition]].
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Merchant of Venice|III|2|text=Like one of two contending in a '''prize''',<br>That thinks he hath done well in people's eyes {{...}}}}
# A [[lever]]; a [[pry]]; also, the hold of a lever.
#: {{synonyms|en|prise}}
