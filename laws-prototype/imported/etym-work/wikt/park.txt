===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:park|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|park}}, from {{der|en|fro|parc|t=livestock pen}}, from {{der|en|la-med|parcus}}, {{m|la|parricus}}, from {{der|en|frk|*parruk|*parrik|t=enclosure, pen, fence}}. Cognate with {{cog|nl|perk|t=enclosure; flowerbed}}, {{cog|goh|pfarrih}}, {{m|goh|pferrih|t=enclosure, pen}}, {{cog|ang|pearroc|t=enclosure}} (whence modern {{cog|en|paddock}}), {{cog|non|parrak}}, {{m|non|parak|t=enclosure, pen; distress, anxiety}}, {{cog|is|parraka|t=to keep pent in under restraint and coercion}}. More at {{l|en|parrock}}, {{l|en|paddock}}.
