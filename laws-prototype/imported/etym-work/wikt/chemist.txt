===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:bor|fr:chimiste|tree=1}}
First attested 1562, borrowed from {{bor|en|fr|chimiste}}, from {{der|en|la-med|chimista}}, from earlier {{m|la|alchimista|lit=[[alchemist]]}}, from {{der|en|ar|كيمياء|الْكِيمِيَاء}}, with article {{m|ar|اَلْ}} + {{der|en|grc|χυμεία||art of alloying metals}}, from {{m|grc|χύμα||fluid}}, from {{m|grc|χυμός||juice}}, from {{m|grc|χέω||to pour}}. The pronunciation with initial {{nowrap|/{{IPAlink|k}}/}} is based on the Graeco-Latin etymology rather than on the French word, where the pronunciation with {{nowrap|/{{IPAlink|ʃ}}/}} is based on native French orthography. {{rfv-etym|en}}

{{etystub|en|Explanation for spelling “chymist”: from French or Greek?}}

As a synonym for {{m|en|pharmacy}}, a {{glossary|metonymous}} use of the proprietor to stand for their shop.
