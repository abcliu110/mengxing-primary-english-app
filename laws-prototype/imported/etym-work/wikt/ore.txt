===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|or}}, {{m|enm|oor}}, blend of {{inh|en|ang|ōra|t=ore, unwrought metal}}, of uncertain origin, and {{m|ang|ār|t=brass, copper, bronze}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*aiʀ}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*aiz}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*h₂éyos}}.

Compare {{cog|non|eir|t=brass, copper}}, {{cog|de|ehern|t=of metal, of iron}}, {{cog|got|𐌰𐌹𐌶|t=ore}}; also {{cog|nl|oer|t=ferrous hardpan; bog iron ore}}. Compare {{cog|la|aes|t=bronze, copper}}, {{cog|ae|𐬀𐬌𐬌𐬀𐬵}}, {{cog|sa|अयस्|t=copper, iron|tr=áyas}}.
