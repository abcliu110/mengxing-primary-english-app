===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*lewbʰ- (cut off)}}
From {{inh|en|enm|librarie}}, from {{der|en|xno|librarie}}, from {{der|en|fro|librairie}}, from {{der|en|la|librarium||bookcase, chest for books}}, from {{m|la|librarius||concerning books}}, from {{m|la|liber||the inner bark of trees; paper, parchment, book}}, probably derived from a {{der|en|ine-pro|-}} base {{m|ine-pro|*lewbʰ-|*leub(ʰ)-|to strip, to peel}}. Displaced native {{ncog|enm|bochous|bochous, bokhus|lit=book house}}, from {{ncog|ang|bōchūs}} (compare {{m|en|bookhouse}}).

{{cog|roa|-}} cognates often mean “bookshop” instead: {{cog|fr|librairie}}, {{cog|it|libreria}}, {{cog|es|librería}}, {{cog|ro|librărie}} and {{cog|pt|livraria}}. This is a relatively recent innovation (16th century in French), which ended up displacing the earlier sense.
