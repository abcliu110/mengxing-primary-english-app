# {{lb|en|[[aviation]]}} The highest altitude at which an aircraft can safely maintain flight.
# {{lb|en|[[meteorology]]}} The measurement of visible distance from ground or sea level to an [[overcast]] cloud cover; under a clear sky, the ceiling measurement is identified as "unlimited."
#: {{ux|en|Even though it was cloudy, there was still enough '''ceiling''' for the [[w:Blue Angels|Blue Angels]] to perform a great show.}}
# {{lb|en|mathematics}} The smallest [[integer]] greater than or equal to a given number.
#: {{ux|en|The '''ceiling''' of 4.5 is 5; the '''ceiling''' of −4.5 is −4.}}
# {{lb|en|nautical}} The inner planking of a vessel.
# {{lb|en|finance}} The maximum permitted level in a financial transaction.
# {{lb|en|architecture}} The overhead interior surface that covers the upper limits of a room.
