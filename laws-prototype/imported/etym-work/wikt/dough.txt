===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:dogh|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|dow}}, {{m|enm|dogh}}, {{m|enm|dagh}}, from {{inh|en|ang|dāg}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*daigaz||dough}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*dʰeyǵʰ-||to knead, form, mold}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|daich}}, {{m|sco|dauch}}, {{m|sco|doach||dough}}, {{cog|frr|dii}}, {{m|frr|Jai||dough}}, {{cog|fy|daai||dough}}, {{cog|nl|deeg||dough}}, {{cog|de|Teig||dough}}, {{cog|nds,lb|Deeg||dough}}, {{cog|wym|taag||dough}}, {{cog|yi|טייג||dough}}, {{cog|da|dej||dough}}, {{cog|fo|deiggj||dough}}, {{cog|is,nb,nn|deig||dough}}, {{cog|sv|deg||dough}}, {{cog|got|𐌳𐌰𐌹𐌲𐍃||dough}}.

The derivation of the second meaning (of money) is obscure, but dates to the mid 19th century.
