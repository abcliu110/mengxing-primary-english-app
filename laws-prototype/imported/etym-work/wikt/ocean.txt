===Etymology===
{{dercat|en|qsb-grc|ine-pro}}
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:occean<alt:*ocean, occean, occian, occyan>|tree=+}}
From {{inh|en|enm|*ocean}}, {{m|enm|occean}}, {{m|enm|occian}}, {{m|enm|occyan}}, from {{der|en|fro|occean}} (later reborrowed or reinforced by {{der|en|frm|ocean}}), from {{der|en|la|Ōceanus}}, originally from {{der|en|grc|Ὠκεανός||Oceanus|pos=a water deity}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|gārseċġ}}. Also commonly referred to as ''the ocean sea'', ''the sea of ocean'' (compare Latin ''mare ōceanum''; Old French ''mer oceane'', ''occeanne mer''). Compare {{cog|stq|Oceoan||ocean}}, {{cog|fy|oseaan||ocean}}, {{cog|nl|oceaan||ocean}}, {{cog|nds-de|Ozeaan||ocean}}, {{cog|de|Ozean||ocean}}, {{cog|da|ocean||ocean}}, {{cog|sv|ocean||ocean}}, {{cog|fr|océan||ocean}}, {{cog|it|oceano||ocean}}. {{dbt|en|Oceanus}}/{{m|en|Okeanos}}.
