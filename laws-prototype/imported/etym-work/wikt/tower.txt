===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|tour}}, {{m|enm|tur}}, {{m|enm|tor}}, from {{inh|en|ang|tūr}}, {{m|ang|tor}}, {{m|ang|torr}} {{gloss|"tower; rock"; > English {{l|en|tor}}}} and {{der|en|fro|tour}}, {{m|fro|toer}}, {{m|fro|tor}}; both from {{der|en|la|turris||a tower}}, {{der|en|grc|τύρρις}} {{q|Hesychius}}, {{m|grc|τύρσις}}.
