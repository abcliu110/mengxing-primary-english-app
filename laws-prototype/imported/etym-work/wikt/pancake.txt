===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:panne cake|:afeq|pan<id:panne>|cake|tree=1}}
{{inh+|en|enm|pancake}}, {{m|enm|panne cake}}, {{m|enm|pankake}}, {{m|enm|ponkake}}. {{surf|+com|en|pan|cake}}.

Perhaps adapted from {{der|en|gml|pankôke}}, {{m|gml|pannekôke}}, from {{der|en|osx|*pannakōko}} (suggested by derivatives {{cog|osx|pannakōkilo}} and {{m|osx|pannakōkilīn}}), where the compound is much older; compare {{cog|goh|phankuohho}} (8th century), whence {{cog|gmh|phankuoche}}, {{cog|de|Pfannkuchen||pancake}}; further
{{cog|stq|Ponkouke}}, {{m|stq|Ponkuuke||pancake}}, {{cog|fy|pankoek||pancake}}, {{cog|nl|pannenkoek||pancake}}, {{cog|nds-de|Pannkook||pancake}}.
The juggling sense is by analogy with a pancake being tossed in a pan.
