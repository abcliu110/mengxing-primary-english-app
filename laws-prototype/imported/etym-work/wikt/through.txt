===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|en|enm|thrugh}}, {{m|enm|þrouȝ}}, {{m|enm|throgh}}, from {{inh|en|ang|þrūh|t=trough, conduit, pipe; box, chest; coffin, tomb}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*þrūhs|t=hollowed-out wood}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*terh₃u-|t=to rub, turn, drill, bore}}. Not directly related with {{noncog|de|Truhe|t=chest}}, which belongs to the root of {{m+|en|trough}}. However, these two Germanic roots seem to have influenced each other early on.
