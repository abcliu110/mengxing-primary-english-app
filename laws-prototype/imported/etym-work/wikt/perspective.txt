==English==
[[Image:Perspectiva-2.jpg|thumb|right|Illustration of the use of perspective to create the perception of depth in a two-dimensional projection.]]

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|perspective|perspective, perspectif}}, attested since 1381, from {{der|en|fro|-}} or {{der|en|frm|-}}, from the first word of the {{der|en|la-med|perspectiva|perspectiva ars|science of optics}}, the feminine of {{der|en|la|perspectivus||of sight, optical}}, from {{m|la|perspectus}}, the past participle of {{m|la|perspicere||to inspect, look through}}, itself from {{m|la|per-||through}} + {{m|la|specere||to look at}}; the noun sense was influenced or mediated by {{cog|it|prospettiva}}, from {{m|it|prospetto||prospect}}.
