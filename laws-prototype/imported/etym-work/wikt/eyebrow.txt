===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|eȝebreu}}, alteration of {{inh|en|ang|ēaganbrū||eyebrow}}, equivalent to {{compound|en|eye|brow}}. The corresponding Old English {{m|ang|ēagbrǣw}} meant "eyelid". Compare Dutch {{m|nl|oogbrauw||eyelid; eyelash; eyebrow}}, German {{m|de|Augenbraue||eyebrow}}.

===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|Q43748}}The [[hair]] that grows over the [[bone ridge]] above the [[eye socket]].
# {{lb|en|construction}} A [[dormer]], usually of small size, whose roof line over the upright face is typically an arched curve, turning into a reverse curve to meet the horizontal line at either end.
# A clump of waste fibres that builds up in a roller machine.
