====Noun====
{{en-noun|~}}

# That which is or may be seized by animals to be devoured.
#: {{ux|en|The deer became '''prey''' to the lion.}}
#* {{RQ:Dryden Fables|Theodore and Honoria|passage=Already sees herself the monster's '''prey'''.}}
# A person or thing given up as a victim.
#* {{RQ:Conrad Heart of Darkness|part=II|passage=[The helmsman] steered with no end of a swagger while you were by; but if he lost sight of you, he became instantly the '''prey''' of an abject funk {{...}}}}

# A living thing, usually an animal, that is eaten by another living thing.
#: {{ux|en|The rabbit was eaten by the coyote, so the rabbit is the coyote's '''prey'''.}}
#: {{usex|en|Stranded without any weapons, we made very easy '''prey'''.}}
#* {{RQ:King James Version|book=Job|chapter=4|verse=2|passage=The old lion perisheth for lack of '''prey'''.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=2013|month=May-June|author=[http://www.americanscientist.org/authors/detail/william-e-conner-1 William E. Conner]
|title=[http://www.americanscientist.org/issues/feature/2013/3/an-acoustic-arms-race An Acoustic Arms Race]
|volume=101|issue=3|page=206–7|magazine=w:American Scientist
|passage=Nonetheless, some insect '''prey''' take advantage of clutter by hiding in it. Earless ghost swift moths become “invisible” to echolocating bats by forming mating clusters close (less than half a meter) above vegetation and effectively blending into the clutter of echoes that the bat receives from the leaves and stems around them.}}
# {{lb|en|archaic}} Anything, such as goods, etc., taken or got by violence; something taken by force from an enemy in war.
#: {{syn|en|spoil|booty|plunder}}
#* {{RQ:King James Version|book=Numbers|chapter=31|verse=12|passage=And they brought the captiues, and the '''pray''', and the spoile vnto Moses and Eleazar the Priest, and vnto the Congregation of the children of Israel, vnto the campe at the plaines of Moab, which are by Iordan neere Iericho.}}
# {{lb|en|archaic}} The act of devouring other creatures; [[ravage]].
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare King Lear|act=III|scene=iv|passage=Hog in sloth, fox in stealth, {{...}} lion in '''prey'''.}}
# The victim of a disease.
#: {{ux|en|He became the '''prey''' of dengue.}}
