===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:bor|frm:democratique<ety:lbor<la-lat:democraticus<ety:bor<grc:δημοκρᾰτῐκός>>>>|la-lat:democraticus|tree=1}}
From {{der|en|frm|democratique|t=pertaining to democracy, democratic}} (modern {{cog|fr|démocratique}}), and its {{glossary|etymon}} {{der|en|la-lat|democraticus|t=pertaining to democracy, democratic; democrat}}, from {{der|en|grc|δημοκρᾰτῐκός|t=of or for democracy; favouring or suited for democracy}}, from {{m|grc|δημοκρᾰτῐ́ᾱ|t=democracy}} + {{m|grc|-ῐκός|pos={{glossary|suffix}} with the sense ‘of or pertaining’ to forming {{glossary|adjective}}s}}.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=adj.'' and ''n|id=49758|date=March 2021|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=adj}}</ref> {{m|grc||Δημοκρᾰτῐ́ᾱ}} is derived from {{m|grc|δῆμος|t=the common people; free citizens, sovereign people; popular assembly; popular government, democracy}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*deh₂-|t=to divide; to share}}) + {{m|grc|-κρᾰτῐ́ᾱ|pos=suffix meaning ‘government; rule’}} (from {{m|grc|κρᾰ́τος|t=might, strength; dominion, power}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kret-|t=insight, intelligence; strength}}) + {{m|grc|-ῐ́ᾱ|pos=suffix forming {{glossary|feminine}} {{glossary|abstract noun}}s}}). {{surf|en|demo-|-cratic}} or {{af|en|democrat|-ic}}.
