===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|wrappen||to wrap, fold}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin. Perhaps related to {{cog|frr|wrappe|t=to press into; stop up}}, dialectal {{cog|da|vrappe|t=to stuff, cram}}, {{cog|gml|rincworpen|t=to envelop, wrap}}, {{cog|gml|wrempen|t=to wrinkle, scrunch the face}}, all perhaps tied to {{der|en|ine-pro|*werp-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*werb-|t=to turn, twist, bend}}.

Compare also similar-sounding and similar-meaning {{cog|enm|wlappen|t=to wrap, lap, envelop, fold}}, {{cog|dum|lappen|t=to wrap up}}, {{cog|roa-oit|goluppare|t=to wrap}} (from Germanic). {{doublet|en|lap#Etymology_2}}; related to {{m|en|envelop}}, {{m|en|develop}}. Also compare {{cog|la|verber|t=whip, lash}}.
