# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[mix]] or combine; often used of metals.
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[reduce]] the [[purity]] of by [[mixing]] with a [[less]] [[valuable]] [[substance]].
#: {{co|en|to '''alloy''' gold with silver or copper, or silver with copper}}
