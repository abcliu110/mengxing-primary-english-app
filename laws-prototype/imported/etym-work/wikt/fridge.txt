===Etymology 1===
The {{glossary|noun}} is a {{clipping|en|refrigerator|nocap=1}}, perhaps influenced by the {{w|Frigidaire}} brand of refrigerators, or {{m|en|frigerator|t=(''dated'') refrigerator}}.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=74639|date=March 2019|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref> The spelling is likely influenced by analogy with ''[[bridge]]'', ''[[ridge]]'', etc.<ref>https://www.merriam-webster.com/video/why-is-d-in-fridge-but-not-refrigerator</ref> The {{glossary|verb}} is derived from the noun.

The fandom slang verb sense alludes to the phrase "{{w|Women in Refrigerators|women in refrigerators}}" {{coinage|en|Gail Simone|nat=the American|occ=comic book writer|nocap=1|nobycat=1}}. Simone was referencing a plot point in ''[[w:Green Lantern (comic book)|Green Lantern]]'' (volume 3, issue 54, 1994), in which the {{w|Kyle Rayner|Green Lantern}}'s {{w|Alexandra DeWitt|girlfriend}} is murdered by a villain, and her body placed in a refrigerator for him to find.<ref>{{cite-book|author=Tim Hanley|chapter=The Mundane Modern Age|title=Wonder Woman Unbound: The Curious History of the World’s Most Famous Heroine|location=Chicago, Ill.|publisher={{w|Chicago Review Press}}|year=2014|section=part 3 (The Bronze Age)|pages=238–239|pageurl=https://books.google.com/books?id=K90EAwAAQBAJ&pg=PA239|isbn=978-1-61374-909-8}}.</ref>
