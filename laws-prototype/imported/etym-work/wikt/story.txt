=====Usage notes=====
* {{sense|soap opera}} Popularized in the 1950s, when [[soap opera]]s were often billed as "[[continuing]] [[stories]]", the term "story" to describe a soap opera fell into disuse by the 21st [[century]] and is now used [[chiefly]] among older people and in [[rural]] areas. Other English-speaking [[countries]] used the term at its [[zenith]] as a "[[loaned]]" word from the United States.
