===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*mey- (change)}}
From {{inh|en|enm|promis|promis, promisse}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|promesse}}, from {{der|en|la-med|prōmissa}}, {{der|en|la|prōmissum|t=a promise}}, feminine and neuter past participles of {{m|la|prōmittō|t=to send forth, to say beforehand, to promise}}, from {{m|la|pro||forth}} + {{m|la|mittere||to send}}; see {{m|en|mission}}. Compare {{m|en|admit}}, {{m|en|commit}}, {{m|en|permit}}, etc. Displaced native {{m|ang|ġehātan||to promise}} and {{m|ang|ġehāt||a promise}}.
