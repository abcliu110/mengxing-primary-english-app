===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:tonnel<ety:bor<fro:tonel>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|tonnel}}, from {{der|en|fro|tonel|tonel(e)}}, diminutive of {{m|fro|tone||cask}}, a word of uncertain origin and affiliation, although some linguists assume that it came from {{der|en|la-lat|tunna}}. Related to {{cog|ang|tunne||tun; cask; barrel}}. More at {{m|en|tun}}.
