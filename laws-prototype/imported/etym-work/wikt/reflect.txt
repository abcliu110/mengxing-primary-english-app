==English==

===Etymology===
From {{uder|en|fro|reflect|reflecter|to bend back, turn back}}, from {{uder|en|la|reflectō||to reflect}}, from {{m|la|re-||again}} + {{m|la|flectō||to bend, to curve}}. Compare {{cog|en|reflex}}.
