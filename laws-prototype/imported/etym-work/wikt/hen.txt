====Usage notes====
Contrast with ''{{l|da|henne}}''; where ''hen'' indicates movement, ''henne'' indicates position. Thus ''hvor løber han henne?'' means "where is he running?", whereas ''hvor løber han hen?'' means "to where is he running?".

==Swedish==

{{was fwotd|sv|2013|March|11}}
[[File:Hen - The Swedish pronoun.svg|thumb|alt=A unified femininity and masculinity symbol with “hen” in the center|“Hen” represented among other personal pronouns in Swedish]]

===Etymology 1===
Created as an alternative to {{m|sv|hon|t=she}} and {{m|sv|han|t=he}}. The coining of the word has probably been influenced by the Finnish {{m|fi|hän}}, a personal pronoun used about human beings and which does not specify gender (Finnish lacks grammatical gender entirely). ''Hen'' was suggested as early as 1966 by linguist {{coinage|sv|Rolf Dunås|w=sv|notext=1|nobycat=1}} in Swedish regional newspaper ''{{w|Upsala Nya Tidning}}'' and was proposed again in a 1994 article by linguist Hans Karlgren, but did not receive widespread attention until around 2010.<ref>{{cite-journal|date=8 March 2012|author=Anders Q Björkman|title=”Hen” föreslogs av språkforskare redan 1994 – i SvD|trans-title=”Hen” proposed by linguists already 1994 – in SvD|journal={{w|Svenska Dagbladet}}|url=http://blog.svd.se/kultur/2012/03/08/%E2%80%9Dhen%E2%80%9D-foreslogs-av-sprakforskare-redan-1994-%E2%80%93-i-svd|accessdate=7 January 2013|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20120820042551/http://blog.svd.se/kultur/2012/03/08/%E2%80%9Dhen%E2%80%9D-foreslogs-av-sprakforskare-redan-1994-%E2%80%93-i-svd/|archivedate=10 April 2013}}</ref>

* This adjective has an alternate, more “senior” comparative in the form of {{m|cy|hŷn}} and an equivalent alternate superlative in the form of {{m|cy|hynaf}}.
* Unlike most Welsh adjectives, this word goes before the noun.
* Like most Welsh adjectives that go before the noun, this word triggers a [[soft mutation]] in the word that follows it.
