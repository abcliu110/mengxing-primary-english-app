==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*Hrewp-}}
From {{bor|en|la|eruptus}}, past participle of {{m|la|ērumpō||to break out (of), to burst out (from)}}, from {{m|la|e||out}} + {{m|la|rumpō||to break}}.
