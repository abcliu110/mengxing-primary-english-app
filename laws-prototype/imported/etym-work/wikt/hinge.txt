===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# A [[joint]]ed or [[flexible]] device that allows the [[pivot]]ing of a [[door]] etc.
#* {{RQ:Mary Shelley Last Man|III|1|passage=The massy portals of the churches swung creaking on their '''hinges'''; and some lay dead on the pavement.}}
# A naturally occurring [[joint]] resembling such hardware in form or action, as in the [[shell]] of a [[bivalve]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1862|author=Charles Darwin|title=The Various Contrivances by Which Orchids Are Fertilized by Insects|text=The pedicel of the pollinium is articulated as before by a '''hinge''' to the disc; it can move freely only in one direction owing to one end of the disc being upturned.}}
# A [[stamp hinge]], a [[fold]]ed and [[gum]]med [[paper]] [[rectangle]] for [[affix]]ing [[postage stamp]]s in an [[album]].
# A [[principle]], or a point in time, on which [[subsequent]] [[reasoning]]s or [[event]]s depend.
#: {{ux|en|This argument was the '''hinge''' on which the question turned.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1840|author=Adam Duncan Tait|title=Remarks on a Pamphlet by the Reverend James Buchanan|page=26|text=But let me say, with all deference, that these positions ''do not appear to me to touch the '''hinge''' of the argument before us''.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2022| title=Lessons|author=Ian McEwan| page=388| passage=These grown-up children were at that '''hinge''' of life when parents must begin to shrink and fold.}}
# {{lb|en|statistics}} The median of the upper or lower half of a batch, sample, or probability distribution.
# One of the four cardinal points, east, west, north, or south.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1697|author=Thomas Creech|title=The five books of Mr. Manilius containing a system of the ancient astronomy and astrology: together with the philosophy of the Stoicks|page=121|text=If when the Moon is in the '''Hinge''' at East, / The Birth breaks forward from its native rest; / Full Eighty Years, if you two Years abate, / This Station gives, and long defers its Fate}}
#* {{RQ:Milton Paradise Regained|book=4|100|In ruine reconcil'd: nor slept the winds / Within thir stony caves, but rush'd abroad / From the four '''hinges''' of the world, and fell}}
# A [[movement]] that [[present]]s itself as [[rotation]] when an [[off-centre]] [[fixed]] [[point]] is [[take into account|taken into account]].
# In [[polyamory]], a person connected emotionally or sexually to two others who are not connected to each other.
#: {{syn|en|swing}}
# {{lb|en|dialectal}} To be in [[poor]] [[health]]; to be [[out of sorts]].
