===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*teh₂g-|id=touch}}
From {{inh|en|enm|taken|t=to take, lay hold of, grasp, strike}}, from {{inh|en|ang|tacan|t=to grasp, touch}}, probably of {{der|en|gmq}} origin, from {{der|en|non|taka|t=to touch, take}}. Alternatively, inherited from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*takan||to take; grasp, touch}}. Both from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*tēkaną|t=to touch}}, from pre-Germanic {{m|ine-pro||*deh₁g-|t=to touch}}, possibly a phonetically altered form of {{der|en|ine-pro|*teh₂g-|*te-th₂g-|id=touch|t=to touch, take}} (see there for details).<ref>{{R:gem:EDPG|head=*takan- ~ *tēkan-|page=507}}</ref><ref>{{R:ine:LIV|head=*teh₂⁽g̑⁾-|pages=616–617|passage=Mit erneuertem Ablaut *''tetōk-''/''tetk-'' für *''tetk-'' < *''dedg-'', assimiliert aus *''te-dg-'' < *''te-tg''- < *''te-th₂⁽g̑⁾-''|translation=With renewed ablaut *''tetōk-''/''tetk-'' for *''tetk-'' < *''dedg-'', assimilated from *''te-dg-'' < *''te-tg''- < *''te-th₂⁽g̑⁾-''}}</ref>

Gradually displaced native {{ncog|en|nim}}, from {{ncog|enm|nimen}}, from {{noncog|ang|niman|t=to take}}.

{{box-top|Cognates}}
Cognate with {{cog|sco|tak|t=to take}}, {{cog|is|-}} and {{cog|nn|taka|t=to take}}, {{cog|nb|ta|t=to take}}, {{cog|sv|ta|t=to take}}, {{cog|da|tage|t=to take, seize}}, {{cog|fy|take}}, {{m|fy|taakje|t=to grab, steal}}, {{cog|nl|taken|t=to take; grasp}}, {{cog|gml|tacken|t=to grasp}}. {{m+|en|thack}} may be from the same root. Compare {{m|en|tackle}}. Despite superficial similarity, unrelated to {{noncog|ine-pro|*tek-||to take by hand, obtain}}, which is instead cognate with {{m+|en|thig|t=to beg}}.
