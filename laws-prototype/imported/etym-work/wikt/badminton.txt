==English==
[[Image:Badminton Peter Gade.jpg|thumb|right|150px|Badminton player Peter Gade.]]

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|Badminton}}
Named after [[Badminton]] House, an estate in [[Gloucestershire]] owned by the {{w|Duke of Beaufort}}, where the game was first played in England. For the house name, see {{m|en|Badminton}}.

===Pronunciation===
* {{IPA|en|/ˈbæd.mɪn.tən/|/ˈbæd.mɪt.ən/}}
* {{audio|en|en-us-badminton.ogg|a=US}}

===Noun===
{{en-noun|~}}

# {{lb|en|uncountable}} A [[racquet]] sport played indoors on a [[court]] by two opposing players (singles) or two opposing pairs of players (doubles), in which a [[shuttlecock]] is [[volley]]ed over a net and the competitions are presided by an [[umpire]] in British English and a [[referee]] in American English.
#* {{RQ:Guardian|date=August 1 2012|author=Peter Walker; Haroon Siddique|url=http://www.guardian.co.uk/sport/2012/aug/01/london-2012-badminton-disqualified-olympics|title=Eight Olympic badminton players disqualified for 'throwing games'
|passage=Four pairs of women's doubles '''badminton''' players, including the Chinese top seeds, have been ejected from the Olympic tournament for trying to throw matches in an effort to secure a more favourable quarter-final draw.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240801052011/https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2012/aug/01/london-2012-badminton-disqualified-olympics}}
# {{lb|en|countable}} A cooling [[summer]] [[drink]] made with [[claret]], [[sugar]]{{,}} and [[soda water]].
