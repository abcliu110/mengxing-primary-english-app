===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|en|enm|ward}}, {{m|enm|warde}}, from {{inh|en|ang|weard|t=watching, ward, protection, guardianship; advance post; waiting for, lurking, ambuscade}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*wardu}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*wardō|t=protection, attention, keeping}}, an extension of the stem {{m|gem-pro||*wara-|t=attentive}} ({{cog|en|wary}}, {{m|en|beware}}), from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*wer-|t=to cover}}.

Cognate with {{cog|de|Warte|t=watchtower}}, {{m|de|warten|t=wait for}}; {{cog|en|guard}} is a parallel form which came via Old French.
