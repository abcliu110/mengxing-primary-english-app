===Etymology 2===
{{ety|pt|id=royal|:inh|roa-opt:real<id:royal>|tree=1}}
{{inh+|pt|roa-opt|real|id=royal}}, from {{inh|pt|la|rēgālis||royal}}, from {{m|la|rēx||king}} + {{m|la|-ālis}}, from {{der|pt|ine-pro|*h₃rḗǵs||ruler, king}}. Equivalent to {{af|pt|rei|-al}}.
* {{sense|currency used in Brazil since 1994}} from {{m|pt||Unidade Real de Valor|Real Unit of Value}}, an intermediary unit {{l|en|peg|pegged|id=fix value}} to the [[US dollar]] which was introduced during a transitional period from the {{l|pt|cruzeiro-real}} as a measure to combat inflation. Although the word {{m|pt||real}} is used with the meaning of “actual value” (hence the plural {{m|pt|reais}}), a connection to the former currency {{m|pt|real|id=royal|pos=plural {{m|pt||réis}}}} is likely also intentional. As such, most dictionaries inform {{m+|la|rēgālis}} as the word’s [[etymon]].
