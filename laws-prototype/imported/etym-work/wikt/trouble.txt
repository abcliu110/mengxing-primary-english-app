==English==

===Etymology===
Verb is from {{inh|en|enm|troublen}}, {{m|enm|trouble}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|troubler}}, {{m|fro|trobler}}, {{m|fro|trubler}}, metathetic variants of {{m|fro|tourbler}}, {{m|fro|torbler}}, {{m|fro|turbler}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*turbulō}}, from {{der|en|la|turbula||disorderly group, a little crowd or people}}, diminutive of {{m|la|turba||stir; crowd}}. The noun is from {{inh|en|enm|trouble}}, {{m|enm|troble}}, from {{der|en|fro|troble}}, from the verb.
