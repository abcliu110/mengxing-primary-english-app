==English==

===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|accesse}}, {{m|enm|acces}}, borrowed from {{der|en|frm|acces||attack, onslaught}} or from its source {{der|en|la|accessus}}, perfect passive participle of {{m|la|accēdō||approach; accede}}, from {{m|la|ad||to, toward, at}} + {{m|la|cēdō||move, yield}}. {{doublet|en|accessus}}. First attested in the early 14th century. The sense "entrance" was first attested about 1380.
