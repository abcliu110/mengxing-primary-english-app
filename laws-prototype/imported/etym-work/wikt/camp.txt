===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*kh₂em-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|kampe||battlefield, open space}}, from {{inh|en|ang|camp||battle, contest, battlefield, open space}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*kamp||open field where military exercises are held, level plain}}, from {{der|en|la|campus||open field, level plain}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kh₂em-|*kh₂emp-|t=to bend; crooked}}. Reinforced circa 1520 by {{der|en|frm|[[can]], [[camp]]||place where an army lodges temporarily}}, from {{der|en|fro-nor|camp}}, from the same {{der|en|la|-}} (whence also {{cog|fr|champ}} from Old French). Cognate with {{cog|goh|champf||battle, struggle}} ({{cog|de|Kampf}}), {{cog|non|kapp||battle}}, {{cog|goh|hamf||paralysed, maimed, mutilated}}. {{doublet|en|campus|champ}}.

The verb is from {{inh|en|enm|campen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|[[campian]], [[compian]]||to fight, war against}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*kampōn||to fight, do battle}}, from {{m|gmw-pro|*kamp||field, battlefield, battle}}, see above. Cognate with {{cog|nl|kampen}}, {{cog|de|kämpfen||to struggle}}, {{cog|da|kæmpe}}, {{cog|sv|kämpa}}.
