===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|spoon}}, {{m|enm|spoune}}, {{m|enm|spone}}, {{m|enm|spon|t=spoon, chip of wood}}, from {{inh|en|ang|spōn|t=sliver, chip of wood, shaving}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*spānu}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*spēnuz|t=chip, flake, shaving}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)peH-|t=chip, shaving, log, length of wood}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|spun}}, {{m|sco|spon|t=spoon, shingle}}, {{cog|fy|spoen|t=chip}}, {{cog|nl|spaan|t=chip, flinders}}, {{cog|de|Span|t=chip, flake, shaving}}, {{cog|sv|spån|t=chip, flake}}, {{cog|nn|spon||chip, spoon}}, {{cog|fo|spónur|t=wood chip; spoon}}, {{cog|grc|σφήν|t=wedge}}(though the connection to the Greek is likely impossible by modern reconstructions of PIE). Eclipsed non-native {{noncog|enm|cuculer}}, {{m|enm|coclear|t=spoon}}, from {{ncog|ang|cuculer}}, {{m|ang|cuceler}}, {{m|ang|cucler}}, borrowed from {{ncog|la|cochlear|t=spoon}}.

The "metaphoric unit of personal energy" sense was {{coinage|en|Christine Miserandino|w=-|in=2003|occ=writer|occ2=disability advocate|nobycat=1|nocap=1}} (see {{m|en|spoon theory}}).<ref name="Miserandino">{{cite-web|url=https://butyoudontlooksick.com/articles/written-by-christine/the-spoon-theory/|title=The Spoon Theory|last=Miserandino|first=Christine|year=2003|site=But You Don't Look Sick|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20191117210039/https://butyoudontlooksick.com/articles/written-by-christine/the-spoon-theory/|archivedate=17 November 2019|accessdate=12 October 2022}}</ref>
