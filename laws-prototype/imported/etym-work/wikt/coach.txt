===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:bor|frm:coche<ety:bor<de:Kutsche>>|tree=1}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|frm|coche}}, from {{der|en|de|Kutsche}}, from {{der|en|hu|kocsi}}, a clipping of "kocsi szekér" (cart from Kocs). According to historians, the coach was named after the small Hungarian town of {{m|hu|Kocs}}, which made a livelihood from [[cart]] building and transport between Vienna and Budapest.

The meaning “instructor/trainer” is from Oxford University slang (c. 1830) for a “tutor” who “carries” one through an exam; the athletic sense is from 1861.<ref>{{R:Online Etymology Dictionary}}</ref>
