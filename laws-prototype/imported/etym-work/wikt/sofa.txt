==English==

===Etymology 1===
{{swp}}
{{ety|en|:bor|fr:sofa<id:couch>|id=couch}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|fr|sofa}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ar|صُفَّة|t=a [[long]] [[seat]] [[made]] of [[stone]] or [[brick]], covered with rich carpets and cushions and used for sitting}}. Cognate with {{cog|arc|צפא}}/{{cog|syc|ܨܦܬܐ|tr=ṣipā’, ṣeppəṯā|t=mat, matting}}. The word may have entered European languages via Muslim Iberia or through Turkish.
