==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:der|la:supervivo<alt:supervīvere>}}
From {{der|en|xno|survivre}}, {{der|en|fro|survivre}}, from {{der|en|la-lat|supervivo|supervīvere|t=to outlive}}, from {{der|en|la|super|t=over}} + {{m|la|vivere|t=to live}}, akin to {{m|la|vita|t=life}}. See {{m|en|vivid}}. Compare {{m|en|devive}}, {{m|en|revive}}. Displaced native {{ncog|en|thrive}}.
