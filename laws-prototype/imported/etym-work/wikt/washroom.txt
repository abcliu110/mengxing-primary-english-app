==English==

===Etymology===
From {{compound|en|wash|room}} in reference to its [[sink]]s, but generally used euphemistically outside of Canada and the United States.

# {{lb|en|Canada|Asia|rarely|United States|euphemism}} A [[room]] with a [[toilet]], ''[[particularly]]'' a [[public]] [[toilet]].

====Usage notes====
The term originated in the US but has been largely replaced by {{m|en|restroom}} except in certain regions of the country. It continues to be the most common word for a public facility in Canadian English, and is also commonly used in parts of Asia where English is prevalent such as Hong Kong, India, and Malaysia.
