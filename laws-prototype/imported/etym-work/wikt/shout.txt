===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|shouten}}. Further origin {{unc|en|nocap=1}}. Possibly related to {{cog|enm|shooten||to shoot (out)}} or from or akin to {{uder|en|non|skúta||to chide, scold}}, {{cog|non|skúti}}, {{m|non|skúta||a taunt}}. See also the second, rare sense of the verb {{m|en|scout||to reject with contempt}}.
