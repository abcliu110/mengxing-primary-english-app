===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:gemetry<ety:bor<fro:geometrie<ety:der<la:geōmetria>>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|gemetry}}, {{m|enm|geometrie}}, from {{der|en|fro|geometrie}} (modern {{cog|fr|géométrie}}),<ref>{{R:MED|id=MED18350|entry=ǧēmetrī(e|pos=n}}</ref> from {{der|en|la|geōmetria}}, from {{der|en|grc|γεωμετρία||geometry, land-survey}}, from {{m|grc|γεωμέτρης||geometer, land measurer}}, from {{m|grc|γῆ||earth, land, country}} + {{m|grc|-μετρία||measurement}}, from {{m|grc|μέτρον||a measure}}. {{surf|en|geometer|-y|id2=abstract noun}} or {{af|en|geo-|-metry}}. {{doublet|en|gematria}}. Displaced native {{noncog|ang|eorþcræft}}, literally, "''[[earth]]-[[craft]]''".
