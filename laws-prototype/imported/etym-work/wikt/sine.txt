====Usage notes====
In various branches of mathematics, the sine of an angle is determined in various ways, including the following:
* The [[y-coordinate|''y''-coordinate]] of the [[point]] on the [[unit]] [[circle]] at the given [[anticlockwise]] angle from the positive [[x-axis|''x''-axis]].
* The [[sum]] of the [[real]] or [[complex]] [[power series]]<br/><br/>&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;<math>\sum_{n=1}^\infty{{(-1)^{n-1}}\over{(2n-1)!}}x^{2n-1} = x - {1\over 6} x^3 + {1\over 120} x^5 - {1\over 5040} x^7 + ...</math><br/><br/>where ''x'' is in [[radian]]s.

==Latin==

===Pronunciation===
* {{la-IPA}}

===Etymology 1===
The meaning "without" was previously expressed by ''sē, sēd'' (see {{m|la|sē-}}, {{m|la|sed}}), from {{der|la|ine-pro|*swé||self}}, thus originally "by itself". Some still refer the ''si-'' in ''sine'' to this root; others refer it to {{der|la|ine-pro|*só||this}}, whence {{m|la|si||if}}. And as sometimes ''nesi'' was also written, with ''-ne'' being {{m|la|nē||not}}, ''sine'' might literally mean "not this". Compare with {{m|la|nisi}}.

Yet others refer ''sine'' to {{der|la|ine-pro|*sénHi|*sen(H)i|for oneself, without}}, itself possibly related to {{m|ine-pro|*swé}} or more likely a locative of {{m|ine-pro|*senH-}}.<ref>{{R:itc:EDL|page=565|head=sine}}</ref><ref>{{R:txb:Adams|head=snai|pages=779–781}}</ref> Thus cognate with {{cog|grc|ᾰ̓́νευ}}, {{m|grc|ἄτερ||without}}, {{cog|sa|सनुतर्|t=away, off}}, {{cog|ang|sundor}}; compare especially {{cog|txb|snai}} and {{cog|sga|sain||separated, different}} ({{cog|cel-pro|*sanis}}), which may reflect the original PIE adverb.

The ablative is from a PIE ablative of separation or a comitative-instrumental analogous to {{m|la|cum}}. Compare {{m+|sa|विना}}.
