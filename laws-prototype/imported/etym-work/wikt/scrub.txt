===Etymology 1===
Late {{inh|en|enm|scrub}} in the sense of "stunted tree," a variant of {{m|enm|schrub||shrub, bush}}, from {{der|en|ang|scrybb}}. {{doublet|en|shrub}}.

The initial {{IPAchar|/skr-/}} does not necessarily imply Scandinavian borrowing: the {{w|Oxford English Dictionary}} notes that native English words can show variation between {{IPAchar|/skr-/}} and {{IPAchar|/ʃr-/}}, as in {{m|en|screed}} and {{m|en|shred}}.<ref>{{R:OED Online|entry=S|pos=''n.''{{sup|1}}|noformat=1|id=1507760929|date=September 2026|footer=Etymology, discussion of initial ''scr''.}}</ref>
