===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=responsibility for wrongdoing|:inh|enm:gilt<ety:inh<ang:gylt<t:guilt, sin, offense, crime, fault>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|gilt}}, {{m|enm|gult}}, from {{inh|en|ang|gylt|t=guilt, sin, offense, crime, fault}}, of obscure origin. Possibly related with {{m+|ang|ġieldan|t=to pay, requite, punish}} (whence {{m|en|yield}}), from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*guldijā}} (whence  {{cog|gmh|gülte|t=debt, fee, financial duty}}, {{cog|gml|gülde}}, {{cog|de|Gülte}}), from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*geldan||to pay for}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*geldaną||to pay}}. However, neither the Old English stem form nor its ending {{m|ang||-t}} (instead of {{m|ang||-d}}) fit the continental form.
