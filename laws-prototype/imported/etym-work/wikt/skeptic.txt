===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|frm|sceptique}} (but with a pronunciation closer to that of the Latin etymon), or possibly directly from {{bor|en|la-lat|scepticus}} (originally attested only in the plural {{m|la|Scepticī|t=the sect of Skeptics}}), from {{der|en|itc-pro|*spektos|t=see, observing}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*speḱ-|t=to see}}, compare to {{m|grc|σκοπέω|t=to view, examine}}.

This word was originally spelled sceptick or sceptic and pronounced like [[septic]], the {{IPAchar|/k/}} is a result of learned influence.
