===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:moneye|tree=1}}
{{root|en|ine-pro|*men- (think)}}
From {{inh|en|enm|moneye}}, {{m|enm|moneie}}, {{m|enm|money}}, borrowed from {{der|en|xno|muneie||money}}, from {{der|en|la|monēta||money, a place for coining money, coin, mint}}, from the name of the temple of Juno Moneta in Rome, where a mint was.

In this sense, displaced native {{ncog|ang|feoh}}, whence {{cog|en|fee}}. {{doublet|en|mint}}, ultimately from the same Latin word but through Germanic and Old English, and of {{m|en|manat}}, through Russian and Azerbaijani or Turkmen.
