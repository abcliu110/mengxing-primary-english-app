===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|sure}}, {{m|enm|seur}}, {{m|enm|sur}}, from {{der|en|frm|sur}} or {{der|en|fro|seür}}, from {{der|en|la|sēcūrus||secure|lit=carefree}}, from {{m|la|sē-||apart}} + {{m|la|cūra||care}} (compare {{cog|ang|orsorg||carefree}}, from {{m|ang|or-||without}} + {{m|ang|sorg||care}}). See {{m|en|cure}}. {{doublet|en|secure}} and the now obsolete or dialectal {{m|en|sicker|t=certain, safe}}.

Displaced native {{noncog|enm|wis|wis, iwis|certain, sure}} (from {{noncog|ang|gewiss|ġewis, ġewiss|certain, sure}}), as well as {{m+|enm|siker||sure, secure}} (from {{cog|ang|sicor||secure, sure}}) with which it was cognate.

{{etystub|en|When and how did the letter"s" come to be pronounced as "sh"? The OED entry points to a source that may have information on this: E. J. Dobson Eng. Pronunc. 1500–1700 (ed. 2, 1968) II. §185 (iii.) and $388. Perhaps early irregular yod-coalescence?}}
