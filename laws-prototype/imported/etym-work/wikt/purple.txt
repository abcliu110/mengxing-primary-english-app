===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:purpel|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|purple}}, {{m|enm|purpel}}, from {{inh|en|ang|purpul|t=purple|pos=adjective}}, taken from {{der|en|ang|purpure|t=purple colour|pos=noun}}, from {{der|en|la|purpura|t=purple dye, shellfish}}, from {{der|en|grc|πορφύρα|t=[[purple-fish]]}}, perhaps of {{der|en|sem|-}} origin. {{dbt|en|purpura|purpure}}.
The sense of "imperial power" is from the wearing of the color purple by emperors and kings.
