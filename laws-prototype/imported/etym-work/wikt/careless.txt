#: {{syn|en|negligent|reckless |Thesaurus:careless}}

#* {{RQ:Fry Liar|page=35|passage=I don't find the pose of '''careless''' youth charming and engaging any more than you find the pose of [[careworn]] age fascinating and eccentric, I should imagine.}}
