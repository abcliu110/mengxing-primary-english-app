==English==

===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:happenen|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|happenen}}, {{m|enm|hapnen}}, augmented from {{der|en|enm|happen|t=to come to pass, happen}}, perhaps from {{inh|en|ang|hæppan|t=to move accidentally, slip}} and/or from {{der|en|non|*happa}}, {{m|non|*heppa}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*hampijaną|t=to fit in, be fitting}}. Equivalent to {{af|en|hap|t1=a chance, occurrence, byfall|-en|id2=inchoative|pos2=verbal suffix}}.
