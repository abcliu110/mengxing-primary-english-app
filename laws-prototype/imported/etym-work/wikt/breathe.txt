# {{C|en|Bodily functions}} {{lb|en|intransitive}} To draw [[air]] into ([[inhale]]), and expel air from ([[exhale]]), the [[lung]]s in order to extract [[oxygen]] and [[excrete]] waste gases.

#: {{ux|en|If you '''breathe''' on a mirror, it will fog up.}}
