===Etymology 2===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:cot|id=cottage}}
From {{inh|en|enm|cot}}, {{m|enm|cote}}, from {{inh|en|ang|cot}} and {{m|ang|cote|t=cot, cottage}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*kutą}}, {{m|gem-pro|*kutǭ}} (compare {{cog|non|kot}}, {{cog|gmh|kūz||execution pit}}), of unknown origin; perhaps from {{der|en|xsc|-}} (compare {{cog|ae|𐬐𐬀𐬙𐬀||chamber}}), but see {{l+|urj-pro|*kota|t=hut, tent, teepee}}. Cognate to {{cog|nl|kot||student room; small homestead}}. {{doublet|en|cote<id:hut><t:hut, shelter for livestock>}}; more distantly related to {{m|en|cottage}}.

|passage=One evening {{...}} we were on a sudden, greatly astonished, by hearing a violent knocking on the outward Door of our rustic '''Cot'''.}}
