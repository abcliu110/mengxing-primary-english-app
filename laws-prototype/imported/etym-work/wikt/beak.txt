Cognate with {{cog|br|beg||beak}}. Compare {{cog|stq|Bäk||mouth; muzzle; beak}}; Dutch {{m|nl|bek||beak; bill; neb}}.

# {{senseid|en|Q31528}} {{lb|en|anatomy}}
## A [[rigid]] [[structure]] [[projecting]] from the front of a [[bird]]'s [[face]], used for [[peck]]ing, [[groom]]ing, [[forage|foraging]], [[carry|carrying]] items, [[eat]]ing [[food]], etc.
##: {{syn|en|bill<id:Q31528>|neb<id:Q31528>|nib<id:Q31528>|pecker<id:Q31528>}}
## A similar pointed structure forming the [[nose]] and [[mouth]] of various animals, such as [[turtle]]s, [[platypus]]es, [[whale]]s, ''etc.''
## The long projecting sucking mouth of some [[insect]]s and other [[invertebrate]]s, as in the {{taxfmt|Hemiptera|order}}.
## The upper or projecting part of the shell, near the hinge of a [[bivalve]].
## The [[prolongation]] of certain [[univalve]] shells containing the [[canal]].
## {{lb|en|botany}} Any process somewhat like the beak of a bird, terminating the fruit or other parts of a plant.
# {{lb|en|by extension}}
## Anything projecting or ending in a point like a beak, such as a [[promontory]] of land.
