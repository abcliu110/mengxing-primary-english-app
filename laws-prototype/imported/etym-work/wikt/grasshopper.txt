===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|Q18398431}}A mostly [[herbivorous]] [[insect]] of the order {{taxfmt|Orthoptera|order}}, noted for its ability to jump long distances and for the habit of some species communicating by [[stridulation]]; they are related to but distinct from [[cricket]]s.
#* {{RQ:Landon Francesca Carrara|page=168|volume=I|text=Even in my happiest moments, while the grass was crowded with flowers beneath me—the sweet monotony of the running water in mine ear, only broken by the cheerful chant of the '''grasshopper'''—the boughs of the chestnut, filled with sunshine, dazzling my eyes, till the golden air seemed thronged with lovely shapes,—even then came pale and mournful shadows, whose white faces looked upon me pityingly.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1901|author=Sylvester D. Judd|title=The Relation of Sparrows to Agriculture|page=91
|passage=But it is as a destroyer of '''grasshoppers''' that the dickcissel excels.}}
#: {{syn|en|grasshop|q=rare}}
#: {{hypo|en|locust}}
## In the strict sense, refers to insects in the suborder {{taxfmt|Caelifera|suborder}}, particularly those in the family {{taxfmt|Acrididae|family}}.
## In a looser sense, also includes the [[katydid]]s (also known as longhorned grasshoppers or bush crickets), which are members of the family {{taxfmt|Tettigoniidae|family}}, of the suborder [[Ensifera#Etymology_2|Ensifera]].
# A [[cocktail]] made with [[crème de menthe]] and optionally with [[crème de cacao]].
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=21 Apr 79|journal=Gay Community News|page=17|author=Greg Jackson|title=He Looks Like Tarzan But He Talks Like Jane|text=Does he order Scotch-on-the-rocks in a low, mean voice? No? A Bud? No? A '''grasshopper''', heavy on the creme de menthe? Right!}}
# {{lb|en|figuratively}} A young [[student]] in the [[initial]] [[stage]]s of [[training]] who has been chosen [[on account of]] their [[obvious]] [[talent]].
#* '''2009''', B.P. Terpstra, ''Quadrant'', November 2009, No. 461 (Volume LIII, Number 11), Quadrant Magazine Limited, page 2:
#*: Although we don't know exactly ''why'' Li is chosen to dance, we witness a man assuring officials that the child isn't from bourgeois stock. Phew. There are no known landowners in the family, so the '''grasshopper''' passes some cultural purity test, in a state often fixated on class warfare, driven by the cult of personality, and bullied by paranoia.
# {{lb|en|slang|[[drug]]s}} A [[frequent]] [[user]] of [[marijuana]].
# {{lb|en|musical instrument}} In ordinary square or upright [[piano]]s of London make, the [[escapement]] lever or jack, so made that it can be taken out and replaced with the key.
#: {{syn|en|hopper}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1894|title=Encyclopædia_Britannica|chapter=Pianoforte|passage=John Geib {{...}}patented the hopper with two separate escapements, one of which soon became adopted in the '''grasshopper''' of the square piano}}
# {{lb|en|chess}} A [[fairy chess]] [[piece]] which moves orthogonally or diagonally, but must hop over a piece to move or capture, landing one square beyond the hopped piece.
