===Etymology 1===
{{onomatopoeic|en}}. Compare {{m|en|huzz}}, {{m|en|hizz}}, {{m|en|hiss}}.

In some noun senses perhaps derived from {{m|en|wizard}}. In the sense “remarkably skilled person”, it may also simply a misspelling of its shortened form {{m|en|wiz}}, or it may be a shortening of {{m|en|whiz kid}}, which may have a different origin, perhaps ''whiz(z)'' in the sense of “something very remarkable”, attested from 1908,<ref>{{R:OED Online|entry=whizz|pos=''n.''{{sup|2}}|noformat=1}}</ref> or the interjection (as in {{m|en|whizz-bang}} or {{m|en|gee whiz}}).

In fact, ''whiz(z)'' in the sense of “something very remarkable” may be derived from ''whizz-bang'' in the sense of “conspicuously talented”, and in military slang ''whizz-bang'' (or ''whizbang'') can mean “an exceptionally skilful person” (attested in 1916 and later),<ref>{{R:OED Online|entry=whizz-bang|pos=n}}</ref> which ''whiz'' in the same sense seems to be a shortening of (whence presumably also ''whiz kid'').
