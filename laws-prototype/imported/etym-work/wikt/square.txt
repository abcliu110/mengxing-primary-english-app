From {{inh|en|enm|square}}, {{m|enm|sqware}}, {{m|enm|squyre}}; from {{der|en|fro|esquarre}}, {{m|fro|esquerre}}, (modern {{cog|fr|équerre}}), from {{der|en|la-vul|*exquadra}}, from {{der|en|la|-}} {{prefix|la|ex|quadro|nocat=1}}, from {{m|la|quadrus}} (compare {{m+|en|quad}} and {{m|en|quadra}}). {{dbt|en|squad|squadra}}.

{{displaced|ang|fēowerecge}} ({{m|ang|fēower}} nominative + {{m|ang|ecg}} accusative, as in "four-edged").
