===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|wilwe}}, {{m|enm|welew}}, variant of {{m|enm|wilghe}}, from {{der|en|ang|weliġ}}, from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*wilig}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*wiligaz}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*welik-}} (compare (Arcadian) {{cog|grc|ἑλίκη}}, {{cog|hit|𒌑𒂖𒆪||tr=welku|grass}}), from {{m|ine-pro|*wel-||twist, turn}}. Cognate to {{cog|fy|wylch}} and more distantly (via {{cog|gmw-pro|*wilgijā}}) to {{cog|nl|wilg}} and {{cog|de|Wilge}}.
