#: {{ux|en|There was some '''truth''' in his statement that he had no other choice.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=2012-01|author=Robert M. Pringle|volume=100|issue=1|page=31|magazine=w:American Scientist|title=[http://www.americanscientist.org/issues/feature/2012/1/how-to-be-manipulative How to Be Manipulative]|passage=As in much of biology, the most satisfying '''truths''' in ecology derive from manipulative experimentation. #: {{ux|en|'''Truth''' to one's own feelings is all-important in life.}}
# {{lb|en|archaic}} [[faithfulness|Faithfulness]], [[fidelity]].
#* {{RQ:Coleridge Christabel|page=32|passage=Alas! they had been friends in youth; / But whispering tongues can poison '''truth'''; {{...}}}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} A pledge of loyalty or faith.
# Conformity to rule; exactness; close correspondence with an example, mood, model, etc.
#* {{RQ:Mortimer Husbandry|passage=Ploughs, {{...}} to make them go true, {{...}} depends much upon the '''truth''' of the ironwork.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1840|author=w:Joseph Whitworth|title=[[s:Miscellaneous Papers on Mechanical Subjects/A Paper on Plane Metallic Surfaces or True Planes|A Paper on Plane Metallic Surfaces or True Planes]]|passage=The process of grinding is, in fact, regarded as indispensable wherever '''truth''' is required, yet that of scraping is calculated to produce a higher degree of '''truth''' than has ever been attained by grinding.}}

#: {{ux|en|The '''truth''' is what is.}}
#: {{ux|en|Alcoholism and redemption led me finally to '''truth'''.}}
#* {{RQ:Keats Lamia|poem=Ode on a Grecian Urn|stanza=5|page=116|passage="Beauty is '''truth''', '''truth''' beauty,"—that is all / Ye know on earth, and all ye need to know.}}
# {{lb|en|countable}} Something acknowledged to be true; a true statement or axiom.
#: {{ux|en|Hunger and jealousy are just eternal '''truths''' of human existence.}}
#* {{RQ:Austen Pride and Prejudice|passage=It is a '''truth''' universally acknowledged, that a single man in possession of a good fortune, must be in want of a wife.}}
# {{lb|en|physics|dated}} [[topness|Topness]]; the property of a [[truth quark]].
# {{lb|en|games}} In the game [[truth or dare]], the choice to truthfully answer a question put forth.
#: {{ux|en|When asked truth or dare, he picked '''truth'''.}}
