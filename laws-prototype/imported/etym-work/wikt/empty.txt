==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|emty}}, {{m|enm|amty}}, from {{inh|en|ang|ǣmtiġ}}, {{m|ang|ǣmettiġ|t=vacant, empty, free, idle, unmarried|lit=without must or obligation, leisurely}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|-}} {{m|gem-pro|*uz-|t=out}} + {{der|en|gem-pro|*mōtijô}}, {{m|gem-pro|*mōtô|t=must, obligation, need}}, {{m|gem-pro|*mōtiþô|t=ability, accommodation}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*med-|t=measure; to acquire, possess, be in command}}. Related to {{m+|ang|ġeǣmtigian|t=to empty}}, {{m|ang|ǣmetta|t=leisure}}, {{m|ang|mōtan|t=can, to be allowed}}. More at [[mote]], [[meet]].

The [[interconsonantal]] [[excrescent]] ''p'' is a [[euphonic]] insertion<ref>{{R:Etymonline|empty}}</ref> dating from Middle English.
