===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:attachen<ety:bor<fro:atachier>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|attachen}}, from {{der|en|fro|atachier}}, variant of {{m|fro|estachier||bind}}, derived from {{m|fro|estache||stick}}, from {{der|en|frk|*stakkā}}, {{m|frk|*stakō|t=stick}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*stakô|t=pole, bar, stick, stake}}. {{doublet|en|attack}}. More at {{m|en|stake}}, {{m|en|stack}}.

Displaced native {{ncog|ang|þīedan}}.

===Etymology===
Prefixed with {{af|sga|ad-}}, from {{inh|sga|cel-pro|*adtekʷom}}. The meaning "refuge" (attested mainly in the Milan glosses, where it is its only sense) is believed to be the original meaning, with its related literal sense vanishing from its associated verb before Old Irish.
