==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*speḱ-}}
From {{bor|en|la|expectāre}}, infinitive form of {{m|la|exspectō||look out for, await, expect}}, from {{m|la|ex||out}} + {{m|la|spectō||look at}}, {{glossary|frequentative}} of {{m|la|speciō||see}}.

===Pronunciation===
* {{IPA|en|/ɪkˈspɛkt/|/ɛkˈspɛkt/}}

* {{hyph|en|ex|pect}}
* {{rhymes|en|ɛkt|s=2}}

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|ambitransitive}} To [[predict]] or [[believe]] that something will happen
#: {{syn|en|anticipate|hope|look for|bargain for}}
#: {{co|en|when you least '''expect''' it}}
#: {{ux|en|I '''expect''' to be able to walk again after [[get over|getting over]] my broken leg.}}
#: {{ux|en|The hostage is '''expected''' to be released later today.}}
#: {{ux|en|I '''expect''' it'll rain this weekend, but I hope it won't.}}
#: {{ux|en|I did not '''expect''' you'd come before I asked you to, but do '''expect''' you to apologize for it.}}
#* {{RQ:Maxwell Mirror and the Lamp|chapter=13|passage=“{{...}} They talk of you as if you were Croesus—and I '''expect''' the beggars sponge on you unconscionably.” And Vickers launched forth into a tirade very different from his platform utterances. He spoke with extreme contempt of the dense stupidity exhibited on all occasions by the working classes.}}
#* {{quote-web|en|year=2018|url=https://learningenglish.voanews.com/a/china-s-melting-glacier-brings-visitors-concern-over-climate-change/4627742.html|title=VOA Learning English > China's Melting Glacier Brings Visitors, Adds to Climate Concerns
|site=learningenglish.voanews.com
|passage=And temperatures are '''expected''' to keep rising. [[File:And temperatures are expected to keep rising.ogg]]}}
# To [[consider]] [[obligatory]] or [[required]].
#: {{syn|en|call for|demand}}
#* '''1805''', {{w|Horatio Nelson, 1st Viscount Nelson|Nelson, Horatio}} via {{w|John Pasco|Pasco, John}}, {{w|England expects that every man will do his duty|signal}} sent at the {{w|Battle of Trafalgar}}:
#*: England '''expects''' that every man will do his duty.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2015|title=I Am 7.5 Billion Human|page=49|author=Sajith Buvi|passage=
I was born and immediately thrown into a society that makes its own rules, standards, and expectations. I am '''expected''' to behave. I am '''expected''' to deliver. I am '''expected''' to live up to the contrived standards of the society.}}
# To consider reasonably [[due]].
#: {{syn|en|hope|want|wish}}
#: {{ux|en|My parents '''expected''' too much of me when I was in college.}}
#: {{ux|en|You are '''expected''' to get the task done by the end of next week.}}
#: {{ux|en|How am I '''expected''' to reach the next level?}}
# {{lb|en|continuous aspect only, of a woman or couple}} To be [[pregnant]], to consider a baby due.
#: {{ux|en|We are '''expecting''' our third in June.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2011|author=Eva Fischer-Dixon|title=The Bestseller
|passage=“You are pregnant?” he asked with shock in his voice. “Yes, Justin, I am '''expecting''' a child,”}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete|transitive}} To [[wait]] for; to [[await]].
#: {{syn|en|await|Thesaurus:wait for}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Merchant of Venice|act=V|scene=i|passage=Let's in, and there '''expect''' their coming.}}
#* '''1825''', {{w|Walter Scott}}, ''{{w|The Talisman (Scott novel)|The Talisman}}'', A. and C. Black (1868), 24-25:
#*: The knight fixed his eyes on the opening with breathless anxiety, and continuing to kneel in the attitude of devotion which the place and scene required, '''expected''' the consequence of these preparations.
#* {{RQ:en:CSL|volume=1|page=11|text=You keep on making me '''expect''' you again and again. Only the other day, when I thought you on the point of arriving I was suddenly put off by you till Quintilis (July).}}

#: {{syn|en|wait}}
#* {{RQ:Sandys Psalmes|passage=I will ''''expect''' until my change in death, / And answer at Thy call}}
