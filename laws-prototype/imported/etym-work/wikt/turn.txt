===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=verb|:inh|enm:turnen|tree=1}}
{{root|en|ine-pro|*terh₁-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|turnen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|turnian}}, {{m|ang|tyrnan|t=to turn, rotate, revolve}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*turnēn|t=to turn, lathe}} (also the source of {{der|en|de|turnen}} and its derivatives) and {{der|en|fro|torner|t=to turn}}, both from {{der|en|la|tornāre|t=to round off, turn in a lathe}}, from {{m|la|tornus|t=lathe}}, from {{der|en|grc|τόρνος|t=turning-lathe: a tool used for making circles}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*terh₁-|t=to rub, rub by turning, turn, twist, bore}}. Cognate with {{cog|ang|þrāwan|t=to turn, twist, wind}}, whence {{cog|en|throw}}. Displaced native {{ncog|enm|wenden}} from {{ncog|ang|wendan}} (see {{m|en|wend}}), and {{ncog|enm|trenden}} from {{ncog|ang|trendan}} (see {{m|en|trend}}), among several other terms.
