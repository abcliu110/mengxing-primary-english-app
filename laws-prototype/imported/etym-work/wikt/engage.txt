# ''To interact contractually.''
## {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[arrange]] to [[employ]] or [[use]] (a worker, a space, etc.).

## {{lb|en|intransitive}} To [[guarantee]] or [[promise]] (to do something).
## {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[bind]] through legal or moral obligation (to do something, ''especially'' to [[marry]]) {{qualifier|usually in passive}}.

# ''To interact mechanically.''
## To [[mesh]] or [[interlock]] (of machinery, especially a [[clutch]]).
##: {{ux|en|Whenever I '''engage''' the clutch, the car stalls out.}}
##* {{quote-journal|en|year=1964|month=April|author=G. Freeman Allen|title=The BRB shows traders the Liner train prototypes|journal=Modern Railways|page=265|text=The Liner train wagon is a simple underframe on bogies, with coned location points that '''engage''' recesses in the container bases.}}
## {{lb|en|engineering|transitive}} To come into gear with.
##: The teeth of one cogwheel '''engage''' those of another.
## {{lb|en|intransitive|or|transitive}} To activate a [[lock]].
##: {{ux|en|He '''engaged''' the lock on the door.}}
##: {{ux|en|The lock '''engaged''' automatically when the door was shut.}}
