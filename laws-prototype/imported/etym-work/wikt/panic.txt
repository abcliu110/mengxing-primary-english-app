===Etymology 1===
The {{glossary|adjective}} is borrowed from {{bor|en|frm|panique}}, a word itself borrowed from {{der|en|grc|πανικός|t=pertaining to Pan}};<ref name="OED">{{R:OED Online|pos=''adj.'' and ''n.''{{sup|2}}|noformat=1|id=136852|date=June 2021|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=panic{{sup|1}}|pos=n}}</ref> [[Pan]], the Greek god of fields and woods, was believed to be the source of mysterious sounds that caused contagious, groundless fear in herds and crowds, or in people in lonely spots.

Adjective sense 3 (“pertaining to or resulting from overpowering fear or fright”) is partly an {{glossary|attributive}} use of the {{glossary|noun}}.<ref name="OED"/>

The {{glossary|noun}} is derived from the adjective, while the {{glossary|verb}} is derived from the noun. Verb sense 1.3 (“to highly amuse, entertain, or impress (an audience watching a performance or show”) is derived from noun sense 4 (“a highly amusing or entertaining performer, performance, or show”).<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=v|id=136853|date=March 2021|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|alt=panic{{sup|1}}|pos=v}}</ref>
