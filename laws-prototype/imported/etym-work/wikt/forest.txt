===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*perkʷ-}}
{{inh+|en|enm|forest}}, from {{der|en|fro|forest}}, from {{der|en|la-eme|forestis}}. The Latin could be:

* from {{m|la|foris|t=outside}}, as in {{m|la|forestis}} ({{m|la|silva}}) "(wood) outside,"<ref>{{R:Lexico}}</ref><ref>{{R:AHD}}</ref><ref>{{R:Online_Etymology_Dictionary}}</ref>

* or from {{der|en|frk|-}} or {{der|en|gmw-pro|*furhisti|t=forest, fir-grove, wooded land}}, equivalent to {{com|en|fir|hurst|nocat=1}}.{{rfv-etym|en}} In which case, related to {{cog|ang|fyrhþe|t=forested land}}, {{cog|goh|forst}}, {{m|goh|forsti|t=forest}}, {{cog|non|fýri|t=pine forest}}. {{doublet|en|frith}}. 

Cognate with {{cog|nl|vorst|t=copse, grove, woodland}}, {{cog|de|Forst|t=forest}}.

In this sense, mostly displaced the native {{ncog|enm|wode}}, from {{ncog|ang|wudu}} (modern {{ncog|en|wood}}) and {{ncog|enm|wald}}, {{m|enm|wold}}, {{m|enm|wæld}}, from {{ncog|ang|wald}}, {{m|ang|weald}} (modern {{ncog|en|wald}}, {{m|en|weald}}, {{m|en|wold}}).
