===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*ǵenh₁-}}
The {{glossary|noun}} is derived from:<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=62225|date=December 2021|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref>

* {{inh|en|enm|enginour|t=one who designs, constructs, or operates military works for attack or defence, etc.; machine designer}}{{nb...|(Late Middle English) engyner, (Middle English) engineour, enginer, engyneour, engyneor, engynour, yngynore|otherforms=1}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=enǧinǒur, -er|pos=n|id=MED13814}}</ref> from {{der|en|xno|enginour}}, {{m|xno|engigneour}}{{nb...|engineor, enginere, engineur, enginor, enginur|otherforms=1}}, and {{der|en|frm|-}} and {{der|en|fro|engigneor}}, {{m|fro|engigneour}}, {{m|fro|engignier|t=one who designs, constructs, or operates military works for attack or defence; architect; carpenter; craftsman; designer; planner; one who deceives or schemes}} (modern {{cog|fr|ingénieur}}), from {{m|fro|engin|t=contraption, device; machine; invention; creativity, ingenuity; intelligence; deception, ruse, trickery}} + {{m|fr|-eor}}, {{m|fr|-or|pos={{glossary|suffix}} forming {{glossary|agent noun}}s}}; {{m|fro||engin}} is derived from {{der|en|la|ingenium|t=innate or natural quality, nature; intelligence, natural capacity; ability, skill, talent; (Medieval Latin) engine; machine}}, from {{m|la|in-|pos={{glossary|prefix}} meaning ‘in, inside, within’}} + {{m|la|gignere}} (the {{glossary|present}} {{glossary|active}} {{glossary|infinitive}} of {{m|la|gignō|t=to bear, beget, give birth to; to cause, produce, yield}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*ǵenh₁-|t=to beget, give birth to; to produce}}) + {{m|la|-ium|pos=suffix forming {{glossary|abstract noun}}s}}; and
* from {{suffix|en|engine|er|id2=occupation|pos2=occupational suffix}}; and
* from {{suffix|en|engine|eer|pos2=suffix forming nouns denoting people associated with, concerned with, or engaged in specified activities}}, possibly modelled after {{der|en|frm|ingénieur}} (a variant of {{der|en|frm|-}}, {{der|en|fro|engigneour}}; see above), and {{der|en|it|ingegniere|t=engineer}} (''obsolete''; modern {{cog|it|ingegnere}}).

The {{glossary|verb}} is derived from the noun.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=v|id=62226|date=December 2019|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=v}}</ref>
