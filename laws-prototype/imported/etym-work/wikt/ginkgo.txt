===Etymology===
From {{der|en|ja|銀杏|tr=ginkyō}}, from {{der|en|zh|-}} {{zh-l|銀杏||silver apricot}}. ''Gink'''g'''o'' is the name that is printed in ''Amoenitatum exoticarum politico-physico-medicarum Fasciculi V [...]'' (1712) authored by {{w|Engelbert Kaempfer}}, the first Westerner to see the species. In his way of transcription ''gink'''y'''o'' would have been ''Gink'''j'''o'' or ''Gink'''i'''o'' but was printed as ''Gink'''g'''o''.<ref>Wolfgang Michel, [//catalog.lib.kyushu-u.ac.jp/opac_download_md/2898/Ginkgo_biloba2_revised_2011.pdf ''On Engelbert Kaempfer’s “Ginkgo”'', 2011]</ref> This was read by {{w|Carl Linnaeus}}, and the misspelling stuck.
