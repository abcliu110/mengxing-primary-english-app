====Usage notes====
* When referring to the global internet, the proper noun [[Wikipedia:Capitalization of "Internet"|was originally usually capitalised]] ({{m|en|Internet}}), reserving {{m|en|internet}} with a lowercase ''i'' for the [[common noun]], referring to any other set of computer networks connected by internetworking; however, increasingly, the proper noun sense also takes a lowercase ''i''. Compare similar examples in how the proper names for the [[Sun#Proper noun|Sun]] (the sun), the [[Moon#Proper noun|Moon]] (the moon), the [[Universe#Proper noun|Universe]] (the [[universe#Noun|universe]]), and the [[World#Proper noun|World]] (the [[world#Noun|world]]) are variably capitalized in {{w|English orthography}}.
* The internet, the [[World Wide Web]] (the Web, the web), and [[cyberspace]] are often [[Appendix:Glossary#loosely|loosely]] treated as [[synonym]]ous, but in careful usage, each of those terms has a more precise meaning, overlapping in a {{w|semantic field}}.
