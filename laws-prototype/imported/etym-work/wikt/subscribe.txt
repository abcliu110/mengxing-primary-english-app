===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:subscriben<ety:bor<la:subscrībō<alt:subscrībere>>>}}
From {{inh|en|enm|subscriben}}, {{m|enm|subskryben}}, from {{der|en|la|subscrībō|subscrībere|t=write beneath}}. {{surf|en|sub-|scribe}}. Compare its native {{noncog|en|-}} equivalent {{m|en|underwrite}}.

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|archaic|outside|law}} To [[write]] (one’s name) at the [[bottom]] of a document; to [[sign]] (one's name).
#* {{quote-book|en|year=c. 1510|author=w:Thomas More|title=The Life of Pico della Mirandola
|passage=[They] '''subscribed''' their names under them.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1906|title=The New York Supplement|page=729
|text={{...}} although the will is holographic, and a holographic instrument cannot be admitted to probate where testator told the '''subscribing''' witnesses that it was not his will, {{...}}}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1957|title=Estates of Incompetent Veterans: Hearings Before the United States House Committee on Veterans' Affairs|page=1968
|passage=Commission to take testimony: testimony of '''subscribing''' witnesses may be taken by commission {{...}}}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[sign]]; to mark with one's signature as a token of [[consent]] or [[attestation]].
#: {{ux|en|Parties '''subscribe''' a covenant or contract; a man '''subscribes''' a bond.}}
#: {{ux|en|Officers '''subscribe''' their official acts, and secretaries and clerks '''subscribe''' copies or records.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|title=US Articles of War|year=1806|passage=[E]very officer who shall hereafter be appointed, shall, before he enters on the duties of his office, '''subscribe''' these rules and regulations.}}
#* {{RQ:Milman Latin
|passage=All the bishops '''subscribed''' the sentence.}}
# {{lb|en|ergative}} To [[sign up]] to have [[copy|copies]] of a publication, such as a [[newspaper]] or a [[magazine]], delivered for a period of time.
#: {{ux|en|Would you like to '''subscribe''' or '''subscribe''' a friend to our new magazine, ''Lexicography Illustrated''?}}
# To pay for the provision of a [[service]], such as Internet access, a cell phone plan, or a streaming service.
#: {{ux|en|I '''subscribed''' to this streaming service years ago.}}
# To [[believe]] or [[agree]] with a [[theory]] or an [[idea]] {{+obj|en|:to}}.
#: {{ux|en|I don’t '''subscribe''' to that theory.}}
# To [[pay]] money to be a member of an organization.
# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To [[contribute]] or [[promise]] to contribute money to a common fund.
#* {{RQ:Roosevelt Autobiography|passage={{...}} under no circumstances could I ever again be nominated for any public office, as no corporation would '''subscribe''' to a campaign fund if I was on the ticket, and that they would '''subscribe''' most heavily to beat me;}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|year=1952|journal=The Children's Newspaper|passage=The culprits are old boys of Ardingly College, Sussex, who, in years gone by, wrote their names on the walls of the organ loft, an act which was strictly forbidden. Now they have been invited to '''subscribe''' towards the £400 required to make improvements to the organ.|page=6|url=https://dn721602.ca.archive.org/0/items/The_Childrens_Newspaper_1736_1952-06-28/The_Childrens_Newspaper_1736_1952-06-28.pdf|location=London|volume=1736}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To promise to give, by writing one's name with the amount.
#: {{ux|en|Each man '''subscribed''' ten dollars.}}
# {{lb|en|business|and|finance}} To agree to [[buy]] [[share]]s in a [[company]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1776|author=w:Adam Smith|title=w:The Wealth of Nations
|passage=The capital which had been '''subscribed''' to this bank, at two different subscriptions, amounted to one hundred and sixty thousand pounds, of which eighty per cent only was paid up.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} To sign away; to yield; to surrender.
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Measure|passage=Admit no other way to save his life , <br>(As I '''subscribe''' not that, nor any other, <br>But in the loss of question) {{...}}}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} To yield; to admit to being inferior or in the wrong.
# {{lb|en|obsolete|transitive}} To declare over one's signature; to publish.
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Much Ado About Nothing|V|ii|passage=I will '''subscribe''' him a coward.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive}} To indicate interest in the communications made by a person or organization.
#: {{ux|en|Please like this video, and '''subscribe''' to my YouTube channel.}}
# {{lb|en|intransitive|programming}} To [[register]] for [[notification]]s about an [[event]] or similar.
#: {{ux|en|If you '''subscribe''' to the MouseClick event, your application can react to the user clicking the mouse.}}
