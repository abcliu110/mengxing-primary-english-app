===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|Q26949}}Any plant of the genus {{taxfmt|Helianthus|genus}}, so called probably from the form and color of its [[floral]] [[head]], having the form of a large disk surrounded by yellow ray flowers.
#* {{RQ:Tolkien Fellowship of the Ring|chapter=I: A Long-Expected Party|passage= The flowers glowed red and golden: snapdragons and '''sunflowers''', and [[nasturtians]] trailing all over the turf walls and peeping in at the round windows.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1980|title=AA Book of British Villages|publisher=Drive Publications Ltd|page=214, about [[Halton Holegate]]|text=The low-beamed Bell Inn, believed to date from 1520, is locally renowned for its garden where giant '''sunflowers''' grow. In 1978, one reached the spectacular height of 23 ft 6 in.}} 
## The commonly cultivated species, {{taxfmt|Helianthus annuus|species}}, a native of America.
# A bright [[yellow]], like that of the flower petals.
#: {{color panel|FFB324}}
# {{lb|en|informal|organic chemistry}} Any flat, radially symmetric [[organic compound]], such as [[coronene]].
# {{lb|en|pathology}} {{synonym of|en|worm-star}}.
