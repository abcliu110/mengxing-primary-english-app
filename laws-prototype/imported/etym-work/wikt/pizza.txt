===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:bor|nap:pizza|etydate=1931}}

{{etydate|1931}}, {{bor+|en|nap|pizza|nocap=1}} (1590), of uncertain origin. Possibly related to {{m+|gkm|πίτα|t=cake, pie}}, attested in 1107 and itself of uncertain origin. In that case it would be a {{doublet|en|pide|pita|nocap=1}}.

The northern Italian dialectal form was {{m|it|pinza}}, the southern (Apulian and Calabrian) form was {{m|scn|pitta}}. This suggests a derivation from {{der|en|la|pīnctus}} ({{m|la|pictus|t=painted, smeared}}) or {{m|la|pinso|pīnsum}}, {{m|la|pinso|pīnsitum}}, {{m|la|pistum|t=pounded}}, but the northern forms appear to be contaminated with {{m|it|pinzare|t=to staple}}.

There are alternative suggestions involving Greek etymologies ({{m|grc|πηκτή}}, {{m|grc|πηκτός|t=compacted, congealed}}; {{m|grc|πήτεα|t=bran}}; {{cog|grc|πιττάκιον|t=patch; tablet; ticket}}), more remote possibilities involve comparison with {{cog|lng|bizzo|pizzo}}, {{m|lng|bizza|pizza|t=bite, morsel, lump, dumpling}}; {{cog|sq|petë|t=layer}}, {{cog|ro|pată|t=blotch, stain, macula}}; {{cog|sq|pite|t=gruel}};
{{der|en|arc|פִיתָּא|tr=pītā|t=piece of bread}}, {{cog|he|פַּת|tr=paṯ|t=bread}}.
