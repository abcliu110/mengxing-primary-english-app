===Etymology 1===
{{etymid|en|from strangers-and-hospitality-referent origins}}
{{etymon|en|id=guest receiver|:inh|enm:hoste<id:host>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|hoste}}, from {{der|en|fro|oste}} (French: {{m|fr|hôte}}), from {{der|en|la|hospitem}}, accusative of {{m|la|hospes||a host, also a sojourner, visitor, guest; hence, a foreigner, a stranger}}, from {{m|itc-pro|*hostipotis}}, an old compound of {{m|la|hostis}} and the root of {{m|la|potis}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gʰóstipotis||master of guests}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*gʰóstis||stranger, guest, enemy}} and {{m|ine-pro|*pótis||owner, master, host, husband}}. Used in English since 13th century.
