===Etymology 1===
Perhaps a corruption of {{der|en|enm|mesh|t=mash}}, compare {{m|en|muss}}, or derived from Etymology 2 "mixed foods, as for animals". Compare also {{cog|ang|mes|t=dung, excrement}}.
