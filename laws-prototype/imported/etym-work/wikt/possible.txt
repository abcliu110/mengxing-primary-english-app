==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₁es-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|possible}}, from {{der|en|fro|possible}}, from {{der|en|la|possibilis||possible}}, from {{m|la|possum|posse, possum|to be able}}; see [[power]]. Displaced {{ncog|enm|acumendlic|t=possible}}, from {{ncog|ang|ācumendlīċ|t=possible}}. Compare also {{ncog|ang|mihtelīċ|t=strong, capable, powerful, possible}}, which was cognate with {{ncog|goh|mahtlīh|t=possible}} and {{ncog|non|máttulígr|t=mighty, possible}}. Compare also {{ncog|nl|mogelijk||possible}} and {{ncog|de|möglich||possible}}.
