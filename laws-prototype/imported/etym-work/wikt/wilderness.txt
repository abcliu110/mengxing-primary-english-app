===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*wel-|*gʷʰel-|*dʰwes-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|wildernes}}, {{m|enm|wildernesse|t=desolate or uninhabited place, desolation}}{{nb...|wildernese, wildernise, wildernosse, wildirnes, wilirnesse, wildirenes, wildurnes, wildurnesse, wildrenes, wildrenesse, wyldernesse, wyldernys|otherforms=1}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=wī̆ldernes(se|pos=n|id=MED52762}}</ref> and then either:

* from {{inh|en|enm|wilderne|t=deserted or uninhabited place, wilderness; land not yet settled}}{{nb...|(Northern England or Northwest Midlands) wildernes, wildrenes, (Southwest Midlands) wulderne, (Early Middle English) wildren|otherforms=1}} (from {{inh|en|ang|wilddeōren|t=savage, wild}}; see below)<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=wī̆lderne|pos=n|id=MED52761}}</ref> + {{m|enm|-nes}}, {{m|enm|-nesse|pos={{glossary|suffix}} forming {{glossary|abstract noun}}s denoting qualities or states}};<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=-nes(se|pos=suf|id=MED29359}}</ref> or
* from {{inh|en|ang|*wildēornes}}, {{m|ang|*wilddēornes}}, either from {{m|ang|wilddēor|t=wild animal}}{{nb...|wildedēor, wildēor, wildīor|otherforms=1}} or {{m|ang|wilddēoren|t=savage, wild}} (from {{m|ang|wilddēor}} + {{m|ang|-en|pos=suffix forming {{glossary|adjective}}s with the sense ‘consisting of; material made of’}}) + {{m|ang|-nes|pos=suffix forming abstract nouns denoting qualities or states}}.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=229003|date=March 2021|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref>

{{m|ang||Wilddēor}} is derived from {{m|ang|wilde|t=savage, wild}} (ultimately either from {{der|en|ine-pro|*wel-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*welw-|t=hair, wool; ear of corn, grass; forest}}, or {{m|ine-pro|*gʷʰel-|t=wild}}) + {{m|ang|dēor|t=beast, wild animal}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*dʰwes-|t=to breathe; breath; soul, spirit; creature}}).

The English word is cognate with {{cog|da|vildnis|t=wilderness}}, {{cog|de|Wildernis}}, {{m|de|Wildnis|t=wilderness}}, {{cog|dum|wildernisse|t=wilderness}} (modern {{cog|nl|wildernis|t=wilderness}}), {{cog|gml|wildernisse|t=wilderness}} ({{cog|nds-de|Wildernis|t=wilderness}}), {{cog|stq|Wüüldernis|t=wilderness}}, {{cog|fy|wyldernis|t=wilderness}}.

Sense 3.3 (“situation of disfavour or lack of recognition”) is a reference to [[w:Book of Numbers|Numbers]] 14:32–33 in the [[Bible]] ([[King James Version]]; spelling modernized): “But as for you, your carcasses, they shall fall in this wilderness. And your children shall wander in the wilderness forty years, and bear your whoredoms, until your carcasses be wasted in the wilderness.”<ref>{{R:King James Version|Numbers|14|32–33|column=1|passage=But as for you, your carkaſes, they ſhall fall in this wilderneſſe. And your children ſhall wander in the wildernes forty yeres, and beare your whoredomes, until your carkaſes be waſted in the wilderneſſe.}}</ref>
