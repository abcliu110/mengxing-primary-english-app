==Swedish==

===Etymology===
{{bor+|sv|en|boots}}.

===Noun===
{{sv-noun|p}}

# {{l|en|boots}}

====Usage notes====
The difference from {{m|sv|känga}} is subtle. The immediate intuition is compact, "chunky" boots, often black leather boots. ''Boots'' might more often be a fashion accessory, but is also idiomatic for certain types of boots, like cowboy boots.
