===Etymology 1===
[[File:Asian House Gecko female.jpg|thumb|A [[common house gecko]] ({{taxfmt|Hemidactylus frenatus|species}})]]
The modern form was probably borrowed into English and other European languages from {{der|en|nl|gekko}} (1718). Contemporary English forms were {{m|en|chacco}} (1711) and {{m|en|jackoa}} (1724). The further origin is not quite clear. Some sources mention a supposed {{der|en|ms|gekok}}, {{m|ms|gekoq}}, or {{m|ms|gekop}}, which would be imitative of the gecko’s [[chirp]]ing sound. Another theory derives it from {{der|en|ace|gèh-gòh||busy}}.
