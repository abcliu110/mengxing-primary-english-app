===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*stebʰ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|staf}}, from {{inh|en|ang|stæf|t=letter of the alphabet}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*stab}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*stabaz}}. Cognate with {{cog|nl|staf}}, {{cog|de|Stab}}, {{cog|da|stav}}, {{cog|sv|stav}}.

Sense of "group of military officers that assists a commander" and similar meanings, attested from 1702, is influenced by or is even from German {{m|de|Stab}}.
