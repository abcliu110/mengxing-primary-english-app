==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|shortnen}}, {{m|enm|schortenen}}, equivalent to {{af|en|short|-en|pos2=verbal suffix|id2=inchoative}}. In some senses, a continuation (in altered form) of {{inh|en|enm|schorten|t=to make short, shorten}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sċortian|t=to become short}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*skurtōną|t=to shorten}}.
