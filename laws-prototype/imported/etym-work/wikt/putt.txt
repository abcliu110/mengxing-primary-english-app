===Etymology 1===
Borrowed from {{bor|en|sco|putt|t=to put}}.<ref>{{R:New English Dictionary}}</ref> Compare {{cog|dum|putten||to dig a pit}}. The {{cog|ang|putian||to push; thrust; put; place}} derivation is commonly assumed, although no longer valid. In Dutch, the word is instanced in a description of golf in an early seventeenth-century edition of Pieter van Afferden's ''Tyrocinium linguae latinae''.<ref>Heiner Gillmeister, “[https://web.archive.org/web/20210920074735/http://www.nga-earlygolf.nl/golfarchief/files/original/d7094f6c187c36f7d07f94ec065bc78d.pdf Über den Ursprung des Golfspiels]”, March 7, 2016.</ref> All derive from {{der|en|gem-pro|*putōną}}.
