==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|kitoun}}, {{m|enm|kytton}}, {{m|enm|kyton}}, {{m|enm|keton|t=kitten}}, of {{unc|en|obscure}} origin. Seemingly from, and usually explained as being from, [[unattested]] {{der|en|xno|*kitoun}}, {{m|fro|*ketun}} (compare {{cog|fro|chitoun}}, {{m|fro|cheton}}, {{m|fro|chaton|t=kitten}}, diminutive of {{m|fro|cat}}, {{m|fro|chat||cat}}); whence Modern {{cog|fr|chaton|t=kitten}}. Similar words of Germanic origin may have reinforced this word; compare {{cog|en|kitling|t=kit, kitten}}, {{cog|nds|Kitten|t=kitten}}, {{cog|is|kettlingur|t=kitten}}, {{cog|enm|chitte}} {{gloss|"whelp, pup", see {{m|en|chit}}}}. The idea that {{m|enm|kitoun}}, rather than being of Anglo-Norman origin, was in fact a purely Germanic derivation from one of these words is etymologically problematic, but cannot be definitively ruled out.
