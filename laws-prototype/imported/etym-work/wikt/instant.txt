From {{inh|en|enm|instant||infinitely short period of time}}, from {{der|en|fro|instant||assiduous, at hand|pos=adj}}, from {{der|en|la|instans|īnstāns, īnstantis|present, pressing, urgent|lit=standing near}}, present active participle of {{m|la|īnstō||to stand upon, be nearby}}, from {{prefix|la|in|stō|t1=after|t2=to stand|nocat=1}}. Compare {{cog|ang|instede||immediately, on the spot, at once}}. More at {{l|en|in}}, {{l|en|stand}}.
