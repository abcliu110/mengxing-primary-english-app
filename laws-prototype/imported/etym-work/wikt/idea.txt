# {{lb|en|philosophy}} An abstract [[archetype]] of a given thing, compared to which real-life examples are seen as imperfect [[approximation]]s; pure [[essence]], as opposed to actual examples. {{defdate|from 14th c.}}
#* {{RQ:Economist|date=2013-10-19|volume=409|issue=8858
|title=[http://www.economist.com/news/asia/21588115-younger-generation-emerges-aung-san-suu-kyis-shadow-and-tries-turn-her-party Trouble at the lab]
|passage=The '''idea''' that the same experiments always get the same results, no matter who performs them, is one of the cornerstones of science’s claim to objective truth. If a systematic campaign of replication does not lead to the same results, then either the original research is flawed (as the replicators claim) or the replications are (as many of the original researchers on priming contend). Either way, something is awry.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} The conception of someone or something as representing a perfect [[example]]; an [[ideal]]. {{defdate|16th|19th c.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} The [[form]] or [[shape]] of something; a quintessential [[aspect]] or [[characteristic]]. {{defdate|16th|18th c.}}
#* {{RQ:Montaigne Florio Essayes|chapter=6|book=II|passage=The remembrance whereof (which yet I beare deepely imprinted in my minde) representing me her visage and '''''Idea''''' so lively and so naturally, doth in some sort reconcile me unto her.}}
# An [[image]] of an object that is formed in the mind or recalled by the memory. {{defdate|from 16th c.}}
#: {{ux|en|The mere '''idea''' of you is enough to excite me.}}
# More generally, any result of mental activity; a [[thought]], a [[notion]]; a way of thinking. {{defdate|from 17th c.}}
#* {{RQ:Churchill Celebrity|chapter=3|passage=Now all this was very fine, but not at all in keeping with the Celebrity's character as I had come to conceive it. #* {{quote-book|en|year=1952|author=w:Alfred Whitney Griswold
|passage='''Ideas''' won't go to jail.}}
# A conception in the mind of something to be done; a [[plan]] for doing something, an [[intention]]. {{defdate|from 17th c.}}
#: {{ux|en|I have an '''idea''' of how we might escape.}}
# A purposeful aim or goal; [[intent]].
#: {{ux|en|Yeah, that's the '''idea'''.}}
#* {{RQ:Lincoln Pratt's Patients|chapter=3|passage=My hopes wa'n't disappointed. I never saw clams thicker than they was along them inshore flats. Clams was fairly scarce over that side of the bay and ought to fetch a fair price.}}
#* {{RQ:Economist|date=2013-06-01|volume=407|issue=8838|page=71
|title=[http://www.economist.com/news/finance-and-economics/21578670-peer-peer-lending-needs-new-name-end-peer-show End of the peer show]
