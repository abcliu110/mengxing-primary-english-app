===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:schep|tree=1}}
{{inh+|en|enm|schep}}, {{m|enm|schepe}}, from {{inh|en|ang-ang|sċēp}} (West Saxon {{m|ang|sċēap}}), from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*skāp}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*skēpą}}, of unknown origin.

Perhaps from the same {{der|en|xsc|-}} word (compare {{cog|os|цӕу|t=goat}}, {{cog|fa|چپش|t=yearling goat|tr=čapiš}})<ref>Vladimir Orel, ''A Handbook of Germanic Etymology'', s.vv. "*keppōn", "*skēpan" (Leiden: Brill, 2003), 213, 340</ref> which was borrowed into {{cog|sq|-}} as {{m|sq|cjap}}, {{m|sq|sqap|t=buck}} and into Slavic (compare {{cog|pl|cap}}). After Kroonen, ''*skēpą'' is instead from the root of {{cog|gem-pro|*skabaną|t=to scratch}} via {{w|Kluge's law}}.<ref>Guus Kroonen (2011), ''The Proto-Germanic ''n-''stems: a study in diachronic morphophonology'' [http://books.google.de/books?id=9stMq8rRnCMC], Rodopi, {{ISBN|9042032936}}.</ref>
