===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|transitive}} To place within a [[fluid]] (generally a [[liquid]], but also a [[gas]]).
#: {{syn|en|submerge}}
#: {{ux|en|Archimedes determined the volume of objects by '''immersing''' them in water.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1883|title=The Electrical Journal|page=501
|passage=... the two plates of platinum '''immersed''' in oxygen and hydrogen gases}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1841|author=William Rhind|title=A history of the vegetable kingdom|page=110
|passage=Even after the process of germination has taken place, if the young plant be '''immersed''' in an atmosphere of either of those gases [hydrogen and nitrogen], vegetation and life will  immediately cease.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1955|author=George Shortley; Dudley Williams|title=Elements of Physics for Students of Science and Engineering
|passage=The buoyant force of the atmospheric air on solids and liquids '''immersed''' in it is for most purposes negligible compared to the weight of solid or liquid, ...}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To [[involve]] or [[engage]] [[deeply]].
#: {{ux|en|The sculptor '''immersed''' himself in anatomic studies.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|mathematics}} To [[map]] into an [[immersion]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=2002|author=Kari Jormakka|title=Flying Dutchmen: Motion in Architecture|page=40
|passage=Thus, in mathematical terms a Klein bottle cannot be "embedded" but only "'''immersed'''" in three dimensions as an embedding has no self-intersections but an immersion may have them.}}
