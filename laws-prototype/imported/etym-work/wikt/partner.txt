===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|partener}}, {{m|enm|partiner}}, alteration (due to {{cog|enm|part}}) of {{cog|enm|parcener}}, from {{der|en|fro|parçonier}}, {{m|fro|parçonneour|t=joint heir}} from {{m|fro|parçon|t=partition}}, from {{der|en|la|partītiōnem}}, singular accusative of {{m|la|partītiō|t=portion}}. The word may also represent {{cog|fro|part tenour|t=part holder}}. Compare also {{noncog|enm|partifere|t=partner}}, {{m|enm|partifelewe|t=partner}}. {{doublet|en|parcener}}.
