===Noun===
{{en-noun|snowmen}}

# {{senseid|en|Q483985}}A [[humanoid]] figure made with large [[snowball]]s [[stack]]ed on each other. Human traits like a face and arms may be fashioned with sticks (arms), a [[carrot]] (nose), and stones or coal (eyes, mouth).
