====Usage notes====
Traditionally, some commentators have objected to the use of "however" at the start of a sentence in the sense of "nevertheless", but in practice this is widely accepted if followed immediately by a comma. The choice of position depends on the word being emphasized, which is usually the one immediately before.

The use of a comma before "however" as a means of connecting independent clauses in the sense of "nevertheless", as in "She wanted to go, '''however''' she decided against it", is traditionally considered an error, equivalent to using "however" as a proscribed conjunction (see below). Compare the prescriptively correct sentence "She wanted to go, <u>but</u> she decided against it", which uses a true conjunction "but". Similarly, mispunctuating it between commas makes for an ambiguous modifier because '''however''' could go with either clause.
