====Verb====
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|ambitransitive}} To [[disturb]] or [[halt]] (an [[ongoing]] [[process]] or [[action]], or the person performing it) by [[interfere|interfering]] [[sudden]]ly, especially by speaking.
#: {{ux|en|A maverick politician repeatedly '''interrupted''' the debate by shouting.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Romeo and Juliet|V|iii|passage=Do not '''interrupt''' me in my course.}}
#* {{RQ:Maxwell Mirror and the Lamp|chapter=3|passage=One saint's day in mid-term a certain newly appointed suffragan-bishop came to the school chapel, and there preached on “The Inner Life.”&nbsp; He at once secured attention by his informal method, and when presently the coughing of Jarvis {{...}} '''interrupted''' the sermon, he altogether captivated his audience with a remark about cough lozenges being cheap and easily procurable.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive}} To divide; to separate; to break the monotony of.
#: {{ux|en|The evenness of the road was not '''interrupted''' by a single hill.}}
# {{lb|en|transitive|computing}} To assert to (a [[computer]]) that an exceptional condition must be handled.
#: {{ux|en|The [[packet]] receiver circuit '''interrupted''' the microprocessor.}}
