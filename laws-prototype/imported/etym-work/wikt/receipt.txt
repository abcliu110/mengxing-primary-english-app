==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*keh₂p-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|receyt}}, recorded since c.&nbsp;1386 as "statement of ingredients in a potion or medicine," from {{der|en|xno|-}} or {{der|en|fro-nor|receite|t=receipt, recipe}} (1304), altered (by influence of {{m|fro|receit||he receives}}, from {{cog|la|recipit}}) from {{der|en|fro|recete}}, from {{der|en|la|receptus}}, perfect passive participle of {{m|la|recipiō}}, itself from {{m|la|re-||back}} + {{m|la|capiō||to take}}. The unpronounced ''p'' was later inserted to make the word appear closer to its Latin root.  {{doublet|en|recept|recipe}}. {{false cognate|fa|رسید|tr=resid}} (whence {{noncog|ur|رسید|tr=rasīd}}).
