====Usage notes====
* The spelling {{m|en||theatre}} is the main spelling in British English, with {{m|en||theater}} being rare.
* The spelling {{m|en|theater}} is the predominant American spelling; it accounts for about 80% of usage in [[COCA]] (the major corpus of American English). However, some Americans may use the two spellings to differentiate between the location {{m|en||theater}} and the art-form {{m|en||theatre}}. Alternatively, some people use {{m|en||theatre}} for things relating to live performances (and sometimes films and cinemas) like musical ''theatre'', with {{m|en||theater}} being used for other uses (such as a ''theater of war'').
