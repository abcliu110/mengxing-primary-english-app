{{ety|en|:inh|enm:oystre|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|oystre}}, from {{der|en|ang|ostre}}, reinforced or superseded by {{der|en|xno|oistre}}, which is from {{der|en|fro|oistre}}, {{m|fro|uistre}} (compare modern French {{m|fr|huître}}); both lines (Old English and Old French) from {{der|en|la|ostrea}}, from {{der|en|grc|ὄστρεον}}. {{dbt|en|ostro|t1=a purple dye}}.

===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# Any of certain marine [[bivalve]] [[mollusk]]s, especially those of the family {{taxfmt|Ostreidae|family}} (the true oysters), usually found adhering to rocks or other fixed objects in shallow water along the seacoasts, or in brackish water in the mouth of rivers.
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Merry Wives|act=II|scene=ii|lines=2–5||page=45|passage=''Piſt.'' Why then the world's mine '''Oyſter''', which I, with ſword will open.}}
#* {{RQ:Swift Polite Conversation|page=120|passage=He was a bold man that first ate an '''oyster'''.}}
# The delicate oyster-shaped [[morsel]] of [[dark meat]] contained in a small cavity of the bone on each side of the lower part of the back of a [[fowl]].
#* {{quote-book|en|author=United States Bureau of Naval Personnel|title=Steward 3 & 2|year=1971|publisher=U.S. Government Printing Office|chapter=Food preparation|page=148|pageurl=https://books.google.com/books?id=X4yPs3HHhZoC&dq=%22oyster%22%20dark%20meat&hl=&pg=PA148#v=onepage&q&f=false|passage=Cut remaining skin on back. Remove the '''oyster''' (choice dark meat in spoon-shaped bone on back) with the leg.}}
# Any of the [[fungus|fungi]] in the genera {{taxfmt|Pleurotus|genus}}, {{taxlink|Hohenbuehelia|genus}}, or other related genera.
# A pale beige color tinted with grey or pink, like that of an oyster.
#: {{color panel|DADFB7}}
# {{lb|en|colloquial|by analogy}} A person who keeps [[secret]]s.
#* {{RQ:Dickens Christmas Carol|page=3|passage=Hard and sharp as flint, from which no steel had ever struck out generous fire; secret, and self-contained, and solitary as an '''oyster'''.}} 
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1928|author=Agatha Christie|title=[[s:The Mystery of the Blue Train/Chapter 32|The Mystery of the Blue Train]]|text=I will not be a ​human '''oyster'''. No, I will lay my cards on the table, which is assuredly a thing that '''oysters''' do not do.}}
# {{lb|en|figurative}} Something [[at someone's disposal|at one's disposal]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1959|year_published=1971|author=w:Vance Packard|title=The Status Seekers|publisher=Pocket Books|page=116|pageurl=https://archive.org/details/statusseekers1959pack/page/116/mode/1up|isbn=978-0-671-77115-7|passage=He asked a major Chicago retailer with a large store in the heart of the city about the socioeconomic character of his customers. The man replied proudly that the entire range of economic classes was his '''oyster'''.}}
