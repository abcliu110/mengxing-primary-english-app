===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*weǵ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|waiten}}, from {{der|en|xno|waiter}}, {{m|fro|waitier}} (compare {{cog|fr|guetter}} from {{cog|fro|gaitier}}, {{m|fro|guaitier}}), from {{der|en|frk|*wahtwēn|t=to watch, guard}}, derivative of {{der|en|frk|*wahtu|t=guard, watch}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*wahtwō|t=guard, watch}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*weǵ-|t=to be fresh, cheerful, awake}}. 

Cognate with {{cog|goh|wahtēn||to watch, guard}}, {{cog|nds-de|wachten||to wait}}, {{cog|nl|wachten||to wait, expect}}, {{cog|fr|guetter||to watch out for}}, {{cog|stq|wachtje||to wait}}, {{cog|fy|wachtsje|t=to wait}}, {{cog|frr|wachtjen||to stand, stay put}}. More at {{l|en|watch}}.

In some senses, merged or influenced by {{der|en|enm|waiten}}, {{m|enm|weiten|t=to do good to, lie in wait for, to contrive good or harm on, catch, snare}}, from {{der|en|non|veita|t=to give help to, assist, grant, cause to happen}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*waitijaną|t=to show, guide, advise, direct}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*weyd-|t=to see, know}}.

Largely overtook native {{ncog|enm|biden}}, from {{ncog|ang|bīdan}}, source of {{m|en|bide}}.
