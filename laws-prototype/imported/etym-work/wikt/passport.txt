===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|Q43812}} An [[official]] [[document]] normally used for international journeys, which proves the [[identity]] and [[nationality]] of the person for whom it was [[issue]]d.
#: {{ux|en|You will have to bring your '''passport''' to prove who you are.}}
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=1951-11|author='Pausanias'|title=To Greece by the "Simplon-Orient Express"|journal=Railway Magazine|page=728|text=Meanwhile, the Italian authorities examine '''passports''' and hand-baggage, except in the case of sleeping-car passengers, who are usually left undisturbed.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1956|author=w:Delano Ames|title=Crime out of Mind|chapter=17|url=http://openlibrary.org/works/OL5915292W|text=Dagobert gave him back his '''passport'''. He re-pocketed it indifferently; a talisman which had lost its potency.}}
# {{lb|en|by extension|informal}} Any document that allows [[entry]] or [[passage]].
# {{lb|en|figurative}} Something which enables someone to do or achieve something.
#: {{ux|en|The tenor's voice was his '''passport''' to the international concert circuit.}}
#* {{RQ:Marlowe Jew of Malta|sig=D2|page=30|passage=Thus like the ſad preſaging Rauen that tolls / The ſick mans '''paſſeport''' in her hollovv beake, / And in the ſhadovv of the ſilent night / Doth ſhake contagion from her ſable vvings; / Vex'd and tormented runnes poore ''Barabas'' / VVith fatall curſes tovvards theſe Chriſtians.|footer=Referring to a notional document that releases a person from the world of the living.}}
# An [[identification]] [[document]] used by [[firefighter]]s, collected when they enter a [[dangerous]] area and returned when they leave, to keep track of who is still in the area.
