===Etymology===
{{ety|en|tree=1|:inh|enm:foreyn|:influence<unc>|reign}}
From {{inh|en|enm|foreyn,forein}}, from {{der|en|fro|forain}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*forānus||outsider, [[outlander]]}}, from {{der|en|la|forās||outside, outdoors}} or {{m|la|forīs||outside, outdoors}}.

Displaced native {{ncog|ang|elþēodiġ||foreign}} and now-dialectal {{ncog|en|fremd}}, from {{ncog|ang|fremde||strange, foreign}}.

The silent ''-g-'' added perhaps by analogy with {{m|en|reign}} (compare also {{m|en|sovereign}} which was similarly altered).

==Norwegian Nynorsk==

===Etymology===
Probably related to {{m|nn|føregangar|g=m}}, in which case it originally had root ''-gjeng-'', what does not explain the origin of the diphthong ''-ei-'' or any other vowel used instead of this diphtong in its dialectal forms.

If related to verb {{m|nn|eiga||to own}}, the semantic origin would be hard to explain, as well as the /u/ or /o/ occuring instead of the diphtong in some dialectal forms.

Most likely it has prefix {{m|nn|for-}}, which is common for many of its synonyms, because it occurs '''before''' the real object shows up. The word {{m|nn|eign||a kind of guarding angel}} is attested in Vefsn, but may be a later formation.

Attested so far south as Gravvik and Foldereid (where pronounced as /forːuŋn/). Further south on the coast there is used its synonym {{m|nn|foring|g=m}}, which is probably not etymologycally related to it, but rather to verb {{m|nn|fara}}. Also probably not related to {{m|nn|gjegn|g=n||weird object or animal}} attested in Innherred.
