===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:plate<ety:bor<fro:plate>>}}
From {{inh|en|enm|plate}}, from {{der|en|fro|plate}}, from {{der|en|la-med|plata|}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*plattus|*plat(t)us}}, from {{der|en|grc|πλατύς||broad, flat, wide}}. Compare {{cog|es|plato}}, {{cog|pt|prato}}, {{cog|fr|plat}}, and {{cog|it|piatto}}.

{{sense|foot}} Cockney rhyming slang, from "plates of meat" for "feet".
