===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:karette|tree=1}}
{{inh+|en|enm|karette}} and {{bor|en|frm|carotte}}, both from {{der|en|la|carōta}}, from {{der|en|grc|καρωτόν}}. {{doublet|en|carotte}} and related to {{m|en|caraway}}. Displaced native {{ncog|enm|more}}, from {{ncog|ang|more}}, {{m|ang|moru|t=edible root, parsnip, carrot}}, related to {{ncog|de|Möhre|t=carrot}}.
* Noun sense of "motivational tool" refers to {{m|en|carrot and stick}}.
* Verb sense in felt manufacture refers to the orange colour of drying furs.
