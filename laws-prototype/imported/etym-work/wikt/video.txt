====Usage notes====
* ''Video'' is used in contrast with {{m|en|audio}}, which is sound only. It is also sometimes used in contrast with {{m|en|film}} to describe all other motion picture formats, such as [[videotape]] and [[digital video]]. The words ''video'' and {{m|en|movie}} have some synonymous overlap, but a feature film on the big screen is only a ''movie'' in {{l|en|idiom|idiomatic}} use (not a ''video''), and web-era user-created videos are usually not called ''movies'' except in certain contexts.
* The plural {{m|en|videmus}} is rare and used for humorous effect. It is the first-person plural form of the Latin verb ("we see") in the same way that {{m|la|videō}} is the singular.

==Latin==

===Etymology===
{{root|la|ine-pro|*weyd-}}
{{etymon|la|id=see|:inh|itc-pro:*widēō<id:see>}}
From {{inh|la|itc-pro|*widēō}}, itself from the root {{m|ine-pro|*weyd-|t=to know, see}}. The term may derive from a Proto-Indo-European stative {{m|ine-pro||*wid-éh₁-yeti|t=to see}},<ref name=EDL>{{R:itc:EDL|page=676|head=videō}}</ref> However, the LIV considers the term to be a more recent innovation.<ref>{{R:ine:LIV|pages=666-667|head=*u̯ei̯d-}}</ref> The perfect form {{m|la|vīdī}} may reflect a Proto-Indo-European root aorist {{m|ine-pro||*wéydt}}, although it may also continue the root perfect {{m|ine-pro|*wóyde}}.<ref name=EDL/>
