====Usage notes====
* {{m|en||Inflammable}} is traditionally used to mean "capable of burning" (compare {{m|en|inflame}} meaning "set on fire"); the term {{m|en|flammable}} is a more recent back-formation. Recently, comparison with other words where ''in-'' means "not" (like {{m|en|inedible}} and {{m|en|inaccurate}}) has led to ''inflammable'' being used to mean "not flammable". Where confusion might arise, especially where this may be a safety hazard, one may prefer to use {{m|en|flammable}} or {{m|en|nonflammable}}, respectively.
