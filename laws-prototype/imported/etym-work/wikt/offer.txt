===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*bʰer-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|offer}}, from {{inh|en|ang|offrian||offer or make a sacrifice}} rather than from {{der|en|fro|offre||offer}}, from {{m|fro|offrir||to offer}}, from {{der|en|la|offerō||to present, bring before}}. Compare {{cog|frr|offer||sacrifice, donation, fee}}, {{cog|nl|offer||offering, sacrifice}}, {{cog|de|Opfer||victim, sacrifice}}, {{cog|da|offer||victim, sacrifice}}, {{cog|is|offr||offering}}. See verb below.

===Etymology 2===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*bʰer-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|offren}}, {{m|enm|offrien}}. In the religious senses inherited from {{der|en|ang|offrian||to offer, sacrifice, bring an oblation}}; otherwise from {{der|en|fro|ofrir}}. Both ultimately from {{der|en|la|offerō||to present, bestow, bring before|lit=to bring to}}, from {{der|en|la|ob}} + {{m|la|ferō||bring, carry}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰer-||to carry, bear}}, later reinforced by {{cog|fro|offrir||to offer}}. Cognate with {{cog|ofs|offria||to offer}}, {{cog|odt|offrōn||to offer}}, {{cog|de|opfern||to offer}}, {{cog|non|offra||to offer}}. More at {{l|en|ob-}}, {{l|en|bear}}.
