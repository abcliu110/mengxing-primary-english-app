===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*pekʷ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|kichene}}, {{m|enm|kichen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|cyċen}}, {{m|ang|cyċene}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*kukinā}}, a borrowing from {{der|en|la-lat|cocīna}}, from earlier {{m|la|coquīna|t=kitchen; cuisine}}, from {{m|la|coquō||to cook}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*pekʷ-||to cook, become ripe}}.

In other languages, the cognate term often refers both to the room and the type of cooking. In English, the distinction is generally made via the etymological twins {{m|en|kitchen||room}} (Latin via Germanic) and {{doublet|en|cuisine|t1=type of cooking|notext=1}} (Latin via {{cog|fr|-}}).
