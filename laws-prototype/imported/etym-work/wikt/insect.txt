===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*sek-}}
{{bor+|en|frm|insecte}}, from {{der|en|la|īnsectum}}, from {{m|la|īnsectus|t=cut into, cut up, with a notched or divided body}}, from perfect passive participle of {{m|la|īnsecō||to cut into, to cut up}}, from {{m|la|in-}} + {{m|la|secō||to cut}}, from the notion that the insect's body is "cut into" three sections ([[head]], [[thorax]], [[abdomen]]). {{cal|en|grc|ἔντομον||insect|nocat=1}}, from {{m|grc|ἔντομος||cut into pieces}}.
