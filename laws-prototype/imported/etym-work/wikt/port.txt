===Etymology 1===
[[File:Dubrovnik-port.jpg|right|thumb|The old port of [[Dubrovnik]]]]
{{etymon|en|id=portus|:inh|ang:port<id:portus>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|ang|port}}, borrowed from {{der|en|la|portus||port, harbour}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*pértus||crossing}} (and thus a distant doublet of {{doublet|notext=1|en|ford}}). The directional sense, attested since at least the 1500s, derives from ancient vessels with the steering oar on the right (see etymology of [[starboard]]), which therefore had to moor with their left sides facing the dock or wharf. {{doublet|en|fjard|fjord|firth|ford|Portus}}.
