===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|thus}}, {{m|enm|þus}}, from {{inh|en|ang|þus|t=thus, in this way, as follows, in this manner, to this extent}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*þus|t=so, thus}}, perhaps originally from a variant of the instrumental form of {{l|en|this}}, related to {{cog|ang|þȳs|t=by this, with this}}, {{cog|osx|thius|t=by this, with this}}. Cognate with {{cog|sco|thus|t=thus}}, {{cog|frr|aldoz|thus}}, {{cog|stq|dus}}, {{m|stq|düs|t=thus}}, {{cog|fy|dus|t=thus}}, {{cog|nl|dus|t=thus, so}}, {{cog|gmh|dus|t=thus, as follows}}. 

Compare also {{cog|nl|zus|t=thus, hence}}, {{cog|nds|sus|t=thus, hence}}, {{cog|gmh|sus|t=thus, hence}}, a similar formation from the stem of {{ncog|gmw-pro|*swā|t=so}}.
