====Usage notes====
* The plural {{m|en|yesterdays}} is unusual and often poetic for the recent past, e.g. “all our yesterdays have come back to haunt us”.
* While pronunciations with {{IPAchar|/ˈjɪ-/}} are now dialectal, they were formerly found in the standard language. For example, writer and [[orthoepist]] {{w|Thomas Sheridan (actor)|Thomas Sheridan}} prescribed such a pronunciation in his work.<ref>{{cite-book|author=Thomas Sheridan|title=A Complete Dictionary of the English Language, Both with Regard to Sound and Meaning|volume=2|url= https://books.google.co.nz/books?id=pJoRAAAAIAAJ|year=1790|publisher=C. Dilly}}</ref>
