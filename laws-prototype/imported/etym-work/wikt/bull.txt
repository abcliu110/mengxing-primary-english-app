From {{inh|en|enm|bole,bul,bule}}, from a conflation of {{der|en|ang|bula||bull, steer}} and {{der|en|non|boli}}, both from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*bulô||bull}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰl̥no-}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*bʰel-||to blow, swell up}}. Cognate with {{cog|fy|bolle}}, {{cog|nl|bul}}, {{cog|nds-de|Bull}}, {{cog|de|Bulle}}, {{cog|sv|bulla}}; also {{cog|sga|ball||limb}}, {{cog|la|follis||bellows, leather bag}}, {{cog|sq|bolle||testicles}}, {{cog|grc|φαλλός||penis}}. Of {{sno|en|boy|etym=false}}, (a man or boy), derived from the Philadelphia English pronunciation of ''boy'', which is practically a homophone of ''bull''.
