===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(H)yeh₁-}}
From {{der|en|enm|esy}}, {{m|enm|eesy}}, partly from {{der|en|enm|ese||ease}} + {{m|enm|-y}}, equivalent to {{suffix|en|ease|y}}, and partly from {{der|en|xno|eisé}} from {{der|en|fro|aisié||eased, at ease, at leisure}}, past participle of {{m|fro|aisier||to put at ease}}, from {{m|fro|aise||empty space, elbow room, opportunity}}, of uncertain origin. See {{l|en|ease}}. Merged with {{der|en|enm|ethe}}, {{m|enm|eathe||easy}}, from {{der|en|ang|īeþe}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*auþuz}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₂éwtus}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₂ew-||to enjoy, consume}}. Compare also {{cog|osx|ōþi}}, {{cog|goh|ōdi}}, {{cog|non|auð-|auðr, auð-}}, {{cog|is|auð-|auð}} (adverb), {{m|is|auð-}}, all meaning "easy." More at [[ease]], [[eath]].
