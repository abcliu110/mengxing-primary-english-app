===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:der|la:Genua}}
From ''Gene(s)'' (also spelled ''Jean(s)''), an obsolete English name for the Italian city of [[Genoa]]. Compare {{m+|fr|Gênes}}. In the context of clothing, the term originally denoted a kind of coarse cotton (known more fully as ''Gene(s) [[fustian]]'') that was associated with Genoa. It began to be used for trousers made of such cloth in the 19th century. At this point, the final -''s'' was reanalysed as a plural ending.
