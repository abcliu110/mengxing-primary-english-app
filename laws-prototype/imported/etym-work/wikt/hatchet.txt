===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)kep-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|hachet}}, a borrowing from {{der|en|fro|hachete}}, diminutive of {{m|fro|hache||axe}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*happia}}, from {{der|en|frk|*happjā}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*hapjǭ}}, {{m|gem-pro|*habjǭ|t=knife}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kop-|t=to strike, to beat}}. Cognate with {{cog|goh|happa}}, {{m|goh|heppa}}, {{m|goh|habba|t=reaper, sickle}}, {{cog|de|Hippe|t=billhook}}, {{cog|nl|heep}}, {{m|nl|hiep|t=billhook}}, and {{cog|grc|κοπίς}}. Mostly displaced native {{ncog|ang|handæx}}, whence {{w|Modern English}} {{m|en|hand axe}}.
