===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:servise|tree=1}}

From {{inh|en|enm|servise}}, from {{inh|en|ang|serfis}}, from {{der|en|fro|servise}} ({{cog|fr|service}}), from the verb {{m|fro|servir}}, from {{der|en|la|servitium}} (compare {{cog|pt|serviço}}, {{cog|it|servizio}}, {{cog|nrf|sèrvice}}, {{cog|es|servicio}}), from {{m|la|servus||servant; serf; slave}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro||*ser-wo-s|guardian}}, possibly from {{m|ine-pro|*ser-||watch over, protect}}.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=''n.''{{sup|1}}|noformat=1|id=1059809588}}</ref> Displaced native {{cog|ang|þeġnung}}.
