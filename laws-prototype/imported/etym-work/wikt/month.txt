===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:moneth<alt:mon(e)th>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|moneth|mon(e)th}}, from {{inh|en|ang|mōnaþ}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*mānōþ}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*mēnōþs|t=month}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*mḗh₁n̥s|t=moon, month}}, probably derived from {{m|ine-pro|*meh₁-|t=measure}} with moon-cycles being used to measure time. Related to {{m|en|moon}}. Cognate with {{cog|stq|Mound|t=month}}, {{cog|nl|maand|t=month}}, {{cog|nds-de|Maand|t=month}}, {{cog|de|Monat|t=month}}, {{cog|da|måned|t=month}}, {{cog|sv|månad|t=month}}, {{cog|is|mánuður|t=month}}.

Eclipsed non-native {{ncog|ang|calend|t=month}}, borrowed from {{ncog|la|kalendae|calendae}}.
