===Etymology===
{{bor+|mul|en|ready}}, from the English-language sequence {{l|en|on your marks}}, {{l|en|ready, set, go}}, of which only ''ready'' is used translingually.

===Interjection===
{{head|mul|intj}}

# {{lb|mul|sports}} The command to make {{l|en|ready}}, regardless of language of competitors, used in multiple sports to get contestants to their marks in preparation to start.

==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₂er-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|redy}}, {{m|enm|redi}}, {{m|enm|rædiȝ}}, {{m|enm|iredi}}, {{m|enm|ȝerædi|ȝerǣdi}}, alteration ({{suffix|en||y}}) of earlier {{m|enm|ired|irēd}}, {{m|enm|irede}}, {{m|enm|ȝerad|ȝerād|ready, prepared}}, from {{inh|en|ang|rǣde}}, {{m|ang|ġerǣde}} (also {{m|ang|ġerȳde}}) {{gloss|"prepared, prompt, ready, ready for riding (horse), mounted (on a horse), skilled, simple, easy"}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*garaidijaz}}, {{m|gem-pro|*raidijaz}}, from base {{m|gem-pro|*raidaz||ready}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₂reh₁dʰ-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*h₂reh₁-||to count, put in order, arrange, make comfortable}} and also probably conflated with {{der|en|ine-pro|*reydʰ-||to ride}} in the sense of ''"set to ride, able or fit to go, ready"''. Cognate with {{cog|sco|readie}}, {{m|sco|reddy||ready, prepared}}, {{cog|fy|ree||ready}}, {{cog|nl|gereed||ready}}, {{cog|de|bereit||ready}}, {{cog|da|rede||ready}}, {{cog|sv|redo||ready, fit, prepared}}, {{cog|no|reiug||ready, prepared}}, {{cog|is|greiður||easy, light}}, {{cog|got|𐌲𐌰𐍂𐌰𐌹𐌸𐍃||arranged, ordered}}.
