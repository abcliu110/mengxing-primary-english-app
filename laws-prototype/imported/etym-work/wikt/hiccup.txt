===Etymology===
From earlier {{m|en|hickop}}, alteration of earlier {{m|en|hicket}}, {{m|en|hyckock}}, from {{m|en|hic}} ({{onomatopoeic|en|title=onomatopoeic}}) + {{m|en|-ock|pos=diminutive suffix}}.

Akin to {{cog|sco|hick}}, {{m|sco|hix|t=hiccup}}, {{cog|fy|hik|t=hiccup}}, {{cog|nl|hik|t=hiccup}}, {{cog|gml|hükup}}, {{m|gml|huckup}}, {{m|gml|hueckup}} (modern {{cog|nds|hick|t=hiccup}}), {{cog|de|hicksen|t=to hiccup|pos=verb}}, {{cog|gsw|Hidsgi|t=hiccup}}, {{cog|da|hikke|t=hiccup}}, {{cog|sv|hicka|t=hiccup}}, {{m|sv|hicka|t=to hiccup|pos=verb}}, {{cog|is|hiksti|t=hiccup}}.

Displaced {{m|en|yex}}, from {{noncog|enm|yexen}}, {{m|enm|ȝisken}}, {{m|enm|ȝixen}}, {{m|enm|ȝoxen|t=to hiccup}}, from {{noncog|ang|ġiscian}}, {{m|ang|ġeocsian|t=to hiccup}}.

The double c in the spelling (as opposed to ck) is a remnant of the unetymological [[hiccough]] spelling.
