===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|calm}}, {{m|enm|calme}}, from {{der|en|frm|calme}}, probably from {{der|en|roa-oit|calma}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin. {{m|it|calma|Calma}} may derive from {{der|en|la-lat|cauma||heat of the midday sun}}, from {{der|en|grc|καῦμα||heat, especially of the sun}}, from {{m|grc|καίω||to burn}}, or possibly from {{der|en|la|caleō}}. Compare also {{ncog|lng|*chalm}}, {{m|lng|*chalma|t=frozenness}}, ultimately from {{noncog|gem-pro|*kalmaz|t=frozenness, cold}}.

Displaced native {{ncog|enm|smilte|t=quiet, still, gentle}} from {{ncog|ang|smylte|t=quiet, tranquil, calm, serene}}.

====Usage notes====
Although a place or environment that makes one calm is usually called ''calm'', a ''thing'' that makes one calm (such as a film, a beverage, or someone's voice) would not be called ''calm''; instead, it would be called {{m|en|calming}}.
