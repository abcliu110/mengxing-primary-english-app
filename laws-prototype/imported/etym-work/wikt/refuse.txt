===Etymology 2===
From {{inh|en|enm|refusen}}, from {{der|en|fro|refuser}}, from {{der|en|la-vul|*refūsāre}}, either from a blend of Classical {{der|en|la|refutō|refūtāre}} (whence also {{m|en|refute}}) and {{m|la|recūsō|recūsāre}} (whence also {{m|en|recuse}}) or from {{der|en|la|refūsus}}, the perfect passive participle of {{m|la|refundo|refundere|to pour back|pos=whence also {{m|en|refund}}}}.
