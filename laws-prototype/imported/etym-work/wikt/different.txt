====Usage notes====
* {{sense|not the same}} Depending on dialect, time period, and register, the adjective {{m|en||different|not the same}} may be construed with one of the prepositions {{m|en|from}}, {{m|en|to}}, and {{m|en|than}}, or with the subordinating conjunction {{m|en|than}}. Of these, {{m|en||from}} is more common in formal registers than in informal ones; {{m|en||than}} is more common in the US; and {{m|en||to}} is more common in the UK, in Australia{{,}} and in New Zealand. Style guides often advocate {{m|en||different from}}, by analogy with {{m|en|differ|differ from}} rather than {{m|en||*differ than}} or {{m|en||*differ to}}, and {{lg|proscribe}} {{m|en||different than}} and {{m|en||different to}}.
