===Etymology===
{{calque|en|zh|龍船節}} or {{m|zh|龍舟節}}, an alternative name for {{m|zh|端午節}} from the races traditionally held on that day in southern China, now popularly connected with the legend of townspeople racing to throw [[zongzi]] into the [[Miluo]] River to preserve the body of the [[Chu]] poet and statesman [[:w:Qu Yuan|Qu Yuan]].
