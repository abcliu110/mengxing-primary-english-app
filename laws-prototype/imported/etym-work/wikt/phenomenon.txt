====Usage notes====
* The most common modern spelling of this term is {{m|en||phenomenon}}. Of the [[#Alternative forms|alternative forms listed above]], {{m|en|phaenomenon}} and {{m|en|phænomenon}} are etymologically consistent, retaining the {{lang|grc|αι}} diphthong from the Ancient Greek [[etymon]] {{m|grc|φαινόμενον}} in its Latinized {{m|en||ae}} form; both are now archaic. The form {{m|en|phainomenon}} is a direct transliteration of the Ancient Greek and is likewise archaic. The form spelled with {{m|en||œ}} has no etymological basis. All these alternative spellings are pronounced identically with {{m|en||phenomenon}}.
* The most common plural form is the classical {{m|en|phenomena}}; the Anglicized {{m|en|phenomenons}} is also sometimes used. The plural form {{m|en||phenomena}} is frequently used in the singular, and the singular form is sometimes used in the plural. Arising from this nonstandard use, the [[double plural]]s {{m|en|phenomenas}} and {{m|en|phenomenae}}, as well as a form employing the [[greengrocer's apostrophe|greengrocer’s apostrophe]] — {{m|en|phenomena's|phenomena’s}} — are also seen.
