====Usage notes====
* ''Mom'' can be capitalized when used as a proper noun, but not when used as a common noun (e.g. with a possessive):
*: {{ux|en|I think '''Mom''' likes my new car.}}
*: {{ux|en|I think my '''mom''' will like you.}}
