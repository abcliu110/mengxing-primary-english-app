===Noun===
{{en-noun}}
{{wikidata lexeme|L4713}}

# {{senseid|en|Q7386}}Any of various [[insect]]s in the family {{taxfmt|Formicidae|family}} in the order {{taxfmt|Hymenoptera|order}}, typically living in large [[colony|colonies]] composed almost entirely of [[flightless]] females.
#* {{quote-journal|en|date=2013-07-26|author=[http://www.theguardian.com/profile/nick-miroff Nick Miroff]
|volume=189|issue=7|page=32|magazine=w:The Guardian Weekly
|title=[http://www.theguardian.com/world/2013/jul/23/mexico-insect-cuisine-sustainable-food Mexico gets a taste for eating insects] {{upright|{{...}}}}
|passage=The San Juan market is Mexico City's most famous deli of exotic meats, where an adventurous shopper can hunt down hard-to-find critters {{...}}. But the priciest items in the market aren't the armadillo steaks or even the bluefin tuna. That would be the frozen ''chicatanas'' – giant winged '''ants''' – at around $500 a kilo.}}
