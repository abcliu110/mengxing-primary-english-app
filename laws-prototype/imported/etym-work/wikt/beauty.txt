===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:beaute|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|bewty}}, {{m|enm|bewte}}, {{m|enm|beaute}}, {{m|enm|bealte}}, from {{der|en|xno|-}} and {{der|en|fro|beauté}} (early Old French spelling {{m|fro|biauté}}), from {{der|en|la-vul|*bellitātem|t=beauty}}, from {{der|en|la|bellus|t=beautiful, fair}}; see {{m|en|beau}}. In this sense, mostly displaced native {{m+|ang|fæġernes}}, whence [[Modern English]] {{m|en|fairness}}.
