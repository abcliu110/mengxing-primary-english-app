===Etymology 1===
From {{inh|en|enm|rokki}}, {{m|enm|rokky|t=rocky}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=rokkī|pos=adj|id=MED37796}}</ref> from {{m|enm|rok}}, {{m|enm|rokke|t=rock; a rock or stone; large rock by a coast or in the sea; rocky outcrop on a mountain, cliff; castle, citadel, stronghold}}{{nb...|roc, rock, rocke, roke|otherforms=1}}<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=rok(ke|pos=''n.''(1)|noformat=1|id=MED37789}}</ref> + {{m|enm|-i|pos={{glossary|suffix}} forming {{glossary|adjective}}s}}.<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=-ī̆|pos=suf|id=MED21577}}</ref> {{m|enm||Rok}}, {{m|enm||rokke}} are derived from:

* {{inh|en|ang|*rocc|t=rock}}; and
* {{der|en|xno|roc}}, {{m|xno|roce}}, {{m|xno|roque}}, and {{der|en|fro|roce}}, {{m|fro|roke}}, {{m|fro|roque}}, variants of {{m|fro|roche|t=rock}};

both from {{der|en|la-med|roca}}, {{m|la-med|rocca}}; further etymology uncertain, possibly of {{der|en|cel}} origin. The English word is analysable as {{af|en|rock|-y|pos2=suffix forming adjectives meaning ‘having the quality of’|id2=adjectival}}.<ref>Compare {{R:OED Online|pos=''adj.''{{sup|1}}|noformat=1|id=166790|date=June 2021}}</ref><ref name="Lexico">{{R:Lexico|pos=adj}}</ref>

===Etymology 2===
