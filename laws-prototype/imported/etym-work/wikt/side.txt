{{etymon|en|id=edge|:inh|enm:side|tree=1}}

Cognate with {{cog|stq|Siede|t=side}}, {{cog|fy|side|t=side}}, {{cog|nl|zijde}}, {{m|nl|zij|t=side}}, {{cog|nds-de|Sied|t=side}}, {{cog|de|Seite|t=side}}, {{cog|da,nb,nn|side|t=side}}, {{cog|sv|sida|t=side}}.
