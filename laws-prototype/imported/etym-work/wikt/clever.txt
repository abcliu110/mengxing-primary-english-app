{{also|Clever}}
==English==

===Etymology===
From [[East Anglia]]n dialectal English {{m|en|cliver||expert at seizing}}, from {{inh|en|enm|cliver||tenacious}}.

* perhaps from {{inh|en|ang|*clifer}}, {{m|ang|clibbor||clinging}};
* or perhaps from {{der|en|nl|-}}, {{der|en|nds|-}}, or East/{{der|en|stq|-}} (compare {{m|stq|kluftich|t=clever, prudent}}, probably derived from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*kleuban|t=to cleave, split}});
* or dialectal {{der|en|no|klover||ready, skillful}}, itself borrowed from {{der|en|gml|klever}}, related to {{m|gml|kleven|t=to stick}}, from {{der|en|osx| klibōn}}, from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*klibēn}}, related to the Old English word above;
* possibly influenced by {{cog|ang|clifer||claw, hand}} (compare {{m|ang|clawian|t=to claw}}).<ref>{{R:Etymonline}}</ref>

Related to {{l|en|cleave}}. Perhaps influenced by {{cog|cy|celfydd||talented, dexterous, expert}}.

Compare typologically {{m+|cs|chytrý}}, {{m+|ru|хи́трый}} (akin to {{m|ru|хвата́ть}}), also note {{m|ru|схва́тывать на лету́}}.
