===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|withoute}}, {{m|enm|withouten}}, from {{inh|en|ang|wiþūtan|lit=against the outside of}}. {{surf|en|with-|out}}. Compare {{cog|nl|buiten|t=outside of, without}}, {{cog|da|uden|t=without}}, {{cog|sv|utan|t=without}}, {{cog|no|uten|t=without}}.

Superseded non-native {{ncog|enm|saunz|sauns}}, {{m|enm|saunz|sans|t=without}}, from {{ncog|fro|sans}}, {{m|fro|sanz}}, {{m|fro|senz|t=without}}.

Compare typologically {{m+|sla-pro|*bez||without}} (<+ {{m+|ine-pro|*h₁éǵʰs||out}}).
