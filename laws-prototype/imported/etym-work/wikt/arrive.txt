==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|arriven}}, {{m|enm|ariven}}, from {{der|en|fro|ariver}}, from {{der|en|la-eme|adripo|adrīpāre|t=to land, come ashore}}, derived from {{der|en|la|rīpa|t=shore, river-bank}}. Displaced native {{m|en|oncome}}, {{m|sco|tocome}}.

For the semantic evolution, compare {{cog|ang|ġelandian}}, {{m|ang|ġelendan}}, {{m|ang|lendan|t=to arrive at land; land}} > Middle English {{m|enm|alenden}}, {{m|enm|landen|t=to arrive; arrive at shore; land}}.
