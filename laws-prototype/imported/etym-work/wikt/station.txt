===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*steh₂-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|stacioun}}, borrowed from {{der|en|xno|estation}}, from {{der|en|la|statiōnem}}, accusative of {{m|la|statiō||standing, post, job, position}}, whence also Italian {{m|it|stazione}}. {{doublet|en|stagione}}. Cognate with {{cog|grc|ἵστημι}}, {{m|grc|στάσις}}, {{cog|ang|standan}} (whence {{cog|en|stand}}).

====Usage notes====
With regard to railway stations, in North America one is usually said to be at or in a station. The UK usage "on the station" is not found in North America.
