===Etymology 2===
{{bor+|en|la|aestimātus}}, {{glossary|perfect}} {{glossary|passive}} {{glossary|participle}} of {{m|la|aestimō||to estimate}} (see {{af|en|-ate|nocat=1}}), older form {{m|la|aestumō||to value, rate, esteem}}; from {{der|en|itc-ola|*ais-temos||one who cuts copper}}, meaning one in the Roman Republic who mints money. Compare {{cog|enm|estymatt|t=reputed}} / {{m|enm|estimat}}. See also the {{glossary|doublet}} {{dbt|en|esteem|notext=1}}, as well as {{m|en|aim}}.
