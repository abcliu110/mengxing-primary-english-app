===Etymology===
Partly {{onom|en|title=imitative}}, also influenced by {{m|en|burble}}. Compare {{cog|dum|bobbe||bubble}} > {{cog|nl|bubbel||bubble}}, {{cog|nds|bubbel||bubble}}, {{cog|da|boble||bubble}}, {{cog|sv|bubbla||bubble}}. The word was first used in its economic sense in association with the collapse of the {{w|South Sea Company}} in 1720, based on the metaphor of an inflated soap bubble bursting.
