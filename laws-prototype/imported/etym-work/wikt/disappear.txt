==English==

===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|disapeeren}}, equivalent to {{pre|en|dis|appear}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|fordwīnan}}.

Displaced native {{ncog|ang|cwincan}}, whose causative persists as {{m|en|quench|t=put out (fire)}}.
