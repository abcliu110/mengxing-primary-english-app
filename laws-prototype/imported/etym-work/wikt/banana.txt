===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:bor|pt:banana|es:banana}}
{{bor+|en|pt|banana}} or {{bor|en|es|banana}}, derived from a {{der|en|nic|-}} language spoken in the [[Guinea]] region.<ref>{{R:OED Online|banana|pos=n|id=28257385}}</ref> Specific derivation is {{unc|en|unclear}}. Possible ancestor or cognate languages include {{der|en|wo|banaana}}, {{der|en|emk|banana}}, and {{der|en|vai|ꕒꘌꕯ}} or {{m|vai|ꕒꕌꕯ|tr=ɓaana}},<ref>{{R:MWO}}</ref><ref>{{R:Dictionary.com}}</ref><ref>{{R:Collins}}</ref><ref>{{cite-book|page=144|url=https://archive.org/details/outlinesofgramma00koeluoft|title=Outlines of a Grammar of the Vei Language: Together with a Vei-English Vocabulary|author=S.W. Koelle|publisher=London Church Missionary House|year=1854}}</ref> possibly from {{der|en|ar|بَنَان||fingertip, banana}}.<ref>Oxford Dictionaries citing John Ayto’s The Diner’s Dictionary: Word origins of food and drink [https://web.archive.org/web/20170710073834/http://blog.oxforddictionaries.com/2014/08/origin-banana/]</ref> However, ''Ay Baati Wolof'' (Munro & Gaye, 1997) posits that {{cog|wo|banaana}} is itself derived from {{cog|pt|banana}}.<ref>{{cite-book|en|title=Ay baati Wolof: A Wolof dictionary|author=Munro, Pamela|author2=Gaye, Dieynaba|year=1997|oclc=938011184|url=https://linguistics.ucla.edu/publications/opl_19.pdf|location=Los Angeles|series=
UCLA Occasional Papers in Linguistics|publisher=Department of Linguistics, University of California, Los Angeles|page=15}}</ref>

The racial slur derives from the notion that they are “[[yellow|Yellow]] (East-Asian) on the outside, but [[white|White]] (Westernized) on the inside”.

==Spanish==

===Etymology===
{{etymon|es|:der|nic:-|:bor<unc>|pt:banana}}
Derived from a {{der|es|nic|-}} language spoken in the [[Guinea]] region,<ref>{{R:es:DCECH|volume=I|entry=banana|page=484}}</ref> probably through {{bor|es|pt|banana}}.<ref>{{cite-web|url=https://etimologias.dechile.net/?banana|year=2024|work=DECEL - Diccionario Etimológico Castellano en Línea|title=Etimología de BANANA|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20260616185458/https://etimologias.dechile.net/?banana|archivedate=16 June 2026}}</ref> Further derivation is {{unc|es|unclear}}. Possible ancestor or cognate languages include {{der|es|wo|banaana}}, {{der|es|emk|banana}}, and {{der|es|vai|ꕒꘌꕯ}} or {{m|vai|ꕒꕌꕯ|tr=ɓaana}}.<ref>{{R:Dictionary.com}}</ref><ref>{{cite-book|page=144|url=https://archive.org/details/outlinesofgramma00koeluoft|title=Outlines of a Grammar of the Vei Language: Together with a Vei-English Vocabulary|author=S.W. Koelle|publisher=London Church Missionary House|year=1854}}</ref> However, ''Ay Baati Wolof'' (Munro & Gaye, 1997) posits that {{cog|wo|banaana}} is itself derived from {{cog|pt|banana}}.<ref>{{cite-book|en|title=Ay baati Wolof: A Wolof dictionary|author=Munro, Pamela|author2=Gaye, Dieynaba|year=1997|oclc=938011184|url=https://linguistics.ucla.edu/publications/opl_19.pdf|location=Los Angeles|series=
