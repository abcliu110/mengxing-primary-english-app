===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:supplien|id=provide}}
From {{inh|en|enm|supplien}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|soupleer}}, {{m|fro|souploier}}, from {{der|en|la|suppleo||to fill up, make full, complete, supply}}.
The Middle English spelling was modified to conform to Latin etymology.
