#* {{RQ:Whewell Philosophy|volume=I|chapter=Aphorisms Concerning the Language of Science. Aphorism XVI. In the Composition and Inflexion of Technical Terms, Philological Analogies are to be Preserved if Possible, but Modified According to Scientific Convenience.|para=4(3)|page=cxiii|passage=As we cannot use ''physician'' for a cultivator of physics, I have called him a ''physicist''. Thus we might say, that as an Artist is a Musician, Painter, or Poet, a '''Scientist''' is a Mathematician, Physicist, or Naturalist.}}
