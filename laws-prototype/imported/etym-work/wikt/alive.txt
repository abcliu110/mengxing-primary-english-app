===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:alyve}}
From {{inh|en|enm|alyve}}, {{m|enm|alive}}, {{m|enm|alife}}, {{m|enm|olive}}, {{m|enm|olife}}, {{m|enm|on live}}, {{m|enm|on life}}, from {{inh|en|ang|on līfe|t=alive|lit=in life" or "in (the) body}}, from {{m|ang|on|t=on, in}} + {{m|ang|līfe}}, dative singular of {{m|ang|līf|t=life}}. In this sense, replaced {{noncog|ang|cwic}} (whence {{noncog|en|quick}}). Equivalent to {{af|en|a-|life}}. Compare {{cog|nl|in leven|t=alive|lit=in life}}, {{cog|de|am Leben|t=alive|lit=at life" or "at living}}.

====Usage notes====
* ''Alive'' is almost always used as a [[predicative adjective]]: "The bee is '''alive'''". [[attributive adjective|Attributively]], the adjectives {{m|en|living}} or {{m|en|live}} may be used with a similar meaning. There are occasional exceptions to this, for example: "An '''alive''' man is better than a dead one," though this may be considered [[proscribed]] by some.
