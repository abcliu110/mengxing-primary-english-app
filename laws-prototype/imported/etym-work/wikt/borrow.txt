{{root|en|ine-pro|*bʰergʰ-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|borwen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|borgian||to borrow, lend, pledge surety for}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*borgōn}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*burgōną||to pledge, take care of}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰergʰ-||to take care}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|borgen||to borrow, trust}}, {{cog|de|borgen||to borrow, lend}}, {{cog|da|borge||to vouch}}. Related to {{cog|ang|beorgan||to save, preserve}}. More at {{m|en|bury}}.
