===Noun===
{{en-noun|teeth}}

# {{senseid|en|anatomy}} A hard, [[calcareous]] structure present in the [[mouth]] of many [[vertebrate]] [[animals]], generally used for [[bite|biting]] and [[chew]]ing [[food]].
#: {{syn|en|Thesaurus:tooth}}
#: {{hypo|en|bicuspid|canine|cuspid|incisor|premolar|molar|Thesaurus:tooth}}
#: {{coi|en|'''tooth''' decay}}
# A sharp [[projection]] on the [[blade]] of a [[saw#Noun|saw]], on a [[file#English:_Q193142|file]] or [[rasp#Noun|rasp]], or on a similar [[tool]] or [[implement]].
#: {{hypo|en|sawtooth}}
# A [[projection]] on the edge of a [[gear]] that meshes with similar projections on adjacent gears, or on the [[circumference]] of a [[cog]] that engages with a [[chain]].
# {{lb|en|cordage}} The property of resisting slippage when knotted.
#: {{ux|en|Jute has more '''tooth''' than polypropylene.}}
# {{lb|en|zoology}} A projection or point in other parts of the body resembling the tooth of a vertebrate animal.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1832|author=Constantine Samuel Rafinesque|title=A monograph of the fluviatile bivalve shells of the river Ohio|page=43|text=Species XXXVI. ''Obliquaria bullata''— (''Unio bullata'') {{...}} Found at the falls of Ohio; rare; breadth almost two inches; ''cardinal and lamellar '''teeth''''' like preceding species; ''apices'' rounded, decorticated, but not truncated}}
# {{lb|en|botany}} A pointed projection from the [[margin]] of a [[leaf]].
# {{lb|en|animation}} The rough surface of some kinds of [[cel]] or other [[film]]s that allows better [[adhesion]] of [[artwork]].
# {{lb|en|figurative}} [[liking|Liking]], [[fondness]] (compare [[toothsome]]).
#: {{syn|en|fondness|appetite|taste|palate}}
#: {{ux|en|I have a sweet '''tooth''': I love sugary treats.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1693|author=w:John Dryden|chapter=The Third Satire of Aulus Persius Flaccus|title=The Satires of Decimus Junius Juvenalis
|passage=These are not dishes for thy dainty '''tooth'''}}
# {{lb|en|algebraic geometry}} An [[irreducible]] [[component]] of a [[comb]] that [[intersect]]s the [[handle]] in exactly one point, that point being distinct from the [[unique]] point of intersection for any other tooth of the comb.
