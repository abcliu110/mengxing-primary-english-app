===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*prew-|*leyg-|id2=like}}
From {{der|en|nl|vrolijk||cheerful}}, from {{der|en|dum|vrolijc}}, from {{der|en|odt|frōlīk}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*frawalīkaz}}. Compare {{cog|de|fröhlich||blitheful, gaily, happy, merry}}.

The first element, ultimately from {{der|en|gem-pro|*frawaz}}, is cognate with {{cog|enm|frow||hasty}}; the latter element, ultimately from {{m|gem-pro|*-līkaz}}, is cognate with {{m|en|-ly}}, {{m|en|-like}}.
