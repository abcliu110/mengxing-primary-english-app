===Etymology===
{{dercat|en|ett}}
From {{inh|en|enm|market}}, from late {{inh|en|ang|market||market}} and {{der|en|xno|markiet}} ({{der|en|fro|marchié}}); all ultimately from {{der|en|la|mercātus||trade, market}}, from {{m|la|mercor||to trade, deal in, buy}}, itself derived from {{m|la|merx||wares, merchandise}}. {{doublet|en|mart}}. Cognate with {{cog|fy|merk||market}}, {{cog|nl|markt}}, {{cog|de|Markt}}, {{cog|da|-}} and {{cog|no|marked||market}}, {{cog|fo|-}} and {{cog|is|markaður||market}}, {{cog|sv|marknad||market}}. Displaced native {{noncog|ang|stapol}}.
