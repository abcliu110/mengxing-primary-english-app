==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)ker- (turn)}}
1706, from {{bor|en|fr|crucial}}, a medical term for ligaments of the knee (which cross each other), from Latin {{m|la|crux|crux, crucis|cross}} (English {{m|en|crux}}), from the {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)ker-||to turn, to bend}}.

The meaning “decisive, critical” is extended from a logical term, ''Instantias [[Crucis]]'', adopted by {{w|Francis Bacon}} in his influential ''{{w|Novum Organum}}'' (1620); the notion is of cross fingerboard signposts at forking roads, thus a requirement to choose.<ref>{{cite-book|la|year=1620|title={{w|Novum Organum}}|trans-title=New Organon|author={{w|Francis Bacon}}|volume=Two|section=XXXVI|passage=Inter praerogativas instantiarum, ponemus loco decimo quarto '''Instantias Crucis;''' translato vocabulo a Crucibus, quae erectae in biviis indicant et signant viarum separationes.}}</ref>
