====Usage notes====
* The alternative spaced form {{m|en|every one}} is used when referring separately to every entity in a specified group ("{{m|en||There were three patients and she helped '''every one''' [of them].}}"). In such cases it cannot be replaced with {{m|en|everybody}} without changing the sense.
* The term is {{lg|conjugation|conjugated}} in the {{lg|singular}} ("{{m|en||'''Is''' everyone here?}}"; "{{m|en||Everyone '''has''' heard of it.}})". However, similar to what occurs with {{lg|collective noun|collective nouns}}, such as {{m|en|crowd}} or {{m|en|team}}, the {{lg|grammatical number}} of a {{lg|plural}} {{lg|pronoun}} referring back to it ([[antecedent]]) is reflected in the verb conjugation ("{{m|en||Everyone '''was''' laughing at first, but then '''they''' all stopped.}}").
* In colloquial speech, it is common to say {{m|en||everyone is not X}} instead of {{m|en||not everyone is X}} (both of which may potentially have the intended meaning that {{m|en||most people are not X}}). The same is true of other universal qualifiers, including {{m|en|everybody}}, {{m|en|everything}}{{,}} and {{m|en|all}}.
* The term logically negates to {{m|en||not everybody}} rather than {{m|en||no one}}.
