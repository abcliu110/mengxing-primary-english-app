==English==
{{number box|en|20}}

===Etymology===
From earlier {{m|en|twenteth}}, {{m|en|twentith}}, from {{inh|en|enm|twentithe}}, {{m|enm|twentiþe}}, from {{inh|en|ang|twēntigoþa}}. Equivalent to {{af|en|twenty|-eth}}.

{{col-top|2|Etymological notes}}
{{m|en|twentith}}, {{m|en|twenteth}} is the expected development from {{m+|enm|twentithe}}, but in time, the opaque ''twent-'' was replaced by a more transparent ''twenty-'', resulting in modern {{m|en|twentieth}} (in the speech varieties this occurred in, the weak vowel in the final syllable of {{m|en|twenteth}}, {{m|en|twentith}} was clearly distinct from that in {{m|en|twenty}}); similar processes explain the modern pronunciation of {{m|en|thirtieth}}, {{m|en|fourtieth}}, etc. This analogy was assisted by [[synizetic]] pronunciations of words such as {{m|en|carrying}}, which allowed {{m|en|twenteth}}, {{m|en|twentith}} to be interpreted as contractions of {{m|en|twentieth}}.
{{col-bottom}}
