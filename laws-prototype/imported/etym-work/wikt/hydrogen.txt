===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:bor|fr:hydrogène|tree=1}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|fr|hydrogène|t=hydrogen}}, {{lg|coined}} by the French chemists {{w|Louis-Bernard Guyton de Morveau}} (1737–1816) and {{w|Antoine Lavoisier}} (1743–1794) from {{m|fr|hydro-|pos={{lg|prefix}} meaning ‘water’}} + {{m|fr|-gène|pos={{lg|suffix}} denoting a producer of something}},<ref>Compare {{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=1557804719|date=September 2024|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref> from the fact that water is produced as a compound when hydrogen is oxidized.

* The prefix {{m|fr||hydro-}} is borrowed from {{der|en|grc|ῠ̔δρο-}}, from {{m|grc|ῡ̆̔́δωρ|t=water}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*wed-|t=water}}.
* The suffix {{m|fr||-gène}} is borrowed from {{der|en|grc|-γενής|pos=suffix meaning ‘born in a certain condition or place’}}, from {{m|grc|γένος|t=descendant, offspring; race; etc.}} (ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*ǵenh₁-|t=to beget, produce; to give birth}}) + {{m|grc|-ης|pos=suffix forming some third-{{lg|declension}} {{lg|adjective}}s}}.

{{surf|en|hydro-|pos1=prefix meaning ‘water’|id1=water|-gen|pos2=suffix denoting a producer of something}}.
