===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|parformen}}, {{m|enm|performen||to perform}}, from {{der|en|xno|performer}}, {{m|fro|parfourmer}}, alteration of {{der|en|fro|parfornir}}, {{m|fro|parfurnir||to complete, accomplish, perform}}, from {{m|fro|par-}} + {{m|fro|fornir}}, {{m|fro|furnir||to accomplish, furnish}}, from {{der|en|frk|*frummjan||to accomplish, furnish}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*frumjaną}}, {{m|gem-pro|*framjaną||to further, promote}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*promo-||in front, forth}}, {{m|ine-pro|*per-||forward, out}}. Cognate with {{cog|goh|frummen||to do, execute, accomplish, provide}}, {{cog|osx|frummian||to perform, promote}}, {{cog|ang|fremman||to perform, execute, carry out, accomplish}}. See also [[frame]], [[from]]. Modernly, rather than {{m+|gem-pro|*frumjaną}}, associated with {{m+|la|formō}}, analyzable as {{m|en|per-}} + {{m|en|form}}.
