===Etymology===
From {{der|en|fro|immediat}} ({{cog|fr|immédiat}}), borrowed from {{der|en|la-lat|immediātus||without in-between, moderation}}, from {{der|en|la|in}} + {{m|la|mediātus}}, {{glossary|perfect}} {{glossary|passive}} {{glossary|participle}} of {{m|la|mediō||to halve, to be in the middle}} (see {{af|en|-ate|id1=adjective|pos1=adjective-forming suffix}}), from {{m|la|medius||middle}}. {{surf|en|in-|alt1=im-|mediate}}.
