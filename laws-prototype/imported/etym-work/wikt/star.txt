====Usage notes====
* In traditional use, the planets are stars but the sun is not; in astronomy, the sun is a star but the planets are not (and the Earth is counted among the planets).

==Northern Kurdish==

===Etymology===
Akin to {{cog|ckb|ستار|t=retire, retreat, take refuge}}, originally a descendant of {{inh|kmr|ine-pro|*steh₂-}} but now conflated with {{cog|ar|سِتَار}}, hence the second sense. Proposed to be the name of an ancient deity by some based on the common expression ''"Ya Star!"'' ("O Star!", said to ask God for endurance and strength).

===Noun===
{{kmr-noun|f}}

# [[standing]], the ability to stand or stay at one's place, strength, energy; not worrying or being bored, calmness.
# [[protection]], [[coverage]]

====Synonyms====
* {{sense|the ability to stand, strength of legs, energy}} {{l|kmr|qidûm}}

====Derived terms====
* [[star kirin]] ("to protect")
* [[star lê hatin]] ("to find the strength to, to find the courage to")
* [[stargeh]] ("refuge")
* [[sitirîn]] ("to calm down, to mellow down, to stay put")
