===Etymology===
A {{calque|en|de|Übermensch|nocap=1}}; {{prefix|en|super-|man}}. The German word was introduced by the philosopher {{w|Friedrich Nietzsche}} (1844–1900) in his work ''Also sprach Zarathustra'' (''{{w|Thus Spoke Zarathustra}}'', 1883), and rendered in English as ''superman'' by Irish playwright {{w|George Bernard Shaw}} (1856–1950) in the play ''{{w|Man and Superman}}'' (1903) and by {{w|Thomas Common}} (1850–1919) in his 1909 translation of Nietzsche’s work.

The “person of extraordinary powers” sense was reinforced by the {{w|DC Comics}}’ character [[w:Superman|Superman]], who first appeared in ''[[w:Action Comics 1|Action Comics #1]]'' dated June 1938.
