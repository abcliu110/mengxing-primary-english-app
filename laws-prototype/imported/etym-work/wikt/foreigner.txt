==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:foreyner|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|forner}}, {{m|enm|foreyner}}, {{m|enm|foroner}}, {{m|enm|forenere}}, augmentation of earlier {{m|enm|forein|t=foreigner}}, from the adjective. Equivalent to {{af|en|foreign|-er|id2=occupation}}. Use of the ''-er'' suffix may be due to analogy with {{m+|enm|straunger|t=stranger}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|elþēodiġ}}.
