===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:mevement<ety:bor<fro:movement>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|mevement}}, from {{der|en|fro|movement}} (modern {{cog|fr|mouvement}}), from {{m|fro|movoir}} + {{m|fro|-ment}}; cf. also {{der|en|la-med|movimentum}}, from {{der|en|la|movere||move}}. {{doublet|en|moment|momentum}}. In this sense, displaced native {{ncog|ang|styring}}, which led to {{w|Modern English}} {{m|en|stirring}}.

Morphologically {{suffix|en|move|ment}}.
