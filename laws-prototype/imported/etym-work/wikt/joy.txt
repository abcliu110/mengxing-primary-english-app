===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:joye}}
The noun is from {{inh|en|enm|joye}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|joie}}, from {{der|en|la-lat|gaudia}}, neuter plural (mistaken as feminine singular) of {{der|en|la|gaudium||joy}}, from {{m|la|gaudēre||to be glad, rejoice}}. {{doublet|en|jo|gaudy|t2=Oxford college reunion}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|ġefēa}}.

The interjection is from the noun.

The verb is from {{inh|en|enm|joyen}}, {{m|enm|joȝen}}, {{m|enm|joien}}, from {{der|en|fro|jöir}}, from the Old French noun (see above).
