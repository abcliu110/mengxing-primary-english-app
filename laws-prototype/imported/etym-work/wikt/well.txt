===Etymology 3===
From {{inh|en|enm|wellen}}, from a merger of {{inh|en|ang|weallan}} (intransitive) and {{m|ang|wiellan}} (transitive), both meaning “to boil.” Further from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*wallaną}} and {{m|gem-pro|*wallijaną}}. Doublet of {{m|en|wall}}.

Cognate with {{cog|de|wallen|t=boil, seethe}}, {{cog|da|vælde|t=gush}}, {{cog|nn|vella}} and outside Germanic, with {{cog|sq|valë|t=hot, boiling}}.
