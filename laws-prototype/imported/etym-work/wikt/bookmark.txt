===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|*boke-merk}}, {{m|enm|*boke-merke}} (attested only in the bare form {{m|enm|merk|t=bookmark}}), equivalent to {{com|en|book|mark}}. Cognate with {{cog|da|bogmærke|t=bookmark}}, {{cog|sv|bokmärke|t=bookmark}}, {{cog|no|bokmerke|t=bookmark}}, {{cog|is|bókamerki|t=bookmark}}. Eclipsed non-native {{ncog|ang|æstel|t=bookmark}}, from {{ncog|sga|astal}}, from {{ncog|la|hastula|t=little spear, splint}}.
