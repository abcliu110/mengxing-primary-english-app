====Usage notes====
* Beans and {{l|en|pea|peas}} are sometimes misidentified (confused with one another); they are both legumes (belonging to the family {{taxfmt|Fabaceae|family}}) and seeds. Peas are a type of bean with smaller, round seeds in the pod, in contrast to the oval or kidney-shaped seeds usually referred to as ''beans''. Because both terms are applied to a wide range of different legumes, the distinction is not always clear: {{m|en|garbanzo bean}} is a synonym of {{m|en|chickpea}}.
* The word ''bean'' has referred to a wide class of seeds since [[pre-Columbian]] times (when only the Eurasian types were known to Germanic language speakers), but, after Columbian contact, it was extended to other seeds belonging to the New World genus {{taxfmt|Phaseolus|genus}} ([[runner bean]]s, [[lima bean]]s, and so on; see ''[[w:bean#Terminology|bean § Terminology]]'' for details). The fruits or seeds of some other non-{{taxfmt|Fabaceae|family}} plants (e.g., coffee beans, cocoa beans, vanilla beans, castor beans) are also referred to as beans, because of their resemblance to beans as named in the stricter sense.
