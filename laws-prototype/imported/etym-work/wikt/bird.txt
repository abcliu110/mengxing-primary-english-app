===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=animal|:inh|enm:brid<id:bird>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|bird}}, {{m|enm|brid}}, from {{inh|en|ang|bridd|t=chick, fledgling, chicken}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin (see {{m+|ang|bridd}} for more). Originally from a term used of birds that could not fly (chicks, fledglings, chickens) as opposed to the general Old English term for flying birds, {{m|ang|fugol}} (modern {{m|en|fowl}}). Gradually replaced {{m|en|fowl}} as the most common term starting in the 14th century.

The "booing/jeering" and "vulgar hand gesture" senses derived from the expression “to give the big bird”, as in “to hiss someone like a goose”, dated in the mid‐18th century.
