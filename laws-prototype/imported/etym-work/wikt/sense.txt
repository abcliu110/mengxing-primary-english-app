===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:sense|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|sense}}, from {{der|en|fro|[[sens]], [[sen]], [[san]]|t=sense, perception, direction}}; partly from {{der|en|la|sēnsus|t=sensation, feeling, meaning}}, from {{m|la|sentiō|t=feel, perceive}}; partly of {{der|en|gem}} origin (whence also {{cog|oc|sen}}, {{cog|it|senno}}), from {{der|en|la-vul|*sennus|t=sense, reason, way}}, from {{der|en|frk|*sinn}} {{gl|"reason, judgement, mental faculty, way, direction"; whence also {{cog|nl|zin}}, {{cog|de|Sinn}}, {{cog|sv|sinne}}, {{cog|no|sinn}}}}. Both Latin and Germanic from {{der|en|ine-pro|*sent-|t=to feel}}.
