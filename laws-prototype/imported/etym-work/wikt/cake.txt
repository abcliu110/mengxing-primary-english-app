===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=dessert|:inh|enm:cake<id:dessert>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|cake}}, from {{der|en|non|kaka||cake}} (compare {{cog|no|kake}}, Icelandic/Swedish {{m|sv|kaka}}, Danish {{m|da|kage}}), from {{der|en|gem-pro|*kakǭ}}, of disputed origin. Likely a distant cognate with {{m|en|kaak}}. Perhaps related to {{m|en|cookie}}, {{m|en|kuchen}}, and {{m|en|quiche}}. {{doublet|en|coca|pos1=pastry}}.
