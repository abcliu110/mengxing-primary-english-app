===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=subway|:bor|fr:métro<id:metro>}}
From {{bor|en|fr|métro}}, clipping of {{m|fr|métropolitain}} (from {{der|en|grc|μήτηρ||mother}} + {{m|grc|πόλις||city}}), as in the ''Chemin de fer métropolitain'', an early name for the Paris Métro. This name was a direct translation of "Metropolitan Railway", which was the original name of the earliest part of the [[London Underground]], constructed in 1863.
