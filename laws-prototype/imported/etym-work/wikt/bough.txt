===Etymology===
{{PIE word|en|bʰeh₂ǵʰús}}
[[File:Lago Dobson 08.jpg|thumb|A bough ''(sense 1)'' of a [[pencil pine]] ({{taxlink|Athrotaxis cupressoides|species}}) in {{w|Mount Field National Park}}, [[Tasmania]], [[Australia]].]]

From {{inh|en|enm|bough|t=branch of a bush or tree, especially a main branch; limb of an animal or person; something resembling a branch (such as a plant root or branch of a nerve); (''figuratively'') Christian cross; descendant, offspring}}{{nb...|boe, bog, bogh, boghe, boght, boh, bohu, bohw, boow, bou, boughe, bouh, bouw, bouȝ, bouȝe, bow, bowe, bowȝe, boȝ, boȝe, boȝh, bugh, bught, buh, buȝ|otherforms=1}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=bǒugh|pos=n|id=MED5695}}</ref> from {{inh|en|ang|bōg}}, {{m|ang|bōh|t=tree bough or branch; arm; shoulder}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*bōgu}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*bōguz|t=shoulder; upper arm}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*bʰeh₂ǵʰús|t=arm}}.<ref>Compare {{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=21973|date=June 2021|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref>

The {{IPAchar|[x]}} sound was lost in pronunciation of this word before it was lost in other words, but it was later re-added to the spelling to distinguish the word from [[bow]].

Cognate with {{cog|stq|Bouch}}, {{cog|fy|boech}}, {{cog|nl|boeg}}, {{cog|nds-de|Boog}}, {{cog|de|Bug}}, {{cog|da|bov}}, {{cog|is|bógur}}, and distantly with {{cog|grc|πῆχυς||forearm, cubit, etc.}}. {{doublet|en|bow}} {{gloss|"front of a ship, prow"}}.
