===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:quiete<ety:der<fr:quiet<pos:adjective><ety:der<la:quiētus>>>>|:root|ine-pro:*kʷyeh₁-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|quiete}}, from {{der|en|fro|quiet|pos=adjective}} and {{m|fro|quiete|pos=noun}}, from {{der|en|la|quiētus}}, past participle of {{m|la|quiesco|quiēscere|to keep quiet, rest}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kʷyeh₁-||rest}}. {{doublet|en|coy|quit|quite|quietus}}. Largely displaced native {{noncog|en|still}} in the sense of "with little or no sound".
