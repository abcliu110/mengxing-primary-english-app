===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|cucumer}}, {{m|enm|cucumber}}, from {{der|en|fro|cocombre}}, ultimately from {{der|en|la|cucumis|cucumis, cucumerem}} (possibly through an {{der|en|pro|-}} intermediate). Probably of Pre-Italic {{der|en|qfa-sub}} origin. {{etydate|late 14c.}} Displaced native {{ncog|en|earthapple}} (see which for more), which has since acquired other senses.
