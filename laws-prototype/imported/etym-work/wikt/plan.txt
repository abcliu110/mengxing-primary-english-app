===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|drawing}} A [[drawing]] showing technical details of a [[building]], [[machine]], etc., with unwanted details omitted, and often using symbols rather than detailed drawing to represent [[door]]s, [[valve]]s, etc.
#: {{syn|en|design|scheme|Thesaurus:diagram}}
#: {{ux|en|The '''plans''' for many important buildings were once publicly available.}}
# {{senseid|en|intention}} A set of [[intend]]ed actions, usually mutually related, through which one expects to achieve a [[goal]].
#: {{syn|en|design|scheme|Thesaurus:design}}
#: {{ux|en|He didn't really have a '''plan'''; he had a goal and a habit of control.}}
#* {{quote-song|en|year=1977|album=Down to Love Town|author=w:[[The Originals (group)|The Originals]]|title=Been Decided|text=Sweet darling, don't you know somewhere / In the land where '''plans''' are made / Someone has written a story / Put down the pen and turn the page}}
#* {{quote-song|en|title=Beautiful Boy (Darling Boy)|author=John Lennon|album=Double Fantasy|year=1980|passage=Life is what happens to you / While you're busy making other '''plans'''}}
# {{senseid|en|floor plan}} A two-[[dimension]]al drawing of a building as seen from above with obscuring or irrelevant details such as [[roof]] removed, or of a [[floor]] of a building, revealing the internal layout; as distinct from the [[elevation]].
#: {{syn|en|floor plan}}
#: {{ux|en|Seen in '''plan''', the building had numerous passageways not apparent to visitors.}}
# {{senseid|en|method}} A [[method]]; a way of procedure; a [[custom]].

# {{senseid|en|subscription}} A [[subscription]] to a [[service]].

#: {{ux|en|an Internet '''plan'''}}
