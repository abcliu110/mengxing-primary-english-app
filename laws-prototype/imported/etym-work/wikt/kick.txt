===Etymology 1===
[[File:Boykickingball-slowmotion-tokyo-20160423.webm|thumb|thumbtime=2|A boy kicking a ball (sense 3).]]
From {{inh|en|enm|kyken|t=to strike out with the foot}}, from {{der|en|non|kikna|t=to sink at the knees}} and {{m|non|keikja|t=to bend backwards}} (compare {{cog|non|keikr|t=bent backwards, the belly jutting forward}}), from {{der|en|gem-pro|*kaikaz|t=bent backwards}}, of uncertain origin. Perhaps from {{der|en|gem-pro|*kī-}}, {{m|gem-pro|*kij-|t=to split, dodge, swerve sidewards}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*ǵeyH-|t=to sprout, shoot}}. Compare also {{cog|nl|kijken|t=to look}}, {{cog|gml|kīken|t=to look, watch}}. See {{l|en|keek}}.
