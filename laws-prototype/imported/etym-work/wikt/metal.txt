## {{senseid|en|Q11646938}} Any of a number of chemical [[element]]s in the periodic table that form a [[metallic bond]] with other metal atoms; generally [[shiny]], somewhat [[malleable]] and [[hard]], often a [[conductor]] of heat and [[electricity]].
##* {{RQ:Economist|date=2014-04-21|volume=411|issue=8884
|url=http://www.economist.com/news/science-and-technology/21601233-exploring-link-between-manganese-and-parkinsons-disease-subtle-effects|title=Subtle effects
|passage=Manganism has been known about since the 19th century, when miners exposed to ores containing manganese, a silvery '''metal''', began to totter, slur their speech and behave like someone inebriated.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20190326033045/https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2014/04/26/subtle-effects}}
##* {{RQ:en:CNN|author=Mindy Weisberger|title=Ancient bricks baked when Nebuchadnezzar II was king absorbed a power surge in Earth’s magnetic field|url=https://www.cnn.com/2023/12/27/world/ancient-bricks-magnetic-field-anomaly-scn|date=2023-12-27|passage=Earth is surrounded by a magnetosphere — an invisible bubble of magnetism generated by the powerful churning of molten '''metals''' at Earth’s core.|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20260312005716/https://www.cnn.com/2023/12/27/world/ancient-bricks-magnetic-field-anomaly-scn|archivedate=12 March 2026}}
