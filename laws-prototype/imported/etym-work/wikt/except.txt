===Conjunction===
{{en-head|conj}}

# {{ng|Used to introduce a clause, phrase, verb infinitive, adverb or other non-noun complement forming an exception or qualification to something previously stated.}}
#: {{ux|en|You look a bit like my sister, '''except''' (that) she has longer hair.}}
#: {{ux|en|I never made fun of her '''except''' teasingly.}}
#: {{ux|en|To survive, I did everything '''except''' steal.}}
#: {{ux|en|Come any time '''except''' between ten and twelve.}}
#* {{RQ:Marshall Squire's Daughter|II|passage="I don't want to spoil any comparison you are going to make," said Jim, "but I was at Winchester and New College." ¶ "That will do," said Mackenzie. "I was dragged up at the workhouse school till I was twelve. Then I ran away and sold papers in the streets, and anything else that I could pick up a few coppers by—'''except''' steal.{{nb...}}."}}
#* {{RQ:Travers Cuckoo in the Nest|chapter=2|passage=Mother{{...}}considered that the exclusiveness of Peter's circle was due not to its distinction, but to the fact that it was an inner Babylon of prodigality and whoredom, from which every Kensingtonian held aloof, '''except''' on the conventional tip-and-run excursions in pursuit of shopping, tea and theatres.}}
# {{lb|en|informal}} {{n-g|Loosely, used to introduce a contrastive statement explaining why something wasn't successful, didn't happen, etc.}}
#: {{ux|en|They fired tear gas at us, '''except''' the wind was blowing the wrong way.}}
#: {{ux|en|I almost walked out, '''except''' I remembered the promise I had made.}}
# {{lb|en|archaic}} [[unless|Unless]]; used to introduce a hypothetical case in which an exception may exist.
#* {{RQ:Tyndale NT|Luke|IX|text=And they sayde: We have no moo but five loves and two fisshes, '''except''' we shulde goo and bye meate for all this people.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Macbeth|I|II|131|2|If I ſay ſooth, I muſt report they were / As Cannons ouer-charg'd with double Cracks, / So they doubly redoubled ſtroakes vpon the Foe: / '''Except''' they meant to bathe in reeking Wounds, / Or memorize another ''Golgotha'', / I cannot tell: but I am faint, / My Gaſhes cry for helpe.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1621|author=w:Robert Burton|title=w:The Anatomy of Melancholy|page=106|year_published=2001|location=New York
|passage=Offensive wars, '''except''' the cause be very just, I will not allow of.}}
#* {{RQ:Richardson Clarissa|passage=I am {{...}} not so clear how you will be able to avoid it, '''except''' you assert the independence to which your estate gives you a title.|letter=27}}
