==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*bʰer-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|referren}}, from {{der|en|fro|referer}}, from {{der|en|la|referre}}. The noun (used in journalism) is from the verb. {{doublet|en|relate}}. See also [[infer]], [[collate]] and [[confer]], [[delate]] and [[defer]], as well as [[prelate]] and [[prefer]] among others.
