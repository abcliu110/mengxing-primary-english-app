===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*(s)ker- (turn)}}
{{inh+|en|enm|coroune}}, from {{der|en|xno|corone}}, from {{der|en|la|corōna||crown, wreath}}, from {{der|en|grc|κορώνη}}. {{doublet|en|corona|korona|koruna|krona|króna|krone}}. {{displaced|ang|corenbēag|t=crown}} and {{ncog|enm|kinehelm}}, {{m|enm|kynehelm}}, from {{ncog|ang|cynehelm|t=crown|lit=kinhelm” or “king's helm}}.<br/>
The paper sizes ({{senseno|en|paper size}}) are so called because the papers were originally watermarked with a crown.
