===Noun===
{{en-noun|~}}

# {{senseid|en|Q16917}}A large [[medical]] [[facility]], usually in a building with multiple floors, where seriously ill or injured [[patient]]s are given extensive medical or [[surgical]] treatment.
#: {{ux|en|Luckily an ambulance arrived quickly and he was rushed to '''hospital'''.|q=UK}}
#: {{ux|en|Luckily an ambulance arrived quickly and he was rushed to the '''hospital'''.|q=US}}
