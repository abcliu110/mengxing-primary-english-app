===Etymology 1===
{{wikidata lexeme|L784}}
From {{inh|en|enm|questioun}}, from {{der|en|xno|questiun}}, from {{der|en|fro|question}}, from {{der|en|la|quaestiōnem}}, accusative of {{m|la|quaestiō||a seeking, investigation, inquiry, question}}, from {{m|la|quaerere||to seek, ask, inquire}},<ref name="Cent1911">{{R:Century}}</ref> of uncertain origin, but possibly from {{der|en|itc-pro|*kʷaizeō}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kʷeh₂-||to acquire}}. {{doublet|en|quest}}.

Partially displaced native {{ncog|ang|āscung}}, whence [[asking]]. Compare also {{cog|gml|quēstie|t=questioning; inquiry}}, {{cog|gmh|questje|t=question}}.
