===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|id=country|:bor|pt:China<id:country>|tree=1}}
{{wp}}
{{commonscat}}
[[File:Flag of the People's Republic of China.svg|thumb|240px|Flag of the People's Republic of '''China''']]

[[File:Zh-Territories of Dynasties in China.gif|thumb|right|250px|A reconstruction of the extent of various Chinese realms since the [[Zhou]].]]

From Spanish and {{bor|en|pt|China}}, borrowed from an Indian language in the 16th century (cf. {{m+|sa|चीन|tr=Cīna}} and its descendants). The name likely derives from {{der|en|zh|秦}}, the name of an important state and dynasty in ancient China, ruling in the 3rd century BC.<ref name="Singh"/><ref name="Mayrhofer"/><ref name="Yule"/> Other etymons have been proposed, however, including {{m+|zh|晉|tr=Jìn}}, the name of another important state;<ref name="Zhengzhang"/> {{m|zh|荊|tr=Jīng}}, the name of the southernmost ancient Chinese state;<ref name="Wade"/> and ''Zina'', the endonym of the people of the [[Yelang]] kingdom.<ref name="Wade"/> See "[[w:Names of China|Names of China]]" and "[[w:Chinas|Chinas]]" at [[w:Main Page|Wikipedia]].

Compare {{m+|grc|Σῖναι}} and {{m+|la|Sinae}}, which reflect an earlier transmission of the name.
