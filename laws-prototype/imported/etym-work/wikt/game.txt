===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:game<id:form of entertainment>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|game}}, {{m|enm|gamen}}, {{m|enm|gammen}}, from {{inh|en|ang|gamen||sport, joy, mirth, pastime, game, amusement, pleasure}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*gaman}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*gamaną||amusement, pleasure, game", literally "participation, communion, people together}}, from {{m|gem-pro|*ga-|pos=collective prefix}} + {{m|gem-pro|*mann-||man}}; or alternatively from {{m|gem-pro|*ga-}} + a root from {{der|en|ine-pro|*men-||to think, have in mind}}.

Cognate with {{cog|yol|gaame}}, {{m|yol|gaaume}}, {{m|yol|gaume||game}}, {{cog|ofs|game}}, {{m|ofs|gome|t=joy, amusement, entertainment}}, {{cog|nl|gemelijk|t=cantankerous, crabbed}}, {{cog|gmh|gamen|t=joy, amusement, fun, pleasure}}, {{cog|da|gammen|t=merriment}}, {{cog|is|-}}, {{cog|nn|gaman|t=joy, fun}}, {{cog|sv|gamman|t=mirth, rejoicing, merriment}}. Related to {{l|en|gammon}}, {{l|en|gamble}}.
