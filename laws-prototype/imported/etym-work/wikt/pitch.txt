===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*peyH-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|picche}}, {{m|enm|piche}}, {{m|enm|pich}}, from {{inh|en|ang|piċ}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*pik}}, from {{der|en|la|pix}}. Cognate with {{cog|grc|πίσσα||pitch, tar}}, {{cog|la|pīnus||pine}}. More at {{m|en|pine}}. Cognate with {{cog|stq|Pik|t=pitch, tar}}, {{cog|nl|pek|t=pitch, tar}}, {{cog|nds-de|Pick|t=pitch, tar}}, {{cog|de|Pech|t=pitch, tar}}, {{cog|ca|pega|t=pitch}}, {{cog|es|pegar||to stick, glue}}, {{cog|frp|pouatche||sap from a pine}} and {{cog|fr|poix||sap}}. The adjective is probably back-formed from {{m|en|pitch-black}}, reinterpreting "pitch" as meaning "intense(ly)".
