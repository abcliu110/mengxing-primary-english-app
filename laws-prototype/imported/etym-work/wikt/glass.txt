===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:glas}}
From {{inh|en|enm|glas}}, from {{inh|en|ang|glæs}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*glas}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*glasą}}, possibly related to {{cog|gem-pro|*glōaną||to shine}} (compare {{m|en|glow}}), and ultimately from the {{der|en|ine-pro|-}} root {{m|ine-pro|*ǵʰleh₁-||to shine, shimmer, glow}}.
