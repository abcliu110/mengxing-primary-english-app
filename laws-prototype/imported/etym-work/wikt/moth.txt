====Noun====
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|Q1725788}} Any flying [[insect]] of the order {{taxfmt|Lepidoptera|order}} not in the [[superfamily]] {{taxfmt|Papilionoidea|superfamily}}, most species of which are [[nocturnal]] and can be distinguished from [[butterfly|butterflies]] by [[feather]]-like [[antenna]]e.
