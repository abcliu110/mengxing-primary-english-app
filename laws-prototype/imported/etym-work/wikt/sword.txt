===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{sid|en|Q12791}}{{lb|en|countable}} A long bladed [[weapon]] with a [[grip]] and typically a [[pommel]] and [[crossguard]] (together forming a [[hilt]]), which is designed to [[cut]], [[stab]], slash and/or hack.
#: {{ux|en| He took out his '''sword''' and stabbed the man in the stomach.}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year={{c.|1515–1516}}|year_published=1568|author=w:John Skelton|title=Againſt venemous tongues enpoyſoned with ſclaunder and falſe detractions &c.|passage=Malicious tunges, though they have no bones,<br>Are ſharper then '''ſwordes''', ſturdier then ſtones.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Henry 6-3|2|2|line=59
|passage=[[unsheathe|Unsheathe]] your [[sword]] and [[dub]] him [[presently]].}}
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1786|author=Francis Grose|title=A Treatise on Ancient Armour and Weapons|page=49|passage=Some [[sword]]s were also made [[solely]] to [[thrust]], and some only to [[cut]]; others were [[equally]] [[adapt]]ed for both.}}
# A [[suit]] in certain playing card decks, particularly those used in Spain and Italy, or those used for divination.
## A [[card]] of this suit.
# {{lb|en|weaving}} One of the end bars by which the [[lay]] of a [[hand]] [[loom]] is suspended.
# {{lb|en|figurative}} Violence; military might.
