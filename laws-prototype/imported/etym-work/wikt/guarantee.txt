==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:der|fro:guarantie|tree=1}}
From {{der|en|fro|guarantie}} (perhaps via a later {{der|en|es|garante}}), from the verb {{m|fro|guarantir||to protect, assure, vouch for}}, ultimately from {{der|en|frk|*warjan||to ward off}}. {{doublet|en|guaranty|warranty}}.
