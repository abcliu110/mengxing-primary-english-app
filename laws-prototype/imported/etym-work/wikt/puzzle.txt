===Etymology===
[[File:Set of various puzzles.jpg|thumb|Various puzzles ''({{senseno|en|game}})'' intended as [[pastime#Noun|pastimes]].]]

The {{lg|verb}}, of {{unc|en|nocap=1}} origin, is attested first. Apparently cognate with {{cog|sco|pousle}}, {{m|sco|pouzle}}, {{m|sco|poozle|t=to trifle; poke or potter around aimlessly; search about with uncertainty}}, {{cog|stq|puzelje|t=to work hard and continuously}}, {{cog|fy|peuzelje|t=to trifle, work slowly; eat slowly and daintifully, snack}}, {{cog|nl|peuzelen|t=to perform insignificant work, dawdle; pick at, eat with relish in small pieces, snack}}, {{cog|nds-de|pusseln|t=to tinker, fiddle; trifle}} and {{m|nds-de|pöseln|t=to work hard, toil; to slave away; suffer at work; work slowly and ineffectively}}, {{cog|de|posseln}}, {{m|de|bosseln|t=to perform trivial work, tinker}}, {{cog|da|pusle|t=to busy oneself with light work or chores; to be occupied with a task requiring ingenuity, care, and patience; to tinker}}, {{cog|sv|pyssla|t=to do light work; tinker; putter or potter around}}, {{cog|nn|pusla}}, {{m|nn|putla|t=to potter about}}, {{cog|fo|putla|t=to trifle; potter about; do one's work slowly; be dilatory}}, {{cog|fo|puss|t=damage, trick}}.

* An early form of the word is {{m|en|pusle}}, which is similar to {{inh|en|ang|puslian|t=to pick out the best bits, carefully select, cull}}.<ref>{{R:Anglo-Saxon Dictionary|entry=puslian|page=779|column=2}}</ref> It is possible that the meaning of the word evolved from “to pick out the best bits”, to “to think long and carefully in bewilderment while choosing what to pick out”, to “to think long and carefully in bewilderment”. However, there is no evidence in {{w|Middle English}} or modern English of any intermediate words with these meanings.<ref name="OED">{{R:OED Online|pos=v|id=8603046235|date=June 2025|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=v}}</ref>

* Alternatively, it has been suggested that the word is from {{affix|en|pose|t1=(''obsolete'') to interrogate, question|-le|id2=frequentative|pos2={{lg|frequentative}} {{lg|suffix}}}}. However, the ''{{w|Oxford English Dictionary}}'' notes that early forms of the word are all spelled with ''-u-'', and that a sound change in Middle English from ''ō'' to ''u'' “is not easily accounted for”.<ref name="OED"/>
* Finally, it has been suggested that the {{lg|past}} {{lg|participle}} form of the word is attested by {{noncog|enm|poselet}}.<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=pọ̄selet|pos=ppl|id=MED34122}}</ref> This is thought to be unlikely by the ''Oxford English Dictionary'' as {{m|enm||poselet}} is attested in only one quotation with the meaning “jostled, pushed”, which does not have any connection with the current senses of the word.<ref name="OED"/>

The {{lg|noun}} appears to be derived from the verb.<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=2361968764|date=September 2025|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref><ref>{{R:Online Etymology Dictionary}}</ref>
