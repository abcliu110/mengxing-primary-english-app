===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=stature|:inh|enm:heighte<ety:inh<ang:hīehþu<id:height>>>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|heighte}}, {{m|enm|heiȝþe}}, from {{inh|en|ang|hēahþu}}, {{m|ang|hēhþo}}, {{m|ang|hīehþu|t=height}}, {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*hauhiþu}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*hauhiþō}} (compare {{m|gem-pro|*hauhaz}}). Equivalent to {{af|en|high|-t|id2=th|pos2=abstract nominal suffix}}. The regular pronunciation is now obsolete {{IPAchar|/heɪt/}} (as with other words in {{m|en||-eight}}); the modern form developed early on, at first as a variant, by analogy with the underlying adjective.
