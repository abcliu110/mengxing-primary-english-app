====Usage notes====
* {{U:en:equal}}
* The most common comparative use is the ironic expression {{m|en|more equal}}.
* Assertions that all people are equal have never meant that all people are identical or have the same talents, although they are sometimes either miscomprehended or misrepresented as having that meaning, especially by enemies of civic equality who are attacking a [[straw man#English:_Q912820|straw man]].
