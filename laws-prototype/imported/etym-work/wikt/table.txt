# {{sid|en|Q14748}}{{lb|en|countable}} Furniture with a top surface to accommodate a variety of uses.
#: {{hyper|en|furniture}}
## An item of [[furniture]] with a [[flat]] [[top]] [[surface]] raised above the ground, usually on one or more legs.
##: {{ux|en|Set that dish on the '''table''' over there, please.}}
##* {{RQ:Lincoln Pratt's Patients|chapter=6|passage=He had one hand on the bounce bottle—and he'd never let go of that since he got back to the '''table'''—but he had a handkerchief in the other and was swabbing his deadlights with it.}}
##* {{RQ:Allingham China Governess|chapter=Foreword|passage=A very neat old woman, still in her good outdoor coat and best beehive hat, was sitting at a polished mahogany '''table''' on whose surface there were several scored scratches so deep that a triangular piece of the veneer had come cleanly away,{{nb...}}.}}
## A flat [[tray]] which can be used as a table.
## {{lb|en|by extension}} A supply of food or entertainment.
##: {{ux|en|The baron kept a fine '''table''' and often held large banquets.}}
## {{lb|en|metonym}} A [[booth]] or [[display]] at an event such as an [[exposition]] or fair.
## {{lb|en|Christianity}} A service of [[Holy Communion]].
## The [[board]] or table-like [[furniture]] on which a [[game]] is played, such as [[snooker]], [[billiards]]{{,}} or [[draughts]].
##: {{hypo|en|billiard table|poker table|pool table|shuffleboard table|snooker table|[[table tennis table]] ([[ping pong table]])}}
## {{lb|en|backgammon}} One [[half]] of a [[backgammon]] [[board]], which is divided into an inner and outer table.
### {{lb|en|plural|mainly|historical}} The game of [[backgammon]], or any of several related games.
# A group of people at a table, for example, for a meal, meeting or game; a meeting at a table.
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Hamlet|V|i|278|1|Alas poore ''Yorick'' {{...}} VVhere be your Jibes now? Your Gambals? Your Songs? Your flaſhes of Merriment that were wont to ſet the '''Table''' on a Rore?}}
#* {{RQ:Churchill Celebrity|chapter=8|passage=The humor of my proposition appealed more strongly to Miss Trevor than I had looked for, and from that time forward she became her old self again;{{...}}. Our '''table''' in the dining-room became again the abode of scintillating wit and caustic repartee, Farrar bracing up to his old standard, and the demand for seats in the vicinity rose to an animated competition.}}
## {{lb|en|uncountable}} The act of assembling to eat; a gathering to eat, especially at a restaurant.
##: {{ux|en|Time to come to '''table'''.}}
##: {{ux|en|Join us at the '''table''' I booked at the restaurant.}}
## {{lb|en|poker|metonym}} The [[lineup]] of players at a given table.
##: {{ux|en|That's the strongest '''table''' I've ever seen at a European Poker Tour event.}}
## {{lb|en|RPG|metonym}} A group of players meeting regularly to play a [[campaign]].
## {{lb|en|waitstaff|metonym}} A group of diners at a given table or tables.
##: {{ux|en|'''Table''' 9 wants another round of beers.}}
##: {{ux|en|John always gets the best tips because he gets the best '''tables'''! It's not fair!}}
## {{lb|en|figurative}} A session or sessions of negotiation or diplomacy; an opportunity or place for negotiations.
##: {{co|en|[[under the table]], [[on the table]], [[turn the tables]]}}
##: {{ux|en|We need to send someone to represent us at the bargaining '''table'''.}}
##: {{ux|en|Some real breakthroughs were made at the summit, but only far from the official diplomatic '''table'''.}}
##: {{ux|en|He has remarkable skills and expertise to bring to the negotiating '''table'''.}}
# A two-dimensional [[presentation]] of data; an [[enumeration]] of data or contents.
#: {{co|en|[[table of contents]], [[tide table]]}}
## A [[matrix]] or [[grid]] of [[data]] arranged in [[row]]s and [[column]]s.
##: {{hyper|en|array}}
##: {{syn|en|grid|vector}}

## A collection of [[arithmetic]] calculations arranged in a table, such as [[multiplication]]s in a [[multiplication table]].
##: {{ux|en|The children were practising multiplication '''tables'''.}}
##: {{ux|en|Don’t you know your '''tables'''?}}
##: {{ux|en|Here is a '''table''' of natural logarithms.}}
## {{lb|en|computing|chiefly|databases}} A [[lookup table]], most often a set of [[vector]]s.
## {{lb|en|sports}} A visual representation of a classification of teams or individuals based on their success over a predetermined period.
##* {{quote-journal|en|date=April 10, 2011|author=Alistair Magowan|work=BBC Sport
|title=[http://news.bbc.co.uk/sport2/hi/football/eng_prem/9446263.stm Aston Villa 1-0 Newcastle]

# Any of various flat surfaces or items with a planar surface.
#: {{hypo|en|water table}}
## A [[tableland]]; a flat, elevated region.
## {{lb|en|geology}} A horizontal [[stratum]] of [[rock]].
## {{lb|en|anatomy}} A flat length of [[bone]].
### The inner and outer plates that make up the [[cranium]] bones; the [[outer table]] and the [[inner table]] ([[vitreous table]]).
## {{lb|en|equestrian}} A [[wide]], [[flat]] [[obstacle]] for a [[horse]] to jump over.
## {{lb|en|architecture}} A [[stringcourse]]; a band of horizontal molding or other decoration around a building.
## {{lb|en|musical instruments}} The top of a stringed instrument, particularly a member of the [[violin]] family: the side of the instrument against which the strings vibrate.
## {{lb|en|jewelry}} The [[flat]] [[topmost]] [[facet]] of a [[cut]] [[diamond]].
## A [[flat]] [[gravestone]] supported on [[pillar]]s.
## {{lb|en|palmistry}} The [[quadrangle]], a space between the [[heart line]] and [[head line]].
## {{lb|en|obsolete|biblical}} A [[writing]] [[tablet]].
## {{lb|en|printing}} A flat surface that supports the slab on which [[ink]] is rolled to manipulate its consistency.
