====Usage notes====
The {{glossary|loose|loosest}} {{taxfmt|Rubus|genus}}-referent sense ({{senseno|en|any Rubus berry that is black or blackish; its plant}}) is used only informally, by speakers who are botanical and horticultural [[layperson]]s. This sense views ''{{w|Rubus fruticosus}}'', ''{{w|Rubus occidentalis}}''{{,}} and ''{{w|Rubus leucodermis}}'' as merely different kinds of blackberry.
