tun (Wiktionary, 2026-11-02 补抓 https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/tun ，English Etymology 1)
From Middle English tunne, tonne ("cask, barrel"), from Old English tunne ("tun, cask, barrel"), from Proto-West Germanic *tunnā ("tun, barrel, cask"), from Latin tunna, probably of Gaulish origin.
Cognate with Dutch ton, German Tonne, Danish tønde, Swedish tunna, Icelandic tunna. Compare also Old French tonne, French tonneau, Medieval Latin tunna. It is uncertain whether the Germanic or the Celtic forms are the original.
