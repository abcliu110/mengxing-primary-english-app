===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*teh₂g-|id=touch}}
Shortened from {{m|en|taximeter cab}}, {{m|en|taximeter||automatic meter that records distance and fare}} from {{der|en|fr|taximètre}}, from {{der|en|de|Taxameter}} (whence also {{cog|en|taxameter}}), coined from {{der|en|la-med|taxa||tax, charge}}.<ref>{{R:Etymonline}}</ref> More at {{m|en|tax}}, {{m|en|task}}.

The aviation sense originally derived from a slang term for training aircraft used for practicing ground operations, which were said to drive around the airfield like a taxicab, and subsequently applied to all aircraft ground movements.
