===Etymology 1===
{{ety|en|:der|enm:trippen<t:tread or atep lightly and nimbly, skip, dance>|id=journey|tree=+}}
From {{der|en|enm|trippen||tread or step lightly and nimbly, skip, dance}}, perhaps from {{der|en|fro|triper||to hop or dance around, strike with the feet}}, from a {{der|en|frk|-}} source; or alternatively from {{der|en|dum|trippen|t=to skip, trip, hop, stamp, trample}} (> Modern {{cog|nl|trippelen||to toddle, patter, trip}}). Akin to {{cog|gml|trippen}} ( > {{cog|da|trippe||to trip}}, {{cog|sv|trippa||to mince, trip}}), {{cog|fy|tripje||to toddle, trip}},  {{cog|de|trippeln||to scurry}}, {{cog|ang|treppan||to trample, tread}}. Related also to {{l|en|trap}}, {{l|en|tramp}}.
