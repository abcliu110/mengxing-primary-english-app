# {{lb|en|psychology|countable}} A [[distortion]] of [[sensory]] [[perception]] where [[real]] [[stimulus|stimuli]] [[lead]] to a [[false]] or [[misleading]] [[impression]] of [[reality]].
#: {{ux|en|We saw what looked like a tiger among the trees, but it was an '''illusion''' caused by the shadows of the branches.}}

#: {{ux|en|Jane has this '''illusion''' that John is in love with her.}}
#* {{quote-song|en|year=1967|album=w:[[Reach Out (Four Tops album)|Reach Out]]|author=w:Four Tops|title=w:Reach Out I'll Be There|text=Now if you feel that you can't go on / Because all of your hope is gone / And your life is filled with much confusion / Until happiness is just an '''illusion'''}}
# {{lb|en|countable}} A [[magician]]’s trick.
# {{lb|en|uncountable}} The [[state]] of being [[deceive]]d or [[misled]].
