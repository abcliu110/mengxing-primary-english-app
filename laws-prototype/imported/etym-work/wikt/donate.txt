==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*deh₃-}}
Circa 1845, back-formation from {{m|en|donation}} on the basis of {{l|en|-ate|id=verb|pos=verb-forming suffix}}, chiefly found in {{w|American English}}. Ultimately from {{der|en|la|dono|dōnāre|to give}}.

===Pronunciation===
* {{IPA|en|/də(ʊ)ˈneɪt/|a=UK}}
* {{IPA|en|/ˈdoʊˌneɪt/|/doʊˈneɪt/|a=US}}
** {{audio|en|en-us-donate.ogg|a=US}}
* {{rhymes|en|eɪt|s=2}}

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# {{lb|en|ambitransitive}} To make a [[donation]]; to [[give away]] something of value to support or contribute towards a cause or for the benefit of another.
#: {{ux|en|She '''donates''' 100 dollars to Red Cross every year.}}
#: {{ux|en|He '''donated''' an etching from his own collection to the new art gallery.}}
