====Usage notes====
* The {{ipachar|/ˈkɹeɪ̯.ɑn/}} (in the US CRAY-ahn, in dialects with the {{w|cot-caught merger|''cot''-''caught'' merger}} also CRAY-awn) and {{ipachar|/ˈkɹeɪ̯.ɔn/}} (in the US CRAY-awn) pronunciations are used by 83% of Americans. The monosyllabic {{ipachar|/kɹæn/ [kɹɛə̯n ~ kɹeə̯n]}} (cran) pronunciation is used by 14%. 1.46% pronounce it the same way as "crown" ({{ipachar|/kɹaʊ̯n/}}), and 1.24% use other pronunciations (one of them {{ipachar|/ˈkɹeɪ̯.ən/}} CRAY-uhn).<ref name="Harvard Dialect Survey"/>
