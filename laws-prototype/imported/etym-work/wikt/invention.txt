#* {{quote-journal|en|year=2013|month=September-October|author=w:Henry Petroski|journal=w:American Scientist|title=The Evolution of Eyeglasses|url=http://www.americanscientist.org/issues/pub/2013/5/the-evolution-of-eyeglasses|passage=Digging deeper, the '''invention''' of eyeglasses is an elaboration of the more fundamental development of optics technology.
