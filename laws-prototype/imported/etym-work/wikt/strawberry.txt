===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=red fruit|:inh|enm:strawberie<id:strawberry>|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|strawberie}}, {{m|enm|strawbery}}, from {{inh|en|ang|strēawberġe}}, corresponding to {{compound|en|straw|berry}}.<ref>{{R:Online Etymology Dictionary|pos=n}}</ref>

Of various theories advanced to explain the name, the two most plausible are:<ref>{{cite-journal|last1=Fridell|first1=Staffan|last2=Svanberg|first2=Ingvar
|year=2024|title=On the etymology of ''strawberry''|journal=Studia Neophilologica|volume=96|issue=2|pages=303–310
|url=https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/00393274.2024.2329185}}</ref><br/ >
1. the fact that wild strawberries grow on straw-like [[runner]]s (or [[stolon]]s) (compare {{cog|nn|stråbær}}, denoting {{w|cranberries}}, which grow in a similar way);<br/ >
2. the practice, still common in parts of Europe, of gathering strawberries by [[string]]ing them on a straw or [[stalk]].
