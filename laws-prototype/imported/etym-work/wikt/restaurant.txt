Borrowed from {{bor|en|fr|restaurant}}, present participle of the verb {{m|fr|restaurer}}, corresponding to {{der|en|la|restaurans|restaurans, restaurantis}}, present participle of {{m|la|restauro||to restore}}, from the name of the 'restorative' soup served in the first establishments.

====Usage notes====
* Contrary to English, in Dutch this word is not used to refer to fast food establishments, which are instead referred to with terms such as {{m|nl|snackbar}} (or non-generic terminology referring to the type of fast food served, such as {{m|nl|dönerzaak}}, {{m|nl|pizzeria}}, and so forth). 
* In Belgium, the plural form ''restauranten'' is common and co-exists with the plural form ''restaurants''. In the Netherlands, the plural form ''restauranten'' is rare.
