===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|houswyf}}, {{m|enm|housewif}}, {{m|enm|huswijf}}, equivalent to {{af|en|house|wife}}; a {{doublet|en|nocap=1}} of {{m|en|hussy}}, which it was long distinguished from and displaced due to gradual negative connotation (see [https://www.etymonline.com/word/housewife ''Online Etymology Dictionary'' entry]). Cognate with {{cog|fy|húswiif}}, {{cog|nl|huiswijf}}, {{cog|nds-de|Huuswief}}, {{cog|de|Hausweib}}.
