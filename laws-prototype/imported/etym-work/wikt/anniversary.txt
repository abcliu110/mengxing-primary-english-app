====Usage notes====
* Despite the loose usage, phrases with an ordinal number preceding {{m|en||anniversary}} are typically understood as being in years. For example, {{m|en||4th anniversary}} or {{m|en||fourth anniversary}} is understood as being four years after the event being remembered.
