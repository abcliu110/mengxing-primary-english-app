===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*med-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|moderat|alt=moderat(e)||moderate, temperate}}, {{bor+|en|la|moderātus|nocap=1}}, {{glossary|perfect}} {{glossary|active}} {{glossary|participle}} of {{m|la|moderor||to regulate, to restrain, to moderate}} (see {{af|en|-ate|id1=adjective|pos1=adjective-forming suffix}}), from {{m|la||moder-}}, {{m|la||modes-}}, a stem appearing also in {{m|la|modestus||moderate, discreet, modest}}, from {{m|la|modus||a measure}}; see [[mode]] and [[modest]]. {{doublet|en|moderato}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|ġemetlīċ||moderate}} and {{m|ang|metegian||to moderate}}. Cognate with {{cog|fr|modéré}}.
