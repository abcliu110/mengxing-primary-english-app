===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*gʷem-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|inventen}}, borrowed from {{der|en|fro|inventer}}, from {{der|en|la|inventus}}, perfect passive participle of {{m|la|inveniō||come upon, meet with, find, discover}}, from {{m|la|in||in, on}} + {{m|la|veniō||come}}; see [[venture]]. Compare [[advent]], [[covent]], [[event]], [[prevent]], etc.

Displaced native {{ncog|ang|āþenċan|lit=to think out}}.

===Verb===
{{en-verb}}

# To design a new process or mechanism.
#: {{syn|en|conceive}}
#: {{usex|en|After weeks of hard work, I '''invented''' a new way to alphabetize matchbooks.}}
#* {{RQ:Marlowe Tamburlaine|part=1|scene=iv|page=34|passage=Accurſt be he that firſt '''inuented''' war}}
# To create something fictional for a particular purpose.
#: {{syn|en|make up|manufacture|spin}}
#: {{usex|en|I knew I had to '''invent''' an excuse, and quickly.}}
#: {{usex|en|We need a name to put in this form, so let's just '''invent''' one.}}
# {{lb|en|obsolete}} To [[come upon]]; to find; to discover.
