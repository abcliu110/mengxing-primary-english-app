===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{senseid|en|small light vehicle with runners and no draft animal}} A small, light vehicle with {{ll|en|runner|id=mechanical part}}s, used recreationally, mostly by children, for sliding down snow-covered hills; no [[draft animal]] pulls it.
#: {{hypo|en|bobsled}}
#: {{ux|en|The child zoomed down the hill on his '''sled'''.}}
# {{senseid|en|vehicle with runners, often with draft animals}} {{lb|en|chiefly|North America}} A [[vehicle]] on runners, used for conveying loads over the snow or ice, and often pulled by [[sled dog]]s.
#: {{hypo|en|dog sled|horse sled}}
#: {{cot|en|sleigh<qq:generally larger>}}
#: {{ux|en|"Mush!" he yelled at the dogs pulling the '''sled'''.}}
# {{senseid|en|snowmobile, informally}} {{lb|en|slang|chiefly|North America}} A [[snowmobile]].
#: {{ux|en|We saw them go by on their '''sleds''' this afternoon, headed toward their grandfather's quarter section.}}
# {{senseid|en|car or truck, informally}} {{lb|en|slang|chiefly|North America|figuratively}} A [[car]] ([[automobile]]) or [[truck]], usually called so with the [[implication]] of [[sledlike]] traits: [[heavy]], low-slung, and prone to going [[fast#English:_quickly|fast]] but not [[brake#Verb|braking]] or [[corner#Verb|cornering]] particularly well.
#: {{nearsyn|en|land yacht|boat<id:large and heavy automobile>}}
#: {{ux|en|He was a bit reckless and frankly we were nervous about riding with him in that big old '''sled''' of his, a Cadillac with a V-8.}}
#: {{ux|en|That '''sled''' had manual drum brakes on all four corners.}}
# A piece of [[exercise]] [[equipment]], used in [[American football]] [[training]] etc., that provides [[resistance]] as it is moved over a flat surface.
