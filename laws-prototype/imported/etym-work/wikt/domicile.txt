===Noun===
{{en-noun}}

# {{lb|en|formal}} A [[home]] or [[residence]].
#: {{ux|en|The call to jury duty was sent to my legal '''domicile'''; too bad I was on vacation at the time.}}
# {{lb|en|legal}} A residence at a particular place accompanied with an intention to remain there for an unlimited time; a residence accepted as a final abode.
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1838|title=Reports of Cases Decided in the Supreme Courts of Scotland|passage=the status of marriage has been indelibly fixed by the English celebration; and by this decision, her '''domicile''', as a married woman, has been held to be that of her husband}}
# {{lb|en|astrology}} The [[zodiac sign]] over which a [[planet]] (a term which in astrology includes the Sun and Moon) is considered to have especially strong influence; the planet is called the sign's [[ruling planet]] or [[sign ruler]].
