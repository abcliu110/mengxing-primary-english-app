===Noun===
{{en-noun|-}}

# {{senseid|en|Q3930}} A game or sport (similar to [[tennis]]) that involves the hitting of a light plastic [[ball]] with [[racket]]s (called [[paddle]]s, or [[bat]]s in Britain) across a [[table]] fashioned like a [[miniature]] [[tennis court]].
