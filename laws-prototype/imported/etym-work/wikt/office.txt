===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₃ep-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|office}}, from {{der|en|fro|office}}, from {{der|en|la|officium||[[personal]], [[official]], or [[moral]] [[duty]]; [[official]] [[position]]; [[function]]; [[ceremony]], ''[[especially|esp.]]'' [[last rites]]}}, contracted from {{m|la|opificium||[[construction]]: the [[act]] of [[building]] or the [[thing]] [[built]]}},<ref>''Oxford English Dictionary''. "office, ''n.''" Oxford University Press (Oxford), 2004.</ref> from {{m|la|opifex||[[doer]] of [[work]], [[craftsman]]}} + {{m|la|-ium||[[-y]]|pos=forming actions}},<ref>''Oxford English Dictionary''. "†&nbsp;opifice, ''n.''"</ref> from {{m|la|opus|op-|work}} + {{m|la|-i-|pos=connective}} + {{m|la|-fex|pos=combining form of {{m|la|faciō||to do, to make}}}}.<ref>''Oxford English Dictionary''. "opifex, ''n.''"</ref>

In the religious sense, Displaced native {{noncog|ang|hād}}.

The computing sense is a {{gentrade|en|title=genericization}} of various proprietary program suites, such as {{w|Microsoft Office}}.
