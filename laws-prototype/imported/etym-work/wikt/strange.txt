===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:straunge|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|straunge}}, {{m|enm|strange}}, {{m|enm|stronge}}, from {{der|en|fro|estrange}}, from {{der|en|la|extrāneus|t=that which is on the outside}}. {{doublet|en|extraneous|estrange}}. Cognate with {{cog|fr|étrange||strange, foreign}} and {{cog|es|extraño||strange, foreign}}. Largely displaced native {{m|en|fremd}}, {{m|en|selcouth}}, and {{m|en|uncouth}}, from {{ncog|ang|fremede}}, {{m|ang|seldcūþ}}, and {{m|ang|uncūþ}}.
