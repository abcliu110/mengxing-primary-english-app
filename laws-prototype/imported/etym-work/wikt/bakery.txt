==English==

===Etymology===
From {{af|en|bake|-ery<t:place of>}}. Replaced earlier {{m|en|bakehouse}}. Originally "place for making bread"; as "shop where baked goods are sold", it was believed by British travelers by 1832 to be an [[American]]-invented term.<ref>{{R:Online Etymology Dictionary}}</ref>
