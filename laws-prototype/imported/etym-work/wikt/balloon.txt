===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:bor|it:pallone|frm:ballon<unc><ety:bor<it:pallone>>|tree=1}}
First use appears c. 1591, "a game played with a large, inflated leather ball" (possibly via {{der|en|frm|ballon}}) from {{der|en|it|pallone||large ball}} from {{m|it|palla||ball}}, from {{der|en|lng|*palla}}. The Northern Italian form, {{m|it|balla||ball-shaped bundle}}, today a doublet, likely derived from {{der|en|fro|balle}}, from {{der|en|frk|*ballu|*balla|t=ball}}, and may have influenced the spelling of this word. Both Germanic words are from {{der|en|gem-pro|*ballô||ball}}, {{m|gem-pro|*balluz}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰoln-||bubble}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*bʰel-||to blow, swell, inflate}}. Akin to [[Old High German]] {{m|goh|ballo}}, {{m|goh|bal||ball}}, ({{cog|de|Ballen||bale}}; {{m|de|Ball}} "ball"). {{doublet|en|ballon}}. More at {{m|en|ball}}.
