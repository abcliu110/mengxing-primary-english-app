===Etymology===
{{etydate|r|1425|1475}}, in {{w|Middle English}}; from {{inh|en|enm|illiterat|illiterat(e)|uneducated, ignorant of Latin}}, borrowed from {{der|en|la|illīterātus}}, {{m|la|illitterātus||unlearned, ignorant}}, itself from {{m|la|in-||un-}} + {{m|la|līterātus}}, {{m|la|litterātus||furnished with letters}} (see {{af|en|-ate|id1=adjective|pos1=adjective-forming suffix}}), from {{m|la|lītera}}, {{m|la|littera||letter, character}}. The {{glossary|noun}} was derived from the {{glossary|adjective}} by {{glossary|substantivization}}, see {{af|en|-ate|id1=substantive|pos1=noun-forming suffix}}.
