===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*perh₃-}}
{{ety|en|id=noun|:inh|enm:part|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|part}}, from {{inh|en|ang|part||part}} and {{der|en|fro|part||part}}; both from {{der|en|la|partem}}, accusative of {{m|la|pars||piece, portion, share, side, party, faction, role, character, lot, fate, task, lesson, part, member}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*par-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*per-||to sell, exchange}}. Akin to {{cog|la|portiō||a portion, part}}, {{m|la|parō|parāre|to make ready, prepare}}. {{displaced|enm|del}}, {{m|enm|dele||part}} (from {{noncog|ang|dǣl||part, distribution}} > Modern English {{m|en|deal||portion; amount}}), {{noncog|enm|dale}}, {{m|enm|dole||part, portion}} (from {{noncog|ang|dāl||portion}} > Modern English {{m|en|dole}}), {{noncog|enm|sliver||part, portion}} (from {{noncog|enm|sliven||to cut, cleave}}, from {{noncog|ang|tōslifan|(tō)slifan|to split}}).
