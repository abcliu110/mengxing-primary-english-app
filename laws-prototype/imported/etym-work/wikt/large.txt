==English==

===Etymology===
From {{der|en|enm|large}}, from {{der|en|fro|large}}, from {{der|en|la|larga}}, feminine of {{m|la|largus||abundant, plentiful, copious, large, much}}, of uncertain ultimate origin; see there for more. Along with {{m|en|big}}, it mostly displaced {{cog|enm|stour|stoor}} and {{m|enm|muchel}} (the latter surviving in {{m|en|much}} under a different meaning).
