Cognate with {{cog|nl|worgen}}, {{m|nl|wurgen}}, {{cog|de|würgen}}. Compare {{cog|la|urgeo|urgere|t=to press, push}}, {{cog|sa|वृहति||to tear out, pluck}}, {{cog|lt|ver̃žti|t=to string; squeeze}}, Russian (poetic) {{m|ru|отверза́ть|t=to open|lit=to untie}}. Related to {{l|en|wring}}.
