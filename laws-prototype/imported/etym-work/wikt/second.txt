====Usage notes====
For added “precision and elegance”, the [[French Academy]] says it is possible to use {{m|fr||second}} when only two items are being considered, reserving {{m|fr|deuxième}} for other situations, i.e. when more than two items are being considered;<ref name="FA">{{cite-web|fr|url=https://www.academie-francaise.fr/second-deuxieme|title=Second / Deuxième|date=2012-01-05|work=Académie française|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20261008190528/https://www.academie-francaise.fr/second-deuxieme|archivedate=8 October 2026}}</ref> although this rule is not mandatory.<ref name="FA"/> The Academy however advises against ever replacing ''second'' with ''deuxième'' in fixed idioms such as {{m|fr|de seconde main}} or {{m|fr|seconde nature}}.<ref name="FA"/>
