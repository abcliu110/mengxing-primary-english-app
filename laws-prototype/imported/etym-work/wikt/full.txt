{{root|en|ine-pro|*pleh₁-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|ful}}, from {{inh|en|ang|full|t=full}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*full}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*fullaz|t=full}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*pl̥h₁nós|t=full}}.

Germanic cognates include {{cog|fy|fol}}, {{cog|nds|vull}}, {{cog|nl|vol}}, {{cog|de|voll}}, {{cog|da|fuld}}, {{cog|nb,nn,sv|full}}. Proto-Indo-European cognates include {{cog|en|plenty}} (via Latin, compare {{m|la|plēnus}}), {{cog|cy|llawn}}, {{cog|ru|по́лный}}, {{cog|lt|pilnas}}, {{cog|fa|پر|tr=por}}, {{cog|sa|पूर्ण|tr=pūrṇá}}.
