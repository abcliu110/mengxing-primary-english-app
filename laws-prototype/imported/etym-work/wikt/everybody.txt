====Usage notes====
* The term is {{lg|conjugation|conjugated}} in the {{lg|singular}} ("{{m|en||'''Is''' everybody here?}}"; "{{m|en||Everybody '''has''' heard of it.}})". However, similar to what occurs with {{lg|collective noun|collective nouns}}, such as {{m|en|crowd}} or {{m|en|team}}, the {{lg|grammatical number}} of a {{lg|plural}} {{lg|pronoun}} referring back to it ([[antecedent]]) is reflected in the verb conjugation ("{{m|en||Everybody '''was''' laughing at first, but then '''they''' all stopped.}}").
* The term logically negates to {{m|en||not everybody}} rather than {{m|en||nobody}}.
