From {{inh|en|enm|magik,magyk}}, from {{der|en|fro|magique|pos=noun and adjective}}, from {{der|en|la|magicus|pos=a}}, {{m|la|magica|pos=noun use of feminine form of {{m|la|magicus}}}}, from {{der|en|grc|μαγικός||magical}}, from {{m|grc|μάγος||[[magician]], [[magus]]}}. Ultimately from {{uder|en|ira-old|-}}, probably derived from {{der|en|ine-pro|*megʰ-|*meh₂gʰ-|to be able to, to help; power, sorcerer}}. {{surf|en|mage|-ic}}. {{piecewise doublet|en|magey}}. {{displaced|ang|ġealdor}} (whence {{ncog|enm|galder}}) and {{m|en|dwimmer}}.
