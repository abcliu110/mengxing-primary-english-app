====Usage notes====
* The comparative forms are infrequent.
* German '''''national''''' in the sense of “nationwide” occurs chiefly in a political context, as shown in the examples above. Its use in other contexts is often modeled on English usage: {{m|de||ein nationaler Gesangswettbewerb|a national singing competition}}. The more idiomatic German word is {{m|de|landesweit}} or, in the cases of Germany and Austria specifically, {{m|de|bundesweit}}: {{m|de||ein landesweiter/bundesweiter Gesangswettbewerb}}.
