===Etymology 1===
[[Image:Goat kid444.jpg|thumb|right|A goat kid.]]
From {{inh|en|enm|kide}}, from {{der|en|non|kið||young goat}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*kidją}}, {{m|gem-pro|*kittīną||goatling, kid}}, perhaps from {{der|en|ine-pro|*gʰaydn-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*ǵʰaydn-||goat}} or {{der|en|ine-pro|*gidʰ-||kid, goatling, little goat}}. Compare {{cog|sv|-}} and {{cog|da|kid}}, {{cog|de|Kitz}} and {{m|de|Kitze}}, {{cog|sq|kedh}} and {{m|sq|kec}}. The sense of {{m|en|child}} has been in use since the 1590s as [[slang]], and since the 1840s in informal use.<ref>{{R:Online Etymology Dictionary}}</ref><ref>{{R:Grose Vulgar Tongue|page=124|passage='''{{smallcaps|Kid}}''', a child.|nodot=1}}</ref>
