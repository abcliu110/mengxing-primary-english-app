====Usage notes====
* Use where {{m|en|I}} could be used is mostly poetic or archaic, except with a coordinating conjunction, such as {{m|en|and}}. However, this usage is non-standard.
* ''[[w:Garner's Modern American Usage|Garner's Modern American Usage]]'' (2009) reports opposition to the non-reflexive use, especially where {{m|en|I}} could be used.
* [http://www.apstylebook.com/ AP Stylebook Online] (2010) reports opposition to the non-reflexive use, as reflexive pronouns (like ''myself'') should not be used instead of objective pronouns (like ''me'').
