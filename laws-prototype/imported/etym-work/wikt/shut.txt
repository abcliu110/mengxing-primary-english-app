===Etymology 1===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:schitten}}
From {{inh|en|enm|schitten}}, {{m|enm|schetten}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sċyttan||to cause rapid movement, shoot a bolt, shut, bolt}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*skuttjan}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*skutjaną}}, {{m|gem-pro|*skuttijaną||to bar, bolt}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*skuttą}}, {{m|gem-pro|*skuttjō||bar, bolt, shed}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)kewd-||to drive, fall upon, rush}}. The Modern English word was originally a dialect form; the Old English word would have normally merged with {{m|en|shit}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|schutten||to shut in, lock up}}, {{cog|nds|schütten||to shut, lock in}}, {{cog|de|schützen||to shut out, dam, protect, guard}}.
