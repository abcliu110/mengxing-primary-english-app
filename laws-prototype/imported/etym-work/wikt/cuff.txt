===Etymology 3===
1520, “to hit”, apparently of {{der|en|gmq|-}} origin, from {{der|en|no|kuffa|t=to push, shove}} or {{der|en|sv|kuffa|t=to knock, thrust, strike}}, from the {{der|en|gem-pro|-}} base {{m|gem-pro|*skuf-||tr=skuƀ}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*skewbʰ-}}, see also {{cog|lt|skùbti||to hurry}}, {{cog|pl|skubać||to pluck}}, {{cog|sq|humb||to lose}}.

Germanic cognates include {{cog|nds|kuffen|t=to box the ears}}, {{cog|de|kuffen|t=to thrash}}. More at {{l|en|scuff}}, {{l|en|shove}}, {{l|en|scuffle}}.
