====Usage notes====
Being a word borrowed from English derived from Greek, the ''y'' in ''system'' is pronounced /ɨ̞, ɪ/ rather than expected /ə/. To preserve consistency between pronunciation and spelling, some prefer to spell this word {{l|cy|sustem}}. Nevertheless, ''system'' is the more common spelling of the two.
