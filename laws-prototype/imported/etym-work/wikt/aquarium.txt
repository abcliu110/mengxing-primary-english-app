===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|id=tank|:bor|la:aquārius<alt:aquārium>|tree=1}}
Borrowed from {{bor|en|la|aquārium}}, neuter of {{m|la|aquārius||of or pertaining to water}}, from {{m|la|aqua}} {{suffix|en||arium}}. {{doublet|en|ewer}}; see also {{m|en|Aquarius}}. Also claimed to be {{coined|en|Philip Henry Gosse|nat=English|occ=naturalist|nocap=1}}, as a {{blend|en|nocap=1|aquatic|vivarium}}.<ref>{{cite-web|en|title=World's first aquarium|work=London Zoo|publisher=[[w:London Zoo|London Zoo]]|archiveurl=https://web.archive.org/web/20240405204332/https://www.londonzoo.org/zoo-stories/history-of-london-zoo/worlds-first-aquarium|url=https://www.londonzoo.org/zoo-stories/history-of-london-zoo/worlds-first-aquarium}}</ref>
