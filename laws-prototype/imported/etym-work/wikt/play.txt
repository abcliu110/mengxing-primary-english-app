===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:pleyen<id:play>|enm:pleye|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|pleyen|pos=verb}} and {{inh|en|enm|pleye|pos=noun}}, from {{inh|en|ang|plegian}} and {{m|ang|plegan}}, {{m|ang|plagian||to play, exercise, etc.}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*plehan||to care about, be concerned with}} and {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*plegōn||to engage, move}}, of uncertain origin.

{{col-top|2|cognates and related terms}}
Cognate with {{cog|sco|play||to act or move briskly, cause to move, stir}}, {{cog|stq|pleegje||to look after, care for, maintain}}, {{cog|fy|pleegje}}, {{m|fy|pliigje||to commit, perform, bedrive}}, {{cog|dum|pleyen}} {{gloss|"to dance, leap for joy, rejoice, be glad"; compare Modern {{cog|nl|pleien||to play a particular children's game}}}}, {{cog|nl|plegen||to commit, bedrive, practice}}, {{cog|de|pflegen||to care for, be concerned with, attend to, tend}}. Related also to {{cog|ang|plēon||to risk, endanger}}. More at {{l|en|plight}}, {{l|en|pledge}}.
{{col-bottom}}

The noun is from {{inh|en|enm|pleye}}, from {{inh|en|ang|plæġ}}, {{m|ang|plega}}, {{m|ang|plæġa||play, quick [[motion]], [[movement]], exercise; (athletic) [[sport]], [[game]]; festivity, [[drama]]; [[battle]]; gear for games, an implement for a game; clapping with the hands, applause}}, deverbative of {{m|ang|plegian||to play}}; see above.
