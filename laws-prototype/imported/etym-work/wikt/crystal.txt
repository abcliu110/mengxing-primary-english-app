===Etymology===
From {{inh|en|enm|crystal}}, {{m|enm|cristal}}, {{m|enm|criȝstall}}, representing a merger of {{inh|en|ang|cristalla}} (from Latin {{m|la|crystallus}}) and {{der|en|xno|cristall}}, {{der|en|fro|cristal}} (from Latin {{m|la|crystallum}}). The Latin is derived from {{der|en|grc|κρύσταλλος||clear ice}}, from {{m|grc|κρύος||frost}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*krews-||hard, hard outer surface, crust}}.
