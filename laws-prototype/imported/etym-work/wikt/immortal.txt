==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*mer-|id=die}}
From {{der|en|la|immortālis}}, from prefix {{m|la|im-||not}} (from {{m|la|in-}}) + {{m|la|mortālis||mortal}} (from {{m|la|mors||death}}, combining form {{m|la|mort-}} + adjectival suffix {{m|la|-alis}}), equivalent to {{af|en|im-|mortal}}. Displaced native {{m|en|undeadly}}, from {{noncog|ang|undēadlīċ}}.

The term referring to the Persian soldiers was a {{cal|en|grc|ἀθάνατος}}. One suggestion claimed that "Immortals" were named by the ancient Greeks because wounded soldiers were quickly replaced, while another claimed that it was because Greek informants confused {{der|en|peo|*anauša-||immortal, without death}} with {{der|en|peo|*anūšiya-||companion, royal bodyguard}}.
