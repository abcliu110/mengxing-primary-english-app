===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:uder|frm:géographie<ety:uder<la:geōgraphia>>|tree=1}}
From {{uder|en|frm|géographie}}, from {{uder|en|la|geōgraphia}}, from {{uder|en|grc|γεωγραφία||a [[description]] of the [[earth]]}}, from {{m|grc|γῆ||[[earth]]}} + {{m|grc|γράφω||[[write]]}}. {{doublet|en|geographia}}.

Use in reference to lavatories derives from the mid-20th century euphemism "show one the geography of the house" in reference to pointing out the toilets.
