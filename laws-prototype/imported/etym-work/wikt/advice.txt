===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:avys|tree=1}}
{{root|en|ine-pro|*weyd-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|avys}}, from {{der|en|fro|avis}}, rebracketed from the phrase {{m|fro||ce m'est a vis|t=I think”, “it seems to me|lit=it is to my view}}, where {{m|fro|vis}} is from {{der|en|la|vīsus#Noun||vision, sight}}. The unhistoric -d- was introduced during the 15th century  due to influence from [[advise]] and [[ad-]], see [[advance]]. {{doublet|en|aviso}}. See {{m|en|vision}}, and compare {{m|en|avise}}, {{m|en|advise}}.

Mostly displaced native {{noncog|ang|rǣd}} (see modern {{m|en|rede}}).
