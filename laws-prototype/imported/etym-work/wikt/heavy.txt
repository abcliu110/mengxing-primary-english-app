===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*kap-|id=seize}}
From {{inh|en|enm|hevy}}, {{m|enm|heviȝ}}, from {{inh|en|ang|hefiġ}}, {{m|ang|hefeġ}}, {{m|ang|hæfiġ|t=heavy; important, grave, severe, serious; oppressive, grievous; slow, dull}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*habīg|t=heavy, hefty, weighty}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*habīgaz|t=heavy, hefty, weighty}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*kap-|id=seize|t=to take, grasp, hold}}. Equivalent to {{suf|en|heave|-y}}. Related to {{m|en|have}}.

Cognate with {{cog|sco|hevy}}, {{m|sco|havy}}, {{m|sco|heavy|t=heavy}}, {{cog|stq|heeuwich}}, {{m|stq|häwich|t=violent, angry}}, {{cog|fy|hevich|t=violent}}, {{cog|nl|hevig|t=violent, severe, intense, acute}}, {{cog|nds-de|hevig|t=violent, fierce, intense, angry}}, {{cog|de|hebig}} (compare {{m|de|heftig|t=fierce, severe, intense, violent, heavy}}), {{cog|is|höfugur|t=heavy, weighty, important}}.

Compare typologically {{ncog|ru|взять,объёмный,ёмкий|ng=akin to {{m|ru|име́ть}}}}, and {{ncog|la|capāx|t=large, wide, roomy, spacious, capacious, capable, apt}}.
