===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₁es-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|sothen|t=to verify, prove the validity of}}, from {{inh|en|ang|sōþian|t=to verify, prove, confirm, bear witness to}}, from {{inh|en|gmw-pro|*sanþōn}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*sanþōną|t=to prove, certify, acknowledge, testify}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₁es-|t=to be}}. Cognate with {{cog|da|sande|t=to verify}}, {{cog|sv|sanna|t=to verify}}, {{cog|is|sanna|t=to verify}}. See also {{m|en|sooth}}. Displaced {{ncog|ang|frēfran|}}, {{m|ang|ġefrēfran||to comfort, console, soothe}}, and partially displaced {{ncog|ang|stillan|}}, {{m|ang|ġestillan||to calm, become calm, pacify, quieten}} (whence modern {{m|en|still}}).

The semantic evolution of "to verify, prove the validity of" →  "to comfort" (first attested in the late 17th century) comes from the notion of assuaging someone by supporting the truth of what they say.
