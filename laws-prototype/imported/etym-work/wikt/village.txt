===Etymology===
From {{der|en|enm|village}}, from {{der|en|fro|village}}, from {{der|en|la|vīllāticus}}, ultimately from {{der|en|la|villa}} (English {{m|en|villa}}).

Broadly overtook {{cog|ang|wic#Noun}}, {{m|ang|þorp#Noun_3}}, and {{m|ang|ham#Noun_18}}; all only surviving in place names as [[-wick]]/ [[-wich]], [[-thorpe]]/ [[-throp]], and [[-ham]], respectively.

The Philippine sense is due to its frequent use in the names of gated communities.
