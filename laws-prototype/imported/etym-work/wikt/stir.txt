From {{inh|en|enm|stiren}}, {{m|enm|sturien}}, {{m|enm|steren}}, from {{inh|en|ang|styrian|t=to be in motion, move, agitate, stir, disturb, trouble}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*sturiz|t=turmoil, noise, confusion}}, related to {{cog|gmw-pro|*staurijan|t=to destroy, disturb}}. Cognate with {{cog|non|styrr|t=turmoil, noise, confusion}}, {{cog|de|stören||to disturb}}, {{cog|nl|storen||to disturb}}.

=====Usage notes=====
* In all transitive senses except the dated one (“to [[change]] the place of in any manner”), {{m|en||stir}} is often followed by {{m|en|up}} with an intensive effect; as, {{m|en||to stir up fire}}; {{m|en||to stir up sedition}}.
