===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*sed-}}
Attested since the 14th century, originally meant a “law or regulation that determines the amount to be paid”, from {{inh|en|enm|syse}}, {{m|enm|sise||regulation, control, limit}}, from {{der|en|fro|cise}}, {{m|fro|sise}}, aphetism of {{m|fro|assise||assize}}, from the verb {{m|fro|asseoir||to sit down}}, from {{der|en|la|assideo|assidēre}}, composed of {{affix|la|ad-|gloss1=to, towards, at|sedeō|gloss2=sit; settle down|nocat=1}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*sed-}}. Displaced native {{ncog|ang|miċelnes|lit=bigness}}.
