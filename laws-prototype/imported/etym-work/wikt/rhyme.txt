===Etymology 1===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*h₂er-|*srew-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|rim}}, {{m|enm|rime}}, {{m|enm|ryme|t=identical letters or sounds in words from the vowel in their stressed syllables to their ends; measure, meter, rhythm; song, verse, etc., with rhyming lines}},<ref>{{R:MED Online|entry=rīm(e|pos=''n.''(3)|noformat=1|id=MED37559}}</ref> from {{der|en|xno|rime}}, {{m|xno|ryme|t=identical letters or sounds in words from the vowel in their stressed syllables to their ends; song, verse, etc., with rhyming lines}} (modern {{cog|fr|rime}}); further etymology uncertain, possibly either:<ref>{{R:OED Online|pos=n|id=8695648867|date=September 2023|nodot=1}}; {{R:Lexico|pos=n}}</ref>

* from {{der|en|la|rhythmus|t=rhythm}}, from {{der|en|grc|ῥῠθμός|t=measured motion, rhythm; regular, repeating motion, vibration}}, ultimately from {{der|en|ine-pro|*srew-|t=to flow; a stream}}; or
* borrowed from {{der|en|frk|*rīm|t=number, order, sequence, series, row of identical things}} (whence {{cog|ang|rīm|t=number, enumeration, series}}), from {{der|en|ine-pro|*h₂rey-|t=to arrange; to count}} and {{m|ine-pro|*h₂er-|t=to fit, put together; to fix; to slot}}.
