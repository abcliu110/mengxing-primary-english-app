====Usage notes====
* ''off'' is used as an [[adverbial]] [[particle]] in a number of [[:Category:English phrasal verbs|phrasal verbs]] (''[[shake off]]'', ''[[show off]]'', ''[[switch off]]'', ''[[take off]]'', and so forth). This is not to be confused with prepositional use (e.g. ''jump off the table'', ''keep off the grass''; see below).
