==English==

===Etymology 1===
From earlier {{m|en|char}}, from {{inh|en|enm|charr}}, {{m|enm|charre}}, {{m|enm|cherre||odd job, turn, occasion, business}}, from {{inh|en|ang|ċerr}}, {{m|ang|ċierr||a turn}}, from {{m|ang|ċierran||to turn}}, from {{inh|en|gem-pro|*karzijaną||to turn}}, from {{inh|en|ine-pro|*gers-||to bend, turn}}.

Cognate with {{cog|nl|keer||time; turn; occasion}}, {{cog|de|Kehre||a turn; bend; wind; back-flip; u-turn}}. Also related to {{cog|stq|kiere}}, {{m|stq|käire||to turn}}, Old Saxon {{m|osx|kērian}}, Old High German {{m|goh|chēran||to turn}} (German {{m|de|kehren||to turn}}, Dutch {{m|nl|keren||to turn}}). See also {{m|en|char}}.
