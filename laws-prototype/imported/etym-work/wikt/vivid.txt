===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:lbor|la:vīvidus|tree=1}}
{{root|en|ine-pro|*gʷeyh₃-}}
{{lbor|en|la|vividus|t=animated, spirited}}, from {{m|la|vivo|vivere|t=to live}}, akin to {{m|la|vita|t=life}}, {{cog|grc|βίος|t=life}}.

The noun sense (a type of marker pen) was genericized from {{w|Bic (company)|Bic}}'s Vivid Marker brand.
