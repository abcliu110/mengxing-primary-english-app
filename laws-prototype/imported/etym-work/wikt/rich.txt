====Usage notes====
* The adjective ''rich'' forms two separate plural nouns: ''the rich'' are the people characterized by being rich, while {{m|en|riches}} are the things that make or might make someone rich. The existence of this separate sense of {{m|en|riches}} generally precludes informal countable use of ''rich'' similar to that seen in {{m|en|poors}} and {{m|en|wealthies}}.
