===Etymology 1===
Origin {{unc|en|nocap=1}}; but probably of {{der|en|gmq}} origin. Probably either a back-formation from {{m|en|foggy||covered with tall grass; thick, marshy}}, from the earlier-attested {{m|en|fog||tall grass}} (see below),<ref>{{R:Dictionary.com|fog}}</ref><ref name="Etymonline">{{R:Etymonline}}</ref> or from or related to {{bor|en|da|fog|t=spray, shower, drift, storm}},<ref name="Etymonline"/> related to {{cog|is|fok|t=spray, any light thing tossed by the wind, snowdrift}}, {{cog|is|fjúka|t=to blow, drive}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*feukaną|t=to whisk, blow}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*pug-|t=billow, bulge, drift}}, from {{m|ine-pro|*pew-}}, {{m|ine-pro|*pow-|t=to blow, drift, billow}}, in which case related to {{cog|de|fauchen|t=to hiss, spit, spray}}.
