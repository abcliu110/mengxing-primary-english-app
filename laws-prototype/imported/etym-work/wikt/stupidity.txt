# {{lb|en|uncountable}} The property of being [[stupid]].
#: {{ux|en|I suppose you can put it down to gross '''stupidity''', but that's not much of an excuse.}}

Soft heartedness caused more harm than good.}}

#: {{ux|en|Jimmy's '''stupidity''' cost him his car.}}

They require that crass '''stupidities''' shall not be played upon the reader as “the craft of the woodsman, the delicate art of the forest,” by either the author or the people in the tale.}}
