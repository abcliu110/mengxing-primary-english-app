==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*kap-|id1=head|*mewH-|*melh₂-|id3=grind|*pel-|id4=skin}}
{{ety|en|:ubor|fr:camouflage|tree=1}}
{{ubor|en|fr|camouflage}}, from {{m|fr|camoufler|t=to veil, disguise}}, alteration (due to {{m|fr|camouflet|t=smoke blown in one's face}}) of {{der|en|it|camuffare|t=to muffle the head}}, from {{m|it|ca-}} (from {{der|en|it|capo|t=head}}) + {{m|it|muffare|t=to muffle}}, from {{der|en|la-med|muffula|muffula, muffla|t=muff}}. This Medieval Latin, from which there is also {{cog|en|muffle}}, is either derived from a {{der|en|frk|*molfell|t=soft garment made of hide}} from {{m|gmw-pro|*mol|t=softened, forworn}} (akin to {{cog|goh|molawēn|t=to soften}}, {{cog|gmh|molwic|t=soft}}) + {{m|gmw-pro|*fell|t=hide, skin}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*fellą|t=skin, film, fleece}}, or, an alternate etymology traces it to a {{der|en|frk|*muffël|t=a muff, wrap, envelope}} composed of {{m|gmw-pro|*mauwu|*mauwa|t=sleeve, wrap}} from {{der|en|gem-pro|*mawwō|t=sleeve}} + {{m|gmw-pro|*fell|t=skin, hide}} from {{der|en|gem-pro|*fellą|t=skin, film, fleece}}.
