# {{senseid|en|Q384593}} A [[peace officer]] who is a [[member]] of a [[police force]].
#: {{syn|en|Thesaurus:police officer}}
#: {{hyper|en|officer#Noun<qq:{{glossary|elliptically}} synonymous when the context is clear>|<|person#Noun}}

====Usage notes====
* This term is the [[gender-neutral]] equivalent of {{m|en|policeman}} (male) and {{m|en|policewoman}} (female).
* The military distinction of [[officer]] versus [[nonofficer]] ranks usually does not apply to this term; in the usual usage, any properly engaged (i.e., qualified and oath-sworn) employee of a police force is an officer thereof.
