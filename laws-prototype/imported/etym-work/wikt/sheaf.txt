# {{lb|en|collective}} A bundle of [[arrow]]s sufficient to fill a [[quiver]], or the [[allowance]] of each [[archer]].

# {{lb|en|collective}} A [[quantity]] of [[arrow]]s, usually [[twenty-four]].

|passage=Arrows were anciently made of reeds, afterwards of cornel wood, and occasionally of every species of wood: but according to Roger Ascham, ash was best; arrows were reckoned by '''sheaves''', a '''sheaf''' consisted of twenty-four arrows.}}
# {{lb|en|mechanics}} A [[sheave]].
# {{lb|en|mathematics}} An abstract construct in [[topology]] that associates [[data]] to the [[open set]]s of a [[topological space]] (i.e. a [[presheaf]]) in such a way so as to make the [[local]] and [[global]] data compatible, generalizing the situation of [[function]]s, [[fiber bundle]]s, [[manifold]] structure, etc. on a topological space. Formally, a [[presheaf]] <math>\mathcal{F}</math> whose [[section]]s are, in a technical sense, uniquely determined by their [[restriction]]s onto smaller sets: that is, given an open cover <math>\{U_i \}</math> of <math>U</math>:
## If two sections over <math>U</math> agree under restriction to every <math>U_i</math>, then the sections are the same.
## Given a family of sections <math>s_i \in \mathcal{F}(U_i)</math> such that all pairs <math>(s_i, s_j)</math> agree under restriction to <math>U_i \cap U_j</math>, there is a (unique) section <math>s</math> over <math>U</math> whose restriction to <math>U_i</math> is <math>s_i</math>.
