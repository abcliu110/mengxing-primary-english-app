==English==

===Etymology===
{{ety|en|:inh|enm:ferien|enm:ferie<id:ferry crossing>|tree=1}}
The verb is from {{inh|en|enm|ferien||to carry, convey}}, from {{inh|en|ang|ferian}}, from {{der|en|gmw-pro|*farjan}}, from {{der|en|gem-pro|*farjaną}}, which see for cognates. This verb is the [[causative]] of {{m+|gem-pro|*faraną|t=to go, travel}}, whence {{m+|en|fare}}; ultimately from the {{der|en|ine-pro|-}} root {{m|ine-pro|*per-|id=fare}}.

The noun is from {{inh|en|enm|ferie|t=place of crossing, ferry|id=ferry crossing}}, which was derived from the above verb under influence of {{der|en|non|ferja|id=noun}}, from {{m+|gem-pro|*farjǭ}}, itself also from the verb {{m|gem-pro|*faraną}}.

{{false cognate|la|ferō}}.
