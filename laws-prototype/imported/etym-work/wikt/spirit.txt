===Etymology===
{{etymon|en|:inh|enm:spirit|tree=1}}
From {{inh|en|enm|spirit}}, from {{der|en|fro|espirit||spirit}}, from {{der|en|la|spīritus||breath; spirit}}, from {{der|en|ine-pro|*(s)peys-||to blow, breathe}}. Compare {{m|en|inspire}}, {{m|la|respire}}, {{m|la|transpire}}, all ultimately from Latin {{m|la|spīrō||to breathe, blow, respire}}. In this sense, displaced native {{noncog|enm|gast}} (from {{ncog|ang|gāst}}), whence modern {{noncog|en|ghost}}. {{doublet|en|spiritus|spirytus|sprite|spright|esprit}}.
