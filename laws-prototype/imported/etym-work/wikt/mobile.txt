{{also|Mobile|-mobile}}
==English==

===Etymology===
{{root|en|ine-pro|*m(y)ewh₁-}}
From {{inh|en|enm|-}}, from {{der|en|fro|-}}, from {{der|en|la|mōbilis|t=easy to be moved, moveable}}, from {{m|la|moveō|t=move}}. The video-gaming sense was coined by {{w|Richard Bartle}} to describe [[NPC]]s or creatures capable of moving "under their own power" in the 1978 video game ''{{w|MUD1|Multi-User Dungeon}}''. Bartle retracted an earlier claim of his that it was from the {{w|kinetic sculpture}} sense of ''mobile'' (for the "[[unpredictable]] but [[limited]]" motion of the hanging [[ornament]]s).
