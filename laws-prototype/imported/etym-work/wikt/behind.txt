===Adverb===
{{en-adv|more,er<l:rare,or,nonstandard>}}

# At or in the rear or back part of something.
#: {{ux|en|The water flows out through the pipe, and the sediment collects '''behind'''.}}
# In a rearward direction.
#: {{ux|en|We ran and ran, without once looking '''behind'''.}}
# So as to come after someone or something in position, distance, advancement, ranking, time, etc.
#: {{ux|en|The slower runners were left a long way '''behind'''.}}
#: {{ux|en|In the cricket match, England finished a long way '''behind'''.}}
#: {{ux|en|The worst thing about autumn is that winter follows '''behind'''.}}
#* {{RQ:Milton Paradise Lost|book=X|passage=I shall not lag '''behind'''.|year=1873}}
# So as to be still in place after someone or something has departed or ceased to exist.
#: {{ux|en|I couldn't be bothered to carry the ironing board, so I left it '''behind'''.}}
#: {{ux|en|He stayed '''behind''' after the war.}}
#: {{ux|en|He left '''behind''' a legacy of death and sorrow.}}
#: {{ux|en|The island was inundated by the sea, leaving not a trace '''behind'''.}}
#* {{RQ:Shakespeare Tempest|IV|i|passage=Yea, all which it inherit, shall dissolve, <br> And, like this insubstantial pageant faded, / Leave not a rack '''behind'''.}}
# Backward in time or order of succession; [[past]].
#* {{RQ:King James Version|book=Philippians|chapter=3|verse=13|passage={{...}} forgetting those things which are '''behind''', and reaching forth unto those things which are before}}
# [[behind the scenes|Behind the scenes]] in a theatre; [[backstage]].
#* {{quote-book|en|year=1890|author=w:Oscar Wilde|title=The Picture of Dorian Gray|publisher=Vintage|year_published=2007|page=68|passage=‘After the performance was over I went '''behind''', and spoke to her.’}}
# {{lb|en|archaic}} Not yet brought forward, produced, or exhibited to view; out of sight; remaining.
#* {{RQ:Locke Human Understanding|4|16|text=We cannot be sure that there is no evidence '''behind'''.}}
