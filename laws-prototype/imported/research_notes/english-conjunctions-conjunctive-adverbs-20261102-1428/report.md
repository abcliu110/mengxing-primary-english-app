# 英语连词与连接副词权威研究报告（主报告 · 第 1 卷）

用途：中国小学 1–6 年级教学内容 + 教师/家长详细层。讲解用中文，英文术语/词表/例句保留英文；逐字引用注明来源名 + URL；verified live = 本次实际打开原页，index = 搜索摘要核对。读取日期：2026-11-02。
**分卷说明**：本卷 = Summary + 第 1 章（连词）。第 2 章（连接副词）见 `notes/part2-conjunctive-adverbs.md`；第 3–4 章（正面对比 + 常见错误）见 `notes/part3-comparison-and-errors.md`；第 5 章（教学亮点/视频）+ Could not verify + Sources 全表见 `notes/part4-teaching-gems-and-sources.md`；来源日志见 `notes/sources-log.md`。

## Summary 核心结论

1. 连词三大类：并列（只有 7 个，FANBOYS）、从属（多，按语义分组）、关联（成对）。Grammarly、Merriam-Webster、Khan Academy、British Council 一致。
2. however/therefore/moreover 不是连词，是连接副词：本身粘不住两个独立分句，必须配句号或分号；只用逗号 = comma splice 错误。公式：`Independent clause; conjunctive adverb, independent clause.`
3. 并列连词恰相反：逗号 + FANBOYS 即可连接两个独立分句（Purdue OWL）。
4. 从属分句不能单独成句（= fragment 错误）；放句首后加逗号，放句末一般不加。
5. 中国学生头号陷阱“双连词”：although…but…、because…so… 必须二选一（汉语 虽然…但是…、因为…所以… 不能搬入英语），Cambridge 语法材料有明确错误示例。
6. 儿童入口：BBC Bitesize "joining words"、《Conjunction Junction》歌曲、FANBOYS / AAAWWUBBIS / ON A WHITE BUS 助记、British Council 举卡片与故事接龙。

## 1. 连词 Conjunctions

### 1.1 权威定义（逐字引用）

> "Conjunctions are words that join together other words or groups of words." —— Merriam-Webster, https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/conjunction ，verified live。

> "a word that joins words, phrases or sentences, for example 'and', 'but', 'or' or 'because'" —— Oxford Advanced Learner's Dictionary, https://www.oxfordlearnersdictionaries.com/us/definition/english/conjunction ，index。

> "Conjunctions are words that link other words, phrases, or clauses together." —— Grammarly, https://www.grammarly.com/blog/conjunctions/ ，index（经 Scribd 转载原文交叉核对）；同页称其作用是让人连贯表达复杂关联意思（"helping us to communicate interconnected and complex things coherently"）。

> "a word such as 'and', 'but', 'while', or 'although' that connects words, phrases, and clauses in a sentence" —— Cambridge Dictionary, https://dictionary.cambridge.org/es/diccionario/ingles-espanol/conjunction ，index。Cambridge Grammar 栏简版："Conjunctions are linking words like and, or, but, then and because"，http://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english-chinese-traditional/conjunction ，verified live。

> "Conjunctions are connecting words that join two or more sentences into a single sentence. They also join words, phrases or clauses." —— British Council, https://africa.teachingenglish.org.uk/classroom/grammar/coordinating-conjunctions ，verified live（最适合改编给小学生）。

> "Conjunctions are the words that join sentences, phrases, and other words together." —— Lumen/LibreTexts, https://courses.lumenlearning.com/waymaker-level1-english-gen/chapter/conjunctions-4-5-1/ ，verified live。

儿童版（BBC Bitesize, KS2）："Conjunctions are joining words." 原文页 https://www.bbc.co.uk/bitesize/articles/z6rvbdm ，index。可译为“胶水词/连接词”。

### 1.2 三类总览（Merriam-Webster 同一词条逐字概括，verified live）

- 并列："A coordinating conjunction connects words, phrases, and clauses of equal importance. The main coordinating conjunctions are and, or, and but."
- 从属："A subordinating conjunction introduces a subordinate clause (a clause that does not form a simple sentence by itself) and joins it to a main clause (a clause that can be used as a simple sentence by itself)." 例：She waited until they were seated. / It had been quiet since the children left.
- 成对："Some conjunctions are used in pairs. The most common pairs are either ... or, both ... and, neither ... nor, and not only ... but (also)."
- Cambridge 并列定义："a word such as and or but that connects words, phrases, and clauses of equal importance"，https://dictionary.cambridge.org/dictionary/english/coordinating-conjunction ，verified live。

### 1.3 并列连词 FANBOYS（完整清单，封闭集合共 7 个）

Grammarly 逐字："The most common coordinating conjunctions are for, and, nor, but, or, yet, and so; you can remember them by using the mnemonic device FANBOYS."（来源同 1.1 Grammarly，index）

逐词义与例句（Lumen/LibreTexts verified live + British Council verified live，两源一致）：

| 词 | 含义/功能 | 例句（来源原文） |
|---|---|---|
| for | presents a reason（表原因，正式，≈because） | They do not gamble or smoke, for they are ascetics. / I eat healthy food for I want to get strong. |
| and | presents non-contrasting items or ideas（并列添加） | They gamble, and they smoke. / I like to read and I like to play. |
| nor | presents a non-contrasting negative idea（否定并列，nor 后常倒装） | They do not gamble, nor do they smoke. / My sister doesn't like to read, nor does she like school. |
| but | presents a contrast or exception（转折） | They gamble, but they don't smoke. / I wanted to go to school but I was sick. |
| or | presents an alternative item or idea（选择） | Every day they gamble, or they smoke. / Would you like to play soccer or go to the beach? |
| yet | presents a contrast or exception（转折，≈but 但更意外） | They gamble, yet they don't smoke. / I like eating apples, yet oranges are even better. |
| so | presents a consequence（结果） | He gambled well last night, so he smoked a cigar to celebrate. / The bus didn't come so we caught a taxi. |

**逗号规则（核心）**：
> "Use a comma to join two independent clauses and a coordinating conjunction (and, but, or, for, nor, so)." 例："Road construction can be inconvenient, but it is necessary." —— Purdue OWL, https://owl.purdue.edu/owl/general_writing/punctuation/punctuation/index.html ，verified live。
> Purdue OWL《Commas: Quick Rules》："Use commas to separate independent clauses when they are joined by any of these seven coordinating conjunctions: and, but, for, or, nor, so, yet."（index，经 UConn 转载 PDF 核对）

细化（Lumen, verified live）：连接两个完整分句必须逗号在前，否则 run-on；只连接两个词/短语（两个主语或两个动词）时**不用**逗号，如 In the 1960s, artificial heart devices did not fit well and tended to obstruct...；三项以上列举用系列逗号（serial comma）。

### 1.4 从属连词（按语义分类）

定义（EnglishClub, verified live）：
> "A subordinating conjunction joins a subordinate (dependent) clause to a main (independent) clause"；常用表："after, although, as, because, before, how, if, once, since, than, that, though, till, until, when, where, whether, while" —— http://www.englishclub.com/grammar/conjunctions-subordinating.htm

Cambridge 清单（verified live）："Common subordinating conjunctions are: after, (al)though, as, before, if, since, that, until, when, whereas, while, once, so, as soon as, provided that. When a clause follows these conjunctions, it becomes a subordinate clause, which needs a main clause to make a complete sentence."

Khan Academy 视频清单（David Rheinstrom 讲授，index，经转录核对）：after, although, as, as if, because, before, even if, even though, if, if only, like, rather than, since, that, though, unless, until, when, where, whereas, wherever, whether, while。课程页：https://www.khanacademy.org/humanities/grammar/parts-of-speech-the-preposition-and-the-conjunction/introduction-to-conjunctions/v/subordinating-conjunctions

按语义分组（综合 EnglishClub / Cambridge / Khan / 多家大学写作中心分类表，各组词与例句均为来源原文或来源例句模式）：

- **Time 时间**：after, as, as soon as, before, once, since, till, until, when, whenever, while — We left after the meeting ended. / Call me when you arrive. / Wash your hands before you eat.
- **Cause/Reason 原因**：as, because, now that, since（as/since 较弱、较正式）— Since the road was blocked, we took another route. / She was absent because she was ill.
- **Contrast/Concession 对比/让步**：although, even though, though, whereas, while — Although the task was difficult, everyone completed it.
- **Condition 条件**：as long as, even if, if, in case, only if, provided that, unless, whether or not — If you study, you will pass. / She won't go unless you invite her.
- **Purpose 目的**：in order that, so that — She wrote down the address so that she would not forget it.
- **Result 结果**：so that, so…that（注：结果义 so that 与目的义同形，靠语境区分）
- **Place 地点**：where, wherever — Sit where you like.（分类见大学写作中心表，index）
- **Comparison 比较**：as, as…as, just as, than — She is taller than her brother is.
- **Manner 方式**：as, as if, as though — He talks as if he knew everything.

**不能独立规则**（EnglishClub 原文，verified live）："A subordinate or dependent clause 'depends' on a main or independent clause. It cannot exist alone." 其儿童化演示极佳：有人对你说 "Hello! Although it was raining." 你什么也听不懂；但 "Ram went swimming." 完全可以独立。Khan Academy 同义：带从属连词的句子不能拆成两个独立句子（Although she loved Sir Reginald 本身不是句子）。

**逗号规则**：从属分句在前 → 分句后加逗号：**Although it was raining**, Ram went swimming.（EnglishClub 结构图原文，verified live）；主句在前、从属分句在后 → 一般不加逗号：Ram went swimming **although it was raining**. Purdue OWL 亦列："Use a comma after an introductory phrase, prepositional phrase, or dependent clause." 例 "Because Dad caught the chicken pox, we canceled our vacation."（verified live）

**位置铁律**：从属连词永远位于其从属分句之首（introduces the clause），可移动的是整个分句，不是连词本身（Cambridge，见第 2 章）。

### 1.5 关联连词 Correlative Conjunctions

Grammarly 逐字："Correlative conjunctions are pairs of conjunctions that work together. Some examples are either/or, neither/nor, and not only/but also."（index）Khan Academy 讲解要点："they're matched pairs… when you see one it's probably time to use the other"（index，转录）。

标准配对 + 例句（Lumen 表，verified live；Merriam 例句已在 1.2）：

| 配对 | 例句 |
|---|---|
| either…or | You either do your work or prepare for a trip to the office. |
| neither…nor | Neither the basketball team nor the football team is doing well. |
| not only…but (also) | He is not only handsome, but also brilliant. |
| both…and | Both the cross country team and the swimming team are doing well. |
| whether…or | You must decide whether you stay or you go. |
| just as…so | Just as many Americans love basketball, so many Canadians love ice hockey. |
| as much…as / as…as | Football is as much an addiction as it is a sport. |
| no sooner…than | No sooner did she learn to ski, than the snow began to thaw. |
| rather…than | I would rather swim than surf. |
| the…the | The more you practice dribbling, the better you will be at it. |

扩展配对（Grammarly 从属/关联表及多源，index）：such…that, scarcely/hardly…when, not…but, if…then, as many/much…as。

**平行结构规则（parallelism）**：配对两侧成分必须语法形式相同——Lumen 表的批注 "(Either do, or prepare)" 即示范两侧同为动词原形短语；Grammarly 关联建议："Make sure that the phrases joined by conjunctions are parallel (share the same structure)."（index，经 Scribd 转载核对）。错误示范：✗ She is not only a good singer but also dances well. → ✓ She not only sings well but also dances well. 另：not only 置于句首时主句部分倒装：Not only is he handsome, but also he is brilliant.（Lumen 表原文）BBC Learning English 配套练习亦要求 "Remember to use parallel form"（index）。

### 1.6 身兼数职的词（教师层重点）

Khan Academy 逐字提醒（index）："some of these you might recognize as being adverbs or prepositions in other contexts… You may notice some of them like, after and before, are prepositions." 判断标准 = **后面跟什么**：跟名词/代词 → 介词；跟完整分句（主语+动词）→ 从属连词；后面什么都不跟、单独修饰动词 → 副词。

- before：We shall discuss it before the lesson.（介词）/ Don't go away before I come back.（连词）/ I had never been there before.（副词）
- after：after school（介词短语）/ after the meeting ended（连词）
- since：since 2020（介词，时间）/ since she won（连词，时间）/ since the road was blocked（连词，原因）
- until/till：until Friday（介词）/ until you call（连词）
- so 的三副面孔：并列连词（结果，FANBOYS 之一）/ 从属用法（目的 so that 的省略式）/ 副词（so tired）。UKEssays 转引研究指出 so 的多重身份是中国学习者最困惑点之一（index）。
- though 的双身份：从属连词（Although/though it rained…）与句末副词（It's expensive. I like it, though.）。
