# 第 3 章 连词 vs 连接副词正面对比 + 第 4 章 常见学习者错误

## 3. 对比总表（依据 Purdue OWL / QuillBot / ThoughtCo / Cambridge / Grammarly 原文规则综合）

| 维度 | 并列连词 (and, but, so…) | 从属连词 (because, although, if…) | 连接副词 (however, therefore…) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 词性 | 连词 | 连词 | **副词**（词性不同！） |
| 语法工作 | 真正连接两个平等成分 | 把一个分句降级为从属分句再挂到主句上 | 不连接；只在分句内部表明与前文的逻辑关系 |
| 两侧地位 | 相等（equal） | 不相等（一主一从） | 两侧仍是两个独立分句（地位相等但各自独立） |
| 连接强度 | 强：逗号+连词即可成句 | 强：从属化本身即完成连接 | 弱/松散：自身无粘合力，必须靠句号或分号 |
| 标点 | `IC, + FANBOYS + IC` | 从属分句在前加逗号、在后不加 | `IC; adverb, IC` 或 `IC. Adverb, IC`；句中插入则双逗号 |
| 位置/移动性 | 固定在两成分之间，不可移动 | 固定在其分句之首；可移动的是整个从属分句（可前可后） | **可移动**：句首/句中/句末均可（移动测试，ThoughtCo 引 Reid） |
| 能否删去 | 删去则连接断裂 | 删去则从属关系消失 | 删去语法仍成立（Grammarly：technically optional），只损失逻辑提示 |
| 语域 | 中性，口语书面皆可 | 中性 | 偏正式、偏书面（BBC：用 However 分句版 "a little more formal and emphasises the transition"，index） |

**功能对应表**（最适合做教学对照卡；对应关系见 MyEnglishPages 对比表 index，并与各源词义一致）：
and ↔ moreover/furthermore（添加）；but ↔ however（转折）；so ↔ therefore/consequently（结果）；yet ↔ nevertheless/nonetheless（让步转折）；or ↔ otherwise/alternatively（否则/或者）。

**来源中的一句话总结**：
- QuillBot："conjunctive adverbs are not strong enough to be used in this way [with only a comma]"（连接副词“不够强”——最适合讲给学生的原话）。
- BBC Learning English（index）："Coordinating conjunctions link two sentences of the same importance. Whereas, subordinating conjunctions link a main clause and a subordinate clause…"；同一节目演示等义改写："I'm tired, but I'm ready to record." → "I'm tired. However, I'm ready."（后者更正式、更强调过渡）。
- Khan Academy 判别法（index）：有并列连词的句子可以拆成两个完整句子；有从属连词的句子拆不开（从属分句那一半不是句子）。

## 4. 常见学习者错误（来源明确警告的）

### 4.1 Comma splice 逗号拼接
定义（Purdue OWL，verified live）："two independent clauses joined only by a comma"。高发场景正是误用连接副词：✗ It was raining, however, we stayed home. 改法三选一（Purdue 原文）："separate the clauses with a period, with a comma followed by a coordinating conjunction, or with a semicolon." → ✓ It was raining; however, we stayed home. / ✓ It was raining. However, we stayed home. / ✓ It was raining, but we stayed home.（注：but 版逻辑为转折时适用）

### 4.2 Sentence fragment 从属分句单独成句
从属连词把完整句“变成”依附成分，单独加句号即残缺：✗ Because I was tired. / ✗ Although it was raining. EnglishClub 的演示（verified live）：单说 "Although it was raining." 听者什么也理解不了。Purdue OWL 对 dependent clause 的定义即 "cannot stand alone; an incomplete sentence"（verified live）。儿童高频版：✗ Because it's fun. 单独回答问题在口语中可接受，但正式写作必须挂回主句——教学时应说明口语/写作差异（此为综合来源口径）。

### 4.3 Run-on 流水句
两个独立分句无标点无连词直接相连。Lumen（verified live）："Run-on sentences are one of the most common errors in college-level writing. Mastering the partnership between commas and coordinating conjunctions will go a long way towards resolving many run-on sentence issues." 修复四法（多家写作中心一致，index）：句号拆句 / 分号 / 逗号+FANBOYS / 改一侧为从属分句。

### 4.4 双连词（中国学生专项，最高优先级）
- 规则（Cambridge Grammar 栏，经 6 年级教材转引核对，index）："we only use one conjunction to connect words or phrases"；其错误示范逐字为：✓ "Because my alarm didn't go off, I was late for work." ✗ "Not: Because my alarm didn't go off, so I was late for work."
- 学理说明（UKEssays 转引 Leung & Holmer 关于中国 ESL 学习者的研究，index）：汉语中 because 与 so "are always used together as pair correlatives"（因为…所以…），although/though 与 but 在汉语里同为成对关联词（虽然…但是…，suīrán…dànshì…）；学习者因此把成对习惯迁移进英语，而英语语法不允许。汉英对比参考页（Hanpanda HSK 语法）亦明示："Unlike English where you use either 'because' OR 'so,' Chinese often uses BOTH 因为 and 所以 together."（index）
- 教学口诀（基于上述来源规则的转述）：**英语一座桥只用一块“连接板”**：选 because 就扔掉 so；选 although 就扔掉 but。✗ Although it was cold, but he didn't wear a jacket. → ✓ Although it was cold, he didn't wear a jacket. / ✓ It was cold, but he didn't wear a jacket.

### 4.5 把 however 当 but 用（= 4.1 的根源）
✗ She was tired, however she continued. ✓ She was tired; however, she continued. 要点重申（MyEnglishPages，index）："'However' is NOT the same as 'but' in punctuation rules." 同时注意 however 的非连接副词用法（“无论多么”）不加逗号，避免矫枉过正（QuillBot，见主报告第 2 章规则 D）。

### 4.6 滥用 and 与句首 and/but 的真相
- 真相：以 and/but 开头**并非语法错误**。Merriam-Webster（conjunction 词条，verified live）："When placed at the beginning of a sentence, a coordinating conjunction may also link two sentences or paragraphs." 例："The preparations were complete. But where were the guests?" 其 and 词条 FAQ（index）："It's perfectly acceptable to begin a sentence with and, as well as with other words that we are often taught to avoid at the start of a sentence, such as but and or. Examples of sentence-initial and date back more than 1,000 years."
- 《Chicago Manual of Style》名言（经 Grammar Girl/Ellii 转引，index）："There is a widespread belief—one with no historical or grammatical foundation—that it is an error to begin a sentence with a conjunction such as and, but or so."
- 但课堂禁令的由来（Merriam-Webster's Dictionary of English Usage 转引，index）：该禁令意在纠正儿童 "string together independent clauses… with ands"（We got in the car and we went to the movie and I bought some popcorn and…）的流水账习惯。对小学生的教学口径应是：**不是“禁止”，而是“少用、用对”**——and 串句时改用 because/so/but/when 等更有信息的连词，或拆句。Lumen 亦设 "Starting a Sentence" 专节并配 Khan Academy 视频说明此非真规则（verified live）。
