# 第 2 章 连接副词 Conjunctive Adverbs（linking adverbs / transition words）

别名：linking adverbs、transitional expressions、sentence connectors、adverbial conjunctions；Cambridge 术语为 **linking adjuncts**；ThoughtCo 称亦作 conjunct / transitional conjunction / cohesive conjunction。注意：BBC Learning English《你问我答》把三者关系讲得最清楚（中文版，index）："a connector is an umbrella term for words or phrases that link ideas and sentences. All conjunctions and transition words are connectors, but not all connectors are conjunctions or transition words." 即 connector（连接手段）是伞形总称，连词与过渡词都是其子集。

## 2.1 定义（逐字引用）与“为何是副词而非连词”

> "In English grammar, a conjunctive adverb is an adverb or adverbial phrase that indicates a relation in meaning between two sequential independent clauses (or main clauses). It is also called a conjunct, a transitional conjunction, or a cohesive conjunction." —— ThoughtCo（Richard Nordquist）, https://www.thoughtco.com/conjunctive-adverb-grammar-1689909 ，verified live。

> "Conjunctive adverbs (e.g., 'however,' 'therefore') are adverbs that act in a similar way to conjunctions, linking the ideas in two sentences or statements together. Rather than modifying a single word, they modify the whole clause to express contrast, cause and effect, comparison, and other connections between two sentences or phrases." —— QuillBot, https://quillbot.com/blog/adverbs/conjunctive-adverb/ ，verified live。

> Merriam-Webster（conjunction 词条末段，verified live）："Some adverbs, such as afterwards, consequently, for example, however, nonetheless, and therefore, act like conjunctions by linking either two main clauses separated by a semicolon, or two separate sentences. They express some effect that the first clause or sentence has on the second one." 例："They didn't agree; however, each understood the other's opinion." / "The team has won its last three games. Thus, its record for the year is now 15-12."

> Cambridge（verified live）："Words and phrases such as above all, anyway, as a result, as well, eventually, firstly, however, overall, rather, then, therefore, though, on the contrary (linking adjuncts) can create similar meanings to conjunctions (e.g. adding, cause and effect). These words are adverb phrases and can come in any position which an adverb can occupy."

**为什么是副词**（综合 ThoughtCo / QuillBot / MIT / SJSU，逻辑一致）：
1. 词性上是副词/副词短语：普通副词修饰一个词，连接副词修饰**整个分句**（ThoughtCo："Unlike a conventional adverb, which typically affects the meaning of only a single word or phrase, the meaning of a conjunctive adverb affects the entire clause of which it is a part."）；
2. 它只是“住在”第二个分句内部的一个成分，**不承担语法连接功能**——真正隔开/连接两个独立分句的是句号或分号；删掉它，两个分句依然各自成立（语法上它可有可无，Grammarly 分号条目："Technically, conjunctive adverbs are optional." index）；
3. 它像副词一样**可以移动**（见 2.4），真连词不能移动。

## 2.2 按功能分类的完整词表（综合 EnglishClub / QuillBot / ThoughtCo 三家分类表，三源重合度高）

- **Addition 添加**：moreover, furthermore, additionally, in addition, also, besides, as well — She loves playing the piano; moreover, she is a great singer.（EnglishClub 例，index）
- **Contrast 对比**：however（核心）, nevertheless, nonetheless, on the other hand, in contrast, conversely, instead, on the contrary, even so, still — He studied hard for the test; however, he didn't pass.（EnglishClub）
- **Cause & Effect / Result 因果结果**：therefore, consequently, as a result, thus, hence, accordingly — She didn't study for the exam; therefore, she failed. / The factory closed down; consequently, many workers lost their jobs.（EnglishClub）
- **Sequence / Time 顺序时间**：first(ly), then, next, finally, meanwhile, subsequently, afterward(s), thereafter, eventually, in the meantime, at the same time — The kids were playing outside; meanwhile, their mother was preparing lunch.（EnglishClub）/ Gather the ingredients. Next, mix them together.（QuillBot）
- **Emphasis 强调**：indeed, in fact, actually, certainly, undoubtedly, after all, of course — I never expected to see him here. In fact, I'd thought he'd moved away.（QuillBot 释义模式）
- **Example 例证**：for example, for instance, namely, typically — …; for example, a mountain chicken is a type of frog.（QuillBot，verified live；namely 例见该页 red panda 例）
- **Comparison / Similarity 比较相似**：similarly, likewise, equally, comparatively — Project A was a success. Likewise, Project B has exceeded our expectations.（QuillBot）
- **Summary / Conclusion 总结**：in conclusion, in summary, overall, in short, in other words —（ThoughtCo 词表含 in conclusion, in short；Purdue OWL 过渡元素表含 in conclusion, in other words，verified live）
- **Alternatives 选择/否则**：otherwise, alternatively —（EnglishClub 表将 otherwise, alternatively 单列 alternatives，index）

## 2.3 核心标点规则（本主题最重要部分）

**规则 A：同句连接两个独立分句 = 分号在前、逗号在后。**
> Purdue OWL（verified live）："Use a semicolon to join two independent clauses when the second clause begins with a conjunctive adverb (however, therefore, moreover, furthermore, thus, meanwhile, nonetheless, otherwise) or a transition (in fact, for example, that is, for instance, in addition, in other words, on the other hand, even so)." 例："Terrorism in the United States has become a recent concern; in fact, the concern for America's safety has led to an awareness of global terrorism."
> QuillBot（verified live）："To link two independent clauses in a single sentence with a conjunctive adverb, you need to use a semicolon after the first independent clause and follow the conjunctive adverb with a comma." 例："She had studied nonstop for months; consequently, she passed her exam with flying colors."

**公式**（SJSU Writing Center，index）："Independent clause; [conjunctive adverb], independent clause."

**规则 B：拆成两句同样正确**——句号 + 连接副词 + 逗号：The road was blocked. Therefore, we turned back. 分号表示关系更紧、句号表示停顿更强，用哪个由写作者决定（QuillBot："Using a semicolon instead of a period before the conjunctive adverb indicates a closer relationship between the two statements. It's usually up to the writer whether to use a period or a semicolon."）。

**规则 C：逗号拼接 comma splice（错误核心）**
> QuillBot（verified live，点名错误）："While coordinating conjunctions (e.g., 'and,' 'but') can link two independent clauses in one sentence with only a comma, conjunctive adverbs are not strong enough to be used in this way." 且："When a conjunctive adverb is used to link two independent clauses with only a comma, this is known as a comma splice. A comma splice is when a comma is used to connect two clauses that should be separated by a period or semicolon." 其正误对照：✗ "The night was cold and dark, however, Gladys was prepared." ✓ "The night was cold and dark. However, Gladys was prepared."（另可用分号版）
> Purdue OWL（verified live）："Avoid comma splices (two independent clauses joined only by a comma). Instead, separate the clauses with a period, with a comma followed by a coordinating conjunction, or with a semicolon."
> SJSU（index）："A conjunctive adverb cannot join two independent clauses with only a single comma; doing so will create a comma splice"，并给出两种改法：①逗号改分号、连接副词后加逗号；②逗号改句号、连接副词大写开头、其后加逗号。

**为什么逗号不够**（给教师的解释口径）：连接副词不是连词，它没有“粘合”的语法力量，只是第二个分句内部的一个副词成分；逗号本身也粘不住两个独立分句，于是错误产生。对照记忆：**but 是钉子（逗号+but 足够），however 只是标签（必须先有分号/句号这面墙）**——此比喻为教学转述，规则本身出自 QuillBot/Purdue 上引原文。

**规则 D：句中插入**——连接副词在分句中间作插入语时，前后各加逗号（见 2.4）。Purdue OWL 另列："Use a comma after a transitional element (however, therefore, nonetheless, also, otherwise, finally, instead, thus, of course, above all, for example, in other words, as a result, on the other hand, in conclusion, in addition)"，例："If you really want to get a good grade this semester, however, you must complete all assignments…"（verified live）。
注意小例外（QuillBot，verified live）：then、now、also 等短词在句首或与分句融合较紧时，逗号有时可省；长短语（in addition, on the other hand 等）则常规加逗号。另 however 作“无论多么”讲时不是连接副词，不加逗号：He couldn't complete the crossword however hard he tried.（QuillBot 原例）

## 2.4 位置灵活性 Mobility（与连词的决定性区别）

**移动测试**（ThoughtCo 引 Stephen Reid《The Prentice Hall Guide for College Writers》，verified live）：
> "If you are uncertain whether a connecting word is a conjunctive adverb, test by moving the connecting word to another place in the clause… Conjunctive adverbs can be moved; subordinating conjunctions (such as if and because) and coordinating conjunctions (but, or, yet, for, and, nor, so) cannot."

Cambridge 同义（verified live）：linking adjuncts "can come in any position which an adverb can occupy"，其演示句把 as a result 分别放在句首、分句中、句末三处；而 "We cannot do this with subordinating conjunctions, which must come at the beginning of the clause."（Cambridge Grammar 栏，经转引核对，index）

**however 三位置标点对照**（MIT Writing Guide，index；QuillBot/Lumen 交叉一致）：
1. 句首（分号后）："The lithium ion battery lasts longer than the NiCad battery; **therefore,** one lithium ion battery may suffice." —— 规则："Conjunctive adverbs at the beginning of a clause must be followed by a comma (and preceded by a semicolon if the clause is linked to the previous clause)."
2. 主谓之间："…; one lithium ion battery, **therefore,** may suffice." —— "Conjunctive adverbs between the subject and the verb must be both preceded and followed by a comma."
3. 句末："…; one lithium ion battery may suffice, **therefore.**" —— "Conjunctive adverbs at the end of a clause must be preceded by a comma and followed by a period."

ThoughtCo 同义例（verified live）：连接副词可出现在分句中作插入成分，"**on the other hand**" 被两逗号夹住。Lumen 例（verified live）："The first artificial hearts… caused excessive clotting and, **therefore**, uncontrolled bleeding." EnglishClub 补充（index）：表严格因果的 therefore/thus/consequently 放句中较少见，多放句首以便清楚引出结果；表对比与强调的 however/indeed/in fact 放句中最自由。
